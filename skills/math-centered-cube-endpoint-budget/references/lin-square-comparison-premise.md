# Square-maximal comparison premise from a nonlocal kernel

In the two-dimensional diamond normalization used by the source, an even \(K\in L^1(\mathbb R^2)\) with \(K\geq\mathbf1_D\) almost everywhere and \(A_\alpha K\geq0\) away from the origin gives the stated centered square maximal comparison bound \(c_2\leq\tfrac12\int K\). The caller supplies a normalization-compatible kernel and a sign certificate for its nonlocal operator.

The source's strict numerical bound also uses a concrete kernel and an external Lean certificate. This reference neither supplies that certificate nor promotes a finite grid sign check to an off-origin sign proof.

Source use. Lin, *Centred maximal constants for squares*, physical pp. 1 and 5–8, Theorem 1 and its transfer proof.
