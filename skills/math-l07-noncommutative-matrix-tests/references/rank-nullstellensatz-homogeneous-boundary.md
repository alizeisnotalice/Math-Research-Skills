# Rank–ideal boundary for homogeneous noncommutative polynomials

For square matrices \(X,Y\), \(I-XY\) and \(I-YX\) have equal rank.  The finite verifier checks this identity by exact rational row reduction.  It realizes the manuscript's nonhomogeneous counterexample \(f=xy-1\), \(g=yx-1\), where equal rank does not imply two-sided ideal membership.

For homogeneous generators over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, the cited source derives its rank-versus-ideal equivalence from the Salomon–Shalit–Shamovich homogeneous Nullstellensatz. This skill does not certify that external theorem or extend the equivalence to nonhomogeneous generators.

Source use. MechMath, *On a Rank Nullstellensatz Conjecture for Noncommutative Polynomials*, physical pp. 2–3, Theorem 2.2 and Proposition 3.1.
