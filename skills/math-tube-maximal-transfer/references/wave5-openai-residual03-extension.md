# Curved extension and elliptic capacity proposal

Proposed input: a positively curved surface packet array, declared cap/tube extension normalization, and a matching Kakeya-maximal or elliptic capacity package. The operation performs the positive tube comparison and level-set/layer-cake transfer, or the source's elliptic packet propagation. It cannot assert an extension/restriction theorem absent the specified packet, Furstenberg, continuation and atomic-array premises.

Sources: *Diagonal Fourier extension for positively curved surfaces in three dimensions*, SHA-256 `098a6acc53131ae5e7ad0405fe19a121f60a31ebd1de49290dc2733167f0cd63`, physical pp. 10 and 20; and *Elliptic capacity propagation and Fourier restriction to the sphere*, SHA-256 `669efdbef915014402795e07bfbcb958ae534b6910f05a8737a6fce0c216d394`, physical pp. 4, 40, 80 and 100.
