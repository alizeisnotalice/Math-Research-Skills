# Three-dimensional tube-to-maximal proposal

Proposed input: a finite tube family, direction/separation data, sample weights and its L3 maximal norm convention. The operation applies the source's finite sampling/maximal reduction with an explicit epsilon-loss ledger. It is not a four-dimensional Kakeya-model certificate and does not replace geometric incidence hypotheses.

Source: *The Kakeya maximal conjecture in three dimensions*, SHA-256 `49cc52aa3de89f0e91d181b00ee8050fb29635ee0da9f29145d52318e7168204`, physical pp. 3, 18--19 and 91--94.
