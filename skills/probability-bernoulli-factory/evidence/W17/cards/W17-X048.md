## Bernoulli race resampling (§§1–4.2, physical pp.1–5)

In a particle filter, write the exact nonnegative weight for particle `i` as `w_i=c_i b_i`, where `c_i>0` is known and `b_i∈[0,1]` is available as a coin. Algorithm 2 proposes `I` with probability `c_i/Σ_k c_k`, flips the `b_I` coin, accepts and returns `I` on heads, and repeats after tails. Proposition 2 (p.4) proves by conditioning on acceptance that

`P(output=i)=P(I=i,Z_i=1)/P(Z_I=1)=c_i b_i/Σ_k c_k b_k`.

The success probability per proposal is `ρ_N=Σ_i c_i b_i/Σ_i c_i`; the number of proposals/coin flips for one accepted particle is geometric with mean `1/ρ_N`. With alias preprocessing, proposals from the `c_i` distribution cost O(1), so the expected resampling work is O(N) when `ρ_N` stays bounded away from zero. The paper’s Proposition 3 and equation (13) assume `ρ_N→ρ∈(0,1)` for a CLT and an O(N) regime; the cost can diverge when the weighted average coin bias tends to zero. This is not uniform over all inputs.

Proposition 1 (p.3) compares the exact-weight PF and random-weight PF: under the stated regularity and unbiased nonnegative-weight model, both have a `√N` CLT and the exact-weight PF asymptotic variance is no larger. The Bernoulli-race scheme enables multinomial resampling from true intractable weights given coins for `b_i`. A [0,1]-valued unbiased estimator `\hat b_i` can be turned into a coin by `1{V≤\hat b_i}` using an auxiliary uniform `V`; that step’s randomization and cost must be charged in its own model.

Propositions 4–5 (pp.5, supplementary pp.10–11) give the minimum-variance unbiased estimator `(N−1)/(Σ_i C_i−1)` of `ρ_N` from `N` independent geometric counts and its asymptotic normal limit. The formula requires `N≥2`: at `N=1`, the displayed ratio is undefined when `C₁=1` and otherwise is not the stated estimator. For `N=1`, `1{C₁=1}` is a separate derived unbiased estimator because a geometric(`ρ_N`) variable equals 1 with probability `ρ_N`; it is not the printed MVUE formula or a silent extension of Propositions 4–5. Theorem 6 (statement p.5; proof pp.11–13) uses these estimates to construct an unbiased particle-filter normalizing-constant estimator.

## Model and proof coverage (§§2–5 and supplement, physical pp.2–19)

The article’s motivating models are locally optimal proposals and partially observed diffusions, where transition/likelihood weights are intractable. The main text pp.6–8 reports Gaussian and diffusion demonstrations; Supplementary pp.14–18 contains additional run-time comparisons and diffusion examples. These are applications, not a general Bernoulli-factory characterization. References occupy pp.9 and 19; the proof supplement begins p.10 and contains proofs of Propositions 3–5 and Theorem 6.

- The exact output is a categorical index with probabilities proportional to `c_i b_i`, not a scalar `f(p)`-coin.
- The geometric cost is per accepted particle and depends on `ρ_N`; a positive finite-pointwise success probability is not a uniform lower bound.
- A black-box factory for each `b_i` may have its own random input-toss cost; the paper’s Bernoulli-race count does not characterize that cost or its higher moments.
- The unbiased-estimator-to-coin step uses auxiliary uniforms and `[0,1]` bounds. Unbounded nonnegative estimators are not covered by that step as stated.
