## Relaxed Exact Algorithm and hypotheses

For the unit-volatility SDE `dX_t=α(X_t)dt+dB_t`, the Girsanov/Itô reduction gives a likelihood involving `φ=(α²+α′)/2−k`, with `φ≥0`. The authors retain differentiability/weak-solution and endpoint-density conditions, assume `(α²+α′)` is bounded below, and relax the original bounded-potential requirement: at least one tail must have finite upper limsup, i.e. the two tail limsups are not both `+∞`. If, for example, `limsup_{u→∞}φ(u)<∞`, conditioning on the path minimum `b` gives the pathwise bound `M(b)=sup_{u≥b}φ(u)<∞`; the other tail case is symmetric.

**Proposition 2 (physical PDF p.7; distribution (7)).** Conditional on Brownian endpoint `W_T=a`, the pair consisting of the path minimum `m_T=b` and its last attainment time `θ_T=t` has density proportional to

`b(b−a)/sqrt(t³(T−t)³) × exp(−b²/(2t)−(b−a)²/(2(T−t)))`,

on `b≤min(a,0)`, `0<t<T`. The proposition samples this pair by first drawing an exponential-based minimum `Z₁`, then a two-branch mixture of inverse-Gaussian variables for `V`, and setting `Z₂=T/(1+V)` for the time. **Theorem 2 (physical p.7; proof p.7)** states that, conditional on the minimum and its time, the pre- and post-minimum pieces are independent rescaled three-dimensional Bessel bridges. This supplies a finite-dimensional skeleton conditional sampler.

**EA2 / efficiency propositions (physical pp.8–11).** EA2 samples the biased-Brownian endpoint, minimum/time, a path-dependent Poisson rectangle of height `M(b)`, and the skeleton at Poisson times; accepting when no Poisson point falls below the `φ` graph gives an exact diffusion skeleton. Proposition 3 controls expected proposed points through `E[M(\hat m)]T`, divided by the acceptance probability. Proposition 4 states a sufficient integrability condition: if `α` is bounded below and `M(b)≤exp(−kb)` for `b≤b₀`, then `E[M(\hat m)]<∞`, so the algorithm has finite expected Poisson-point count. These are sufficient conditions for this diffusion algorithm, not bounds in Bernoulli-factory input tosses.

## Full manuscript map

- Front page and §1 Introduction, physical pp.1–2: diffusion model, unit-volatility transformation, Exact Algorithm background, relaxed tail condition and applications.
- §2 Retrospective rejection sampling, pp.3–5: Brownian-path likelihood, Theorem 1 Poisson representation `P(N=0|ω)=exp(−∫φ)`, EA1 for bounded `φ`, and exactness mechanism.
- §3 extension when `φ` is not bounded, pp.5–9: one-sided tail condition, Brownian minimum density (7), Proposition 2 inverse-Gaussian sampler, Theorem 2 independent Bessel(3) bridges, and EA2.
- §4 efficiency and user-impatience bias, pp.9–11: Proposition 3 proposal/count estimates; Proposition 4 exponential bound on `M(b)` yielding finite expected point count; discussion of run-time-dependent selection bias.
- §5 logistic growth model, pp.11–12: drift transformation and tail-condition verification.
- §6 exact inference applications, pp.12–18: likelihood setup, conditional transition/skeleton calculations, Monte Carlo estimators, conditioned diffusion simulations, and numerical illustrations.
- §7 conclusion, pp.18–19: scope and limitations.
- References, pp.19–20; Appendix proofs, pp.20–22: proofs for Propositions 1–4, including the minimum-time density and efficiency bounds.

The manuscript explicitly targets exact diffusion simulation/likelihood methods. Its random Poisson-point work and Brownian skeleton cost cannot be interpreted as p-coin toss moments without a separate reduction and cost analysis.
