# Stable torus quotient: bounded metric and natural-invariant section return

**Source.** *Bounded Anticanonical Metrics on klt Pairs and Torus Quotients*,
pp. 2–3, 11–12, 17–18, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Bounded-anticanonical-metrics-on-klt-pairs-and-torus-quotients-September-26-2026/main.pdf).

**Input.** Let a torus act effectively on a smooth projective rationally
connected \(Z\), and let \(L=-K_Z\) carry a smooth semipositive metric with
its natural differential linearization. Provide the source's GIT stable quotient
\((W,D)\), a resolution-level bounded psh metric certificate for
\(P=-(K_W+D)\), its klt/Q-factorial and nef ledger, and the quotient section
identity on the stable locus \(U\).

**Procedure.** Apply the source's bounded-metric nonvanishing result to obtain
\(\sigma\ne0\) in a Cartier multiple of \(P\). Pull it back to a naturally
invariant section \(\widetilde\sigma\) of the same multiple of \(L\) on
\(U\). Bounded quotient norms and smooth frame bounds make its local
coefficient bounded near \(Z\setminus U\); removable singularities extend it
across divisors and then codimension two. Equality of torus translates on the
dense stable locus gives global natural invariance.

**Output.** A nonzero naturally \(T\)-invariant anticanonical section on
\(Z\).

**Boundary.** Do not replace the natural linearization by an auxiliary
character. This ledger checks supplied quotient/divisor/metric data; it does
not construct the GIT quotient, psh metric, klt property, or nonvanishing
section.
