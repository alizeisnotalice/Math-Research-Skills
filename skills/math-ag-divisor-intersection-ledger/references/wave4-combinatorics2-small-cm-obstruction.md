# Numerical obstruction for a small Cohen--Macaulay module

Source: *A Complete Local Domain without a Small Cohen--Macaulay Module*,
SHA-256 `370f49638a5305de053dac3dc3a82a6757cc49efb70a3e2d80912cbf3ca0ed2f`,
Sections 3--7 (physical pp.7,10,14--15).

The source constructs a completed-cone local domain from an explicit polarized
surface, extends a hypothetical finite module to a regular model, balances its
slopes by modifications, and applies a divisor/Chern-character inequality to
obtain a numerical contradiction.  The transferable bookkeeping requires the
specified normal complete three-dimensional domain, the regular model and
exceptional surface, the stated ample globally generated polarization, and an
already supplied maximal Cohen--Macaulay candidate.

This is an obstruction template: it does not decide whether an arbitrary
complete local domain has a small Cohen--Macaulay module, and it does not
replace the surface-specific Chern-number calculations.
