# Integral `CH_0` under point blowup

Physical pp.5--6, Lemma 2.1 gives a compact push--pull operation: for the blowup `pi: X_tilde -> X` of a smooth projective complex surface at a point, `pi_*: CH_0(X_tilde) -> CH_0(X)` is a degree-preserving integral isomorphism, with inverse induced by the blowup formula. Thus `p_g`, `q`, and any `CH_0` assertion pass through a finite sequence of point blowups resolving a birational map.

Use only with the stated point-center and smooth-projective-surface hypotheses. The source's proof uses `pi_* O=O`, `R^1 pi_*O=0`, extension of two-forms, and the *integral* Chow blowup formula; do not downgrade or silently replace it by a rational cohomology statement.
