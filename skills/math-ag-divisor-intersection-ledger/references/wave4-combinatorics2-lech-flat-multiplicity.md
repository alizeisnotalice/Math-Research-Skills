# Flat-local multiplicity comparison boundary

Source: *Lech's multiplicity conjecture*, SHA-256
`1fbf2cdd8ba384fa9d711c68746b0114240b37cafc56a27e32bac52f179d3e31`,
Theorem 2.1 and Lemmas 2.2--2.6 (physical pp.5--13).

The source's usable contract begins with a flat local map of nonzero Noetherian
local rings and compares the Hilbert--Samuel multiplicities \(e(R)\) and
\(e(S)\).  Its proof packages parameter-quotient calculations in a selected
complete normalization/tower algebra and uses finite-stage normalized-length,
Koszul-homology, norm, and reduction comparisons.  In particular the parameter
system, annihilator category, and finite generically separable base cannot be
dropped from any imported sublemma.

This reference records the reduction infrastructure behind the claimed
inequality \(e(R)\le e(S)\); it does not implement multiplicity computation or
verify flatness of a presented ring map.
