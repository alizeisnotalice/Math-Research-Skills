# Common-resolution negativity transfer

Sources: *Log abundance in characteristic zero*, SHA
`87b191f75e8c852683847be18fb99e2a226e11e55967624c8d2bbba41c509be0`,
pp.8--9; and *Minimal metrics and interior injectivity for nef adjoints*, SHA
`b2a7d738f44a3d34e5d22e0a7fb8a8d38e97b6e2858f6c77d70cb9b03193fbd6`, p.5.
Scoped reference.

Given supplied common-resolution divisor rows, record `u^*(K+Delta)=v^*(K'+Delta')+F`,
with `F` effective and v-exceptional. If the source adjoint is declared nef, the cited
negativity step sets `F=0`; this ledger does not verify nefness or construct the model.

For supplied positive Hermitian curvature matrix `theta` and covector `partial g`, the
rank-one condition `theta >= a partial g partial g^*` gives
`<theta^{-1}partial g,partial g> <= 1/a`. This is matrix arithmetic after positivity is
provided, not a complete-Kahler L2 solver.
