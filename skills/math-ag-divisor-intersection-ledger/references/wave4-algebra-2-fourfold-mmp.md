# Scoped reference. Fourfold birational comparison interfaces

The following OpenAI statements are retained as conditional birational interfaces, without proof audit.  They require actual geometric models, divisors, contractions and pullbacks; no finite divisor table alone certifies their hypotheses.

## Compact Kähler ordinary programs

Source: *Finite ordinary minimal model programs on compact Kähler fourfolds*, pinned [PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Finite-ordinary-minimal-model-programs-on-compact-Kahler-fourfolds-October-6-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `b1b9511572d6c717db55176347c5fdb90013f93df7b20fa95dd8801c86b19172`, physical pp. 1, 3--4, 23, 50.

For a compact Kähler klt fourfold pair with effective rational boundary in the stated globally Weil-Q-factorial category, an ordinary negative-ray program has ordinary steps.  A maximal program ends at a nef model when the adjoint is pseudo-effective, and otherwise at a projective Mori fibre space.  A supported use must record the pair, every negative extremal contraction, the chosen rational boundary, relative Bott--Chern dimension one, and whether the endpoint is being used only as `nef` or as the stated Mori-fibre conclusion.  This is not abundance and does not construct contractions.

## Projective lc diagram comparison

Source: *Termination for projective log canonical fourfolds with rational boundary*, pinned [PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Termination-for-projective-log-canonical-fourfolds-with-rational-boundary-September-24-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `8ac06a1f5c9a11c5b22ebdae27dd2c0b51d966e0aed19996713f7acafe737f0a`, physical pp. 3--6.

For `X^- -> Z <- X^+` with normal fourfolds, connected fibres, `X^+ -> Z` small and projective, Q-Cartier `D^-=K+B^-`, `D^+=K+B^+`, and the opposite relative ampleness signs, the normalized graph projections `u,v` carry

`H=u^*D^- - v^*D^+ >= 0`,

with support over the bad locus, exceptionalness over `X^+`, and the displayed discrepancy difference `a(xi;X^+,B^+)-a(xi;X^-,B^-)=xi(H)`.  Supply the graph, both pullbacks, ampleness signs and valuation convention before using this ledger row.

Their pinned OpenAI PDFs are respectively [lc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Termination-of-generalized-log-canonical-flips-on-compact-Kahler-fourfolds-October-7-2026/termination-generalized-lc-kahler-fourfolds.pdf) and [canonical](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Termination-of-generalized-canonical-flips-on-compact-Kahler-fourfolds-October-6-2026/termination-generalized-terminal-flips-compact-kahler-fourfolds.pdf).

They do not establish existence of flips, contractions, or a scaling program.
