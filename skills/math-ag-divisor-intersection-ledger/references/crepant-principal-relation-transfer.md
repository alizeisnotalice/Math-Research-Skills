# Crepant principal-relation transfer

Source: *Arithmetic Stein-degree bounds for log Calabi--Yau pairs*, SHA
`41101b5f371f3fde9ff08c3bcec93af68dc06ecb2fb74bf01a58b50f28a024e2`,
physical p. 4, Lemma 2.2.

When the input already gives an lc pair `(V,D)`, compatible canonical divisors,
a birational contraction or flip, and a named integer `n` with a rational
function witness for `n(K_V+D)`, the same principal-divisor identity pushes to
the new model. Its pullbacks to a common resolution agree, which is the
source's crepancy transport step. Record the exact `n` and function witness.

This does not compute canonical divisors, check that a map is a flip or
contraction, establish lc, or supply a complement/MMP theorem.
