Freeze a smooth projective source, a resolved rational fibration, its relatively algebraically closed ratio field, and every horizontal/vertical prime. The drafts use affine relations among divisors at three indices, an unbounded sequence to obtain a nonnegative horizontal slope, normalization of vertical coefficients, and a regular relative section. Regularity must cover all horizontal divisorial valuations, including higher models.

The cohomological-transfer draft states that nonzero invariant `s_i in H^0(X,M+iP)^T` for unbounded `i`, with `P=-K_X` as an ordinary line, smooth semipositivity, and `-M` pseudoeffective, give an invariant section in some `mP`; its ratio-field step uses `(s_b^(q-p))/(s_p^(b-q)s_q^(b-p))`. The exact-orders draft assumes `N_j ~ m_j L+M`, zero intersection against a fixed class, and `v(N_j)/m_j -> 0`, then states effective rational `N ~_Q L` with `v(N)=0`. The index draft defines `I_T(m)=sum_q(-1)^q dim H^q(F,(-K_F)^m)^T` for the natural linearization and states polynomiality on a divisible progression with endpoint `P(0)=I_T(0)`. Metric descent separately requires rank-one generic adjoint spaces and bounded semipositive metrics.

The ledger cannot construct the ratio field, prove pseudoeffectivity/curvature, verify generic rank one, run multiplier ideals, or conclude nonvanishing. Preserve natural versus character-shifted linearizations, integrality, and all model maps as external hypotheses.
