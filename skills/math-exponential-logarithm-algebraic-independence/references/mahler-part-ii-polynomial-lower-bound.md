# Mahler Part II: a polynomial nonvanishing bound

Let \(z\ne0\) be either the real logarithm of a positive rational number or
\(2\pi i\).  Mahler's Satz 5, read with its preceding derivation, gives a
constant \(K>1\), and for every natural \(q\) a positive constant \(C(q)\),
such that every nonzero **integer-coefficient** polynomial

\[
 R=A_0+A_1z+\cdots+A_qz^q,
 \qquad A=\max_j|A_j|\geq1,
\]

satisfies

\[
 |R|\geq C(q)A^{-K^q}.
\]

Here "ganzen rationalen Koeffizienten" is the source's terminology for
rational integers, not arbitrary elements of \(\mathbb Q\).  The preceding
page first proves \(|R|\geq\Lambda_0A^{-\lambda_0}\), then derives
\(\lambda_0\leq K^q\); this yields the displayed bound.  The inequality
glyph in the printed Satz 5 has been reported/read as reversed in the scan,
so this reference follows the preceding proof rather than copying that
ambiguous display.  The source also notes a corresponding statement for
\(\pi\).  This is an existential nonvanishing/height theorem: the constants
are not supplied in a form enabling finite numerical certification, so a
caller must not invent them or turn the theorem into an effective
irrationality measure.

der Exponentialfunktion und des Logarithmus. Teil II*, *J. Reine Angew. Math.*
**166** (1932), 137--150, SHA-256
`1f366a2b5f4f23504dd60143087fab95784aa6380ffa4b61530a79afea9f120f`.
Its physical p. 14 corresponds to original printed p. 150.  The authoritative
2019 EMS reprint is distinct: *Documenta Mathematica*, Extra Volume Mahler
Selecta, pp. 281--314; reprint p. 313/original p. 149 gives the

polynomial logarithm/exponential-bound continuation and records its
pi-transcendence application.
