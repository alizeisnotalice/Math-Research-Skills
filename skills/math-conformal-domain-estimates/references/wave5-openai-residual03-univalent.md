# Univalent inverse integral-means proposal

Proposed input: a normalized univalent map on the disk together with its dyadic annulus/inverse-first-power integral data. The operation evaluates the stated strict inverse-first-power transfer and its inverse-square endpoint/disk-to-domain consequence. It requires the source's univalent normalization and strictness closure, so it must not be invoked for arbitrary holomorphic maps or without the Brennan-domain model.

Sources: *A strict inverse-first-power bound for univalent functions*, SHA-256 `b258aff1bca50ec32ec39d26b3e501e3b31df5e4e6db2807d3073c15826c196f`, physical pp. 2, 4 and 14; and *Brennan's conjecture and sharp inverse-square integral means*, SHA-256 `27a773e887282fd97d9de50d45c1a28b335713a4d2707d613f54a6a8ebc593bd`, physical pp. 2 and 26--30.
