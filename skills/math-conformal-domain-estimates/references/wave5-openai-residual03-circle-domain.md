# Circle-domain coordinate and trace-energy proposal

Proposed finite input: a normalized circle-domain coordinate family, boundary components, compatible finite coordinate energies, and a trace/length-area ledger. The operation transfers the compatible coordinates to a compact limit and records the source's extension/removability consequence. It must keep normalization and conformal-removability hypotheses explicit; it is not a generic planar compactness routine.

Sources: *Koebe's Circle-Domain Conjecture*, SHA-256 `f0080bd36e38b2cd844e872509f286af431d8c061de1954bc2a27b18c22b1b4d`, physical pp. 12--17 and 60--61; and *Removable Boundaries and Rigidity of Circle Domains*, SHA-256 `2793106e2bc200b83a2aac02de379412ffc6355ba8c80647c5c01dfffe537a52`, physical pp. 13--17 and 22--23.
