# Entropy averaging and the van der Waerden lower bound

Source: Suvrit Sra, *A new proof of van der Waerden's inequality, and beyond*, author preprint v1, 31 physical pages; reviewed physical pp. 3--6 (with the Lorentzian extension on pp. 9--11).  Local source identity: SHA-256 `847c0df5f3dfaa5a6a87bb8c226ee0ec77ba0341d98d51ea3ab48d0ee9b247ed`.

The source proves the permanent lower bound for a positive doubly stochastic square matrix by an entropy/correlation functional and pair averaging.  Its proof uses Gibbs parameters and a reversible stochastic transition matrix, with the cofactor quadratic inequality as the decisive correlation input.  Boundary cases are handled only by the source's limiting/continuation argument.

## Use boundary

Use this as a source-preserving proof route only after fixing a square matrix, positivity versus a boundary approximation, doubly-stochastic normalization, and the required cofactor Alexandrov--Fenchel inequality.  The broader Lorentzian statement has additional M-convex-support and quadratic-contraction inertia hypotheses.  This reference neither supplies those hypotheses nor turns a numerical permanent calculation into a general proof.
