# Trace moments for generalized-sun screening

Dong, *The minimum order of a counterexample to a conjecture on integral generalized sun graphs*, version 2, uses elementary adjacency trace moments as a finite screening component. Its local PDF has SHA-256 `40208497f595e0d75f36cebdf860f96958aacb99bf965c3b269f7a7cb9b85fe2` and 18 physical pages.

Physical pp. 4–5 state \(\operatorname{tr}(A^2)=2e\) and
\[
\operatorname{tr}(A^4)=2e+4\sum_v\binom{d_v}{2}+8c_4.
\]
They also state that if the shortest odd cycles have length \(g\), then lower odd trace moments vanish and \(\operatorname{tr}(A^g)=2g c_g\). The installed checker verifies precisely these finite identities for a caller-supplied graph.

The source's minimum-order theorem uses additional imported spectral reduction and finite classifications. Those ingredients are outside this finite trace-moment interface.
