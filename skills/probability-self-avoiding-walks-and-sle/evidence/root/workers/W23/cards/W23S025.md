## Model and theorem statements

The graph is the finite `N`-dimensional hypercube `Q_N=(Z_2)^N`, volume `V=2^N`; a vertex is a binary string, and a step flips one coordinate. `c_n^(N)` counts `n`-step SAWs from a fixed origin. The susceptibility is the finite polynomial `χ_N(z)=Σ_{n=0}^{V−1}c_n^(N)z^n`. For `λ>0` with `λV^{1/2}≥1`, `z_N=z_N(λ)` is defined by `χ_N(z_N)=λV^{1/2}`, and the paper calls `μ_N=1/z_N` a connective constant even though it depends on `N` and `λ`.

**Theorem 1.1 (p. 2).** There are `λ_0>0` and `K>0` such that `c_n^(N)≤K μ_N(λ_0)^n` for all `n,N` with `λ_0V^{1/2}≥1`.

**Theorem 1.2 (pp. 2–3).** For `λ∈(0,λ_0]` and each fixed `p∈(0,1/2)`, there is `ε_p>0` such that, if `λV^{1/2}≥1` and `n≤V^p`,
`c_n^(N)=A_N μ_N^n(1+O(n^{-ε_p}))`.
Here `A_N` is independent of `n` but can depend on `λ,p`; the error constant depends on `λ,p`, not on `N,n` over the stated range. The theorem excludes `p=1/2`.

**Theorem 1.3 and expansion (pp. 3, 41–42).** For small `λ_0`, fixed `m∈N`, fixed `c>0` independent of `N`, and `χ_N(z)∈[cN^m,λ_0V^{1/2}]`,
`z=Σ_{j=1}^m a_j N^{-j}+O(N^{-m-1})`,
with universal integer coefficients independent of this `z`; the error constant may depend on `m,λ_0,c`. The paper displays
`z_N=N^{-1}+N^{-2}+2N^{-3}+7N^{-4}+39N^{-5}+O(N^{-6})`,
`A_N=1+N^{-1}+4N^{-2}+26N^{-3}+231N^{-4}+O(N^{-5})`, and hence
`μ_N=N−1−N^{-1}−4N^{-2}−26N^{-3}+O(N^{-4})`.
The `z` expansion holds for the `z_N(λ)` choice for all fixed `λ∈(0,λ_0]`; the amplitude's finite-`N` value can depend on `λ,p`, although its displayed expansion coefficients do not.

**Theorems 1.4–1.5 (p. 4).** With `β=N^{-1}+λ^2` and `z∈[0,z_N]`, the susceptibility is bounded below by `1/(λ^{-1}V^{-1/2}+1−z/z_N)` and above by the Eq. (1.13) expression with numerator `2−z/z_N` and denominator `λ^{-1}V^{-1/2}+(1−O(β))(1−z/z_N)`. For the length `L` measured in visited vertices, `[1−O(β)]χ_N(z)≤E_z^(N)L≤χ_N(z)`; at critical `z_N`, `E L=λV^{1/2}[1+O(β)]`. The text explicitly attributes the easier submultiplicative half of these bounds to transitive-graph arguments, while the other half uses a bubble condition.

The critical window around `n≈V^{1/2}` and dense phase are **conjectural discussion** in §1.4 (pp. 4–5), not conclusions of Theorems 1.1–1.5.

## Proof route and dependencies

- **Theorem 1.1:** submultiplicativity gives the connective-constant exponential bound through the reciprocal susceptibility `F_N=1/χ_N` and a Tauberian estimate. The relevant Tauberian lemma is cited, not reproved in full.
- **Theorem 1.2:** estimate the reciprocal generating function near `z_N`, use a linear approximation and coefficient extraction under the finite-volume cutoff `n≤V^p`, then obtain the relative error. The cutoff is the point where the error remains small; it does not cover `p=1/2`.
- **Lace expansion (Sections 3–6, pp. 12–36):** define hypercube Fourier transform, lace graphs, and lace coefficients; compare with hypercube random walk and prove diagram bounds. A bootstrap argument establishes convergence and the bubble/derivative estimates needed for susceptibility and expected length.
- **All-orders expansion (Section 7, pp. 36–42):** show independence of the asymptotic coefficients from the auxiliary fugacity, bound lace-expansion terms, then inductively expand the reciprocal susceptibility. The first coefficients are obtained by finite lace-graph counts listed in Eqs. (7.33)–(7.34); this is a coefficient computation inside the proof after the analytic error control, not a numerical proof of the expansion.
