## Model and main theorem

Let \(h:\mathbb R^d\to[0,\infty)\) be bounded, piecewise continuous, invariant under lattice symmetries, supported in \([-1,1]^d\), and normalized by \(\int h=1\). For large spread parameter \(L\),
\[
D(x)=\frac{h(x/L)}{\sum_{y\in\mathbb Z^d}h(y/L)},\qquad \sigma^2=\sum_x|x|^2D(x).
\]
The finite-range step distribution is \(D\). The paper treats separate models: weighted SAW (step weight \(pD\)); independent bond percolation with occupation probability \(pD(x-y)\), where the parameter \(p\) is not itself a probability; and lattice trees/animals with the paper’s bond-set weights. Their upper critical dimensions are respectively 4, 6, and 8.

- **Theorem 1.2 (physical/printed p. 6).** For the model-specific critical two-point function \(U_{p_c}(x)\), if \(d>d_c\), fix any \(\alpha>0\). There are \(A=1+O(L^{-2+\alpha})\) (depending on \(d,L\), model) and \(L_0(d,\alpha,\text{model})\) such that for \(L\ge L_0\), as \(|x|\to\infty\),
  \[
  U_{p_c}(x)=\frac{a_dA}{\sigma^2|x|^{d-2}}\left[1+O\!\left(\frac{L^{\epsilon_2+\alpha}}{|x|^{\epsilon_2-\alpha}}\right)+O\!\left(\frac{L^2}{|x|^{2-\alpha}}\right)\right],
  \quad a_d=\frac{d\,\Gamma(d/2-1)}{2\pi^{d/2}},\quad \epsilon_2=\min(\epsilon,2),
  \]
  with \(\epsilon=2(d-4)\) for SAW, \(d-6\) for percolation, and \(d-8\) for trees/animals. The source says the constants in the error terms and in \(A-1\) depend on \(\alpha\). The theorem is about sufficiently spread-out finite-range models above the relevant upper critical dimension; it is not a nearest-neighbour low-dimensional theorem.
- **Corollary 1.3 (p. 6).** Above the same critical dimensions and for large \(L\), the SAW bubble condition, percolation triangle condition, and tree/animal square conditions hold. **Corollary 1.5 (p. 7)** gives \(1\le p_c\le1+O(L^{-2+\alpha})\) only for SAW and percolation; it does not state that critical-point bound for trees/animals.

## Proof chain and hypotheses that cannot be dropped

The lace identity is \(G_z=\delta+\Pi_z+zD*G_z+\Pi_z*zD*G_z\) (Eq. (1.24), physical p. 12; model conversion is (1.22)–(1.23)). The proof assembles four ingredients: the random-walk asymptotic (Prop. 1.6, pp. 10–11), convolution estimates (Prop. 1.7, pp. 10–11), model-specific diagram bounds (Prop. 1.8, pp. 11–12; proofs in §4), and a model-independent error-convolution estimate (Prop. 1.9, p. 12; proof in §7). The bootstrap in §2 (pp. 12–18) establishes the required critical two-point decay, bounds \(p_c\), and then writes \(G_{z_c}=S_1*H\); the mass and second-moment cancellations determine \(A=\widehat H(0)=1+O(L^{-2+\alpha})\). The random-walk asymptotic is proved in §6 (pp. 41–48).

The diagram hypotheses remain model-specific. In Prop. 1.8, if \(G_z(x)\le\beta(|x|+1)^{-q}\) for \(x\ne0\), \(q<d\), \(\beta/L^{q-d}\) bounded away from zero, and \(\beta\) sufficiently small: (a) SAW, \(z\le2\), \(d/2<q<d\), gives \(|\Pi_z(x)|\le c\beta\delta_{0,x}+c\beta^3(|x|+1)^{-3q}\); (b) trees/animals require fixed \(R>0\), \(\rho^{(a)}_{p(z)}(0)\le R\), and \(3d/4<q<d\), with tail exponent \(3q-d\); (c) percolation, \(z\le2\), \(2d/3<q<d\), has tail exponent \(2q\). Substituting \(q=d-2\) gives the unified weakened \(\Pi\)-decay with positive \(\epsilon\) exactly when \(d>d_c\).

**Proposition 1.9 (p. 12), exact displayed condition.** Fix \(z\le z_c\), \(0<\gamma<1\), \(\alpha>0\), \(\kappa>0\), and \(\kappa_2=\kappa\wedge2\). Assume \(z\le C\) and \(|\Pi_z(x)|\le\gamma(|x|+1)^{-(d+2+\kappa)}\). For sufficiently large \(L\), a constant \(c=c(C,\kappa,\alpha)\), independent of \(z,\gamma,L\), satisfies
\[
|(E_z*S_{\mu_z})(x)|\le c\gamma L^{-d}\quad(x\ne0),\qquad
|(E_z*S_{\mu_z})(x)|\le c\gamma L^{\kappa_2}(|x|+1)^{-(d+\kappa_2-\alpha)}\quad(\text{all }x).
\]
The proof uses \(\mathbb Z^d\)-symmetry and the chosen cancellation of the zeroth and second moments of \(E_z\); without those moment conditions, the stated faster convolution error does not follow from the proposition.

Section map: §1 setup/results/proof overview (pp. 2–12); §2 main theorem and corollary proofs (pp. 12–18); §3 lace-expansion derivation, with percolation separately in §3.3 (pp. 18–26); §4 model-specific diagram bounds, with percolation in §4.5 (pp. 26–39); §5 convolution estimates (pp. 39–41); §6 random-walk two-point estimates (pp. 41–48); §7 error estimate and Prop. 1.9 proof (pp. 48–51); references (pp. 51–53). In the percolation branch, inclusion-exclusion and BK control the nested pivotal-bond expansion; the remainder must vanish under Prop. 1.8(c) before the lace equation is asserted at criticality.
