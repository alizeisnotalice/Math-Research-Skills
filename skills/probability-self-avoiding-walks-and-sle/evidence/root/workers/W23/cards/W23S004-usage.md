# W23S004 — Canonical-use note for Beffara's SLE trace-dimension theorem

This note reuses canonical source `PR-a55bd0ea17e1`, independently read in W08 as M0179 (full 33-page PDF; SHA-256 `2b3b24b13b5770b05590588764be1805d862ad6a9e70cb010bfc3dc4ead33ffc`). It is not a second full-text reading or an additional unique work.

For chordal SLE\(_\kappa\) in the upper half-plane, Beffara proves almost surely
\[
\dim_H\gamma([0,\infty))=\min\{2,1+\kappa/8\}.
\]
At \(\kappa=8/3\), this gives the **continuum SLE trace** dimension (4/3). It can be used only after a separate theorem establishes that the chosen, rescaled lattice SAW curves converge to chordal SLE\(_{8/3}\) in a specified curve topology with a compatible parametrization/crosscut setup. Weak convergence of curves alone also needs the appropriate continuity or separate argument to transfer Hausdorff dimension; dimension is not generally continuous under uniform convergence.

The source does not prove SAW convergence, tightness, a lattice domain-Markov property, or identification of any subsequential lattice limit. The Honeycomb observable paper W23S001 expressly leaves the SLE scaling statement conjectural. Consequently this canonical use is continuum background only, auxiliary for C107, and does not resolve C107. R03 accepted this continuum-only usage; its corrected metadata row is awaiting rereview.
