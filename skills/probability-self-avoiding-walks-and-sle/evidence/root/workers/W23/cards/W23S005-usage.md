# W23S005 — Canonical-use note for Rohde–Schramm SLE setup and trace properties

This note reuses canonical source `PR-dc86eaebf6b4`, independently read in W08 as W08-R006 (complete 51-physical-page PDF; SHA-256 `c99b1f570d8f7f2ad3aec13cdd918d81050f8cdac50bc6296e1790c06bafb622`). It is not a second full-text reading or an additional unique work.

The paper defines chordal SLE via the Loewner equation driven by \(\sqrt\kappa B_t\); Theorem 5.1 gives existence and continuity of the trace for \(\kappa\ne8\), and Theorem 6.1 proves simplicity for \(\kappa\in[0,4]\). These statements help state a candidate continuum target and distinguish trace from hull.

They are properties of an already defined continuum SLE. They do not show that the discrete honeycomb SAW has a DMP, tightness, a scaling limit, chordal boundary conditions, or an SLE identification. A lattice curve convergence proof must supply its own discrete law/conditioning argument and pass any required properties to the limit. This reuse is therefore auxiliary for C107 and cannot be counted as evidence that the scaling-limit conjecture is proved. R03 accepted this continuum-only usage; its corrected metadata row is awaiting rereview.
