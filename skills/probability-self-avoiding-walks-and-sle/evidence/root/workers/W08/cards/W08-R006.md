**Identity/version.** Steffen Rohde and Oded Schramm, arXiv:math/0106036v4, revised 20 Jan 2004; published in *Annals of Mathematics* 161 (2005), 879–920, DOI [10.4007/annals.2005.161.883](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2005.161.883). Complete 51-page physical PDF read, including the final page. SHA-256: c99b1f570d8f7f2ad3aec13cdd918d81050f8cdac50bc6296e1790c06bafb622. Independent review: pending.

## Theorems read

- **Chordal/radial setup (PDF pp. 5–7).** Chordal SLE in H is driven by ξ(t)=√κ B(t); radial SLE has an interior target. The paper says appropriately formulated results transfer between the two, citing LSW01b, Proposition 4.2, PDF p. 7. It does not assert equality in law of complete chordal and radial traces. The cited proposition was not independently read in this batch.
- **Theorem 5.1 (PDF pp. 20–21).** For κ≠8, almost surely the Loewner trace limit exists for every time, the trace is continuous, and the unbounded component of H minus the trace segment is the Loewner domain. The authors' later update says the κ=8 case was proved in LSW.
- **Theorem 6.1 (PDF pp. 23–24).** For κ∈[0,4], the trace is almost surely simple and stays in H∪{0}. The proof uses a Bessel-process boundary non-hitting argument and the Markov/scale structure to rule out self-touching at separated times.
- **Theorem 6.4 (PDF pp. 29–31).** For 4<κ<8, each fixed z∈H\{0} is swallowed almost surely; for κ outside (4,8), no such fixed z is swallowed almost surely. This is a hull/trace distinction: swallowed points lie in the hull but not on the trace. The proof combines the zero-drift local martingale h(g_t(z)−ξ_t), the swallowing-time estimates, and the earlier boundary-avoidance results.
- **Theorem 8.1 (PDF pp. 35–36).** For every nonempty bounded open A with closure contained in H, the limsup of log E N(ε,γ[0,∞)∩A)/|log ε| is 1+κ/8 if κ<8 and 2 if κ≥8. Here N is the minimum number of radius-ε disks covering the indicated trace portion. This is an expected-covering exponent, not itself a Hausdorff-dimension equality.
- **Corollary 8.2 (PDF p. 36).** For κ<8, the trace has almost-sure Hausdorff dimension at most 1+κ/8. For κ=6 this source yields only ≤7/4; Beffara (M0179) supplies the exact chordal trace dimension 7/4.
- **Theorems 8.3 and 8.6 (PDF pp. 37–41); Corollary 8.7 (PDF p. 42).** The hull-boundary covering argument defines δ(κ)=1+κ/8 for κ≤4 and δ(κ)=1+2/κ for κ>4 (Theorem 8.3, PDF p. 37); Theorem 8.6 then gives the covering estimate for every a>δ(κ), and Corollary 8.7 concludes dim_H ∂K₁≤1+2/κ for κ>4 (PDF p. 42). This is an upper bound for the boundary of the time-1 hull, not a dimension equality. At κ=6 the bound is 4/3.

## Migration limits

For SLE6, keep three facts distinct: this paper gives trace upper bound 7/4 and hull-boundary upper bound 4/3; Beffara M0179 proves the chordal trace equality 7/4; M0175 concerns the planar Brownian frontier equality 4/3. These objects are not interchangeable. A radial-to-chordal transfer cited here is local and suitably formulated. A statement about an entire radial loop-erased curve requires the exact conformal map, stopping segment, direction of absolute continuity, and a passage from local pieces to the whole curve. This paper alone does not establish a B061 Jordan arc of dimension 1 or evaluate the infimum over all such arcs.
