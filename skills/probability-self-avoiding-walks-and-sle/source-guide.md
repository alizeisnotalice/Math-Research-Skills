# W23 source guide — final R03 mother binding

This guide indexes the 30 distinct works in the portable final scope. The 30-count comprises **28 independent full-text reads plus two accepted canonical-use credits**. W23S004 and W23S005 reuse the accepted W08 full reads and are not duplicate W23 reads. W23S006 is one complete proceedings-chapter/lecture-notes contribution (73-page arXiv article; *Clay Mathematics Proceedings* 15, pp. 395–467), not a complete monograph; no whole-book read is claimed.

R03 accepted the W23 mother binding at 30 sources. The final R03 scope review passed with publication approval. This portable runtime package remains pending R07 stage review; it has not been published or installed. Legacy `pending` labels in the exact original cards and the archived mother status describe earlier review stages; see [stage status](evidence/W23/status.json), [final R03 review](evidence/review/review.json), [review-index.json](review-index.json), and [the immutable snapshot map](portable_path_bindings.json). Those old labels are not used as the final binding decision, and theorem/model caveats remain unchanged.

The original acquisition/search log is preserved verbatim at [the W23 search log](evidence/root/workers/W23/search-log.md). Each card contains exact model definitions, theorem numbers, physical page locations, proof mechanisms, constants, and source-specific caveats. Read the card before transferring a method. The source JSON records identity, version, canonical aliases, PDF hashes, and provenance.

## Cross-source interfaces

- **C107 (honeycomb SAW curve limit / SLE(8/3)):** S001 is a scalar connective-constant theorem with a parafermionic observable; S002 supplies polygon-insertion counting; graph, bridge, weighted, adsorption, hyperbolic, high-dimensional, random-map, weak-SAW and local-holomorphicity papers are auxiliary under their specific models. S004–S005 are continuum SLE inputs only. No source in this set proves lattice-curve tightness, discrete domain-Markov/conditioning, convergence in a stated curve topology, or SLE identification.
- **C108 (quenched SAW on the supercritical planar percolation cluster):** S003 proves a deterministic root-independent limsup rate and strict quenched/annealed gap for planar bond percolation. It does not prove existence or root-independence of the full almost-sure nth-root limit; the source calls this conjectural. High-dimensional percolation (S017), deterministic lattices, finite hypercubes, and spread-out models do not bridge that interface.
- **Model selection:** distinguish strict SAW from weak SAW, walk from trail, fixed Euclidean lattice from random planar maps or hyperbolic graphs, finite hypercube from infinite graph, and quenched from annealed law. Distinguish topological convergence of curves from metric/dimension conclusions. A result about scalar growth, a limsup, an upper-density-one subsequence, or a finite calculation is not a path-space limit or a full nth-root limit.
