# OpenAI SLE gauge ledgers

For a specified chordal SLE trace and stopping-time conformal-radius data, the two drafts provide separate upper-cover and lower-mass ledgers: stopped multipoint estimates, a Green-potential trace mass, and finite-batch covers. Keep the chosen gauge, stopping rule, and trace normalization in the input; the interface is not a generic Hausdorff-gauge calculator.

Sources: *An exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE*, SHA-256 `513ca9aedce0f3421f979721c2274be13c3e9f03a754e8a4ff582f528c280a53`, physical pp. 8--21 and 25--31, Propositions 3.1, 4.1 and 4.4; and *An explicit exact Hausdorff gauge for SLE*, SHA-256 `c6e7116ea33f29eeb0628a91c4dcf391bfb53696890c1379f05bd74fb3e7bb85`, physical pp. 10, 15--16 and 40, Lemma 3.1, Proposition 3.7 and Proposition 6.4.
