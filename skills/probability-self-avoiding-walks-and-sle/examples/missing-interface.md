# Claims not supplied by this source set

## C107: honeycomb SAW curve limit

A valid proof still needs a curve-space setup (parametrization and topology), tightness, a discrete conditioning/domain-Markov interface, subsequential convergence, and identification of every limit as chordal SLE(8/3). S001’s parafermionic observable and S023’s local discrete-holomorphic identities are lattice/model-specific algebra; neither proves that interface. S004’s Beffara dimension theorem and S005’s SLE trace properties apply only after the continuum law has been established.

## C108: quenched full nth-root limit

S003 proves a deterministic root-independent \(\limsup_N Z_N(v,\omega)^{1/N}\) and a strict quenched/annealed separation in dimension two for \(p\in(1/2,1)\). Its Remark 1.2 says full-limit existence is conjectural. A limsup theorem is not an almost-sure full limit. The high-dimensional result S017, deterministic graph theorems, finite hypercube, and spread-out lace expansion do not fill this two-dimensional random-environment gap.

## Other non-equivalent models or conclusions

- Random quadrangulations with GHPU metric scaling (S024) are not fixed Euclidean-lattice SAW curves; the SLE/LQG identification uses cited GM16 results.
- Finite hypercube SAW (S025) is finite-volume and its stated asymptotic window is \(n\le V^p\) for fixed \(p<1/2\); it is not an infinite percolation cluster.
- Continuous-time weak SAW (S030) penalizes intersections rather than forbidding them.
- A metric-space convergence or Hausdorff-dimension theorem does not itself prove convergence in the topology required for a lattice curve or preserve curve dimension.
