# Positive method transfer

## Exact honeycomb connective constant

For rooted nearest-neighbour strict SAW on the honeycomb lattice, S001 proves existence of the scalar connective constant by submultiplicativity and identifies it as \(\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}\). The proof uses a parafermionic observable and boundary identity at the critical fugacity. This is a valid direct claim about scalar growth on that exact lattice.

**Do not append a curve-limit conclusion.** The paper explicitly leaves convergence to SLE(8/3) conjectural.

## Graph bridge theorem under graph hypotheses

A graph-SAW problem can use S011/S013 if it supplies the exact infinite, locally finite, quasi-transitive graph and the appropriate height-function/bridgeability hypotheses. Then the source’s bridge-count or bridge-constant theorem applies in its stated scope. Report the graph action, height function, unimodularity (where required), and whether the conclusion concerns rooted or supremal counts.

**Do not infer Euclidean lattice curve convergence** from a scalar graph connective-constant identity.
