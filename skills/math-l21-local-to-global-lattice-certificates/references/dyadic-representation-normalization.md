# Dyadic representation normalization

Sources: *Unrestricted pro-modularity at the prime two*, SHA
`ef74b48557930663cab287cabd5280854f059de298e3016cc2172106eba1a1db`,
physical p. 4; and *Fontaine--Mazur modularity at the prime 2*, SHA
`3c8797c7d468cdcccbfedf719c9a758a083954ba3c3005975736eb911d26a636`,
physical p. 8.

For supplied odd dyadic character data, the first source's normalization chooses
a finite coefficient extension and an integral element `u` reducing to one,
then defines a continuous character on `Z_2^x` from its powers. The resulting
twist has the stated determinant and preserves oddness when `beta(-1)=1`.
Keep the finite extension, integrality/reduction condition, and determinant
identity in the certificate.

For a supplied continuous finite-dimensional profinite representation over
`Qbar_2`, the second source's Lemma 2.1 gives two operational steps: its matrix
entries are contained in one finite extension, and compactness of the image
allows the orbit of a standard lattice to be replaced by a stable lattice.
This is descent/integrality bookkeeping only; it neither proves modularity nor
tests irreducibility.
