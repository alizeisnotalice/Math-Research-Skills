# Two-adic determinant polynomial law

Source: *The Dimension of the Two-Adic Hecke Algebra at Odd Level*, SHA
`b15081ee4a06c6c609eee447ee2dffd69578ce3550cbcd923e7206c7443333c5`,
physical p.4. Scoped reference.

For compatible continuous functions `t,d` obtained from dense classical data, the
two-dimensional determinant on a finite sum `sum_g x_g g` is the quadratic polynomial
`sum_g d(g)x_g^2 + sum_{g<h}(t(g)t(h)-t(gh))x_gx_h`. Its symmetry uses
`t(gh)=t(hg)`; coefficientwise classical specializations supply normalization and
multiplicativity. This records a polynomial-law construction, not a representation recovery.
