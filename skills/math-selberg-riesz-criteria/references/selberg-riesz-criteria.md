# Selberg 类 L-函数的 Riesz 型判据

Gupta--Vatwani, arXiv:2211.02954v1, physical pp. 4--9 and 20--24, defines the Selberg data \((Q,\alpha,\beta,\omega)\), its weighted sum \(P_{\alpha,\beta,0}(y)\), and the subclass \(S^*\) with polynomial Euler product and rational \(\alpha\)-parameters. Theorem 2.4(c) states that the source asymptotic bound for \(P_{\alpha,\beta,0}(y)\) implies GRH under the listed Selberg axioms. Parts (a)--(b) give the corresponding GRH implications under their extra \(S^*\) and \(j_F\) conditions.
