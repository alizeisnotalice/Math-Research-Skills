# Source note: centred-coordinate identity

Source: *The maximum Manhattan length of a path through all lattice points of a box*, author manuscript (19 Nov 2026), physical pp. 1--5 of 10. Local SHA-256: `634bbf691adc91458f25e575111c9de05a983a27ccc4d0c2c325246aa6506006`.

Physical p. 1 defines the free-endpoint ordering objective. Physical p. 5 gives the centred-coordinate loss identity. The asset independently exhausts only small explicit boxes and checks that identity on the found maximizer. It deliberately excludes the source's all-box optimum formula, seed/inflation construction, Lean assertion, and Yuan-dependent bound.
