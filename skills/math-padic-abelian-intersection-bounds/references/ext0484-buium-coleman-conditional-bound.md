# Buium--Coleman bounds for a curve in an abelian variety

Dogra and Pandit, *A Buium--Coleman bound for the Zilber--Pink conjecture for curves inside abelian varieties*, local `arXiv:2608.24474v1`, physical pp. 1--4 and 19--20.

Theorem 1 assumes the stated unramified setup, \(p>2g_X\), and a generating curve. It supplies explicit reduction bounds for the codimension-at-least-two subgroup locus. The full \(X(\mathbb Q_p)\) intersection bound also assumes property \((*)\). Propositions 9 and 10 provide the degree ingredients for the source formula.

Use only with certified model, genus and degree inputs, reduction partition and property \((*)\) when the full count is requested. The route does not treat ramified discs or validate the source's arithmetic-jet and degree hypotheses.
