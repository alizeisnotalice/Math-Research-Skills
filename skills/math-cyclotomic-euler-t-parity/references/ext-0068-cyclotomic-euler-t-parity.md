# Cyclotomic Euler T-sum parity via contour residues

Source reviewed: local arXiv:2509.06706v2, *Contour Integrations and Parity
Results of Cyclotomic Euler T-Sums and Multiple t-Values*; physical pp.4 and
16--18 of the reviewed 25-page PDF.

Theorem 5.1 (pp.16--17) derives the displayed parity combination from a
contour integral. Its proof separates integer and half-integer residues and
shows that the remaining terms have order below (r). Remark 5.3 (p.18)
derives the introduction's modulo-products form from this theorem.

Theorem 1.3 about multiple t-values (p.5) instead invokes Panzer's
multiple-polylogarithm parity theorem, while Conjecture 1.2 is explicitly
unproved. Neither is included in this interface.
