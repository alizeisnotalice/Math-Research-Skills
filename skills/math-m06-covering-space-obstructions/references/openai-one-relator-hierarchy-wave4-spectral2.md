# Hyperbolic one-relator hierarchy interfaces

Scoped reference. *Baumslag--Solitar-free one-relator groups are hyperbolic*
(OpenAI, 25 Sep 2026), SHA-256
`b57883368214111506c2a050d54f486630c66b36ec467fe9cf746ed133557b93`,
physical pp. 8--18, Theorem 3.1 and Lemmas 3.2--3.11.  For the HNN extension
`G=<H,t | t a t^-1=phi(a)>`, require torsion-free hyperbolic `H`, finitely
generated free quasiconvex `A,B`, cyclic-or-contained conjugate
intersections, and strong-inertia bounds for `I=A intersection B` and
`J=t^-1 I t`.  The fixed-window ledger is
`sum_[W] rbar(Stab_G(W)) <= rbar(A)`.  Failure of hyperbolicity produces
essential algebraic annuli; after current induction, a finite list of
double-coset transition types gives a bounded current trajectory.  The
source's contradiction then gives hyperbolicity when no `BS(m,n)` occurs.

the filling criterion for the original primitive extension.

Neither interface supplies hyperbolicity, quasiconvexity, strong inertia,
the no-Baumslag--Solitar premise, filling avoidance data, or a cubulation.
