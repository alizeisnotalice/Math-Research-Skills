# Directed-diagram and choice reduction references

For a set-indexed directed AEC diagram of strong embeddings, form the disjoint-union quotient by common-successor equality and retain the given common-target compatibility; the output is its coherent direct limit. The associated ordinal Ramsey thinning applies only with the stated cardinal setup.

Scoped reference. The partition-principle reduction requires an ordinal-indexed family of surjective presentations, choice for ordinal-indexed nonempty families, and supplied bijections to nonwellorderable images. It produces the stated reverse injection for each surjection; it is not a forcing or consistency constructor.
