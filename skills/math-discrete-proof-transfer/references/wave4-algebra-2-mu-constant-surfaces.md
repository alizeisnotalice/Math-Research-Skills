# Scoped reference. Mu-constant surface-family topological right triviality

Source: OpenAI, *Topological triviality of mu-constant families of surface singularities*, pinned [PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Topological-triviality-of-mu-constant-families-of-surface-singularities-September-24-2026/main.pdf), SHA-256 `133a57483691850c2e22906e53e0b662047c1d6caf262418ac509d6b4fd1a928`, physical pp. 1--3 and 21--28.

For a holomorphic family `F:(C^3 x Delta,{0}xDelta)->(C,0)`, assume every `f_t` vanishes at the origin, has an isolated critical point there, and has constant Milnor number

`mu(f_t,0)=dim_C O_(C^3,0)/(partial f_t/partial x_1,partial f_t/partial x_2,partial f_t/partial x_3)=mu(f_0,0)`.

After shrinking representatives, the source concludes that there is a jointly continuous homeomorphism germ `Phi(x,t)=(phi_t(x),t)` with `phi_t(0)=0`, `phi_0=id`, and `f_t(phi_t(x))=f_0(x)`.  Use it only after the analytic family, isolated-critical-point condition, Jacobian-algebra Milnor numbers, and required shrinking have been established.  It is a conditional transfer statement, not an algorithm for constructing `Phi`.
