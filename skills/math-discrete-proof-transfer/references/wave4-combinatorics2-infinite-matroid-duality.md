# Self-dual infinite-matroid reduction

Source: *A Counterexample to the Infinite Matroid Packing/Covering Conjecture*,
SHA-256 `320eb5d308aabe2d7ab5d206329aed63091745888f021a77a9ad97d625477b9e`,
Lemmas 2.1--2.2 (physical pp.4--5) and Lemmas 3.3--3.4 (physical p.8).

For an infinite matroid satisfying (I1)--(I3),(IM), Lemma 2.1 records that
closure commutes with restriction, spanning is equivalent to containing a
basis, and spanning is dual to an independent complement.  Lemma 2.2 turns a
mixed packing/covering partition for two labelled self-dual matroids into a
cover by one independent set of each.  The maximal-extension axiom (IM) is
essential in both implications.

The source additionally defines an ultrafilter-dependent ordinal \(\rho\) on
large subsets modulo an ideal \(\mathcal K\).  It is invariant under
\(\mathcal K\)-small symmetric difference and monotone under modulo-\(\mathcal K\)
inclusion; Lemma 3.4 performs rank-raising thinning below continuum.  These are
set-theoretic construction hypotheses, not finite-matroid algorithms.
