# Dominant projection for abelian and toric exponential graphs

Aslanyan, Kirby and Mantova, *A geometric approach to some systems of exponential equations*, local `arXiv:2105.12679v2`, physical pp. 3--6. The published version is *International Mathematics Research Notices* 2023(5), 4046--4081, DOI `10.1093/imrn/rnab340`.

Theorems 1.4 and 1.5 give the stated existence and Zariski-density conclusions for a complex abelian variety, and for the torus case, when the algebraic subvariety in the Lie-algebra product has dominant projection to the Lie-algebra factor. In the zero-dimensional setting the paper describes sufficiently large points using a multivalued analytic map of the period lattice.

Provide the variety, exponential map, proof of dominant projection, period lattice and the branch and singular-locus control required by the selected conclusion. The theorem does not decide projection dominance, enumerate all solutions, or extend to arbitrary rotund varieties.
