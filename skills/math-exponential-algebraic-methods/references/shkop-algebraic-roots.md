# Algebraic roots under Schanuel's conjecture

Source. Ahuva C. Shkop, *Schanuel's Conjecture and Algebraic Roots of Exponential Polynomials*, arXiv:0910.2989v1, physical pp. 1, 6--10. SHA-256 `a5a1ea2ed95231967dde49deadf7f4eb7e8fec9220901f2d040dfa430040d665`.

Theorem 3 states that Schanuel's conjecture implies that an exponential polynomial in the source's Ψ-algebraic coefficient ring has finitely many algebraic zeros. Theorem 16 gives the source's refined-decomposition collapse for an algebraic zero, and Corollary 19 is the stated restricted Shapiro consequence for exponential sums.

Use only after fixing the exponential-field structure, the algebraic coefficient field, the source-defined refined exponential decomposition and the height induction. The finite-root conclusion is conditional on Schanuel. It is not a root-finding algorithm or an unconditional bound.
