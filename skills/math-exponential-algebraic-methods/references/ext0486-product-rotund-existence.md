# Additively free rotund products

Source `arXiv:2203.13767v2`, *Exponential Sums Equations and Tropical Geometry*, physical pp. 1--4 and 40.

Theorem 8.8 states that an additively free, rotund product \(L\times W\subseteq\mathbb C^n\times(\mathbb C^\times)^n\), with \(L\) linear and \(W\) algebraic, intersects the complex exponential graph. The source defines rotundity by its full family of dimension inequalities and additive freeness by exclusion of rational affine constraints.

Use only after the product decomposition, every relevant dimension inequality and additive freeness have been proved. The source proof uses amoebas and tropical compactification, with an additional real-defined branch. It supplies an existence assertion, not an effective point finder or a proof of the unrestricted exponential-algebraic closedness conjecture.
