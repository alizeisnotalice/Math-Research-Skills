# Power-map Schanuel property

Source. Jonathan Kirby, *A Schanuel property for exponentially transcendental powers*, arXiv:0810.4457v1, physical pp. 1--3. SHA-256 `56aa23e205a98b4de89bbf8bc4045e83d523deef1885b694ce355210f45a388d`.

Theorem 1.1 takes an exponentially transcendental real exponent λ and a multiplicatively independent positive tuple y. It gives the source's transcendence-degree lower bound for `(y, y^lambda)` over λ. Theorem 1.2 gives the exponential-field version used to derive it.

Exponential transcendence is a property in the specified exponential field. It is stronger than ordinary transcendence and is not certified by a finite calculation or a numerical approximation. The caller must separately establish it and the required multiplicative independence.
