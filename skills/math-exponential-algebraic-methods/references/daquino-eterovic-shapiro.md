# Polynomial-coefficient Shapiro conclusion under Schanuel

Source. Paola D'Aquino and Sebastian Eterović, *Revisiting the conjectures of Shapiro and Schanuel*, arXiv:2611.11404v1, physical pp. 1--3, 16, 22, 26. SHA-256 `503dcb7c75bc203c077e09ed5e6fd834ad3eb7d44b1cf6ecfc0cb5f902029ea9`.

Theorem 1.4 is obtained from Theorem 6.7. For the source's LEC-field satisfying Schanuel's conjecture and its irreducibility and factorization hypotheses, infinitely many common zeros of the relevant exponential polynomials yield the stated exponential-polynomial factor. The proof uses the factorization setup, a finiteness input attributed to Günaydin and the source's Bays/Mordell--Lang-type step.

The source supplies a conditional theorem interface. A caller must certify the LEC-field, Schanuel axiom, source-defined functions and all factorization hypotheses. It supplies neither an unconditional common-zero algorithm nor a finite numerical test.
