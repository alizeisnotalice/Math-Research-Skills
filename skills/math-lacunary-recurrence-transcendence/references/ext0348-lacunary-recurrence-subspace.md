# Lacunary reciprocal series criterion

Nguyen, arXiv:2009.02446v1, Theorem 1.3 on physical page 3, proves transcendence
of \(\sum_k c_{n_k}/u_{n_k}\) under a real quadratic-unit conjugate-root form,
sublinear logarithmic heights, nonvanishing selected terms and an all-index gap
\(n_{k+1}/n_k\ge c>2\). Physical pages 1--3 specialize it to Fibonacci and Lucas
denominators. The proof uses the Subspace Theorem. The strict constant and each
infinite-sequence condition are indispensable.
