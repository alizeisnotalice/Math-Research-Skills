# Lacunary reciprocal recurrence series

Use this skill for a series \(\sum_k c_{n_k}/u_{n_k}\) with a supplied real quadratic
unit \(\alpha\), conjugate \(\beta\), and exact representation
\(u_n=a_n\alpha^n-b_n\beta^n\). The source theorem applies only when the coefficient
heights are sublinear in \(n\), every selected numerator and denominator is nonzero,
and the infinite index sequence has \(n_{k+1}/n_k\ge c>2\).

For Fibonacci or Lucas denominators, give the Binet representation and prove the
strict gap condition for the entire selected sequence. A finite prefix can check indexing
and nonzero terms but cannot establish the height limit or the infinite gap condition.
Read [the theorem contract](references/ext0348-lacunary-recurrence-subspace.md).
