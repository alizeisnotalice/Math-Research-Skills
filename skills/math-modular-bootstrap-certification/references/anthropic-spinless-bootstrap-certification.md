# Spinless modular-bootstrap certification contract

Source: Claude (Anthropic), *The Spinless Modular Bootstrap*, official preprint, 131 physical pages; reviewed physical pp. 5, 9, 13--14 and 50--56.  Local source identity: SHA-256 `dcda16c21f6e14ab7bfb0ee483d57e2fc50bd6a97c98638dd5aea1e2dc18faec`.

Theorem 2 is a transfer statement, separate from the preprint's computer-assisted attainment construction.  For \(c\geq40\), if a functional \(g\in\bar A\) is feasible at \(T>0\), then every source-admissible spectrum has a primary with \(t_n<T\), hence \(\Delta_1<T+(c-1)/12\).

Here feasibility means the global sign \(g(t)\geq0\) for every \(t\geq T\) together with the strict source vacuum inequality \(g_{\rm vac}>0\).  Membership in \(\bar A\) includes the source Fourier anti-eigenfunction condition and finite Cardy-weighted absolute mass; these hypotheses are what justify applying the crossing sum rule beyond finite derivative functionals.

## Boundary

This is a conditional transfer only.  A finite grid of signs does not prove feasibility, numerical optimization does not prove \(g\in\bar A\), and the contract does not reproduce the source's explicit attainment functional, its uncomputed threshold, its interval table, or the asymptotic slope claim.
