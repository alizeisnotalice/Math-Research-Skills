# Third-kind coordinates for abelian t-modules

Source: Yen-Tsung Chen and Changningphaabi Namoijam, *On the third kind periods for abelian t-modules*, arXiv:2408.11998v1, physical pp.4–5, SHA-256 `1c14c3044b457a1cf966b32a4c760e270f598cd75dee15952e43ca78f5850d23`.

Given the stated abelian t-module, `(phi,[. ]_n)`-biderivation, and algebraicity assumptions, form its extension and period lattice. Theorem 1.2.5 places the final coordinate of a period vector in the displayed span of `tilde pi^n`, periods/quasi-periods, and quasi-logarithms; its `n=1` clause gives the explicit biderivation formula.

Keep every module, endomorphism, degree, and algebraic-point hypothesis explicit. This reference does not assert a general independence theorem.
