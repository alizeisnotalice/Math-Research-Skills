# Local elliptic comparison-period gates

Source: *Rational Elliptic Period Relations Across Realizations: Complex
Lattices, Local p-adic Periods, and Drinfeld Modules*, local SHA-256
`e4640a58dad67e7ee34f5e0a2d211d30b0b6621fc44abcbcbed8e023b14111bf`.

For an elliptic curve over a finite \(\mathbb Q_p\)-extension, the source
provides conditional-on-data, not numerical, gates. Theorem 3.4 (p. 8) states
that for \(\operatorname{End}_K(E)=\mathbb Z\), a rational quasi-period ratio
forces the holomorphic period ratio to be non-rational. Proposition 3.7 (p.
10) detects all linear relations among the four comparison entries after
reduction modulo \(t^2\). Theorems 4.2 and 4.4 (pp. 12--14) classify the
transcendence degree and normalized relation ideal after the reduction and
connected-monodromy type are certified.

Keep the curve, local field, de Rham and cycle bases, determinant normalisation
and monodromy/reduction classification explicit. The Tate calculation (pp.
16--18) is a specialized model, not a general recipe. Proposition 10.10 is
explicitly conditional on the crystalline period prediction (pp. 36--37).
