# p-adic Tate-uniformization density gate

Source. *Hensel minimality, p-adic exponentiation and Tate uniformization*, local 27-page preprint, physical pp. 1--3, 9--11. SHA-256 `631e8ec3c8e29c1b0125672f7512f999e8b25972d0965d8c0c3396af9b4af7d7`.

Theorem 3.1 constructs commuting derivations respecting empty-set-definable C1 functions in a specified 1-h-minimal theory. Corollary 3.2 specializes this to `C_p`, a Tate parameter q with positive valuation and the p-adic exponential/Tate uniformization. Theorem 1.3 gives the source's density result for an irreducible rotund subvariety of `G_m^n x E_q^n`, a p-adically dense Q in `E_q(C_p)`, and the stated blurred uniformization points.

Before use, provide the valued field, language and 1-h-minimal expansion, q with positive valuation, a certified irreducible rotund variety and the dense set. The result does not turn a finite list of p-adic samples into density, rotundity or a Schanuel certificate.
