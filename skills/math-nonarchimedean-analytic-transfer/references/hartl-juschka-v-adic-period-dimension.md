# v-adic period dimension interface

Source: Urs Hartl and Ann-Kristin Juschka, *On v-adic periods of t-motives*, arXiv:1105.6243v1, physical pp.3–4, SHA-256 `f569ea7a32451f0f6dd1345c49ff1b8241749c7b49fb3d50295c587a09064fcb`.

In the paper's v-adic difference-module category, Theorem 1.1 states representability of `Gamma`, equality of its dimension with the transcendence degree of the stated period field, and a natural group-scheme isomorphism. Section 3.1 fixes the semilinear `phi`-module convention needed to form the data.

Use only after supplying the ring, endomorphism, finite module, semilinear bijectivity, category, and period specialization. It is not a numeric transcendence computation.
