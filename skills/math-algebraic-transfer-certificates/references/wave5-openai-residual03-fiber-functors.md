# Finite symmetric tensor fiber-functor proposal

Proposed input: a finite symmetric tensor category in positive characteristic, a Deligne-product presentation, a strong symmetric monoidal functor candidate, and rational group-scheme data. The operation checks the source's formal comparison/extension conditions and records the fiber-functor output. It is a categorical data contract, not an algorithm for constructing categories or group schemes.

Source: *Fiber functors for finite symmetric tensor categories in positive characteristic*, SHA-256 `49778ddd4cc611b5380fdc7227c695f1cb2de65983cf07783cae9be22bcb7071`, physical pp. 4--6 and 9, Lemmas 2.1--2.2 and Proposition 3.2.
