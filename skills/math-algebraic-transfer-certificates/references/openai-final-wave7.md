# anisotropic-local finite-defect reduction

**Source.** *The Margulis--Platonov Conjecture over Number Fields*, pp. 3–7,
[pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-Margulis-Platonov-conjecture-over-number-fields-September-23-2026/paper.pdf).

**Input.** Let \(G\) be absolutely almost simple and simply connected over a
number field, with finite anisotropic nonarchimedean set \(A\), diagonal map
\(\delta:G(k)\to G_A=\prod_{v\in A}G(k_v)\), and a noncentral normal subgroup
\(N\) of finite index. Choose an exponent \(e\) killing \(G(k)/N\).
With \(G(k)^e\) denoting the subgroup generated by \(e\)-th powers, set
\[
R=G(k)^e,\qquad P_0=\delta(R),\qquad V_0=\delta^{-1}(P_0),\qquad V=V_0/R.
\]

**Admission/output.** Proposition 2.3 gives an explicit residual branch:
\(R\) has finite index, \(P_0\) is open normal, \(V\) is finite, and proving
\(V=1\) for each \(e\) suffices for the normal-subgroup classification. It
also permits representatives of a class in \(V\) to be arbitrarily close to
identity at prescribed finite places (or meet nonempty local opens away from
\(A\)), plus finitely many Zariski-open conditions.

**Boundary.** This reduces the abstract-closure gap; it does not eliminate the
finite defect. Coset-preserving approximation, norm-torus Hasse/weak
approximation, type-by-type finite-quotient elimination, and classification
inputs remain required.
