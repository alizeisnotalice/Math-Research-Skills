# Picard--Vessiot obstruction for bounded-singularity G-functions

The source is a published version of arXiv:2405.12568v2. Physical pp. 4--5,
Proposition 1 and Theorem 1, give the usable contract. Let \(f\) be a nonzero
solution over \(\mathbb C(x)\), with Picard--Vessiot field \(K_f\). If
\(f\in\mathbb C(x)(f_1,\ldots,f_k)\setminus\mathbb C(x)\), its
differential-Galois group is noncommutative and has no nontrivial proper
normal algebraic subgroup, then \(K_f\subseteq K_{f_i}\) for some \(i\).

The source constructs, for each finite singularity budget \(N\), a G-function
outside the rational-function field generated by solutions of equations with
at most \(N\) singularities. Its explicit witness uses an Apéry-number
G-function and a third-order equation with group \(\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb C)\).
The result concerns function representations. It does not itself rule out an
Euler--Mascheroni expression or prove a value-transcendence claim.
