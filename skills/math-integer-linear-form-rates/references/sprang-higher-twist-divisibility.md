# Higher-twist arithmetic-progression divisibility

Source: Johannes Sprang, *Infinitely many odd zeta values are irrational. By elementary means*, arXiv:1802.09410v1, physical pp.2–3, SHA-256 `e259b1c8fa64652c57bc2a1e709d96f4d3ba29fa0819d789e33e4e452008fcc1`.

For positive integers `D,n,k` and integer `i`, Lemma 1.2 gives the exact divisibility used to clear denominator factors of a higher twist:

\[
 \frac{n!}{\gcd(D^n,n!)}\;\mid\;\prod_{j=k}^{k+n-1}(Dj+i).
\]

Use the exact checker for a finite instance. This is only the arithmetic-progression integrality step; higher-twist partial fractions, Hurwitz-zeta elimination, and asymptotics require their own inputs.
