# Rank transfer through multiplication by an irrational scalar

Let \(x\notin\mathbb Q\), and suppose
\(1,y_1,\ldots,y_k\) are linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\).  Then
there is a subset of \(k-1\) of the products \(xy_i\) such that those
products together with \(1,x\) are linearly independent over
\(\mathbb Q\).

This is a finite linear-algebra gate.  If every choice of \(k-1\) products
gave a rational relation with \(1,x\), compare two such relations omitting
different products.  A relation with zero constant term contradicts the
independence of \(1,y_i\); otherwise eliminate the constant terms.  Dividing
the result by \(x\) yields a nonzero rational relation among
\(1,y_1,\ldots,y_k\), a contradiction.

The gate requires independently established irrationality of \(x\) and an
exact independence proof for the listed \(y_i\).  It is not a method for
establishing either premise, and it makes no statement about a conjectural
MZV basis.
