# Published odd-zeta span lower bound

Stéphane Fischler, *Linear independence of odd zeta values using
Siegel's lemma*, Journal of the London Mathematical Society 113 (2026),
e70535, DOI [10.1112/jlms.70535](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.70535).
The local 46-page preprint is the source version inspected here.

For every sufficiently large odd integer \(s\), Theorem 1 gives
\[
 \dim_{\mathbb Q}\operatorname{Span}_{\mathbb Q}
 \{1,\zeta(3),\zeta(5),\ldots,\zeta(s)\}
 \ge 0.21\sqrt{s/\log s}.
\]
This is an asymptotic span statement. It provides no concrete threshold,
no certificate for a prescribed finite \(s\), and no conclusion about a
particular odd zeta value.

The proof route uses non-explicit coefficients from Siegel's lemma, a
refined linear-independence criterion, and a Shidlovskii-type multiplicity
estimate. Use this source only when its published theorem is the required
conclusion. For an effective finite certificate, supply a separate sequence
of integer linear forms, its height and remainder bounds, and the relevant
nonvanishing proof.
