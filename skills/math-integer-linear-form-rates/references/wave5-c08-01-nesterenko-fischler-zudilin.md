# A Minkowski bound behind a linear-independence criterion

Source: Chantanasiri Amarisa, *On the criteria for linear independence of Nesterenko, Fischler and Zudilin*, **Chamchuri Journal of Mathematics** 1 (2009), 1-13; local PDF SHA-256 \`3afe3013c605a88ac90c5b8b7d6e2df25b08f139c19ffc9fbea2593aa92e0e5e\`, physical pp. 1 and 4-7.  Dependency check: Proposition 2.1 is proved by constructing the displayed symmetric convex body and applying Minkowski.

Let \(L_n=\sum_{i=0}^{r_n}\ell_{i,n}X_i\) have integer coefficients, evaluated at \(\xi=(1,\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_{r_n})\).  Under the source's hypotheses
\[
\sum_i|\ell_{i,n}|\le Q_n,\quad
0<|L_n(\xi)|\le A_n^{-1},\quad
|L_{n-1}(\xi)L_n(\xi)|\le B_n,
\]
Proposition 2.1 derives, for sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
A_n\le 2^{r_n+1}(B_nQ_n)^{r_n}.
\]
Its proof uses the body \(|x_0|\le |L_n(\xi)|/2\) and
\[
|x_0\xi_i-x_i|\le |2L_n(\xi)|^{-1/r_n},
\]
whose volume is \(2^{r_n+1}\), then excludes \(x_0=0\) with the coefficient bound.

This is a finite-dimensional quantitative interface.  It does not assert linear independence unless the theorem's further asymptotic/rank hypotheses are supplied.
