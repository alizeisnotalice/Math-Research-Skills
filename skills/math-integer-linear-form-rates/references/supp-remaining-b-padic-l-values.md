# Scoped reference. p-adic L-value dimension bound

Source: Johannes Sprang, *A Linear independence result for p-adic L-values*, local arXiv:1809.07714v3 SHA-256 `64c7b70f46dda5fb673802a9bcf2efafd58ab6befa1ef5d381769712a9d524f6`, physical pp. 1--2.  The formal version was verified as *Duke Mathematical Journal* **169**(18) (2020), DOI [10.1215/00127094-2020-0043](https://doi.org/10.1215/00127094-2020-0043).

For a Dirichlet character `chi`, a number field `K` in the specified p-adic algebraic closure, every `epsilon>0`, and sufficiently large positive integer `s`, Theorem 1.1 states

`dim_K(K + sum_(i=2)^(s+1) K L_p(i, chi omega^(1-i))) >= (1-epsilon) log(s)/(2[K:Q](1+log 2))`.

The `chi=1` corollary is the corresponding `zeta_p(i)` statement.  Record the field embedding, character, epsilon and the theorem's asymptotic threshold; the theorem does not supply an effective threshold, values, Padé forms or a finite certificate for a given `s`.
