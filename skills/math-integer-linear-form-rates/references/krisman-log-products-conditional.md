# Conditional logarithm-product holonomy route

Shawn Krisman, *Products of two logarithms and arithmetic holonomy
bounds*, arXiv:2611.00703v1. The local 27-page source states that every
result depends on the unrefereed Calegari--Dimitrov--Tang preprint
arXiv:2408.15403v2.

If the caller has independently verified Calegari--Dimitrov--Tang,
Theorem 7.0.1, then Krisman's Theorem 1 yields, for each integer
\(26\le q\le53\), linear independence over \(\mathbb Q\) of
\[
 1,\quad\log(1+1/q),\quad\log(1-1/q),\quad
 \log(1+1/q)\log(1-1/q).
\]
The source derives corresponding consecutive-log-product irrationality
for \(25\le N\le31\). This card retains that statement only under the
named holonomy premise. It does not certify the premise, the claimed
interval-arithmetic monodromy computation, or the source's broader
two-parameter theorems.
