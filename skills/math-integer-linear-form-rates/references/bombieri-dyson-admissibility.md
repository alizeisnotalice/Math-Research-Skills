# Dyson admissibility prerequisite for an auxiliary polynomial

Enrico Bombieri, *On the Thue--Siegel--Dyson theorem*, Acta Mathematica 148
(1982), 255--296, physical pp. 1--2 and 10--11, Theorem 1 and Dyson's lemma.

The theorem is a feasibility obstruction for a supplied nonzero bivariate
auxiliary polynomial. It requires an admissible finite family of points,
positive degree vector \((d_1,d_2)\), and multiplicity thresholds in the
source's weighted derivative spaces. If the vanishing space is nonzero, the
weighted multiplicity sum satisfies the source inequality with its explicit
degree-ratio error term.

Use it to audit an already constructed auxiliary-polynomial argument before
turning its resulting integer forms into rate data. It does not construct the
polynomial, make the ineffective Roth/Thue threshold effective, or supply the
height, integrality, nonvanishing and remainder bounds required by this skill.
