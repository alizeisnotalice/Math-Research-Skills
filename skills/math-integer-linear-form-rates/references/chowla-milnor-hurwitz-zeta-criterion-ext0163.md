Theorem 1.5 (physical pp. 3--4) gives the logarithmic lower bound for the
antisymmetric Hurwitz-zeta span. The source constructs rational-function
forms and arithmetic/LCM properties (pp. 8--10), then uses a saddle-point
nonvanishing subsequence and its independence criterion (pp. 39--40). Fix
\(k,q,r\), retain all divisibility/height estimates, and prove the same
nonzero subsequence before calling this an integer-linear-form certificate.
