# Normalized odd-zeta forms and span dimension

Source: Tanguy Rivoal, *La fonction zêta de Riemann prend une infinité de valeurs irrationnelles aux entiers impairs*, arXiv:math/0008051v1; local PDF SHA-256 \`f9921534f4a2fc32b4579ed01e9791db3b8b2b8545f669385efd4957eaf927c6\`, physical pp. 1-4.  Dependency check: the source records the rational-function construction, integer normalization, asymptotic bounds, and the final Nesterenko-criterion substitution.

For odd \(a\ge3\), the source writes \(\delta(a)\) for the rational dimension generated by \(1,\zeta(3),\ldots,\zeta(a)\).  Its normalized forms have the shape
\[
\ell_n=p_{0,n}+\sum_{i=1}^{(a-1)/2}p_{i,n}\zeta(2i+1),
\qquad p_{i,n}\in\mathbb Z,
\]
after multiplying by the displayed \(d_{2n}^a\).  The cited asymptotic linear-form bounds provide parameters \(\alpha,\beta\); the source then applies a Nesterenko criterion with \(N=(a+1)/2\) and obtains
\[
\delta(a)\ge {f(a,r)\over g(a,r)}
\]
for \(1\le r<a/2\), with \(f,g\) explicitly displayed on p. 4.  Choosing \(r=r(a)\) yields its logarithmic lower growth.
