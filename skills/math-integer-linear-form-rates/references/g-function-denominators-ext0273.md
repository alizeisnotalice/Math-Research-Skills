# 几何 G-算子的 Taylor 分母界

Fischler--Rivoal, arXiv:1606.00706v1, physical pp. 1--5, Theorem 1, proves an lcm-exponential denominator form for a G-function annihilated by a G-operator under its local-solvability conjecture. The source makes this denominator statement unconditional when the supplied operator is geometric. The published version is *Kyushu Journal of Mathematics* 71 (2017), 287--298, DOI `10.2206/kyushujm.71.287`.

For a linear-form use, provide the operator, a proof of geometricity or the stated local-solvability input, coefficient normalization, and then separately prove integrality, nonzero forms and contraction. A generic G-function label alone does not establish the geometric case.
