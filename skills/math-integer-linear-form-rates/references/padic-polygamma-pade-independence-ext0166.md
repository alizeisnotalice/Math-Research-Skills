# p-adic polygamma Pade independence gate

Source: `arXiv:2410.06789v1`; local SHA-256
`227beae21dbe63f4fde05fd54e7cdab198f038da0f86d10c9b4a76bcf1b153d6`.

Theorems 1.4 and 1.6 (physical pp. 3--4) provide parameterised independence
conclusions. The proof builds second-kind Pade forms (pp. 19--20), arithmetic
and p-adic estimates (pp. 33--35), then uses a nonsingular determinant and
p-adic criterion (pp. 41--43). For a concrete instance retain shifts,
multiplicities, \(p,x\), the stated inequality, determinant, denominators and
valuation estimates; the parameter inequality alone is not a test.
