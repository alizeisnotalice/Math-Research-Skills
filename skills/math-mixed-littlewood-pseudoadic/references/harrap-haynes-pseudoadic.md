# Pseudo-absolute mixed Littlewood theorem

Source checked: Stéphane Harrap and Alan Haynes, *The mixed Littlewood conjecture for pseudo-absolute values*, arXiv:1012.0191v2, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1, physical pp. 1--3.  The local version corresponds to the published article in *Mathematische Annalen* 357 (2013), 941--960, DOI: 10.1007/s00208-013-0928-z.

Theorem 1 states that if \(a\ge2\), every member of the pseudo-absolute sequence \(\mathcal D=(n_k)\) is divisible only by finitely many fixed primes coprime to \(a\), and \(\log n_k\le k^\delta\) for some \(\delta>0\) and all \(k\ge2\), then every real \(\alpha\) satisfies

\[
\inf_{n\ge1}n|n|_a|n|_{\mathcal D}\lVert n\alpha\rVert=0.
\]

The stated corollary covers bounded-ratio sequences subject to its coprimality condition.  This reference intentionally keeps the theorem non-effective: the proof invokes the rigidity theorem identified in the source and Baker--Wüstholz estimates, so a finite prefix or a numerical search cannot establish the infinite hypotheses or produce witnesses.
