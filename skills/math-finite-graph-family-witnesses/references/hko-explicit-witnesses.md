# Source note: HKO finite witnesses

Source: *Answers to three problems of Hak, Kozerenko and Oliynyk on the triameter of graphs*, version 2 (September 2026), physical pp. 1--5 of 13. Local SHA-256: `a6357d250a51afa65a5d4e642358e048c2247292647bb58e5f2497c79cf5cb79`.

Physical pp. 2 and 5 give the two labelled eight-vertex edge lists and their claimed metric witnesses. The checker independently evaluates precisely those finite graphs. It does not check the minimality/uniqueness enumeration or use the external Bandelt--Mulder theorem.
