# Source note: decorated-tree polynomial signs

Source: *Arbitrarily large antichains of trees of equal order in the independence polynomial order*, author manuscript (22 Nov 2026), physical pp. 2--5 of 10. Local SHA-256: `090da46a2500847c9bb51995ad7e33c31f1aab1dd1f2633af519361e28a5c4aa`.

Physical pp. 2--4 define the gadgets and transfer recurrence. The finite checker independently carries out that recurrence and compares the listed finite family at the two rational test points. It does not infer incomparability: that needs the source's zero-free/root-interval lemma and the definition of the comparison order.
