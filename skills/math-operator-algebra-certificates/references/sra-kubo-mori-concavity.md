# Kubo--Mori scalar-curvature source contract

The source states strict concavity on \((0,\infty)^3\) for Dittmann's symmetric function \(h\).  Its route writes \(h=-1/(8M)\), proves a Stieltjes representation for \(1/M\) along positive lines, then uses reciprocal-Stieltjes concavity and homogeneity.  The analytic step depends on slit-plane continuation, a keyhole reconstruction, and the boundary-sign computation in the latter pages.  The scalar-curvature corollary additionally imports Dittmann's Theorem 3.
