# Finite positive Virasoro coefficient rule

OpenAI, *Virasoro Constraints for Projective Complete Intersections*, physical pp. 5--6, Proposition 2.1; SHA-256 `01a2df978a0bfae2ae243765734233ef1d98f825a2f0e26736b01c993c0544ca`; [PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Virasoro-Constraints-for-Projective-Complete-Intersections-September-24-2026/article.pdf).

With paired loop space, standard polarization, `ell_0=z d/dz+1/2+mu+Rz`, and `ell_k=ell_0(z ell_0)^k`, the source states that each labelled coefficient of `L_k Z_X` for `k>0` is a finite combination of: add the unit marking; modify one marked leg; add two legs with coevaluation; or remove two primary markings and pair them against `R^(k+1)`. Its quadratic Hamiltonian means at most two existing labels are nonordinary in a summand.

A finite coefficient calculation may be checked after the super-pairing, signs, mode cutoffs, and Hodge degrees are supplied. It does not define the full potential, prove Virasoro commutators, validate a degeneration/cap construction, or prove the source's constraints.
