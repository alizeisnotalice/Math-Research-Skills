# Tsallis S-divergence metric source contract

Source: Suvrit Sra, *Tsallis Metrics on Density Matrices via the S-Divergence*, author preprint v1, 14 physical pages; reviewed physical pp. 1--8.  Local source identity: SHA-256 `af9de7547106524c4e3bb1b878cd5b3a3784a37d15ab46dcb07f0e3add7f03ca`.

The reviewed theorem route treats positive-definite density matrices and the stated range \(1/2\leq\alpha<1\), with \(p=1/\alpha\).  It reduces the metric claim to a trace-power kernel and a four-point negative-type argument for an S-divergence log-determinant kernel.  The proof uses a trace-power integral representation and Schatten-norm geometry; the source also gives a failure construction beyond its range.

## Use boundary

Fix positive definiteness, normalization as density matrices, and the exact \(\alpha\) range before use.  Do not extend the source metric statement to \(p>2\), singular inputs, or a different divergence without independently verifying the integral and endpoint transfer.  No numerical matrix calculation replaces the four-point kernel argument.
