# Finite symmetry, Fock, CP, and loop-trace components

This note groups four finite algebraic interfaces; each remains bounded to its
displayed data and does not import its paper's thermodynamic or operational
conclusion.

1. *Ground-state condensation in the dilute hard-sphere gas* (OpenAI,
   24 Sep 2026), SHA-256 `3f861f3b57ec1d6ad17e2f01f1924a80bb1c48e4416471756b2257b90d2472f7`,
   physical p. 8, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Ground-state-condensation-in-the-dilute-hard-sphere-gas-September-24-2026/paper.pdf). The RMS symmetrization
   `F(X)=(|S_n|^{-1} sum_pi |f(pi X)|^2)^(1/2)` preserves the L2 norm and
   does not increase the Dirichlet form on the stated permutation-invariant
   domain. It does not prove condensation.
2. *A Fock-space inequality and the Laughlin spectral gap* (OpenAI,
   24 Sep 2026), SHA-256 `46a8f75298d339060d7a8ed72fbb7bd52e7b85e8b39f0438be55f19ae217ec0b`,
   physical p. 5, Lemma 2.1, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/A-Fock-space-inequality-and-the-Laughlin-spectral-gap-September-24-2026/A-Fock-space-inequality-and-the-Laughlin-spectral-gap-September-24-2026.pdf). Its normal-ordered square separates two-,
   three-, and four-body wedge-map terms. Use only after a finite exterior
   algebra basis and the wedge maps have been supplied; it is not a gap bound.
3. *Entanglement with zero distillable secret key in local dimension ten*
   (OpenAI, 27 Sep 2026), SHA-256 `be18f63c5a1d11a2d28822b8ed3cb5bffb96c46b16722918d0b2107542450bb6`,
   physical pp. 8–10, Lemmas 3.1–3.2, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Entanglement-with-zero-distillable-secret-key-in-local-dimension-ten-September-27-2026/paper.pdf). A represented bipartite state whose
   local maps and input transposes are CP satisfies the four-effect trace-norm
   inequality. It is not an assertion for arbitrary positive maps or states.
4. *Bloch's Law for Finite-Range Heisenberg Ferromagnets in Three Dimensions*
   (OpenAI, 5 Oct 2026), SHA-256 `4884bc0984dfd0f680c5faf0fe6fb0121f923f2aed6d33267960e887eebde860`,
   physical pp. 5–6 and 29–30, Proposition 2.1 and Lemma 6.1, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Blochs-Law-for-Finite-Range-Heisenberg-Ferromagnets-in-Three-Dimensions-October-5-2026/bloch-law-heisenberg.pdf). A finite
   exchange/seam construction gives the loop trace and spectral magnetization
   law; the ideal walk has the stated finite-range return kernel. This is not
   Bloch's-law asymptotics without the source's pin and comparison arguments.
