# Kirchberg--Rordam character criterion

Source: OpenAI, *The Kirchberg--Rordam character criterion*, 25 September 2026, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-Kirchberg-Rordam-character-criterion-September-25-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `a0e2d22982358ba17e10b17f35b88bd5b99667cbec11356d8f7400e2239fc5ea`.

**Scoped reference.** For a nonzero unital separable complex C*-algebra `A` and a free ultrafilter `omega`, the stated criterion is
\[
 F_\omega(A)\text{ has no characters}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\mathcal Z .
\]
The finite construction used by that route says that a nonzero unital characterless complex C*-algebra `D` has some `m >= 1` and a unital *-homomorphism
\[
 I(2,3)\longrightarrow D^{\otimes_{\max}m},\qquad
 I(2,3)=\{f\in C([0,1],M_2\otimes M_3):f(0)\in M_2\otimes1_3,\ f(1)\in1_2\otimes M_3\}.
\]
The operation is to keep maximal and minimal tensor products distinct, then feed the resulting finite dimension-drop map into the stated central-sequence/infinite-minimal-tensor criterion. The checked output is only that source interface, not a finite-power absorption claim. Locus: physical pp. 1--3, with the order-zero/full-positive construction overview on p. 3.
