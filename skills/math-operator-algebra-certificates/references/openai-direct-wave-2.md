# Finite Heisenberg matrix-unit sector

Source: *A Counterexample to Kaplansky's Direct-Finiteness Conjecture in Odd Characteristic*, family 197, SHA-256 `dc312586744d8d1b4fe49672ee36323fd56328f7ff3b9d8a481ddfb12b6965fe`, [official PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/A-Counterexample-to-Kaplanskys-Direct-Finiteness-Conjecture-in-Odd-Characteristic-September-26-2026/paper.pdf), physical pp. 20--21, Proposition 7.1 and Lemma 7.2.

If a field contains a primitive \(M\)-th root and \(M\) is invertible, the finite Heisenberg group on \((\mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z)^q\) gives a central idempotent sector. Fourier projections \(D_v\) and shifts define \(\Omega_{uv}\) with \(\Omega_{uv}\Omega_{st}=\delta_{vs}\Omega_{ut}\) and diagonal sum equal to the sector idempotent. The explicit representation on the coordinate basis certifies linear independence.

Input must pin the field, root, invertibility, dimension, group law, and representation. Output is a finite matrix-unit block, not a direct-finiteness counterexample or a ring-theoretic transfer without the paper's extra hypotheses.
