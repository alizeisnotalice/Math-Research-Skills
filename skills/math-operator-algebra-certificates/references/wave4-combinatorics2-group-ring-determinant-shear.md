# Group-ring idempotent shear determinant ledger

Source: *A Counterexample to the Group-Ring Determinant Conjecture*, SHA-256
`3524b6f2390f17d5af1f06d76bfa0a6560cbdc3cb6d1d316fcdf17a715d0de40`,
Lemmas 3.1--3.2 and Proposition 4.3 (physical pp.4--7).

Let \(P=P^*=P^2\in M_m(\mathbb Q[G])\), let odd \(D\) clear its denominators,
and suppose its mod-2 reduction factors \(\bar P=uv\) through \(r\) columns.
For \(q=2^k\equiv1\pmod D\), Lemma 3.1 lifts the factorization modulo \(q\),
and Lemma 3.2 builds an integral, rationally invertible block-shear matrix \(A\).
Proposition 4.3 gives
\[
 \log\det_{\mathcal N(G)}T_A=(r-\tau_m(P))\log q,
\]
with positive-square spectrum bounded away from zero.

Use only when the supplied self-adjoint idempotent, denominator clearing,
finite mod-2 factorization, and congruence choice have been checked.  The
ledger does not find those inputs or establish a group-theoretic consequence
such as nonsoficity.
