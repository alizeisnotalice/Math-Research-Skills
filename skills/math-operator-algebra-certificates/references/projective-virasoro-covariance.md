# Projective covariance for a quantized loop-space operator

OpenAI, *Virasoro Constraints under Projectivization*, physical pp. 5--7, Lemma 2.2; SHA-256 `573917aea43c708a8cbef22a209158e5d6ea0e9d71f7786967608483be4709e6`; [PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Virasoro-Constraints-under-Projectivization-October-5-2026/virasoro-constraints-under-projectivization.pdf).

The source fixes a super state space and residue pairing, defines normally ordered quadratic quantization after dilaton translation, and calls `A` projectively satisfied when `Z^{-1} op(A) Z` is descendant-independent. Given a symplectic map `M` for which both quantizations exist, Lemma 2.2 states `U_M op_eta(A) U_M^{-1}=op_eta'(M A M^{-1})+scalar`.

Thus projective satisfaction transfers in both directions, and multiplying a potential by an invertible scalar is harmless. A finite matrix calculation may verify supplied conjugacy and commutators. It cannot prove that formal actions exist coefficientwise, establish localization/continuation hypotheses, or prove a Virasoro theorem.
