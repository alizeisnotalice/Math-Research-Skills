For a split semisimple group over a global function field and a nilpotent dual-group orbit `O`, fix a Jacobson--Morozov map and the rational Satake normalization. Equation (1.3) defines `R_{r,O}` using the compact-at-every-embedding rational semisimple points of the centralizer. The subspace `C_{D,O,Q}` is then the cuspidal function subspace whose unramified Hecke characters have Satake class in `R_{q_x,O}` at every good point. Theorem 1.1 states the rational and `Q_l` direct-sum maps (1.4) over all nilpotent orbits are isomorphisms.

Operationally, preserve the pinned rational form, Haar normalization, divisor/level, all-good-place condition, and orbit label. This is a decomposition ledger for supplied Hecke data, not a finite algorithm for constructing automorphic representations.
