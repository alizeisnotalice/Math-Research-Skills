# Ordinary Lie lattice fiber

Source: *The Deligne--Drinfeld conjecture*, SHA
`5ae63f326803a634e7c6dc5684ce7062ea0ac6940a88a75c7052875382dbc762`,
physical pp.4--5. Scoped reference.

For a finite alphabet (or a fixed bounded-weight quotient), Hall--Lyndon brackets give
an integral ordinary free-Lie lattice whose reduction in characteristic two remains an
ordinary Lie subspace of the free associative algebra. Keep ordinary-Lie membership:
`x^2` can be primitive in characteristic two without being ordinary Lie.

For the declared infinitesimal-braid presentation, forgetting the last strand has free
ordinary Lie kernel on `s_i=t_{iq}` and the stated semidirect action; ordered PBW products
give the module normal form. This entry does not establish any associator or global
Deligne--Drinfeld claim.
