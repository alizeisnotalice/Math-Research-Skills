## Read full-text cards

- M0108, Auffinger–Damron–Hanson, *50 Years of First-Passage Percolation*: complete 160-page full-book manuscript read (arXiv:1511.03262v2; title page 26 Sep. 2016; later published in 2017 as University Lecture Series 68). `full_book_acquired=true`; the local PDF is not the publisher-typeset edition. Subadditive time constants, the Cox–Durrett shape theorem, geodesic/Busemann results and max-flow/min-cut are lattice-FPP statements; they do not control the shared-SIRSN fixed-origin iid-endpoint route maximum. See [card M0108](../evidence/W20/cards/M0108.md).
- M0091, Aldous–Ganesan, *True scale-invariant random spatial networks*: short axiomatic overview; not a proof of the maximum-route question. See [card M0091](../evidence/W20/cards/M0091.md).
- M0092, Aldous, *Scale-invariant random spatial networks*: exact SIRSN axioms and Open Problem 34. The one-route statistic Δ and finite p(1) do not answer the iid-endpoint supremum. See [card M0092](../evidence/W20/cards/M0092.md).
- M0093, Kahn, *Improper Poisson line process as SIRSN in any dimension*: Poisson-line SIRSN, one specified geodesic moments and truncated Poisson-pair network intensity; no fixed-origin supremum theorem. See [card M0093](../evidence/W20/cards/M0093.md).
- M0094, Kendall, *From random lines to metric spaces*: planar geodesic mean and pre-SIRSN local finiteness; keep its historical “pre-SIRSN” claim distinct from Kahn's later result. See [card M0094](../evidence/W20/cards/M0094.md).
- M0095, Aldous, *Optimal spatial transportation networks where link costs are sublinear in link capacity*: finite-city cost/efficiency optimization, contextual for spatial network design rather than SIRSN maximum integrability. See [card M0095](../evidence/W20/cards/M0095.md).
- M0096, Kendall, *Rayleigh Random Flights on the Poisson line SIRSN*: full paper on scattering, stationary relative environments, and speed-neighborhood recurrence; no A136 star-family bound. See [card M0096](../evidence/W20/cards/M0096.md).
- M0100, Aldous, *Route lengths in invariant spatial tree networks*: Theorem 2 gives a lower tail of order r/d and infinite mean for D_r, the route length of a typical unordered Poisson-point pair sampled from pair intensity conditional on Euclidean separation ≤ r. The proof on p. 9 converts long-pair counts into this typical-pair tail; it does not define a global all-pairs supremum or settle A136. See [card M0100](../evidence/W20/cards/M0100.md).
- M0102, Blanc–Curien–Kahn, *Geodesics in planar Poisson roads random metric*: full paper on no-pausing geodesics, hubs, and cut loci; it gives no route-length integrability for the A136 endpoint family. See [card M0102](../evidence/W20/cards/M0102.md).
- LIN2017HARMONIC, Lin, *Harmonic measure for biased random walk in a supercritical Galton–Watson tree*: full paper; strict dimension comparison and endpoint limits are proved, while dimension monotonicity is explicitly open. See [card LIN2017HARMONIC](../evidence/W20/cards/LIN2017HARMONIC.md).
- ROUSSELIN2017HARMONIC, Rousselin, *Invariant Measures, Hausdorff Dimension and Dimension Drop...*: full paper on flow-rule invariant densities and dimension formulas under algebraic/integrability assumptions; it does not prove bias monotonicity. See [card ROUSSELIN2017HARMONIC](../evidence/W20/cards/ROUSSELIN2017HARMONIC.md).

## Third full-text reading batch

- M0097, Aldous–Shun, *Connected Spatial Networks over Random Points and a Route-Length Statistic*: \(R=\sup_d\rho(d)\) is a supremum over distance scales of mean stretch, not a maximum over realized pairs. See evidence/W20/cards/M0097.md.
- M0098, Voss et al.: the read local 2009 Ulm manuscript is titled *Density estimation of shortest path lengths in spatial stochastic networks*. Crossref DOI metadata separately identifies the 2011 publication, *Densities of shortest path lengths in spatial stochastic networks*, *Stochastic Models* 27(1), 141–167, publisher Informa UK Limited; the official Ulm publications list matches the citation. The author manuscript is not claimed to be the typeset article. It treats a Palm-typical telecom route, not an A136 endpoint maximum. See [card M0098](../evidence/W20/cards/M0098.md).
- M0099, Coupier–Hirsch, *Coalescence of Euclidean Geodesics on the Poisson-Delaunay Triangulation*: fixed-direction coalescence and expected branch-count decay; branch counts do not bound route-length maxima. See evidence/W20/cards/M0099.md.
- M0101, Hirsch–Willhalm, *Upper Large Deviations for Power-Weighted Edge Lengths in Spatial Random Networks*: influence-zone rate and conditional condensation for edge-sum functionals under explicit graph conditions; not a SIRSN route maximum. See evidence/W20/cards/M0101.md.
- AIDEKON2014SPEED, Aïdékon, *Speed of the Biased Random Walk on a Galton–Watson Tree*: invariant-environment speed formula for the toward-root walk in the positive-speed regime; speed is distinct from harmonic-measure dimension. See evidence/W20/cards/AIDEKON2014SPEED.md.

## Fourth reading batch: tree walks, finite-ball harmonic measure, and Poisson identities

- **LYONS1990RWPT**, Russell Lyons, *Random Walks and Percolation on Trees*: the complete scanned article was read. Its finite-energy unit-flow criterion, strict biased-walk threshold, and \(p_c=1/\operatorname{br}(T)\) result are tree transience/percolation tools; the critical bias equality is not covered by the strict theorem. See [card](../evidence/W20/cards/LYONS1990RWPT.md).
- **PZ2008CLT**, Peres–Zeitouni: the complete paper was read. The marked-ray change of root has IGW law (kp_k/m), IGWR law ((m+k)p_k/(2m)), and density ((m+d_o)/(2d_o)); preserve the random denominator. The toward-root, leafless-GW model yields quenched CLTs, not harmonic-dimension monotonicity. See [card](../evidence/W20/cards/PZ2008CLT.md).
- **BAFGH2012LEAVES**, Ben Arous–Fribergh–Gantert–Hammond: the complete paper was read. In the away-root sub-ballistic regime, the raw Q-trap root-relative conductance mass has infinite mean, while bottom-relative (S_\infty) has finite expectation by Lemma 3.5 and Proposition 3.1; the limit law uses the latter. Do not transfer this to leafless or group-graph models without a proof. See [card](../evidence/W20/cards/BAFGH2012LEAVES.md).
- **CURIENLEGALL2017BALLHARM**, Curien–Le Gall: the complete paper was read. It proves a universal dimension exponent for harmonic measure on critical finite-radius GW balls and the continuum reduced-tree boundary; this is a comparator, not an infinite supercritical biased-walk boundary theorem. See [card](../evidence/W20/cards/CURIENLEGALL2017BALLHARM.md).
- **M0109**, Last–Penrose, *Lectures on the Poisson Process*: complete book acquired; the complete Chapter 4 §§4.1–4.5 (physical pp. 38–49) was read. Factorial measures sum over distinct occurrence indices, not necessarily distinct spatial values; Theorem 4.5 removes selected occurrences successively on the left. These identities justify point/pair-count expectations only; no route dependence or maximum-tail bound follows. See [card](../evidence/W20/cards/M0109.md).
