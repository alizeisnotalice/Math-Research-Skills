# Negative example: invalid transfer

Do not infer that harmonic-measure dimension is monotone in λ because the walk speed is monotone on some bias interval. These are different functions; the assumptions may also differ on leaves, offspring tails, and bias orientation. Likewise, finite mean length for one SIRSN route does not prove integrability of the maximum over a countable route family. Kahn's finite-length-intensity theorem for a truncated network joining Poisson endpoints also does not itself prove an integrable supremum of routes from one fixed origin to iid endpoints.


Do not use M0100’s typical close-Poisson-pair route D_r as a counterexample for A136: it is sampled from pair intensity conditional on separation ≤ r, not routes from one fixed source to iid endpoints. Do not turn M0096 speed-neighborhood recurrence or M0102 geodesic hub structure into a route-length tail bound.

Do not treat LPP1996 mutual singularity, a one-tree Fibonacci formula, or a speed derivative as proof that GW harmonic-measure dimension is monotone in bias; LPP1997 states that as Question 3.4. Do not transfer Aldous pair-summed route linearity or L1 shape to A136’s shared-origin iid endpoint maximum.
