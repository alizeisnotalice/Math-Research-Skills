# Omega bound for modified Dirichlet characters

M. Aymone, A. P. Chaves and M. E. Ramos, *Omega-bounds for the partial sums of some
modified Dirichlet characters II*, arXiv:2503.18228v1, physical pp. 1--10.

Theorem 1.1 (physical p. 2) applies to a modification `f` of a primitive character
`chi` at a finite set `S`, with unit-modulus values at every exceptional prime, and states

```
sum_{n <= x} f(n) = Omega((log x)^((|S|-3)/2)).
```

The proof is read through its dependencies: physical p. 4 factors the Dirichlet series as
the finite Euler factors times `L(s,chi)`; pp. 5--7 use Plancherel, the functional equation
and a zero-free estimate; p. 7 invokes a Baker-type lower bound for the independent prime
logarithms; pp. 8--10 derive a quantitative discrepancy bound and close the integral
argument. This is a preprint theorem-gate interface: it neither supplies explicit source
constants for a new character nor gives a finite certificate for the asymptotic omega claim.

The nearby Theorem 1.2 changes the exponent by combining earlier papers and an auxiliary
integer `N`; it is deliberately not admitted here.
