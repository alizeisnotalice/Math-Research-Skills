# W08-R011 — Coalescence of synchronous couplings

**Reading record:** Full 31-page PDF (PDF pp. 1–31), read. Identity verified from PDF p. 1: Burdzy–Chen, University of Washington repository copy; paper dated 2002 in the source bibliography. Review pending.

## Exact theorem

Theorem 1.1 (PDF pp. 2–3) states that for a bounded planar domain $D$, a synchronous coupling of two normally reflected Brownian motions satisfies 
\[
|X_t-Y_t|\longrightarrow0\quad\text{a.s.}
\]
if either (i) $\partial D$ is a polygon or a finite union of disjoint polygons, or (ii) $D$ is a lip domain: the region between two graphs meeting at both endpoints, with both graph functions Lipschitz with a common constant $c_0<1$, and with the additional local Lipschitz-domain condition (PDF pp. 2–3, 9–10).

The coupling has the same Brownian driver in both Skorokhod equations, so their displacement remains constant on intervals when both particles are strictly inside. In a bounded Lipschitz domain the authors construct a weak synchronous coupling by approximating with smoother domains (Theorem 2.4, PDF pp. 13–19). The lip-domain convergence theorem applies to every synchronous coupling as defined in §2; for polygonal domains the strong solution is unique in law (PDF p. 3).

## Proof interface and limits

The polygonal case is reduced to behavior near a polygon vertex; the lip-domain case uses an order/monotonicity property inherited from Burdzy–Kendall. The paper proves convergence, not finite-time meeting or a sharp exponential rate. It does not cover every bounded Lipschitz domain, and its (c_0<1) lip condition must not be weakened to arbitrary Lipschitz graphs.

The authors explicitly distinguish the smooth-domain problem and say the number of holes matters there (PDF p. 2); the subsequent smooth-domain result is W08-R003. This paper does not answer B062 for general smooth multiply-connected domains and does not supply A020’s mirror-coupling tail estimate.
