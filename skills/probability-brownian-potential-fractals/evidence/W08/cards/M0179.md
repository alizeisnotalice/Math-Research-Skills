# M0179 — The dimension of the SLE curves

**Reading record:** Entire 33-page PDF read (PDF pp. 1–33; printed pagination pp. 1421–1452, with the PDF’s reprint page numbering also shown). Title, author, journal metadata verified on p. 1. Independent review: pending.

## Theorem and scope

For the trace \(H=\gamma([0,\infty))\) of **chordal** SLE\(_\kappa\) in the upper half-plane, \(\kappa>0\), almost surely
\[
\dim_H H=\min\{2,1+\kappa/8\}
\]
(Theorem on PDF p. 2; proof across pp. 4–31). In particular the chordal SLE\(_2\) trace dimension is \(5/4\), while the chordal SLE\(_6\) trace dimension is \(7/4\). The statement concerns the SLE trace, not the frontier when \(\kappa>4\); in particular, the Brownian frontier value \(4/3\) is not the SLE\(_6\) trace dimension.

## Proof mechanism

1. (pp. 4–5) A random-set first/second-moment criterion is stated. For nested neighborhoods \(C_\varepsilon\) of a random set, a one-point estimate \(\mathbb P(x\in C_\varepsilon)\asymp\varepsilon^s\) plus a positive conditional-area condition yields the a.s. upper bound \(2-s\); the one-point and two-point upper estimate \(\mathbb P(x,y\in C_\varepsilon)\le C\varepsilon^{2s}|x-y|^{-s}\) gives a positive-probability lower bound. A zero–one law for SLE trace dimension upgrades the lower bound to a.s.
2. (pp. 6–10) For the one-point estimate, Koebe’s \(1/4\)-theorem compares distance from a fixed \(z_0\) to the hull with \(\operatorname{Im}g_t(z_0)/|g'_t(z_0)|\). A conformal recentering and time change turn the boundary angle into a diffusion
\[
 d\alpha_s=\sqrt\kappa\,dB_s+\frac{\kappa-4}{2}\cot(\alpha_s/2)\,ds,
\]
up to absorption at 0 or \(2\pi\). For \(0<\kappa<8\), \(\sin(\alpha/2)^{8/\kappa-1}\) is a positive eigenfunction of the generator with eigenvalue \(1-\kappa/8\); optional stopping gives the sharp up-to-constants interior ball-hit estimate with exponent \(1-\kappa/8\), including the boundary-angle factor (Proposition 4, PDF p. 6; derivation pp. 7–10). For \(\kappa\ge8\), the comparison with a Bessel diffusion gives space filling and dimension 2.
3. (pp. 10–28) For two separated disks, the strong Markov property alone is insufficient because the curve can create a fjord. The proof introduces separator crosscuts and harmonic measures of the two sides; Beurling’s estimate controls the crosscut scale. Lemma 8 bounds the chance of returning into a fjord by a positive power of its relative width. For two target points, discovery times record successive shrinking distances and separator scales. The “reduction” lemma forces a fjord-closing jump to reach the separator scale before another inward approach. Summing all allowable jump orders gives the two-point estimate; constants depend on \(\kappa\) (and an auxiliary small scale ratio), not on the two locations at the stated separation regime. The initial geometric setup assumes both imaginary parts exceed a fixed multiple (18) of half the separation (p. 11); conformal/harmonic-measure formulation handles the general placement.
4. (pp. 28–31) Frostman/occupation-density discussion records the resulting dimension and distinguishes the SLE hull boundary for \(\kappa>4\). The paper only proves boundary dimension for the special \(\kappa=8\) case by the UST/LERW relation; it states the general boundary exponent as conjectural in this paper.

No universal numerical prefactor for the Hausdorff measure is claimed. Dimension exponents are exact.

## Migration limits for W08

- The theorem is chordal SLE. Zhan’s W08-R007 constructs the **reversal of radial** SLE\(_2\) as a Brownian loop-erasure. A radial-to-chordal local absolute-continuity or equivalent dimension argument is an interface that must be cited/proved before combining these cards into \(\dim_H=5/4\) for that Jordan arc.
- A simple SLE\(_2\) arc contained in the Brownian trace would be an admissible candidate for B061’s infimum over Jordan arcs, but this source alone does not establish a Brownian-trace lower bound or the claimed equality with 1.
- The Brownian frontier is a different set from the trace’s collection of Jordan arcs. Do not transfer the frontier value 4/3 from M0175 to B061’s path-infimum.
