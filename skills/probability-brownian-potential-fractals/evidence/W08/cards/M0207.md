## Checked probability estimates

For three independent planar Brownian motions $Y^0,Y^1,Y^2$ started at 0, let $T^j_0$ be the first time $Y^j$ hits the unit circle and $T^j_r$ the first time it hits the circle of radius $e^r$. Retain only the path segment
\[
Y^j_r=Y^j[T^j_0,T^j_r],\qquad j=0,1,2.
\]
Equivalently, these are Brownian motions started uniformly on the unit circle and run until first hitting the circle of radius $e^r$. Set
\[
Z_r=\mathbb P\bigl[Y^0_r\cap(Y^1_r\cup Y^2_r)=\varnothing\mid Y^1_r,Y^2_r\bigr],\qquad a_r(\lambda)=\mathbb E[Z_r^\lambda].
\]
Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 3; path setup on PDF p. 2) says that for every $\lambda_0>0$ there are constants $0<c_1<c_2<\infty$, independent of $0<\lambda\le\lambda_0$ and $r\ge2$, such that
\[
c_1e^{-r\xi(2,\lambda)}\le a_r(\lambda)\le c_2e^{-r\xi(2,\lambda)}.
\]
Corollary 1.2 (PDF p. 3) gives $\mathbb P[Z_r>0]\asymp e^{-r\xi(2,0)}$, with $\xi(2,0)=\lim_{\lambda\downarrow0+}\xi(2,\lambda)$. Here $Z_r>0$ is exactly the event that $Y^1_r\cup Y^2_r$ does not disconnect the inner circle from the outer one.

The paper also proves up-to-constant estimates for excursion/extremal-distance quantities $b_r$, and explains equivalence among motion, excursion, and extremal-distance definitions. The proof uses excursion measure, restriction/conformal invariance, sub/supermultiplicativity, “nice” configurations, and pasting across annuli (§§2–6; the main conversion to $a_r$ is in §7, PDF pp. 19–22).

## Limits

This is an estimate in terms of the exponent, not an exact calculation of ξ(2,0) or a proof of a dimension formula by itself. It is a technical input used by the exponent papers, including the companion analyticity argument; for the Brownian frontier conclusion see M0205 and separately verify its analyticity dependency. It does not address B061’s infimum over arbitrary planar Jordan arcs.
