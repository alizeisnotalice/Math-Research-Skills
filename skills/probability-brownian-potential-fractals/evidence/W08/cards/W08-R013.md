## Results and hypotheses

- **Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 7).** For a proper simply connected subdomain D⊊C containing 0, the loop-erasure of simple random walk started at 0 and stopped on leaving the lattice approximation to D converges weakly, in the unparameterized path metric ρ, to the radial SLE2 image from 0 to the boundary. The theorem does not require a smooth Jordan boundary; boundary regularity issues concern endpoint/prime-end identification rather than the stated interior-target path convergence.
- **Corollary 1.2 (PDF p. 8).** The UST branch conclusion has its separately stated C¹-smooth Jordan-boundary hypothesis. Keep this stronger boundary assumption attached to the UST statement.
- **Theorem 1.3 (PDF p. 10).** The UST Peano path converges to chordal SLE8 under the theorem's C¹-smooth simple closed boundary and wired/free boundary-arc setup.
- **Theorem 3.7 (PDF pp. 27–30).** Observable/harmonic-measure estimates and a Skorokhod embedding yield convergence of the driving process.
- **Theorem 3.9 (PDF pp. 31–37).** Tightness and tameness upgrade the driving convergence to unparameterized path convergence.

## What this does not establish

The convergence theorem concerns discrete LERW scaling limits. The introduction (PDF pp. 11–14) emphasizes that planar Brownian motion has loops at every scale and no first loop, so it is a separate problem to define a deterministic continuous loop-erasure of a Brownian path. This paper is not a theorem about Brownian loop-erasure. It is supporting conformal background only; neither the Brownian Jordan-arc infimum in B061 nor a direct dimension statement for a Brownian loop-erased arc follows from it.
