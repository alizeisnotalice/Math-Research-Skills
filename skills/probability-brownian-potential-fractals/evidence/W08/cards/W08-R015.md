## Theorems and geometric assumptions

- **Theorem 1 (PDF p. 4).** In a bounded CAT(0) domain equipped with its intrinsic metric, assume the uniform exterior-sphere and interior-cone conditions specified in the paper. Then reflected Brownian motion has no shy **co-adapted** coupling.
- **Theorem 2 (PDF p. 5).** A simply connected bounded planar domain satisfying those boundary conditions has no shy co-adapted reflected-Brownian coupling. The proof invokes the paper’s cited result that every bounded simply connected planar domain is CAT(0). Convexity is not required here, but simple connectivity and the regularity conditions are.

## Proof chain

1. **Metric geometry (§2, PDF pp. 6–25).** Uniform cone and exterior-sphere conditions give finite intrinsic diameter (Lemma 10), local comparison between Euclidean and intrinsic metrics, and a continuous unique-geodesic direction field $\chi$ (Proposition 12). The intrinsic distance has the stated first-variation/Gauss-lemma gradient away from the diagonal (Proposition 14).
2. **Deterministic pursuit (§3, PDF pp. 26–31).** The lion moves along the geodesic toward the man. Lemma 16 gives the distance derivative $dL/dt=-(1-|y'|\cos\alpha)$. Proposition 17 proves uniform finite-time $\varepsilon/2$-capture in bounded CAT(0) domains under the same geometry, uniformly over initial points and the man’s measurable speed-at-most-one control. The proof bounds the lion’s curvature and compares geodesic triangles by Reshetnyak majorization to control accumulated turning.
3. **Stochastic-to-deterministic passage (§4, PDF pp. 31–38).** Write a co-adapted pair with predictable $J,K$, $J^\top J+K^\top K=I$. Add a finite-horizon drift in the geodesic pursuit direction and use Cameron–Martin–Girsanov absolute continuity; time-rescale so noise vanishes. Tightness, Skorokhod convergence and continuity of the Skorokhod map give a deterministic pursuit limit. Proposition 20 plus compactness yields a positive probability, uniform over starting pairs, of reaching every chosen $\varepsilon$-distance; Markov iteration excludes shy-ness.
4. **Further questions (§5, PDF pp. 38–41) and references (pp. 41–42).** The paper lists higher-dimensional and topology extensions as conjectural/open directions. These are not theorem statements.

The result does not cover arbitrary non-co-adapted couplings. The planar theorem requires simply connectedness; do not apply it to a multiply connected domain with holes. The CAT(0) theorem requires the stated metric and boundary hypotheses, and the article proves approach to any positive $\varepsilon$, not a sharp exponential tail rate. Review pending.
