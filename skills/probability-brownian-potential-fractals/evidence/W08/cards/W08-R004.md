## Results and proof chain

- **Definition 3.6 (PDF p. 28).** A mirror coupling is efficient exactly when its coupling-time tail estimate P(τ>t | X₀=x,Y₀=y)≤c(x,y)e^(−μ₂t) holds for some starting points x,y with φ₂(x)≠φ₂(y). Efficiency here is a property of the mirror coupling, not an optimization over all possible couplings.
- **Lemma 3.5 (PDF pp. 27–28).** If φ₂(x)≠φ₂(y) and a tail bound with exponent μ holds, then μ≤μ₂. The proof takes A={z:φ₂(z)>0}, for which the difference of the two transition probabilities has a nonzero leading term c₁(x,y)e^(−μ₂t); coupling makes that difference at most P(τ>t)≤c₂(x,y)e^(−μt). Comparing as t→∞ gives μ≤μ₂.
- **Theorem 3.7 (PDF p. 28).** For reflected Brownian motion in a triangle, mirror coupling is efficient when the triangle is obtuse. If all three angles are strictly acute and pairwise distinct, mirror coupling is inefficient. The proof uses the geometry of the reflected difference process and the spectral obstruction; the paper notes that removing the distinct-angle condition in the acute case would require additional work. Equal-angle acute triangles are not covered by the stated inefficiency conclusion.
- **Inefficiency argument, §§3.8–3.10 (PDF pp. 31–42).** It constructs a coupling of two mirror-coupled pairs with swapped starting points and compares survival probabilities to contradict efficiency. This auxiliary four-process coupling is not itself asserted to be a co-adapted or Markovian coupling. The conclusion is inefficiency of the mirror scheme in the stated case, not nonexistence of any efficient coupling.

## Normalization audit and scope

Equation (3.1), PDF p. 21, writes the spectral decay as e^(−μₙt). Example 3.1, also on physical PDF p. 21, prints μ₂=π for the unit interval and π/100 for an interval of length 100. Those are wave-number scalings; they do not match the eigenvalues (π/L)² of −Δ, nor the usual Brownian semigroup rate (π/L)²/2. Preserve the source's notation and flag the mismatch; do not silently square or rescale it. A separate local calculation is recorded in evidence/W08/calculations/A020_generator_scale.md.

For A020, first verify that the proposed coupling is the mirror coupling and that the problem's generator/eigenvalue convention matches the source. The theorem does not rule out arbitrary Markovian or non-Markovian couplings, does not cover every acute triangle, and does not settle the problem's universal existence question. No implication to B061 is used.
