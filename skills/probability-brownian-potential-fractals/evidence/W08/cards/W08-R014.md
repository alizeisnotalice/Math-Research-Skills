## Framework and theorems

The paper constructs continuous LERW in finitely connected planar domains for interior-point, prime-end and boundary-side-arc targets. It works with the paper's “almost H-domain” setup and keeps the target type and boundary regularity conditions separate.

- **Theorem 3.1 (PDF p. 9).** For fixed admissible initial data and driving function A, the Loewner equation with drift term λ∫₀ᵗ Xᵝₛ ds has a unique maximal solution. In the interior-target Brownian setting, A=√2 B and λ=2. Do not omit the specified domain/setup or claim global existence past the maximal time.
- **Theorem 3.2 (PDF pp. 10–13).** The continuous LERW is conformally invariant with the stated time change. In the simply connected case it agrees with radial SLE2 up to a deterministic linear time change (PDF p. 10).
- **Theorem 4.1 (PDF pp. 15–18).** The normalized generalized Poisson kernel is a local martingale under the theorem's interior-target coupling.
- **Theorem 4.2 (PDF p. 20).** With finite-connected D, boundary prime end 0⁺, interior target z_e, and the paper's lattice approximation, the discrete LERW and continuous LERW can be coupled to be uniformly close with high probability after reparametrization, after the path exits a neighborhood U of the starting point. For prime-end or side-arc targets, the extra flat-boundary and lattice-alignment assumptions in PDF pp. 20–21 and §§8.1–8.2 are required; do not transfer the interior-target hypothesis set to those cases.

## Proof chain and limits

The convergence proof combines the discrete martingale approximation (Proposition 6.1), Skorokhod convergence of the driving terms to √2B (Theorem 6.1, PDF p. 42), stability of the Loewner equation (Theorem 6.2, PDF p. 42), and quasi-loop/tightness estimates in §§7–8. This establishes the stated discrete-to-continuous LERW limit; it does not establish Brownian continuous loop-erasure as used in B061, and it contains no theorem evaluating the infimum of dimensions over Jordan arcs in Brownian trace.
