# Brownian geometry and potential tools

本次整理与历史阅读/审核状态的区别，以及可选本地附件的访问方式，见 [证据与迁移说明](../math-research/references/evidence.md)。

Use this skill when a proof involves Brownian ranges, their frontiers or subarcs, reflection or synchronous couplings, hitting probabilities, Hausdorff dimension, or planar SLE.

First identify the precise random set and quantify the claim: trace, frontier, cut points, Jordan arcs, or an intersection of independent ranges are different objects. For couplings, specify the driving noises, reflection rule, co-adapted/Markov requirements, domain geometry, and which marginal laws must be preserved. For potential theory, state the process, killing convention, reference point, and kernel before comparing hitting probabilities to capacity.

Read [references/method.md](references/method.md) for proof interfaces and known traps. Use [references/sources.md](references/sources.md) to locate the source cards; only claims with a card marked as read have been checked against full text. Seed records without cards are bibliographic leads, not verified theorem support.
