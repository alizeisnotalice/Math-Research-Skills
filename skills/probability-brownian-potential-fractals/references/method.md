# Proof interfaces and method map

This map organizes the 35 accepted scoped-source rows. It guides source selection; it is not a broader theorem catalogue. Exact statements and conditions are in [sources.md](sources.md) and source_admissions.json（本机附件：`references/source_admissions.json`）.

## Brownian sets, disconnection, and capacity

- **Disconnection versus nonintersection.** Define non-disconnection by the origin lying in the unbounded component of the complement; disconnection is the complementary event. Stop independent paths on their own radius exits. PR-f7109db62ff7 supplies the accepted two-arm planar radius-scale comparison. PR-58f59904e178 supplies only the large-packet Proposition 1 asymptotic after the corrected outer connector and ordered limits. Neither is a mutual-avoidance event.
- **Cut points and times.** PR-9c36cf792ab0 supports the specific cut-time and cut-point dimensions only after the sharp radius exponent, separated neighborhoods, exit/two-cell controls, repaired macro event, anchored-past protection, and accepted simultaneous Kaufman dependency are all present. The numerical exponent is not supplied by that row.
- **Energy and capacity.** State the kernel, exponent, support, and integrability range. The accepted M0224 positive-alpha energy method requires finite energy; the Gaussian negative moment condition is 0<alpha<d and time integrability requires alpha<2. Its admitted Martin comparison is limited to unkilled Brownian motion in d>=3 from 0 and compact A avoiding 0, with factor 1/2 retained. Do not substitute a planar killed-process kernel or translated target.
- **Local frontier and Jordan-arc interfaces.** PR-15114922f01b admits conditional exact-gauge surgery and measure-budget construction, with the named external gauge and no-local-double-cut hypotheses still explicit. PR-79d79b3b00c8 admits only its tree-filtration offspring/thinning interface and Lemma 6.2 local surround-or-exit estimate. Neither certifies a complete frontier theorem or a Jordan-arc infimum.

## Spectral and survival-rate bookkeeping

If the reflected Brownian generator is Delta/2 and -Delta phi=mu phi, the mode decays as exp(-mu t/2). Under generator Delta the rate is mu. If a coalesced-after-meeting pair has correct marginals and phi(x) differs from phi(y), the bounded-eigenfunction comparison yields
P(T>t) >= |phi(x)-phi(y)|/(2 ||phi||_infinity) exp(-mu t/2).
This is a lower bound on survival. It rules out a universal upper tail that decays faster than the mode, but gives no coupling construction or matching upper bound. Do not infer an A020 meeting-time tail from an eigenvalue alone.

The Brownian source scopes do not close the remaining acute-triangle spectral-tail problem. For source-by-source conditions and exclusions, read [sources.md](sources.md).
