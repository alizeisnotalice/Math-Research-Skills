# Condition-missing cases

## Scalar versus vector, and weak versus strong

**Claim.** Infer C207’s sharp vector strong-Lp constant for 1<p<infinity from AEP-007’s scalar-coordinate weak-Lp constants.

**Decision.** Stop. AEP-007 concerns one real scalar coordinate and a specific f** Lorentz quantity; C207 asks for the best Euclidean-vector strong-Lp operator norm. Neither the output norm nor the operator claim matches.

**Claim.** Infer a sharp vector weak-(1,1) constant from CZ-04’s vector upper bound 2 or CZ-05’s real scalar-coordinate claim.

**Decision.** Stop. CZ-04 proves a real-data vector upper bound and does not claim sharpness. CZ-05 is a v1 real scalar-coordinate claim; its independent proof review remains pending. Neither supplies a sharp vector endpoint.

## Weighted or martingale conclusion does not automatically transfer

**Claim.** Deduce C208’s dimension-free scalar Riesz weak-(1,1) estimate from AEP-040’s weighted A2 theorem.

**Decision.** Stop. The main theorem is weighted strong-L2 with C(T) carrying dimension and kernel data. Its weak-(1,1) building-block statement is for bounded shifts with O(u) dependence; the paper does not prove the needed dimension-free endpoint for T.

**Claim.** Pass an AEP-026 martingale weak-L1 estimate through the Brownian conditional expectation and call it a Riesz weak-(1,1) theorem.

**Decision.** Stop. The survey explicitly warns that conditional expectation need not preserve weak type. Its Riesz conclusions are strong-Lp and its projection interface does not supply the missing endpoint.

## Operator Hardy-space proof gap

**Claim.** Use AEP-EXT-014’s general analytic-semigroup Theorem 3.4 as a verified weak-(1,1) theorem.

**Decision.** Stop until the printed Proposition 3.8 formula and downstream molecule estimates are independently repaired. The self-adjoint Gaussian Theorem 1.6 has a separate source-supported proof chain under its own L2, H_L^p, doubling, heat-kernel, and atom hypotheses; do not conflate the two settings.
