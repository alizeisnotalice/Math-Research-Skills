# Held honeycomb transfer references

Scoped reference. The cylinder/bridge, disk-transfer, and marked-polygon operations require their exact rapidity, determinant/Pfaffian normalization, port, residue, positivity and renewal inputs. Their concrete outputs are respectively the source's cylinder amplitude transfer, confined-bridge mass bound, and marked one-arc moment bound; do not use them as free boundary-flux deductions.
