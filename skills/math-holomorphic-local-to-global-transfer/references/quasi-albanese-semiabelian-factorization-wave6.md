Physical p.9 builds the specific `a:X -> E` whose general fibre is `P^1` minus the prescribed finite set and gives connectedness of all fibres. Physical p.14 proves the concrete factorization: for every algebraic `f:X -> G` to a semiabelian variety, the abelian quotient is constant on the punctured-line fibres, while the torus quotient is a unit and is constant by the source's lemma; hence

`f=(f circ s) circ a`.

With a based section this identifies `Alb(X)=E`. To reuse, supply the same fibre geometry, section, and unit-constancy premise; no claim is made for arbitrary special quasi-projective varieties.
