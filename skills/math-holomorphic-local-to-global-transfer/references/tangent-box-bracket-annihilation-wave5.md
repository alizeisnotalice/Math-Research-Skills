# Tangent boxes and rational-curve bracket annihilation

Canonical screening HEAD: `2f01a39d278f8709bb4fb29c4a096db4dc0e7ff7`. Source SHA-256: `cb23bde917710f73321f4342b88dba8135aeb84b4c553f57df7d5532de73c01d`, cached OpenAI source, 16 physical pages.

For a specified splitting `TX=E1 direct-sum E2`, physical p.3, Proposition 2.1 constructs a tangent box `F:C x C -> X` around each holomorphic curve `g:C->X`, with `F|Delta=g` and the two factor tangent directions mapping into the supplied summands. The cited source constructs a formal map, rationally reconstructs it near the diagonal, then contracts exceptional curves to make it holomorphic globally on the product.

The actual integrability operation is the final p.3 argument: tangent-box transport makes the complementary target trivial on a factor rational curve; sections of `g^*E_i` that vanish at one marked point and span at another make the bracket tensor `wedge^2 E_i -> E_j` a constant section of a trivial bundle, hence zero. Rational connectedness extends this vanishing from general curves to all `X`. This is a proposed category because the installed taxonomy lacks a specified-holomorphic-distribution/tangent-box interface.
