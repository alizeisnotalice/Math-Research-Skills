# Product-chart monodromy continuation

Canonical screening HEAD: `2f01a39d278f8709bb4fb29c4a096db4dc0e7ff7`. Source SHA-256: `14d3e3f23539fd98735adb5042214f7f6eac9499a47e3303018932e00d29d333`, cached OpenAI source, 33 physical pages.

Physical p.5, Lemma 2.1 turns complementary *integrable* holomorphic subbundles into local product charts with blockwise transition maps. Physical p.17 then uses continuation of mutually inverse box germs along leaf and ambient paths. On the simply connected covers, the monodromy theorem gives mutually inverse holomorphic maps `Y1 x Y2 <-> X_tilde`, and the wrong-factor differentials vanish globally by the identity theorem.

This produces the compatible universal-cover product only after integrability, compact Kähler hypotheses, established continuation of boxes, and passage to ordinary simply connected covers. It is a proposed category; finite local charts alone do not produce the conclusion.
