# Finite smooth-cover trace descent

Canonical screening HEAD: `2f01a39d278f8709bb4fb29c4a096db4dc0e7ff7`. Source SHA-256: `5fdf543649f1469a097badf9e3cb603031110f8dff1dbacb85f49fcbc99c125d`, cached OpenAI source, 98 physical pages.

Physical p.8 gives an exact transfer interface. For a finite surjection `pi:Y -> U` from a smooth complex `n`-fold onto a normal connected neighborhood, define the trace of a locally constant section by summing its values over the fibre weighted by local generic degrees. The source proves this is locally constant and that trace after constant-section inclusion is multiplication by `deg(pi)`. Hence `Q_U` is a direct summand of `pi_*Q_Y`; proper base change then transfers the local rational-homology concentration from `Y` to `U`.

Physical p.7, Lemma 3.1 supplies one construction of such a cover from a proper equidimensional Lagrangian fibration via a general smooth complete intersection. The transfer itself is independent of that construction. Preserve finite surjectivity, normality/connectedness, smooth source, and rational coefficients.
