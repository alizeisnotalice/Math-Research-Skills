- **Self-dual random-cluster interfaces below one** (SHA `bf15…5210`, pp. 10--12): for finite graph, q>0 and positive odds, the conditional opening probability is v/(1+v) if endpoints are connected and v/(q+v) otherwise.
- **Cardy's formula for critical Poisson--Voronoi percolation** (SHA `1334…eafb`, pp. 8--10): finite even-sector path flip and cubic-root Fourier identity require a fixed oriented triangulation, independent fair colours and colour-independent masks.

These identities alone do not assert RSW, SLE, scaling, arm exponents, or an infinite-volume result.
