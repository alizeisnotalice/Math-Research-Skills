# Negative examples and missing conditions

## High marginal probability does not imply product FKG

Let (X,Y) take (1,0) and (0,1) with probability 1/2 each. Both marginals are Bernoulli(1/2), but P(X=Y=1)=0<1/4=P(X=1)P(Y=1). This explicit dependent law is not a product measure and violates positive association. Hence a block field's good-site marginals alone do not justify iid domination or product FKG.

## Joint chemical-distance bound is not automatically conditional

The Antal–Pisztora source theorem bounds P(0↔y, D(0,y)>ρ|y|₁). To divide by P(0↔y), establish a positive denominator in the same model. For another geometry or dependent law, the needed connection-probability lower bound is separate.

## Essentiality is not the uniform enhancement surgery

In AG91's two-parameter setting, ∂p r=E(Np+)−E(Np−), whereas ∂s r=E(Ns+). The original p derivative need not be nonnegative. A strict shift requires a uniform geometric comparison between the pivotal counts and a valid integration path. The reviewed source row admits only the conditional transport; Grimmett Q6.1 identifies the exterior-uniform non-touching surgery condition as a necessary methodological boundary, not a proved all-dimensional result.

## Sequential weak saturation is not synchronous time

A weak-saturation ordering can add one legal edge at a time. Graph-bootstrap rounds add all eligible edges simultaneously. For K₃ bootstrap on P₄ the source-backed synchronous trajectory takes two rounds, while the cited complete sequential schedule has three edge additions. A sequential count is not the exact round count.

## Exploration step count is not generation time

Janson's independent-mark queue exploration gives a final-size stopping identity for fixed seeds independent of G(n,p). Its adapted exploration clock is a proof device, not synchronous bootstrap generation time. Host-dependent seed selection or a dependent graph invalidates the stated fresh-mark argument.

## A finite Hamming seed need not trigger the infinite process

For the row/column-count rule with threshold 3, a fully occupied 2×2 square is closed: every exterior site sees at most two occupied sites on its row or column in total. Thus it does not grow. The finite-to-infinite rectangle theorem for the reviewed Hamming-plane source additionally requires the rectangle to contain the rule's zero-set and the exact rule to match; a finite-box check alone supplies neither premise.

## Theorem hypotheses do not merge across branches

- Duminil-Copin–Tassion: finite range is needed for spatial exponential decay, even though the sharpness threshold interface has a broader coupling class.
- Holroyd: this current row admits the π²/18 integral and convex diagonal path-cost input, not the entire hierarchy-to-threshold chain.
- Kalinichenko: the admitted weak-saturation statement uses fixed δ(F)≥2 and constant p; δ(F)=1 uses a separate route.
- Janson: seed independence from the host is part of the exact mark-array construction.
- Durrett: the one-dependent contour packing is for the specified oriented lattice and its proved separation range.

When an assumption is missing, report the interface as conditional or outside the admitted scope. Do not replace the missing proof with a finite simulation.
