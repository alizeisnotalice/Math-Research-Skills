# Motivic multiple-t regularisation boundary

Source: Steven Charlton, `arXiv:2112.14613v1`; local SHA-256
`137e4bf5d62e17da99b125956549b41d60905bbdedbad741dabafeeec38480fd`.

Theorem 1.1 (physical pp. 2--3) is an evaluation of regularised multiple
t-values in a chosen motivic convention. The basis assertions use coactions
and derivations (pp. 19--22, 30--31), then invertible level matrices and
induction (pp. 36--40). It organises a motivic calculation only after the
period convention, regularisation and coaction data are fixed; it is not a
numerical period-independence theorem.
