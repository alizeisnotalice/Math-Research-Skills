# Geometric regularized double-shuffle scope

arXiv:2604.26357v4, physical pp. 3--6, Propositions 1--5, formulates regularized
double-shuffle compatibility through multiplicative convolution, vanishing
cycles and the homological pentagon equation for the stipulated pro-unipotent
perverse-sheaf setting. It supports source-scoped use of the geometric
regularization framework. It does not evaluate an Euler--Mascheroni linear
form or turn a finite shuffle calculation into an irrationality proof.
