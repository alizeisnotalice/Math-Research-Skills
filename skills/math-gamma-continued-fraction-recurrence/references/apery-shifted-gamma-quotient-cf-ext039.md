# Shifted Apéry recurrence for a Gamma quotient

Verified source: arXiv:2501.10090v3, SHA-256 `e0901974899d5586080816194e8bccfa7b473aa4497eab44e3ab7e710bc93808`, 16 physical pages.

Physical p.3, Theorem 2.1 gives a continued fraction for `(Gamma(1/4)/Gamma(3/4))^4`; physical p.4 gives the common recurrence

`(2n+1)^2 u_{n+1} = (44n^2+1)u_n + (2n-1)^2u_{n-1}`.

The source derives it through a half-shift of the small Apéry recurrence and independently through an Euler--Beukers integral plus creative telescoping. It is usable as an exact recurrence for convergents of that Gamma quotient.

The same p.4 states the common denominators grow essentially like the square of the lcm of odd integers up to `2n-1`, too fast for its convergence rate to imply irrationality. This is not a method for Euler's constant gamma.
