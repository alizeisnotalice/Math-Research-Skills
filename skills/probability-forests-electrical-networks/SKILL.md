# Forests, electrical networks, and random walks

Use this skill for spanning forests, electrical-network observables, effective resistance, random-walk representations, and finite graph sampling. Fix the graph class, conductances, boundary/wiring, rooted or unrooted forest law, and observable before applying a source result.

Read [method routing](references/method.md) for finite/infinite and wired/free distinctions, and [source cards](references/sources.md) for theorem-specific conditions. Check that a proposed transfer preserves conductances, terminal convention, graph exhaustion, and probability measure. A finite calculation may certify only its given graph.

For a supplied finite tree response use the packaged exact checker and examples. The [positive](examples/positive.md) and [negative](examples/negative.md) cases separate connected and disconnected input. Additional finite tools have their own boundaries in [swap-invariant forest certificates](references/swap-invariant-forest-zero-one.md), [packing-preserving sparsification](references/packing-preserving-sparsification.md), and [FUSF causal sampling](references/openai-fusf-causal-sampler.md).

Historical records and unavailable source material are located through [the central evidence guide](../math-research/references/evidence.md). They do not add a free/wired or finite/infinite transfer rule.
