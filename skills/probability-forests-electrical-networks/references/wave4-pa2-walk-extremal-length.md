# Finite walk extremal length and traffic

Source: OpenAI, *Spectral convergence for critical FK--Ising planar maps*, SHA-256 `eeb5895c7058765f47abbbd62b52201464e8e6caad49ff88e49efec059ef48f4`, p. 20, (4.1)--(4.2), Lemma 4.1.

For a finite undirected multigraph and nonempty family `F` of finite walks, with edge traffic `tau_mu(e)=E_mu N_e(pi)`, the source states `EL_G(F)=inf_mu sum_e tau_mu(e)^2`, with finite-support laws sufficient. For walks between disjoint ports `A,B`, it states `EL_G(F_G(A,B))=R_eff^G(A,B)=(inf_{u|A=0,u|B=1} sum_e (d_eu)^2)^(-1)`. Keep edge occurrences, loops, port wiring, and the empty/disconnected conventions explicit; this does not imply spectral convergence.
