## Recent-status correction

The 2026 Li–Yu preprint proves the zero-one law for swap-invariant FSF/WSF events on every connected locally finite infinite network. In particular, the number of FSF components and total excessive ends are a.s. constant. This updates older open-question notes about those statistics, but it does not imply the mutual singularity asked by A025. The preprint identifies a stronger update-stability conjecture as not resolved by its theorem.
