# Square-step Fourier fibre contraction

Source: *A power saving for square-difference-free sets*, SHA-256
`5a8fe70cdef077a7a73e668e8248e60f8cf535daa53af80f5c9dedb355cb0369`,
Proposition 4.1/Lemma 4.3 (physical pp.19--21) and Lemma 7.1/Proposition 7.2
(physical pp.36--37).

For the finite reflection-positive prime law, the diagonal form satisfies the
signed Hölder estimate \(|Z_P((g_v))|\le\prod_v Z_P(g_v)^{1/d}\).  Under the
source's exact character support condition \(\widehat g_v(\xi)=0\) for
denominator \(q(\xi)\le R\), Lemma 4.3 yields conductor decay
\(R^{-\gamma}\|g_v\|_2\prod_{w\ne v}\|g_w\|_2\).

For a square-difference-free \(A\subset[N]\), a fixed residue fibre has the
exact coefficient identity (7.6).  If \(q_B\le N^\tau\), Proposition 7.2
compares it with a shorter interval \(N'=\lceil N/(M_0q_B)^2\rceil\), with
the displayed \(\lambda^d\) factor and error \(C_{tr}N^{-\sigma_0}\).
This transfer requires the prime-law, lift cutoffs, conductor bounds, and
square-difference-free premise; it is not an unconditional density bound.
