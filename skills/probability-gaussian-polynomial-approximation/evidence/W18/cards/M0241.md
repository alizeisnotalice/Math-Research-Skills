## Core result actually reduced to a proof

The proved coordinate form is Theorem 2: if X=(X_1,...,X_(n1+n2)) is a centered Gaussian vector and t_i > 0, then

\[
\mathbb P(|X_i|\le t_i\ \forall i\le n_1+n_2)
\ge
\mathbb P(|X_i|\le t_i\ \forall i\le n_1)
\mathbb P(|X_i|\le t_i\ \forall n_1<i\le n_1+n_2).
\]

The strip-intersection argument transfers this to the Gaussian correlation inequality for **closed symmetric convex** K,L subset R^d under a centered Gaussian measure. There is a source wording defect: local Theorem 1 says “closed symmetric sets” with no convexity, but the abstract states convexity and p.2 reduces sets to intersections of strips, a representation valid for closed convex symmetric sets, not arbitrary closed symmetric sets. This card records the proved convex-set scope and does not endorse the broader theorem wording.

## Proof chain and assumptions

For a positive-definite covariance matrix C, split the Gaussian coordinates into two blocks and interpolate

\[
C(\tau)=\begin{pmatrix}C_{11}&\tau C_{12}\\\tau C_{21}&C_{22}\end{pmatrix},\quad 0\le\tau\le1.
\]

At tau = 0 the blocks are independent; at tau = 1 the original covariance is recovered. Put Z_i(tau)=X_i(tau)^2/2. The rectangle probability is monotone if its derivative in tau is nonnegative. The Gaussian quadratic Laplace transform is

\[
\mathbb E e^{-\sum_i\lambda_i Z_i(\tau)}=|I+\Lambda C(\tau)|^{-1/2}.
\]

Expand the determinant as 1 + sum over nonempty J subset [n] of |C(tau)_J| product_(j in J) lambda_j. For J=J_1 union J_2 crossing the blocks, the Schur-complement calculation gives

\[
|C(\tau)_J|=|C_{J_1}|\,|C_{J_2}|\prod_r(1-\tau^2\mu_r),\qquad 0\le\mu_r\le1,
\]

so each coefficient has nonpositive derivative. Laplace inversion represents the transformed derivative as a positive sum of mixed derivatives of the multivariate gamma density h_(3,C(tau)); integration over the rectangle and integration by parts leave nonnegative boundary terms. Lemmas 4–8 justify the quadratic Gaussian transform, determinant/Schur complement identities, differentiation under the integral, gamma-density derivative identities and inverse Laplace transform. Degenerate centered Gaussian measures are covered in the stated GCI by approximation; the proof first assumes C positive definite.

## B167 use and non-implication

Role: **auxiliary Gaussian correlation fact**. It concerns dependence of symmetric convex events under a centered Gaussian measure. It has no polynomial degree parameter, no constraint Var(p) >= 1, and no estimate of E[(|p|-1)^2]. No sign-distance or sharp-constant interface is proved here.
