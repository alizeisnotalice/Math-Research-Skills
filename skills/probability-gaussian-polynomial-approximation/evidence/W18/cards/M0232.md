# M0232 — Latała, *Estimates of Moments and Tails of Gaussian Chaoses*

**Source/version.** Rafał Latała, *Annals of Probability* 34(6) (2006), 2315–2331, DOI 10.1214/009117906000000421; arXiv math/0505313v2 reprint. The local PDF has 17 physical pages; its first page identifies the title, author, journal version, and DOI. All pp. 1–17 were read.

## Model and exact statements

For a finite order-d array A=(a_(i_1,...,i_d)), the paper studies the decoupled chaos S=sum_(i_1,...,i_d) a_(i_1,...,i_d) g^(1)_(i_1)...g^(d)_(i_d), where the d Gaussian families are independent standard normals. For a partition P=(I_1,...,I_k) of [d] into nonempty blocks, ||A||_P is the corresponding tensor contraction norm, and m_p(A)=sum_(k=1)^d p^(k/2) sum_(P in S(k,d)) ||A||_P.

- **Theorem 1 (physical p. 3).** For every chaos order d and p>=2, C(d)^(-1)m_p(A) <= ||S||_p <= C(d)m_p(A), with constants depending only on chaos order d, not on array dimensions.
- **Corollary 1 (physical p. 3; proof/completion p. 17).** For t>0 and d>=2, the two-sided tail is comparable up to constants depending on d to exp(-c_d min_(1<=k<=d, P in S(k,d))(t/||A||_P)^(2/k)); the paper gives separate upper/lower constants.
- **Theorem 2 (physical pp. 3–4).** An auxiliary bound for expected norms of random Gaussian matrix contractions drives the upper half of Theorem 1. The proof proceeds through entropy/chaining estimates for Gaussian processes (§3), then induction on chaos order; the tail corollary follows from moment comparison and Paley–Zygmund.

Under the paper's natural symmetry assumptions, the coupled homogeneous chaos and its decoupled form have comparable moments/tails with constants depending only on d. The result supplies general degree-dependent moment/tail control; it does not identify a sharp L2 distance to a sign function or the B167 leading constant.
