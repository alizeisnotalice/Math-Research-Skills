## Conventions and theorem statements

The polynomial-side Gaussian measure is `dγ_d(x)=π^(−d/2)e^(−|x|²)dx`, i.e. covariance `(1/2)I_d`, not `N(0,I_d)`. The physicists' Hermite polynomials `H_k` give normalized one-dimensional basis `H~_k=2^(−k/2)(k!)^(−1/2)H_k`, orthonormal in this `γ`; the degree-one basis is `sqrt(2)x`. Thus the standard-normal coordinate is `Z=sqrt(2)X`.

The OU generator is `L=−Δ+2x·∇`, with `L H_α=2|α|H_α`. The Hermite oscillator is `ℒ=−Δ+|x|²`, with `ℒ h_α=(2|α|+d)h_α`. **Normalization erratum noticed in the original PDF:** p. 5, Lemma 3.1 prints `U f(x)=π^(d/4)e^(−|x|²/2)f(x)` and p. 6 proof correspondingly prints a `π^(d/2)` factor, but p. 1 defines `dγ_d=π^(−d/2)e^(−|x|²)dx`. Those printed signs do not yield the claimed isometry: direct substitution gives a factor `π^d` in the squared norm. The isometry with the stated `dγ_d` requires `U f(x)=π^(−d/4)e^(−|x|²/2)f(x)` (and `π^(−d/2)` in the squared-norm line). I record this as a source inconsistency, not a silent correction. The scalar sign change does not affect the generator/semigroup intertwining identities in Proposition 3.3(ii)–(iii), since they are linear operator identities; the norm assertion requires the corrected normalization. For mean-zero polynomials, the printed proposition states the negative-power correspondence and Riesz-transform identities (Prop. 3.3(iv)–(v)).

The paper's main results concern weighted maximal operators and Riesz transforms, not the B167 extremal problem. Theorem 2.1 characterizes weighted L² boundedness of a heat-semigroup maximal operator by the integrability condition `∫v^(−1)(x)e^(−|x|²)dx<∞`; Theorems 2.2–2.5 give principal-value, UMD, and weighted A₂ results for Riesz transforms under their stated function-space assumptions. They do not give dimension-free sharp `L^p` constants or polynomial sign-approximation bounds.

## Proof mechanism and limits

Section 3 intends to establish the unitary bridge by a direct weighted-norm calculation for `U`, eigenfunction identities and spectral formulas for negative powers, but the printed normalizing sign in Lemma 3.1 conflicts with that calculation and with the measure on p. 1. Section 4 proves the weighted maximal/Riesz assertions using semigroup results and kernel bounds. The authors separate the `L²(γ_d)` polynomial side from the `L²(dx)` function side; substituting standard Gaussian normalization without the `sqrt(2)` rescaling changes the basis and generator eigenvalues.

## B167 migration boundary

Useful only to fix basis/measure conventions and transfer operator identities. It does not derive `E(|p(Z)|−1)^2≥c/sqrt(d)`, identify a minimizing sequence, or prove the dimension-uniform sharp constant. A valid use must first convert to `Z=sqrt(2)X`; the isometry alone is not a theorem about the B167 objective.
