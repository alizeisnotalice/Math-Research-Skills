## B167-specific usage card

Use the accepted canonical Theorem 2.1 only as an auxiliary comparison for Gaussian homogeneous chaos moments. Its hypotheses distinguish a finite Banach-valued coefficient array from independent standard Gaussian families in each of d decoupled slots. For every moment p >= 1, the theorem gives a lower bound by the sum over slot subsets and partition contractions, with p^(number of partition blocks/2); its upper bound uses the paper’s mixed partition-contraction norms. Constants depend on chaos order d. The accepted card defines each norm and states the precise spaces and theorem conditions.

This can help compare the moments of a polynomial’s homogeneous Gaussian-chaos pieces, but B167’s objective is E[(|p(G)|-1)^2], which is not a homogeneous chaos norm. The source supplies neither a reduction from this residual to the Banach-valued chaos bounds nor a sharp degree-leading constant. It is not a direct proof route.
