# M0230 — Distributional and \(L^q\) norm inequalities for polynomials over convex bodies

**Source.** Anthony Carbery and James Wright, *Mathematical Research Letters* 8(3) (2001), 233–248, DOI [10.4310/mrl.2001.v8.n3.a1](https://doi.org/10.4310/mrl.2001.v8.n3.a1). The 16-page D02 PDF title page matches the journal article and authors; the International Press full text and DOI record were checked online. SHA-256 and local path are recorded in sources.json.

## Definitions and exact bounds

For a Banach-space-valued polynomial \(p:\mathbb R^n\to X\) of degree at most \(d\ge1\), define
\[
p^\#(x)=\|p(x)\|^{1/d}.
\]
Let \(K\subset\mathbb R^n\) be a convex body of volume 1.

- **Theorem 1 (physical p. 1; printed p. 233).** For \(0\le r\le q\le\infty\),
  \[
  \|p^\#\|_{L^q(K)}\le C
  \frac{[nB(n,q+1)]^{1/q}}{[nB(n,r+1)]^{1/r}}
  \|p^\#\|_{L^r(K)},
  \]
  where the limiting cases \(r=0,q=0,\infty\) use the paper's geometric-mean/\(L^\infty\) conventions. The absolute \(C\) is independent of \(p,d,K,n,q,r,X\).
- **Theorem 2 (physical pp. 2–3; printed pp. 234–235).** For \(0\le q\le\infty\) and \(\alpha>0\),
  \[
  \|p^\#\|_{L^q(K)}\,\alpha^{-1}
  \bigl|\{x\in K:p^\#(x)\le\alpha\}\bigr|
  \le Cn\,[nB(n,q+1)]^{1/q}.
  \]
  The paper gives the sharper corollary constant \(C\max(q,1)\) when \(q\le n\), and \(Cn\) when \(q\ge n\). These are distributional/sublevel estimates; their constants and normalizations must not be dropped.
- **Theorem 7 (physical PDF p. 12; printed p. 244).** For a Banach-valued polynomial \(p:\mathbb R^n\to X\) of degree at most \(d\), a log-concave probability measure \(\mu\) on \(\mathbb R^n\), and \(0<r\le q<\infty\),
  \[
  \left(\int \|p(x)\|^{q/d}\,d\mu(x)\right)^{1/q}
  \le C\frac{\max(q,1)}{\max(r,1)}
  \left(\int \|p(x)\|^{r/d}\,d\mu(x)\right)^{1/r}.
  \]
  Equivalently, this is the stated comparison for \(p^\#=\|p\|^{1/d}\), with a **ratio** of the max terms.
- **Theorem 8 (physical PDF p. 12; printed p. 244).** For the same polynomial/measure setting and every \(0<q<\infty\) and \(\alpha>0\), the paper prints
  \[
  \left(\int \|p(x)\|^{q/d}\,d\mu(x)\right)^{1/q}\alpha^{-1/d}
  \mu\{x:\|p(x)\|\le\alpha\}\le Cq.
  \]
  This printed bound cannot hold uniformly for \(0<q<1\): take the constant polynomial \(p\equiv1\) and \(\alpha=1\); its left side is 1 for every probability measure, while \(Cq\to0\). Preserve this as a source-statement defect. For B167, use only the source statement with \(q\ge1\) (in particular \(q=d\), for integer \(d\ge1\)); do not silently replace \(q\) by \(\max(q,1)\). Both theorems are presented for log-concave probability measures, including Gaussians; they do not identify B167's sharp constant.

## Proof mechanism

The Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits localization theorem reduces the scalar convex-body statements to weighted one-dimensional inequalities. Theorem 3 (physical pp. 3–8) proves the moment comparison by a Remez-type estimate and Beta-function weighted integration. Theorem 4 (physical pp. 8–11) proves the sublevel inequality: a one-dimensional Cartan sublevel bound is combined with weighted integration. Section 5 (physical pp. 11–12) extends the argument from scalar to Banach-valued polynomials through plurisubharmonic functions of class \(L\). Section 6 (physical pp. 12–15) passes to log-concave measures using the corresponding KLS localization statement and records further consequences; physical p. 16 completes the references.

## Boundary for B167

The source supplies dimension- and moment-sensitive bounds for polynomial values and sublevel probabilities. B167 instead asks for the sharp leading asymptotic constant in
\[
\inf_{\deg p\le d,\ \operatorname{Var}(p)\ge1}
\mathbb E[(|p(G)|-1)^2]
\]
over Gaussian dimensions. No theorem here identifies that extremizer or the sharp asymptotic constant. The paper is an auxiliary distributional/moment source; verify the Gaussian normalization and \(p^\#\) degree scaling before transferring any estimate.
