## Core source boundaries

- **M0110, Simons 2014 problem collection, PDF p. 11:** states the degree-(d), variance-at-least-1 objective and asks for the sharp leading constant at the known (d^{-1/2}) scale. W10 read this page; the W18 ledger records it as a cross-tool source.
- **M0230, Carbery–Wright (2001):** [International Press full text](https://intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/mrl/2001/0008/0003/MRL-2001-0008-0003-a001.pdf). Full 16-page D02 PDF read. Theorems 1–2 give degree-normalized moment/sublevel estimates on convex bodies; Theorems 7–8 extend to log-concave measures including Gaussians. Preserve the Beta factors, \(p^\#=\|p\|^{1/d}\) normalization, and dimension/parameter dependence. On rendered physical p.12/printed p.244, Theorem 7 has ratio max(q,1)/max(r,1), while Theorem 8 prints Cq for all q>0; p=1, alpha=1 contradicts q<1. Restrict B167 use to q>=1 and keep the printed small-q defect visible. This does not establish B167's sharp leading constant.
- **W18-X003, Kindler–O’Donnell (CCC 2012 manuscript):** [author-hosted PDF](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/papers/gaussian-noise-sensitivity.pdf). Full 16-page D02 author manuscript read; Theorem 1.3 is physical p. 2, Theorem 2.9 p. 5, and its proof p. 6. The derived B167 implication gives only `Omega(d^{-1/2})`, not the sharp constant. This two-author manuscript is distinct from the three-author 2018 journal version M0111.
- **M0235, MOO:** the complete source is reused canonically from W10 (PR-0493b4701433; same PDF hash); W10’s frozen card contains the full distillation and R05-accepted theorem/proof checks. R06 accepted the conditional W18 B167-use card at evidence/W18/cards/M0235.md (evidence/review/review.json, usage evidence only; no new full-text read). Theorem 3.18/3.19/3.20 require their own product-ensemble, moment/hypercontractivity, multilinearity, degree, variance, individual-influence, and (where relevant) smoothing hypotheses. B167’s unrestricted real-polynomial objective does not imply them; this accepted canonical reuse is not a new W18 full-text read.
- **M0229, Latała (1999):** full eight-sheet two-page-spread scan read (article pp. 39–53). Theorem 1 is on physical scan sheet 2 / printed p. 40; Theorem 2 is on sheet 2 / printed p. 41. For convex tails N_i(t)=-ln P(|X_i|>=t), the normalization is inf{t:N_i(t)>=1}=1; Nhat_i(t)=t^2 for |t|<=1 and N_i(|t|) otherwise. The vector gauge is sup{sum_i a_i b_i: sum_i Nhat_i(b_i)<=p}; the matrix gauge is sup{sum_ij a_ij b_i c_j: sum_i Nhat_i(b_i)<=p, sum_j Nhat’_j(c_j)<=p}. The estimates concern log-concave-tailed independent variables, not the standard-Gaussian sign-distance problem.
- **M0232, Latała, Theorem 1, PDF p. 3:** full 17-page paper read. Theorem 1 gives two-sided decoupled Gaussian-chaos moments for p>=2 at general order d, with constants depending only on d; Corollary 1’s tail bound is explicitly stated only for d>=2. Both are auxiliary, not the sharp B167 constant.
- **M0236, Kane, Theorems 1–2, PDF pp. 1–4:** full seven-page manuscript read. The arXiv v1 stamp is 14 Dec 2009, while the first-page body date is 14 Aug 2018; retain both as printed, unresolved. The source prints the arcsin noise bound without a range, but the proof’s principal angle has `cos(theta)=|1-epsilon|` and matches the coupling correlation `1-epsilon` only for `0<=epsilon<=1`. Use the bound only on that range; for `f(x)=sgn(x)`, the exact halfspace formula already contradicts it at `epsilon=3/2`. The coupling itself is defined through `epsilon<=2`. These are PTF boundary quantities, not B167’s objective.
- **M0237, Kane, Theorem 1, PDF pp. 3–4:** full 57-page arXiv v2 read. Physical p. 2 defines an (epsilon,N)-diffuse tuple by box mass <= epsilon^m N (N outside the exponent) and E|q_i(X)|^2<=1; Theorem 1 is on pp. 3–4. Theorem 32 is stated on physical p. 35; Theorem 34 is stated on p. 36 and proved on pp. 43–45. Full 57-page arXiv v2 read, covering diffuse invariance/regularity and PTF noise-sensitivity/PRG applications. The arXiv header/body dates differ (2012 vs 2018); the effective decomposition-size bound may be Ackermann-scale. This is structural support, not a sharp B167 constant.
- **M0111:** 2018 three-author journal version of Gaussian noise sensitivity/Hermite-tail work; distinct from two-author CCC 2012 manuscript W18-X003 and not read in this pass.

## Newly integrated L02 Gaussian B5 reads

- **M0244, Mossel–Neeman (2015):** full 50-page JEMS article read. Its quantitative Gaussian noise-stability rigidity is a half-space near-extremizer result with deficit, correlation, and volume assumptions; it gives no direct reduction from B167’s polynomial objective.
- **M0250, Abu-Falahah–Torrea (2006):** full 13-page article read. It fixes the variance-1/2 Gaussian/Hermite normalization and maps the polynomial side to Hermite functions. The source defines `dγ_d=π^(−d/2)e^(−|x|²)dx`, but physical p.5 Lemma 3.1 prints `U f=π^(d/4)e^(−|x|²/2)f` as an isometry into Lebesgue `L²`; direct substitution yields squared-norm factor `π^d`, not 1. Keep the printed formula and derived correction `π^(−d/4)` distinct pending independent review; the finite d=1 MATLAB check is numerical evidence only.
- **W18-X012, Diakonikolas–Kane (FOCS 2020 manuscript):** full 56-page arXiv v1 manuscript read. Its deterministic near-zero-set covering and moment-learning results are structural context; they do not yield B167’s sharp leading constant.
- **W18-X014, Tu–Boczar (2023):** full 6-page preprint read. The source Theorem 1.2 is stated for nonnegative degree-two Gaussian polynomials with positive expectation, which is needed for its normalized lower-tail expression; zero is excluded. The card separately labels an elementary extension to nonzero nonnegative degree-at-most-two polynomials. Neither claim is a general-degree sharp approximation theorem.
- **W18-X015, Lovett (2010):** full 14-page ECCC report read. The interval anti-concentration argument applies to arbitrary-degree Gaussian polynomials with the weaker exponent stated in that report; preserve its normalization and degree dependence.
- **W18-X018, Diakonikolas–Raghavendra–Servedio–Tan (read arXiv v1):** full 31-page version read. Separate Boolean-cube and Gaussian-input average/noise-sensitivity results; the local read is the 2009 arXiv v1, linked to the 2014 journal work. Preserve the two input models and the printed noise conventions. Theorem 1.5’s printed ε∈[0,1] range is defective near ε=1 (a linear halfspace has noise sensitivity tending to 1/2 while the printed bound tends to 0); the proof supports only the small-noise regime, so do not cite a global corrected bound. Bibliographic publication year is 2014; do not treat the arXiv v1 as a line-compared journal file.
- **W18-X019, Harsha–Klivans–Meka (read arXiv v1):** full 21-page version read. Sensitivity bounds for PTFs are auxiliary and keep their exact input distribution and degree assumptions; they are not a direct B167 L² bound.

The seven B5 additions brought B167 to 13 full reads at that stage (one partial, 25 unread, and the M0235 canonical use was then separately pending) among 40 candidates.

## Newly integrated L02/L06 Gaussian B7 reads

These ten local full-text reads are merged into the W18 mother cards and section map. R06 accepted the normalized mother paths and hashes in its B7 mother review; source rows retain review_status=pending under the ledger convention. See evidence/W18/reader_batches/B7_mother_sync_manifest.json.

- **M0228, Latała–Oleszkiewicz (1999):** equal-measure dilation comparisons for centered Gaussian symmetric convex sets and strips; Corollary 3 gives a moment comparison for a convergent Banach-valued Gaussian linear series. No reduction to B167’s objective is given.
- **M0231, Latała–Łochowski (2003):** order-only moment/tail estimates for positive-coefficient decoupled chaos generated by independent positive variables with logarithmically concave tails. It does not cover signed Gaussian polynomials.
- **M0233, Latała et al. (2007):** Theorem 2.5 is an independent-coordinate subgaussian linear-image small-ball estimate. The card isolates the source’s \(AA^\mathsf T\) display and uses \(A^\mathsf TA\) under the column-vector convention; this is not polynomial anti-concentration.
- **M0234, Latała (2009):** for \(p\ge3\), independent symmetric variance-one variables with logarithmically concave tails admit an additive Gaussian \(L^p\) approximation for linear sums, with error \(p\|a\|_\infty\). Dependent monomials of one Gaussian vector are outside this statement.
- **M0238, Adamczak–Latała (2012):** partition-norm moment/tail bounds for independent symmetric decoupled chaos with normalized two-sided convex tail rates. The lower bound holds at all orders, the general upper bound only for \(d\le3\), and all-order upper bounds are available for symmetric exponentials. No all-order general log-concave-tail upper bound is claimed.
- **M0240, Adamczak–Wolff (2015):** two-sided centered Gaussian-polynomial moment/tail comparisons with constants depending on degree \(D\); applying the theorem to \(|p|\) is invalid because it need not be a polynomial.
- **M0241, Latała–Matlak (2017 author manuscript):** Gaussian correlation inequality for closed symmetric convex sets. The local Theorem 1 omits “convex,” while the abstract and proof use convexity; the card records the proved convex-set scope.
- **M0242, Kolesko–Latała (2015):** conditional decoupled/tetrahedral chaos comparison under the HMO-supported \(N(t)=-\log\Pr(|X|\ge t)\) concave class (log-convex survival). The paper’s p.2 convexity definition conflicts with its heavy-Weibull example and cited HMO input; no Gaussian-tail extension is made.
- **M0248, Latała–Strzelecka (2026):** finite Gaussian matrix operator-norm estimates with deterministic variance profile; Theorem 2 has \(1<p\le2\le q<\infty\), while \(p=1\) and \(q=\infty\) are separate §1 identities.
- **M0251, Zhang (2022):** higher-order Gaussian CDF/maxima anti-concentration and Slepian comparison bounds under the stated covariance/threshold conditions; the corrected Theorem 1 comparison is one-sided and does not map a scalar polynomial to a Gaussian-vector maximum.
- **M0247, Adamczak–Latała–Meller (2021):** reuse accepted canonical Theorem 2.1 for Banach-valued Gaussian homogeneous-chaos moments with independent Gaussian decoupled slots and \(p\ge1\). The 36-page published W18 seed is not reread; the accepted canonical source is a 38-page arXiv version and no page-by-page equivalence is claimed. R06 accepted the mother path/card reconciliation; this remains a canonical-use record, not a new full read.

R06 accepted the ten B7 mother full-text rows and M0247 usage after checking normalized paths and hashes; the source rows retain review_status=pending under the ledger convention. M0247 is a separate canonical-reuse record, not an eleventh full read.

## Newly integrated L02 Gaussian B12 reads

These five helper-reviewed complete reads are copied into the W18 mother ledger with source-specific physical-page coverage and normalized evidence paths. R06 accepted the mother versions and hashes; source rows retain review_status=pending under the ledger convention. Their conditional use is fixed-chaos or homogeneous-sum Gaussian approximation; none establishes the B167 sharp constant or applies to arbitrary polynomials without the source hypotheses.

- **W18-X020, Nualart–Peccati (2005):** full 18-page official arXiv v1 manuscript read. The fixed-order chaos criterion equates variance-one normal convergence, fourth-moment convergence and vanishing self-contractions; use only for a sequence already in a fixed Wiener chaos.
- **W18-X021, Nualart–Ortiz-Latorre (2008):** full 16-page arXiv v2 manuscript read. The fixed-chaos criterion uses the source’s explicitly scaled symmetric-tensor norm; Theorem 8 extends to centered L2 sequences under its chaos-projection and uniform-tail conditions. Its first-page body date discrepancy is preserved. These are convergence diagnostics, not a B167 objective bound.
- **W18-X022, Nourdin–Peccati (2009):** full 39-page official arXiv v5 manuscript visually read page by page because the embedded Type3 text is unusable. The source’s scaled Hermite convention has `Var(H_q(N))=1/q!` and `q!H_q(h)=I_q(h^{⊗q})`; physical pp. 20–26 prove a scalar Kolmogorov bound for a specifically normalized fractional-Brownian Hermite-increment sum. Do not mislabel it as a multivariate Wasserstein result or standard-Hermite normalization.
- **W18-X023, Nourdin–Peccati–Réveillac (2010):** full 18-page arXiv v2 manuscript read. The core multivariate Malliavin–Stein theorem is kept distinct from §4.1’s fractional-Brownian application. The latter prints `σ²=q!Σρ_H²` using ordinary increment correlation, which conflicts for general `q` with `E[H_q(X)H_q(Y)]=q!ρ^q`; retain the printed source formula and limit that application to the consistent `q=2` case unless the general-q issue is resolved.
- **W18-X024, Nourdin–Peccati–Reinert (2010):** full 40-page official arXiv v2 manuscript read. Universality/invariance and quantitative bounds apply to diagonal-free homogeneous sums under the stated input moment, influence and chaos assumptions. The corrected Theorem 4.1(3) carries `sqrt(max_i Inf_i)`, and Theorem 5.1’s exponents were checked from the source page. No B167 transfer is claimed.
