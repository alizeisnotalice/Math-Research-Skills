# Drinfeld j Ax--Schanuel through Hasse--Schmidt leaves

Source `arXiv:2609.37595v1`, *Ax-Schanuel for the Drinfeld j-function*, physical pp. 3, 28 and 30. The local 41-page preprint is the reviewed source.

Assume that the characteristic parameter `q` is odd. Supply an irreducible algebraic subvariety \(V_0\) of the stated Drinfeld modular phase space, a product Ramanujan Hasse--Schmidt leaf \(L\), and an irreducible analytic \(V\subseteq V_0\cap L\). Theorem 3.31 gives the source's dimension-excess alternative, with the projection contained in a proper Hasse--Schmidt-special subvariety. Theorem 3.32 identifies those Hasse--Schmidt-special subvarieties with the stated weakly special ones.

This is a conditional theorem route. It requires the source's Hasse--Schmidt leaf construction, p-sparsity, irreducibility and special-locus classification. It does not provide a characteristic-zero Ax--Schanuel transfer, a finite leaf test, or an algorithm for weakly special closure.
