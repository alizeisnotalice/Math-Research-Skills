# Effective function-field Zilber--Pink gate

Source checked: Jonathan Pila and Thomas Scanlon, *Effective transcendental Zilber-Pink for variations of Hodge structures*, arXiv:2105.05845v1 (2021), abstract and Corollary 1.1, physical pp. 1--2.  The arXiv record remains v1; the authors' public bibliography lists it as under review, so this is a preprint interface rather than a published theorem.

The source's Corollary 1.1 says: for \(n>0\) and \(S=Y(1)^n\), there is an explicit constant \(C=C(n)\) such that every natural \(\ell\) and irreducible \(X\subseteq S_K\) with \(\dim X+\ell<n\) have a proper \(Y\subsetneq X\) satisfying

\[
(X(K)\setminus X(\mathbb C))\cap S_S^{[\ell]}\subseteq Y,
\qquad \deg Y\le C\deg(X)^{\dim S}.
\]

To cite this conditional result, retain the chosen quasi-projective embedding, field extension \(K/\mathbb C\), irreducibility and dimension certificates, and the source definition of \(S_S^{[\ell]}\).  Do not present \(C(n)\) as evaluated: the checked statement supplies existence/explicitness but no usable formula.  The proof imports Ax--Schanuel and differential-algebraic degree machinery.
