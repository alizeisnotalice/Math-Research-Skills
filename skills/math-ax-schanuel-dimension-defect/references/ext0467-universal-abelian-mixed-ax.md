# Universal abelian varieties and mixed Ax--Schanuel

Ziyang Gao, *Mixed Ax--Schanuel for the universal abelian varieties and some applications*, local `arXiv:1806.01408v2`, physical pp. 1--3. The published version is *Compositio Mathematica* 156 (2020), 2263--2297, DOI `10.1112/S0010437X20007447`.

For the uniformisation \(u:X^+_{2g,a}\to\mathcal A_g\), let \(Z\) be an irreducible complex analytic subvariety of its graph. Theorem 1.1 states
\[
 \dim Z^{\mathrm{Zar}}-\dim Z\geq\dim Z^{\mathrm{biZar}},
\]
where \(Z^{\mathrm{biZar}}\) is the smallest bi-algebraic subvariety containing the projection of \(Z\). Its equality clause requires the stated analytic-component hypothesis.

Supply the Kuga datum, level/polarisation data, uniformisation graph, irreducibility, Zariski closure and bi-algebraic closure. Betti-map, weakly optimal and finiteness applications retain their own hypotheses. This interface does not compute a closure or decide bi-algebraicity.
