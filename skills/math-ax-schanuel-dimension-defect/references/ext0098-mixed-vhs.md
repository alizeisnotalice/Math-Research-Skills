# Mixed-VHS period-map dimension defect

Source. Chiu, *Ax-Schanuel for variations of mixed Hodge structures*, arXiv:2101.10968v3, 6 March 2024, physical pp. 1--4 and 7--9, SHA-256 `b7101d643e996a85c00462b5ba04728d094ab318cdf16ed6e705a704a700d369`.

Theorem 1.2 requires a smooth quasiprojective irreducible \(X\) with an admissible graded-polarized mixed integral VHS. It fixes the monodromy closure \(P\), its unipotent radical, the associated mixed weak Mumford--Tate domain \(D\), and the period-map fiber product \(W\). For irreducible analytic \(U\subset W\), with Zariski closure \(V\subset X\times\check D\),
\[
 \dim V-\dim U<\dim\check D
\]
implies that the \(X\)-projection lies in a proper weakly special subvariety. Theorem 1.4 gives the corresponding statement before replacing the monodromy group by a finite-index subgroup.

The source constructs a real-split retraction and applies point counting on definable quotients. Its proof imports the definability of mixed period maps and uses normality, fundamental-set and growth arguments. An input must therefore include the admissible GPVMHS, the monodromy/period-map data, the analytic component and a valid closure and dimension computation.
