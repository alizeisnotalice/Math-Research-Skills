# Pure-VHS period-map dimension defect

Source. Bakker and Tsimerman, *The Ax--Schanuel conjecture for variations of Hodge structures*, arXiv:1712.05088v1, 14 December 2017, physical pp. 1--3, SHA-256 `a60d609ebb57ad818bde9bcfe89cdf2124758cf3e03e53d44a80a7a4cf61cb49`.

Theorem 1.1 starts with a smooth complex algebraic \(X\) supporting a pure polarized integral VHS, its generic Mumford--Tate group, monodromy closure \(G\), weak Mumford--Tate domain \(D(G)\), and period map. If \(V\subset X\times\check D\) is algebraic and \(U\) is an irreducible analytic component of its intersection with the period-map fiber product \(W\), then
\[
 \operatorname{codim}(U)<\operatorname{codim}(V)+\operatorname{codim}(W)
\]
forces the projection of \(U\) into a proper weak Mumford--Tate subvariety of \(X\).

Weak Mumford--Tate subvarieties arise from normal \(\mathbb Q\)-algebraic subgroups with the stated dense monodromy condition. They are not interchangeable with arbitrary algebraic subsets of \(X\).

The proof uses a definable fundamental domain built through degenerations of Hodge structures and a Griffiths-transverse volume estimate, stated as Theorem 1.2. Applications retain those period-domain, monodromy and transversality structures as source premises.
