# Semiabelian exponential differential equations

Jonathan Kirby, *The theory of the exponential differential equations of
semiabelian varieties*, Selecta Mathematica (N.S.) 15 (2009), 445--486,
DOI `10.1007/s00029-009-0001-7`. The local 42-page author version is the
same work in scope. The source's physical pp. 4--5 introduce the differential
field setting and state the one-derivation Schanuel property at Theorem 3.8.

Use the route only after specifying a characteristic-zero differential field,
its constant field, a semiabelian variety over the constants, the logarithmic
derivative relation defining \(\Gamma_S\), and the relevant rotund irreducible
subvariety of the tangent bundle. The existential-closedness and Weak CIT
consequences retain their predimension, differential-algebraic group and
proper-subvariety hypotheses.

This admits a source-scoped differential-algebraic conclusion. It does not
provide a finite solver, a test for rotundity, a classical complex-exponential
decision procedure, or an arithmetic transcendence theorem for selected values.
