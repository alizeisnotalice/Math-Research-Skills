# Modular \(j\) dimension defect

Source. Pila and Tsimerman, *Ax-Schanuel for the \(j\)-function*, arXiv:1412.8255v2, 14 January 2015, physical pp. 1--6, SHA-256 `da67ca422a980996d9f0217e6d2c3016185c9f9bf31bf8d2bb2657cb709d4513`.

For an algebraic \(V\subset(\mathbb P^1)^n\times Y(1)^n\) and an irreducible analytic component \(U\) of \(V\cap\Gamma_j\), Theorem 1.1 says that exclusion of a proper weakly special subvariety from the \(Y(1)^n\)-projection gives \(\dim V=\dim U+n\). Thus a proven strict defect \(\dim V<\dim U+n\) yields the contrapositive special-locus alternative.

Theorem 1.2 is the second-jet form. It requires an algebraic \(V\subset J^2\mathfrak H^n\times J^2Y(1)^n\), an irreducible component of its intersection with the jet graph, no weakly-special containment in the target projection, and no source jet projection contained in \(J^0_2\mathfrak H\). Its equality is \(\dim V=\dim U+3n\). The differential-field form in Theorem 1.3 additionally requires the displayed derivative identities, the third-order equation for \(j\), nonconstant \(j_i\), and nonvanishing of every relevant modular polynomial \(\Phi_N(j_i,j_k)\).

The proof route in the preprint uses definability in \(\mathbb R_{\mathrm{an},\exp}\), volume estimates and Pila--Wilkie counting. A finite set of numerical \(j\)-values does not certify any of these hypotheses or weakly-special exclusion.
