`rho(f+s w)^2 <= rho(f)^2+3s^2 rho(v)^2`.

Procedure: first construct the exact forest norm and `Q_H` decomposition; next certify the companion midpoint-lens hypotheses; then apply the tail bound before the two-clip/inward-crossing calculation. Neither displayed inequality is a general Banach-space statement without that structure.
