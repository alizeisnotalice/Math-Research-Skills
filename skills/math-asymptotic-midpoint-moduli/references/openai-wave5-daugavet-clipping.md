Required hypotheses are a subspace `E` of `L1`, precompactness in measure of its unit ball as used in the source, and its Daugavet slice property. For an epsilon-net `b_i` of the compact set, put `sigma_i=sgn(b_i)` and take the intersection `F` of the associated kernels. The source's finite gate gives, for `y` in the unit sphere of `F`, `||C(y)||_1 <= 1/2+epsilon`, yielding `H_E(x,t)>=t/2`. Each net, sign functional, and compactness claim must be supplied explicitly.
