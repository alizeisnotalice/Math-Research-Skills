# Mumford–Shah collar localization

Source 1. OpenAI, *Interior regularity of planar Mumford–Shah minimizers*, physical pp.4–5 and 8–9, SHA-256 `5b03d80e25997de7403e22b6b0a0db1179ad2bc52a921929b6cfe3e47382db1b`.

For a reduced absolute minimizer and smooth `W` compactly contained in the ambient domain, extend a compactly supported competitor by the original pair outside `W`. The unchanged collar preserves admissibility and local absolute minimality; record the energy finiteness and collar agreement.

This localization observation is not a planar regularity or blow-up classification result.
