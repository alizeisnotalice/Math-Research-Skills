# maximal-crossing special-Lagrangian torus

**Source.** *The Strong Hyperkähler SYZ Conjecture*, pp. 1–2, 5–8, 13–16,
24, 31, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-Strong-Hyperkahler-SYZ-Conjecture-September-23-2026/main.pdf).

**Input.** A projective family \(\pi:Y\to\Delta\) with smooth total space,
reduced SNC central fibre, and smooth connected projective Calabi--Yau
\(d\)-fold fibres with \(d\ge2\) for \(t\ne0\). Supply a polarizing line bundle; a
nowhere-zero fibrewise holomorphic \(d\)-form extending logarithmically;
constants \(c,C>0\) with
\[
c(-\log|t|)^d\le\int_{Y_t}|\eta_t|^2\le C(-\log|t|)^d;
\]
and a central point where exactly \(d+1\) components meet with local model
\(z_0\cdots z_d=t\) and nonzero logarithmic coefficient of \(\eta\).

**Admission/output.** The source's Theorem 2.1 admits this branch and returns,
for every sufficiently small nonzero \(t\), a smooth embedded special
Lagrangian real \(d\)-torus in the Ricci-flat Kähler metric in the polarizing class on \(Y_t\), isotopic to the angular coordinate
torus. The conditions above are the theorem's actual metric-scale and
maximal-residue conditions.

**Boundary.** This neither constructs the degeneration nor proves the volume
asymptotic, Ricci-flat estimates, semistable reduction, hyperkähler rotation,
monodromy, or semiampleness. It is not a general SYZ decision rule.
