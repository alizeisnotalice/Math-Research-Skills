# Infinity-harmonic linear lift

Source 1. OpenAI, *Uniform Interior C1,alpha Estimates for Infinity-Harmonic Functions*, physical pp.2–5 and 9–17, SHA-256 `8c01e0c934ce3ef1149a40ad953d7b64702ab4297a64f150de2de905842c87d4`.

For a supplied viscosity solution `u`, the lift `U(y,b)=u(y)+b` is infinity-harmonic. Transfer an upper or lower test function through the affine vertical graph as in Lemma 2.1, checking separately the zero horizontal-gradient case. This supports the source's later local-flatness setup.

The lift alone does not certify the uniform C1,alpha theorem.
