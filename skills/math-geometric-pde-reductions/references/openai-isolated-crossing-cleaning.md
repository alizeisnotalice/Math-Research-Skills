# Isolated crossing cleaning

Source 1. OpenAI, *Strong diffeomorphic approximation in three dimensions for 1≤p≤2*, physical pp.18–19 and 23–25, SHA-256 `245e4bde0050d2096b8f8f0ee7576c1b61703ea81ebc4e60caf7ea027e50d3eb`.

Use Lemma 3.5 only with a locally flat disk, a straight interval, one isolated interior intersection, and a coordinate neighborhood containing no other relevant sheet. The stated local ambient modification turns the local intersection into either none or one standard crossing while retaining the specified collar and piecewise-C1 compatibility.

It does not provide the paper's full diffeomorphic approximation theorem.
