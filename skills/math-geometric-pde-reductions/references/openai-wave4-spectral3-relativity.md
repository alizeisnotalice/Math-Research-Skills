## Enclosing-area deformation

*The spacetime Penrose inequality and enclosing area* ([pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-spacetime-Penrose-inequality-and-enclosing-area-September-27-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `9bb0c350914915db56195840fc40dc31c8ee792628d488148a66f86940d1e35d`, physical pp. 8--10) defines a prepared exterior by (2.1): a one-ended asymptotically flat initial-data exterior with compactly supported momentum correction, the stated decay, strict `mu-|J|_g >= c rho^{-n-beta}`, negative outward null expansion on every boundary component, zero ADM momentum, and positive full-cut infimum `A_0`.

Under precisely those conditions, Theorem 2.2 states that for fixed `0<epsilon<1` and all sufficiently large integers `N`, one obtains a complete connected enclosing-set exterior `X_N` with compact included frontier and smooth ambient metric.  Its stated output is nonnegative integrable scalar curvature, one Euclidean end, `E(g_N) <= E_0+P_N(e^{-N epsilon}+e^{-N epsilon/2})`, and full frontier area at least `e^{-(n-1)epsilon} A_0`; it is outer and locally perimeter minimizing, with the stated singular-set dimension.  The usable pipeline is: make the analytic deformation on a smooth filling, then use the height argument to select a detached minimizing frontier.  Do not use it without the decay, strict dominant-energy margin, complete-frontier convention, and full-cut area convention.

## Charged full-cut and obstacle interface

*The spacetime Penrose inequality with charge and original-data rigidity* ([pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-spacetime-Penrose-inequality-with-charge-and-original-data-rigidity-October-5-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `84ca5bc05bc907a1da73b742544fe87e6fb9d22ce263c2fbe8f84752f9e5151f`, physical pp. 5, 7--10) fixes an asymptotically flat exterior data set, the ADM energy/momentum (2.5)--(2.6), and closed electromagnetic flux forms (2.8), with no assumed extension through the compact boundary.  A full enclosing cut is the entire intrinsic boundary of the specified connected exterior; all components/contact count.

Lemma 3.1 supplies the safe local tool: every smooth closed two-form has equal flux over each full cut, a distant sphere, and the original boundary.  Proposition 3.2 constructs a bounded-comass closed two-form of unit full-cut flux, hence `0<a_g(S)`.  Lemma 3.3 confines the perimeter obstacle problem by a positive end-foliation divergence and obtains a finite-perimeter minimizer; Proposition 3.4 identifies that auxiliary minimum with the smooth full-cut infimum and gives the stated regularity/minimality.  Theorem 2.3 is the source's charged spacetime inequality, but is not a transferable algebraic inequality: retain its Definition 2.1, sign choice for expansion, flux normalization, and upper-area convention.

At positive proper time it defines the transported positive metric `k_{d,w}` (3.2), developed loop length, and based parallel transport (3.3).  Lemma 3.1 compares it with `g_d+2U^flat tensor U` by the explicit factor `c(w)` (3.4).

Definition 3.2's passing test is a usable *conditional* output: for every seed in a fixed relatively compact basis set, finite lifetime and the small-developed-loop estimate `||P_ell-I|| <= B L(ell)` hold.  Lemma 3.3 says this test set is relatively closed, using a compact strict witness and compact-subset Cauchy stability.  It must not be applied to a merely Lorentzian norm, to a noncompact witness, or as continuity of the maximal lifespan.
