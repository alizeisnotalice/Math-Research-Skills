# Two-Cauchy-pair scalarization

Source 1. OpenAI, *Nonuniqueness for bounded measurable scalar conductivities in three dimensions*, physical pp.5–6 and 30–31, SHA-256 `35017573c88b681f3f029ffb443091aa6fd55d31f6bc7a1b3566e5a6675aa7bb`.

Theorem 3.1 preserves the full Cauchy data for exactly two supplied A-harmonic voltages. Its finite diagonal joining step takes `M>max alpha_i`, puts `d_i^2=1-alpha_i/M`, and joins the eight scalar leaves `M t_1 t_2 t_3` with `t_i=1+-d_i`; their arithmetic mean is one and harmonic mean is `alpha_i/M` in direction i. Use the helper to check this exact scalar arithmetic before treating the field construction as an input.

Do not upgrade preservation of two pairs to equality of full Dirichlet-to-Neumann maps.
