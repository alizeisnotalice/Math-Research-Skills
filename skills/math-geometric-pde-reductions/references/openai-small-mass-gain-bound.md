# Small-mass gain bound

Source 1. OpenAI, *Nonuniqueness with local conservation for the hard-sphere Boltzmann equation*, physical p.14, SHA-256 `640850bc08703544037791cecd9d71f11d558b8333d7f990abbe3a604d805489`.

Lemma 3.8 states that, for every `B` and `epsilon`, a measurable density satisfying `0<=j<=B H` obeys `P(H,j)(v)<=H(v)(epsilon+C_{B,epsilon} integral j)` almost everywhere. Apply it only after preserving the source normalization of `P`, its Gaussian-sheet majorant `H`, and the finite mass measurement.

This is a gain estimate, not a continuation or nonuniqueness statement.
