# Retarded-characteristic Jacobian

Source 1. OpenAI, *Global classical solutions of the three-dimensional relativistic Vlasov–Maxwell system*, physical pp.13 and 15, SHA-256 `56691a44941097fa1a5131470d44c5bc23930faeda22836e177d2391252817d8`.

For supplied smooth characteristics with bounded momentum, strict subluminal velocity, and a chosen retarded branch satisfying `D>0`, differentiate the retarded-time equation to check `ds/dt=D`. Then record the cone Jacobian `r^2 D` and apply the stated change of variables to the selected finite occupation bins.

This is a conditional change-of-variables certificate, not a global PDE continuation criterion.
