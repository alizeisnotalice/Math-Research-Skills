# Augmented elasticity transport recursion

Source 1. OpenAI, *Global Uniqueness for the Smooth Isotropic Elasticity Inverse Problem*, physical pp.5–12, SHA-256 `5cadfeb6eb45bea20b6ceb4879266d49938154c38dbf34a6531b47b4f3cfbff9`.

For smooth strongly elliptic isotropic coefficients, compactly supported data, and a fixed complex null direction, form the paper's augmented displacement-divergence pair whose principal part is scalar Laplacian. On the indicated transverse plane, use the invertible three-component transport system to construct a finite WKB coefficient recursion and impose the physical divergence correction.

This reference assumes all coefficient and support hypotheses and does not claim global uniqueness.
