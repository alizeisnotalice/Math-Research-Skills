# Dyadic two-interval transport

Scoped reference. *Thompson's group F is nonamenable* (OpenAI, 23 Sep 2026),
SHA-256 `a474b04a7e94e172d4260fc42c3643f1f9a4924d7383e12448f4ba8d636175e5`,
physical p. 4, Lemma 2.2. Two ordered pairs of internal dyadic basic intervals
can be matched by a piecewise affine map with dyadic breakpoints and slopes
that are powers of two; the source also records exact interval-rescaling
covariance. [Pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Thompsons-group-F-is-nonamenable-September-23-2026/paper.pdf).

It is an ordered two-interval construction, not a finite-grid injection proof
or a nonamenability certificate.
