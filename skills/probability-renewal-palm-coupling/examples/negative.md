# Negative example: invalid upgrades of queueing evidence

Avoid these moves:

- **Invalid:** infer the A007 renewal limit from the Poisson formula and simulation tables. **Why:** the paper explicitly leaves general renewal arrivals as Conjecture 4.2; its Poisson proof uses Poisson structure.
- **Invalid:** use the two-queue Chernova–Foss–Kim example as the exact stability region for the A008 three-queue model. **Why:** the routing/adaptive mechanism differs; A008 remains open for $l_2>1$.
- **Invalid:** infer total-variation convergence from weak convergence alone. **Why:** weak convergence does not control the supremum over measurable events; provide an actual coupling bound or a theorem that controls TV in the target state and epoch.

- **Invalid:** apply PASTA to deterministic-renewal arrivals. **Why:** with gap 2 and a uniform stationary phase, ordinary-time age is Uniform0,2) (mean 1), while the age just before an arrival is 2. Stationarity alone does not equate Palm and time averages. - **Invalid:** use marginal stationarity of arrival and service processes to invoke a stationary-ergodic Lindley theorem. **Why:** Györfi–Morvai requires the net-increment sequence itself to be two-sided stationary ergodic with negative mean; marginal stationarity does not establish this joint property.
- **Invalid:** substitute the Palm law of a time-stationary batch process without size bias or average away periodic phases. **Why:** Sigman–Whitt averages over every point in a batch and keeps each phase's intensity/Palm mark law in periodic Little's law.
- **Invalid:** call Winands' uniform waiting-time limit a polling-epoch queue-length law. **Why:** the tagged-customer waiting-time limit and the own-queue polling-epoch deterministic queue-length limit are different statements with different observables.
- **Invalid:** infer TV convergence from paths that are close under a shared random input. **Why:** `X~Unif[0,ε]`, `Y=X+ε` has path distance exactly ε, no meeting, and `dTV=1`. This is a reader example, not a queueing theorem.
- **Invalid:** use the A008 limited-service stability bounds as a general iff test. **Why:** Chernova–Foss–Kim obtain an iff only in the stated `l_2=1` case; the source leaves a gap for `l_2>1`.

- **Invalid:** quote Zverkina's printed `K=k=2` polynomial TV rates as a consequence of the stated Key Condition and finite second moments. **Why:** R14's targeted counterexample uses `P(ζ>s)=(1+s)^(-5/2)` and initial age zero, which satisfy the Key Condition and have `Eζ=2/3`, `Eζ²=8/3`. The stationary-age tail is `(1+t)^(-3/2)`, so the source-convention TV distance has `t² d_TV→∞`, contradicting the printed order-2 bound. R06 rejects only Corollary 1 (physical p.21) and Theorem 2 (physical p.26) at `K=k=2`; this is not a verdict on the whole paper. The `α=3/2` first-moment branch, exponential branch, strengthened assumptions, other local claims, and queue applications remain unassessed.
