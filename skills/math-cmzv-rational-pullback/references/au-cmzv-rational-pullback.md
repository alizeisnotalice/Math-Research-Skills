# Rational pullback into a cyclotomic MZV space

Kam Cheong Au, *Iterated Integrals and Multiple Polylogarithm at Algebraic
Arguments*, arXiv:2201.01676v4, physical pp. 2, 14, Corollary 3.6.

For \(N\ge3\), Corollary 3.6 applies to the set
\(S_N=\{0,\infty,1,\mu,\ldots,\mu^{N-1}\}\), where \(\mu=e^{2\pi i/N}\),
and a rational function satisfying
\(R^{-1}(R(S_N))=S_N\) and \(\{0,1,\infty\}\subset R(S_N)\). A convergent
iterated integral with its first and last letters away from the endpoint poles
then belongs to \(\mathrm{CMZV}_N\) in the stated weight.

The proof uses tangential base points and a regularized iterated-integral
formalism. The caller therefore supplies the rational map, its exact fiber
condition, the word, endpoint regularization and convergence argument. The
source's later Galois-descent statements include conjectural material and are
outside this interface.
