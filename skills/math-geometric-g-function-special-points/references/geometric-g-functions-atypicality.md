# 退化周期的 G-函数高度接口

Urbanik, *Geometric G-functions and Atypicality*, physical pp. 1, 5--8, Theorems 1.3--1.5, derives height bounds for stated special-moduli sets of a supplied smooth projective family over a punctured curve. The hypotheses include the specified degeneration, generic special Mumford--Tate group, numerical Hodge-endomorphism conditions and, in Theorem 1.3, the Hodge conjecture for the relevant endomorphisms.

The resulting height bound is a conditional family-level conclusion. It does not constitute an effective period evaluator or a standalone test for atypicality.
