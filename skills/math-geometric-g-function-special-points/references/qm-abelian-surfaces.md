# QM 阿贝尔曲面纤维的局部 G-函数值

Papas, arXiv:2510.19683v2, physical pp. 1--5, Theorems 1.1--1.2, applies to a Hodge-generic one-parameter family of principally polarized abelian surfaces with a distinguished quaternionic-multiplication fibre. It constructs a nonfunctional algebraic relation of degree at most eight for the specified G-function matrix and derives a height statement.

Provide the family, polarization, QM fibre, endomorphism action, archimedean or crystalline period data and reduction type. The theorem does not supply these geometric data and it is not a generic numerical G-function relation.
