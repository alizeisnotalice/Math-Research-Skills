# CM 椭圆曲线纤维的局部 G-函数值

Papas, arXiv:2510.11814v3, physical pp. 1--5, considers a supplied one-parameter elliptic family with a chosen CM fibre. Theorem 1.1 constructs a nontrivial algebraic relation at each specified proximity place from its de Rham--Betti or de Rham--crystalline period matrices. Theorem 1.2 turns these relations into a height bound by the André--Bombieri Hasse principle.

Supply the family, base point, CM field, local parameter, comparison matrix, place and proximity condition. The relation concerns entries of the source period/G-function matrix. It does not apply to an arbitrary G-function or identify a period with a proposed arithmetic constant.
