# 模曲线强特殊交的条件高度界

Papas, arXiv:2605.00766v2, physical pp. 1--6, assumes Conjecture 1.5 on simultaneous supersingular reduction for two non-CM, nonisogenous elliptic curves. Under that assumption, Theorems 1.2 and 1.4 give the indicated finiteness results for Hodge-generic curves in \(Y(1)^n\). Theorems 2.4--2.6 express the height step using a supplied curve, special-intersection pattern and elliptic families.

State Conjecture 1.5 explicitly in any application. Finite prime samples, modular-polynomial calculations or a local reduction computation do not prove this sparsity assumption.
