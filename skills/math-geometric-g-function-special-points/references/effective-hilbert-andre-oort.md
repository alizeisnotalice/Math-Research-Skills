# 非紧 Hilbert 模曲线的有效 André--Oort

Binyamini--Masser, arXiv:2101.06412v1, physical pp. 1--6, proves the effective result in Theorem 1 for a supplied non-special irreducible curve in a Hilbert modular variety subject to its noncompactness and generic-simplicity hypotheses. The proof needs explicit height, discriminant, period-map and Q-function complexity data.

Use only after giving the modular curve, its defining data, the required degeneration or bad-reduction condition, and the effective height/counting inputs. A citation to the paper does not compute the special points of an unspecified family.
