# Transverse rigidity for triangular billiards

Source: OpenAI, *Weak mixing of triangular billiards with an irrational angle*, SHA-256 \`2d03664d20b89a527c73f2eb1fa746bee055ff436ae8e0a274131b8cdb153492\`, physical pp. 5 and 7-12, Proposition 2.1 and Sections 3-4. Pinned snapshot: https://github.com/openai/math/tree/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6

For the regular oriented double \(M\) of a fixed nondegenerate Euclidean triangle, a bounded flow eigenfunction \(f\circ\Psi_t=e^{i\lambda t}f\) satisfies the source's conclusion
\[
Yf=0,\quad Xf=i\lambda f,\quad
\|\nabla f\|_2^2=\lambda^2\|f\|_2^2.
\]
The procedure first localizes away from vertex cones, smooths there, and applies coefficient estimates to \(A=X-i\lambda\).  For an angular tail \(U=\sum_{j\ge m}P_jf\), its boundary field obeys \(AU=B\); long phase-corrected averages control the endpoint identity and obtain square summability.

The conclusion requires the fixed triangle, normalized Liouville measure, bounded eigenfunction, and the stated regular-double flow.  It is not a general billiard spectral regularity rule.
