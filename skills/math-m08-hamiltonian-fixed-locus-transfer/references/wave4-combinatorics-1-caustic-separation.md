# Caustic separation barrier

Source: OpenAI, *Continuous Phase Foliations Create Analytic Caustic Collars*, SHA-256 \`357beeb0c0854e2643802e176bcd394c7875954f1f954f2c0d96a351ec0b31a6\`, physical pp. 6-9, Lemma 2.1 and Proposition 2.2. Pinned snapshot: https://github.com/openai/math/tree/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6

For two leaves, let \(x_j=\theta_{b_2}(G_{b_2}^j(\beta))-\theta_{b_1}(G_{b_1}^j(\alpha))\).  At a zero, source inequalities yield \(x_{j-1}x_{j+1}<0\), so \((x_j,x_{j+1})\ne0\).  Lemma 2.1 chooses a continuous argument \(A\) with
\[
-3\pi/2<A_{j+1}-A_j<\pi/2,
\qquad A_j\le\pi/2+\pi m\Rightarrow A_{j+1}<\pi/2+\pi m.
\]
Opposite strict signs prevent a return of \(A_j\) arbitrarily close to its starting value.

The proposition then gives graph leaves, monotone leaf order, and the stated rational-rotation conclusion.  It applies only to the source foliation and its simple/disjoint leaf hypotheses.
