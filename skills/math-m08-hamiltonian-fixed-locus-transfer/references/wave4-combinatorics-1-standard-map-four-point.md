# Standard-map four-point comparison

Source: OpenAI, *Positive Metric Entropy for the Standard Map at Large Parameters*, SHA-256 \`35559ad1965dc13fa41d6ac4a39d6cd0dcd9e1b58b60f10b730dff9f8c51e2ac\`, physical pp. 27-28, Lemmas 6.1-6.3. Pinned snapshot: https://github.com/openai/math/tree/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6

With \(D_j=\|T_{i,j}\|\) and \(R_{jp}=M^{g(j,p)-g(i,j)-g(i,p)}\), the source states an absolute \(C_0\) such that
\[
R_{jp}\le C_0\max\{R_{j\ell},R_{\ell p}\}.
\]
It obtains this from \(Q_{jp}=\max\{|a_j\wedge a_p|,D_j^{-2},D_p^{-2}\}\) and \(Q_{jp}\le R_{jp}\le3Q_{jp}\).

Its cut construction feeds this comparison into the stated doubling estimate
\[
e_n\le e_{2n}\le C e_n+Cn
\]
for sufficiently large parameters.  The quantities, determinant-one transfer matrices, and scale assumptions belong to the source orbit model; do not use the inequality for arbitrary cocycles.
