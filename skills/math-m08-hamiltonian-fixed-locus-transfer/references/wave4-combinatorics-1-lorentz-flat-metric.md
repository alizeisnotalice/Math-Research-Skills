# Lorentz-model geodesic and flat interface

Source: OpenAI, *A counterexample to the Colin de Verdière chromatic conjecture*, SHA-256 \`97677c7a183884f099bff03396dacf857ae723f03aa9485819bfb1c52273d4be\`, physical p. 8, Lemma 2.1. Pinned snapshot: https://github.com/openai/math/tree/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6

On the future unit hyperboloid, the source uses \(d_H(x,y)=\operatorname{arcosh}(-[x,y])\).  In Lorentz coordinates about \(x\), a point has form \((\cosh r,\sinh r\,u)\), and
\[
-[y,z]\le\cosh(r+s)
\]
gives the triangle inequality.  The unit-speed segment from \(x\) to \(y\), with length \(\ell\), is
\[
\gamma(t)=\frac{\sinh(\ell-t)}{\sinh\ell}x+
\frac{\sinh t}{\sinh\ell}y.
\]
For a positive subspace \(P\), the associated flat is \(F_P=P^\perp\cap\mathbb H^d\).

This is the source's hyperbolic metric interface; require future-unit and positive-subspace hypotheses.
