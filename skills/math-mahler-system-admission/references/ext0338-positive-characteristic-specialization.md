# Positive-characteristic Mahler specialization

Adamczewski and Faverjon, arXiv:1808.00719v1, give a specialization/lifting criterion
over a positive-characteristic function field. The main statements on physical pages
2--3 and the regular-extension formulation on page 6 require a regular field extension
and a regular algebraic point. The branch with \(p\nmid d\) retains that arithmetic
condition. This reference supplies no decision procedure for regularity.
