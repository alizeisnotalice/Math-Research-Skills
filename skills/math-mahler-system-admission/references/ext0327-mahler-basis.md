# Complete basis of a Mahler equation

Faverjon and Poulet, arXiv:2511.18877v1, state an algorithm for a complete solution
basis of a linear \(p\)-Mahler equation. Physical pages 1--2 define the equation over
an effective field and pages 15--26 construct Puiseux, Hahn and constant parts of a
fundamental matrix. Its use requires exact operations in the stated coefficient field,
representations for Puiseux and Hahn series, and every certified truncation step in
Algorithms 1--4. This interface does not expose a numerical truncated-series solver.
