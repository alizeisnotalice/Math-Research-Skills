# Regular-singular multivariate Mahler lifting

Adamczewski and Faverjon, arXiv:1809.04823v1, Theorem 2.1 and Corollary 2.2 on
physical pages 5--7, lift algebraic relations at a regular admissible algebraic point
of a regular-singular multivariate Mahler system to functional relations. The caller
must provide the transformation, a regular-singular gauge, an admissibility proof and
regularity along the full orbit of the point. These conditions are theorem inputs.
