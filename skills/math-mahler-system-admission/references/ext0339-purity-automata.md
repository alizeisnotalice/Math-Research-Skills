# Mahler purity and finite-automaton use

Adamczewski and Faverjon, arXiv:2012.08283v1, use admissible regular systems and
independent orbit data to prove purity of algebraic relations among value tuples.
Physical pages 6--9 state the simultaneous-system forms. Applying an automata
consequence additionally requires a verified translation from the stated automaton to
the relevant Mahler system. The finite automaton alone does not establish the required
regularity, admissibility or orbit independence.
