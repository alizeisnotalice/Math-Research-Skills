# Mahler-system theorem admission

Use this skill when a Mahler equation or system is already fully specified. Choose the
relevant source interface below. Keep the coefficient field, the endomorphism, the
solution space, the algebraic point and every orbit condition explicit.

- For a complete solution basis of one equation, read [basis computation](references/ext0327-mahler-basis.md).
- For multivariate characteristic-zero lifting at a regular singular system, read [regular-singular lifting](references/ext0335-multivariate-lifting.md).
- For positive-characteristic specialization, read [regular extensions](references/ext0338-positive-characteristic-specialization.md).
- For simultaneous systems and finite automata, read [purity and automata](references/ext0339-purity-automata.md).

The source theorems are conditional workflows. A finite coefficient prefix, numerical
specialization, or an automaton transition table does not certify a Puiseux/Hahn
representation, regular singularity, admissibility, regular field extension, orbit
regularity or the required independence condition. Do not transfer a
positive-characteristic statement to characteristic zero.
