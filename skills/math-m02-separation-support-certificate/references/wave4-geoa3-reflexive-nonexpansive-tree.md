# Scoped reference. Finite-tree witness for nonexpansive fixed points

**Source.** OpenAI, *Fixed Points of Nonexpansive Maps in Reflexive Banach Spaces*, Theorem 1.1, Proposition 5.1, and conclusion, PDF pp. 1, 3, 8, 11--17; SHA-256 `0e1524096dc221e727fb67d508473d394aaf0cc9a5a1c75b6c3b5831fcddf06a`.

## Input contract

Let `X` be a real reflexive Banach space, `C` nonempty norm-closed bounded convex, and `F:C -> C` nonexpansive in the given norm.  The source’s contradiction route further passes to a separable weakly compact minimal invariant convex set of diameter one and uses the explicitly constructed finite branching tree, budgets, functionals, and weak compactness.

## Reuse

At finite depth, Proposition 5.1 yields a leaf and a common unit-ball witness detected by all functionals on its root-to-leaf path.  Compactness selects a branch with one common witness; the branch functionals converge pointwise to zero on the difference span, producing the contradiction.  Under the stated contract this establishes a fixed point.

The entry is limited to real reflexive spaces, bounded closed convex domains, and nonexpansive selfmaps.  It does not cover nonreflexive spaces, arbitrary metric convexity, or approximate/nonexpansive maps with error.
