# Extended-order reduction before the gap argument

Takafumi Miyazaki and István Pink, *Number of solutions to a special type of unit equations in two unknowns, II*, arXiv:2205.11217v2, has local SHA-256 `787d87f4a8837955a3203459eee45b5effc5dc4024197724c4cb40e631eb321b` and 52 physical pages. Lemmas 2.1--2.3 on physical pp. 8--9 define the extended order and prove the exponent-divisibility and rescaling step recorded here.

For a positive solution of \(a^x+b^y=c^z\), reduction modulo \(d>2\) yields divisibility of \(x,y\) by the rescaling factors. This finite lemma is independent of the later complex/p-adic logarithmic estimates.

The rest of the paper uses a three-solution gap principle, non-Archimedean logarithmic bounds, and MAGMA computations. None of those uniqueness claims is supplied by this skill.
