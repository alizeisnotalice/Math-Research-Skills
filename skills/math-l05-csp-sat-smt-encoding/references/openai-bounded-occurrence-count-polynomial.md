For a supplied Boolean CNF with at most three occurrences per variable, encode each clause by its multilinear satisfaction polynomial `Phi_e`; summing their product on `{0,1}^n` gives the exact count (2.2). When replacement forms and a design are supplied, the paper's measurement decomposition is (2.6). For a truncated shift with `deg_X [z^j] G <= j`, equations (2.7)–(2.9) recover the coefficients by Boolean inclusion–exclusion over `U subset E`.

This reference is limited to those exact finite identities. It does not assert the paper's construction of replacement forms, a complexity bound, or any statement about ETH.
