# Finite-product divisibility transfer

Source: *Hilbert's tenth problem over the rational numbers*, SHA
`f79236c22b981fe66aaf3ba494a466bf26db212117db47d95a31bf0315af2b30`,
physical p.17. Scoped reference.

For an explicitly enumerated squarefree product `M` and fixed `K`, if every prime factor
of `M` divides a supplied nonzero integer `delta` to order at least `K`, then `M^K|delta`.
This is the sole finite transfer used here. Preserve the factor list, squarefreeness, each
valuation certificate and the fixed integer `delta`; it does not encode or decide H10(Q).
