# Finite congruence-lattice graph criterion

Source: OpenAI, *Finite congruence lattices: characterization and undecidability*, SHA-256 `d26d6cd6285de4f24158d37d1583bdee84a6426190e1b6df67853128e57d80f9`, physical pp. 7--8, Definition 2.1 and Theorem 2.2.

For a supplied nonempty finite lattice `L`, finite vertex set `A`, and symmetric coloring `d:A×A→L` with zero diagonal, check: nonzero off-diagonal colors and `d(p,q)<=d(p,u) join d(u,q)`; every `a not<= b` has a pair colored at most `a` but not `b`; and every nonempty edge set `S` connects all pairs of color at most `a_S` in the graph of images of `S` under all color-nonincreasing self-maps. The theorem identifies these finite conditions with realization as a full congruence lattice. Construct the witnessing unary algebra only after enumerating every admissible self-map. Failed bounded search is not a nonrepresentability certificate.
