# SAT and guarded Diophantine operations

Scoped reference. The balanced-comparisons recurrence in the fixed-clause SAT source is usable only after its near-supermin comparison and cited recurrence hypotheses are supplied; it outputs the stated threshold-sequence comparison, not a threshold oracle.

For nonnegative polynomial systems, guarded mutually exclusive cases whose inactive auxiliaries are uniquely zeroed can be composed, and finite conjunction can be compiled to one equation by a sum of squares. Do not use this compiler over unrestricted integers/rationals or overlapping guards without a separate uniqueness certificate.
