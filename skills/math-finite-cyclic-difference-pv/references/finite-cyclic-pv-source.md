# Finite cyclic branch: verified scope

Source: Ruyong Feng, *Inverse Problem in Difference Galois Theory*, author preprint
(local SHA-256 `945652e0952b6f59f6372d83e3f8cd9c8e9e0d020a4ffcd24ac84b44a6747d37`), physical p. 11,
Section 4.4.

For a cyclic group generated by a finite-order matrix `h` of order `m`, the source diagonalizes
`h=P diag(zeta^e_i) P^-1`, forms `K[t]/(t^m-1)` with `sigma(t)=zeta t`, and sets
`Z=P diag(t^e_i)P^-1`.  Its displayed algebra verifies `sigma(Z)=hZ`; the condition
`gcd(m,e_1,...,e_n)=1` lets Bezout recover `t` from the displayed diagonal entries and their
inverses.

The general inverse theorem elsewhere in the source additionally uses coefficient groups,
singularity conditions, rational-line rigidity, and a cited Picard–Vessiot criterion.  None of
those global inputs is certified by this finite helper.
