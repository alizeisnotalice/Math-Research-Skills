# Source card — Nacu (2003), “Glauber Dynamics on the Cycle is Monotone”

**Reading:** complete 10-page PDF; proof dependencies checked on pp.1–8.

**Model and clock (PDF pp.1–2).** Zero-field ferromagnetic Ising model on an n-cycle with edge couplings `J_i>0`. One discrete step chooses a vertex uniformly and resamples its spin from its conditional Gibbs law. Nacu defines `τ₂=[n(1−μ₂)]⁻¹`, so the corresponding continuous-time convention has rate one at each site (`L=n(P−I)`). The theorem does not include an external field.

**Proof chain.** Lemma 3 (PDF pp.3–4) shows the heat-bath operator preserves increasing functions and constructs an increasing eigenfunction for the second eigenvalue by taking long-time limits of even/odd monotone starts. Lemma 4 (p.4) uses FKG covariance to show that a strictly increasing eigenfunction cannot belong to an eigenvalue above `μ₂`; this identifies the eigenvalue needed for the gap. The operator restricted to the span of single-spin observables is written on pp.5–6 as `A=(1−1/n)I+(1/n)M`, where `M` is an irreducible nonnegative nearest-neighbor matrix with edge-dependent coefficients involving `sinh(2J_i)` and `cosh(2J_i)`. Perron–Frobenius gives a positive eigenvector, hence a strictly increasing eigenfunction; consequently its Perron eigenvalue `λ₁(M)` is the relevant `μ₂` and `τ₂=1/(1−λ₁(M))`.

On PDF p.6, Lemma 7 (equations (8)–(9)) obtains cyclic ratios of adjacent Perron coordinates from the eigenvector equation. Proposition 8 (p.7) deduces
`(x_i²+x_{i+1}²)/(x_i x_{i+1}) ≤ 2c_i/s_i`.
After symmetrizing M with diagonal weights `q_i=c_i+c_{i+1}`, Lemma 9 (p.7) gives
`λ₁(M)=sup_y [Σ_i 2s_i y_i y_{i+1}]/[Σ_i c_i(y_i²+y_{i+1}²)]`.
The Perron eigenvalue is simple. Differentiating its eigenvalue equation (equations (10)–(12), pp.7–8) and substituting Proposition 8 yields `∂λ₁/∂J_i>0`; the strict inequality uses `J_i>0` and `λ₁<1`. The boundary case of zero couplings follows by continuity.

**Conclusion and scope.** Since `τ₂=1/(1−λ₁)`, this proves non-decrease (in fact strict increase in the positive interior) of relaxation time when any one cycle edge coupling increases, holding the other cycle edges fixed. No field and cycle geometry are essential to this proof. It is not arbitrary-graph A040.
