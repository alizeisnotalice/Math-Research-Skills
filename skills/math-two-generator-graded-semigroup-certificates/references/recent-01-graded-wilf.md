# Two-generator graded-semigroup count

For coprime `2 <= a < b`, the source identifies the gap cone of the total-degree `T=<a,b>`-graded semigroup in dimension `d` with total degrees at most `F=ab-a-b`. It gives `c=binom(F+d,d)`, `n=sum_{t in T, t<=F} binom(t+d-1,d-1)`, and `e=binom(a+d-1,d-1)+binom(b+d-1,d-1)`. The command checks these finite formulae for its supplied parameters and the resulting inequality `d*c <= e*n`.

This source component is on physical pp. 3--5. The tool makes no statement about non-graded semigroups or any parameter outside the explicit coprime two-generator hypothesis.
