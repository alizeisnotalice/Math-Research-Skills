## Recursion and theorem scope

The model is a point process of nonnegative weights $(X_1,\ldots,X_L)$, with $0\le\mathbb P(L=\infty)\le1$, and conditionally iid nonnegative children $W_i$, independent of the point process:
\[
W\overset d=\sum_{i=1}^{L}X_iW_i,
\qquad
\phi(s)=\mathbb E\prod_{i=1}^{L}\phi(X_is).
\tag{2–3}
\]
An $\alpha$-elementary fixed point, $\alpha\in(0,1]$, has Laplace–Stieltjes transform with $\lim_{s\downarrow0}(1-\phi(s))/s^\alpha=m\in(0,\infty)$. For $\alpha=1$ this is a finite-mean fixed point. The root condition is $D_\beta:\mathbb E\sum_iX_i^\beta=1$. Proposition 1 (physical pp.5–6) states that any fixed point has at most two candidate indices $\beta_1\le\beta_2\in(0,1]$ solving the weight equation; for $\beta_1$ it requires only
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}S_n^{(\beta_1)}=-\infty\quad\text{a.s.},
\]
which permits an oscillating walk. Separately, Proposition 1 asserts the full regular-variation limit $(1-\phi(sz))/(1-\phi(s))\to z^{\beta_1}$ for every $z\ge0$.

**Theorem 2 (physical p.6).** For $\alpha\in(0,1]$, an $\alpha$-elementary fixed point exists iff $\beta_1=\alpha$, $D_{\beta_1}$ holds, and one of:

1. $-\infty<\mathbb E R_{\beta_1}<0$ and $\mathbb E[(\sum_iX_i^{\beta_1})\log^+(\sum_iX_i^{\beta_1})]<\infty$;
2. $\mathbb E R_{\beta_1}=-\infty$ and $I_{R_{\beta_1}}(\mathcal L(\sum_iX_i^{\beta_1}))<\infty$;
3. $\mathbb E R^+_{\beta_1}=\mathbb E R^-_{\beta_1}=+\infty$, $I_{R_{\beta_1}}(\chi^*_{\beta_1})<\infty$, and $I_{R_{\beta_1}}(\mathcal L(\sum_iX_i^{\beta_1}))<\infty$.

Here $R_{\beta_1}=\log B^{(\beta_1)}$ for size-biased point-process law $\mathcal L(B^{(\beta_1)})=\chi^*_{\beta_1}$, and
\[
I_R(\sigma)=\int_{(1,\infty)}
\frac{\log x}{\int_0^{\log x}\mathbb P(R\le-y)\,dy}\,\sigma(dx).
\]
Conditions (a)–(c) are equivalent to (d) $S_n^{(\beta_1)}\to-\infty$ a.s. (full drift, stronger than Proposition 1's liminf condition) and (e) $I_R(\mathcal L(\sum_iX_i^{\beta_1}))<\infty$. Proposition 3(a)–(b) (p.7) gives uniqueness within the elementary class for each normalization and the stable-transform construction for $0<\alpha<1$. Proposition 3(c) additionally asserts that there are no other fixed points in $\mathcal P_+$ when the criterion holds.

Proposition 4 (pp.8–9): for a nonconstant finite-mean fixed point and fixed $p>1$, $\mathbb EW^p<\infty$ iff $\mathbb E(\sum_iX_i)^p<\infty$ and $\mathbb E\sum_iX_i^p<1$. Proposition 6 (p.9) gives contraction in its $r_p$ characteristic-function metric for $1<p<2$ under its displayed integrability/first-moment assumptions, with coefficient $\mathbb E\sum_iX_i^p$. These remain results for this exact weighted BRW recursion.

## Proof route and transfer limit

For $\alpha=1$, the proof connects finite-mean fixed points to a change-of-measure perpetuity and applies Goldie–Maller conditions to obtain mean convergence of the Biggins martingale (Lemma 14, Appendix). For $0<\alpha<1$, a stable transform reduces to the 1-elementary result for powered weights. The renewal/random-walk argument handles infinite summands. Tail statements later in Section 2 require their additional weight moment and arithmetic/nonarithmetic assumptions; they are not part of the fixed-point criterion.
