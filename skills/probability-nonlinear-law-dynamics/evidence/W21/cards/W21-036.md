## Poisson order-statistic RDE and global iteration

For integer `d≥1`, let `ξ_1,ξ_2,...` be the points of a rate-one Poisson process on `[0,∞)`. Let `X_i` be iid with law `F`, independent of the process. The operator `T` maps `F` to the law of the `d`th smallest value among `ξ_i−X_i`. In complementary-CDF notation the paper gives

`TF(x) = exp(−IF(x)) Σ_{i=0}^{d−1} IF(x)^i/i!`,  `IF(x)=∫_{−x}^∞ F(t)dt`.

The domain `D` is complementary CDFs of proper real random variables with `∫_0^∞ F(t)dt<∞` (PDF pp.5–6). Equation (2) is the mean-field minimum-weight `d`-factor RDE; matching is `d=1`, TSP is `d=2`.

**Theorem 1 (PDF p.5; proof pp.5–16).** For every integer `d≥1`, `T` has a unique fixedpoint `F_d`. For each `F∈D` there is a real shift `γ` such that, for every `x∈R`,

`T^(2k)F(x) -> F_d(x−γ)`,  `T^(2k+1)F(x) -> F_d(x+γ)`.

This is alternating convergence modulo a shift, not direct one-step convergence of every iterate to the unshifted fixedpoint.

**Proof chain.** Define the location-dependent shift between `F` and `TF`; Lemma 2 (PDF pp.6–7) turns a global shift interval `[m,M]` into the reflected interval `[-M,−m]` after applying `T`, so even iterates have monotone lower/upper shift envelopes. Lemma 1 shows `T^kF` enters a strictly decreasing 1-Lipschitz class after two steps. Lemma 3 bounds `T^4F`; Lemma 4 proves strict improvement of sup/inf for nonconstant iterates. Lemma 5 bounds shifts uniformly over all later iterates, and Lemma 6 controls their tails uniformly outside compact intervals. Arzelà–Ascoli plus dominated convergence gives a subsequential even-iterate limit; the shift envelopes and strictness force it to be constant. Centering that constant constructs a fixedpoint. Comparing any two fixedpoints with the shift function and Lemma 4 forces their relative shift to be constant, and applying `T` then forces that shift to vanish. The same bounds imply convergence of the full even/odd subsequences for every starting `F∈D`.

The introduction notes that endogeny is still open for the associated recursive tree process when `d≥2`; convergence of the law operator alone therefore does not complete every belief-propagation optimality argument.

## Transfer boundary

This is an RDE tied to Poisson points and a `d`th-minimum recursion for mean-field combinatorial optimization. Its powerful global convergence argument depends on that explicit order-statistic transform, the complementary-CDF order, tail integrability, and the shift symmetry. It is a method example for RDE iteration only; no such recursive representation or shift structure has been derived for `Phi(theta)=pi(K_theta)`.
