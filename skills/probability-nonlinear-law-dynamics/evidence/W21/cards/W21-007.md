## Invariant-law passage under a deterministic process limit

Let `(E,d)` be Polish. For each `N`, let `Y^N` be a Feller stochastic process, in discrete or continuous time, with initial support `E_N`; let `Π_N` be an invariant probability supported on `E_N`. The deterministic limit is only a measurable family of maps `φ_t:E→E`: the paper assumes neither continuity nor a semiflow law at this stage.

**Hypothesis 1 (PDF p.3).** For every fixed `t≥0`, every `y_0∈E`, and every sequence `y_0^N∈E_N` with `y_0^N→y_0`,

`Law(Y^N(t) | Y^N(0)=y_0^N) ⇒ δ_(φ_t(y_0))`.

**Theorem 1 (PDF p.4).** Every weak limit point `Π` of invariant probabilities `Π_N` is invariant for `φ`: for each fixed `t` and `h∈C_b(E)`,

`∫ h(φ_t(y)) Π(dy) = ∫ h(y) Π(dy)`.

Proof: take a subsequence with `Π_Nk⇒Π`; Skorohod representation on the Polish state space gives `X_k∼Π_Nk`, `X∼Π`, and `X_k→X` almost surely. Since `X_k∈E_Nk`, Hypothesis 1 makes the conditional expectation `E[h(Y^Nk(t))|Y^Nk(0)=X_k]` converge almost surely to `h(φ_t(X))`. It is bounded by `||h||∞`, so dominated convergence passes the invariant identity for `Π_Nk` to the displayed identity for `Π`.

**Corollary 1 (PDF p.5).** If additionally `φ` is a continuous semiflow, `{Π_N}` is tight, and every deterministic trajectory converges to the same `y*`, then `Π_N⇒δ_(y*)`. More generally, Poincaré recurrence places the support of each invariant limit in the closure of the recurrent set. These are conclusions about invariant probabilities as system size grows.

## Transfer boundary

This is a stationary-regime limit theorem for a sequence of stochastic systems `Y^N` whose fixed-time conditional laws approach a deterministic process. It does not say that iterates of a single law operator converge, and it does not require the limiting process to be continuous or even a flow for Theorem 1. Its corollary needs tightness and a globally attracting deterministic semiflow. A121 instead iterates the stationary-law map `θ↦π(K_θ)` at a fixed finite model size; no such `N→∞` approximation has been established.
