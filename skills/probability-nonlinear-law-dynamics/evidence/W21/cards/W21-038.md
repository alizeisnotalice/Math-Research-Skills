## Finite-chain stationary-law sensitivity

Let \(P,\widetilde P\) be finite \(n\times n\) row-stochastic matrices, each with a single irreducible class, so each has a unique stationary row law \(\pi^T,\widetilde\pi^T\). Their irreducible classes need not coincide. Put \(E=\widetilde P-P\). The paper recalls Schweitzer's fundamental-matrix identity
\[
\widetilde\pi^T-\pi^T=\widetilde\pi^T E Z,\qquad
Z=(I-P+\mathbf 1\pi^T)^{-1}.
\]
For row vectors, define
\[
\tau_p(P)=\sup_{\substack{\delta^T\mathbf1=0\\\|\delta^T\|_p=1}}\|\delta^TP\|_p.
\]
It is submultiplicative: \(\tau_p(P^k)\le\tau_p(P)^k\).

For \(p=1\), the readily computed coefficient is
\[
\tau_1(P)=\tfrac12\max_{i,j}\sum_s|p_{is}-p_{js}|
=1-\min_{i,j}\sum_s\min(p_{is},p_{js}).
\]
Thus \(\tau_1(P)<1\) exactly means that every pair of rows has positive common mass (the matrix is scrambling). This also implies aperiodicity of the single irreducible class.

**Equation (3), PDF p.1.** Under \(\tau_1(P)<1\),
\[
\frac{\|\widetilde\pi^T-\pi^T\|_1/\|\widetilde\pi^T\|_1}
     {\|E\|_1/\|P\|_1}
\le \frac1{1-\tau_1(P)}.
\]
Since \(\|\pi^T\|_1=\|P\|_1=1\) for the source's row-vector/right-multiplication convention, this is the absolute bound
\[
\|\widetilde\pi^T-\pi^T\|_1\le
\frac{\|E\|_1}{1-\tau_1(P)}.
\]
Here \(\|E\|_1\) is the induced bound for right multiplication of row vectors, equivalently the maximum absolute row sum.

**Equation (4), PDF p.2.** More generally, if \(p\ge1\), the chain has its single irreducible class, and \(\tau_p(P)<1\), then
\[
\|\widetilde\pi^T-\pi^T\|_p
\le \frac{\|\widetilde\pi^T\|_p\|E\|_p}{1-\tau_p(P)}
\le \frac{\|E\|_p}{1-\tau_p(P)}.
\]
The proof uses \(\tau_p(P)<1\) to get aperiodicity and the convergent series for \(Z\), writes \(a^T=\widetilde\pi^TE\) (so \(a^T\mathbf1=0\)), and expands \(a^T Z=\sum_{k\ge0}a^TP^k\). The zero-sum contraction gives \(\|a^TP^k\|_p\le\tau_p(P)^k\|a^T\|_p\); summing the geometric series proves the first inequality. The second uses \(\|\widetilde\pi^T\|_p\le1\). Section 3's examples show that choosing a different \(p\) can improve the coefficient estimate for a particular matrix; they are examples, not a universal improvement claim.

## Parameterized-kernel interface and limits

For a finite family \(P_\theta\), this can bound the stationary-law difference using a base-kernel coefficient:
\[
\|\pi(P_\eta)-\pi(P_\theta)\|_1
\le \frac{\|P_\eta-P_\theta\|_1}{1-\tau_1(P_\theta)}
\]
whenever both chains meet the source's unique-class assumptions and \(\tau_1(P_\theta)<1\). A usable outer-map Lipschitz bound still requires a bound on \(P_\eta-P_\theta\), uniform control of the denominator on the parameter stratum, and compatible norms. An outer contraction requires the resulting Lipschitz constant to be strictly below one. The paper is finite-state and supplies no such uniformity or kernel-variation estimate for A121/A122.
