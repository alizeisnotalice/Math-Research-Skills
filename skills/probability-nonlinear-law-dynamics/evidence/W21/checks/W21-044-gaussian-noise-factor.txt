For m != 0, mean(G^{ {n} })=n*m and variances stay below 2*sigma^2.
Chebyshev: P(|G^{ {n} }|<=R) <= 2*sigma^2/(n*|m|-R)^2 -> 0 for fixed R.
Hence the noise-factor sequence is not tight and has no weak limit.
