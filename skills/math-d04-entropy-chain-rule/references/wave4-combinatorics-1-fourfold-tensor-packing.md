# Finite-span tensor packing for selected laws

Source: OpenAI, *Pointwise convergence of fourfold ergodic averages for mixing transformations*, SHA-256 \`dbb86e1a3da44a8bcccb1b8ab6bd0b8083ebfbebd64c67f880db59f8ddbada7e\`, physical pp. 1, 4, 7, 10-16 and 34-35, Theorem 4.1, (4.8), Definition 5.1, Proposition 5.3 and Appendix A. If \(x_n,y_n\) are unit-ball Hilbert vectors whose large inner products are contained in exceptional graphs of maximum degree \(L\), then for rank-\(d\) orthogonal \(P\) and \(0<\epsilon\le1\),
\[
\#\{n:\|P(x_n\otimes y_n)\|\ge\epsilon\}
\le C(1+L)\epsilon^{-2}
\exp(C\epsilon^{-2}\sqrt d\log(2+d)).
\]
The source also records the averaged version (4.8).  Its proof removes high-variance directions, nets coefficient vectors, and compares a Gaussian process.

The channel construction has three rational-speed output groups.  Proposition 5.3 preserves this class under selected laws, with tensor packing used precisely when all three groups occur.  Keep the exceptional-graph, rank, order-of-quantifiers, and channel hypotheses.
