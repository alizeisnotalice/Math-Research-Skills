# Selected finite-grid laws for mixing averages

Source: OpenAI, *Pointwise Multiple Ergodic Averages for Mixing Transformations*, SHA-256 \`b9d8fcffac455475a2d846d1e28904f920e10b73dff498b718783023524f7d77\`, physical pp. 1-8 and 20-21, Lemma 3.1, Proposition 3.2, Proposition 5.3 and Theorem 1.1. Pinned snapshot: https://github.com/openai/math/tree/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6

Lemma 3.1 replaces an unbounded search for an averaging length by a measurable length \(N_a\in\{c2^k:c\in F,k\ge a\}\), where \(F\subset[1,2]\) is finite.  It uses the deterministic bound that changing a bounded average from length \(L\) to \(M\) costs at most \(2|M-L|/\max(M,L)\).  A continuous phase witness converts the selected discrete average to a flow average.

Proposition 3.2 defines the selected joining law by an ultralimit of those finite averages, proves its marginals and diagonal-flow invariance, and establishes functoriality under extensions.  Proposition 5.3 states closure of its source-specific channel class.  These selected laws are not ordinary independent products; apply only with the paper's channel and mixing assumptions.
