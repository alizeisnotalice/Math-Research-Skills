# Trinomial unfair-polynomial theorem

Baravelli, *The unfair 0–1 polynomial conjecture for trinomial factors*, arXiv:2611.00674v2, proves the normalized-trinomial theorem on physical pp. 1–5. The premise is a binary polynomial product with two monic nonnegative-real factors and one factor \(1+aX^r+X^m\). The conclusion is that \(a=1\), followed by the binary conclusion for the other factor.

The local v2 file has SHA-256 `62e2bdedb2d26c5376e6e4cf840c326308a6005df40e9e2bcd0e301ac335d72c` and eight physical pages. The coefficient-array recurrence and the four-row/widest-strip setup are on pp. 2–3. Physical pp. 3–5 prove the conjugate bound \(1<R\le2\), construct the Fourier multiplier, and use Appendix A's \(\ell^1\) coefficient estimate to finish the contradiction. The cited earlier papers are background for other cases and are not premises of this trinomial proof.

The theorem assumes a genuine polynomial factorization. It does not supply a factorization algorithm or a result for factors with more than three nonzero terms.
