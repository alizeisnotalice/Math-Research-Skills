# Finite-mode Fock energy and Gibbs regularization

Source: *The entropy photon-number inequality* (OpenAI, 24 Sep 2026),
SHA-256 `d826f8ebe18596419871100e79611868dff2a45a4aa157433c58e6f49a234d82`,
physical pp. 6–7 (Lemmas 2.1–2.2) and pp. 15–16 (Lemma 4.1). Pinned source:
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-entropy-photon-number-inequality-September-24-2026/paper.pdf>.

For a fixed finite number of bosonic modes, a bound on `Tr rho N` is used with
number cutoffs to obtain trace-norm compactness and entropy continuity. The
additional regularizer `K=k(1+N)^4+(1+N)^(1/2)B(1+N)^(1/2)`, with `k>0` and
bounded `B>=0`, is a positive self-adjoint form on the stated domain and its
Gibbs state has finite seventh number moment. This extends the ordinary Gibbs
identity only after its Fock-space domain and cutoff hypotheses are checked;
it neither proves the photon-number inequality nor generic infinite-dimensional
attainment.
