# Orbital-profile score bound

On physical pp. 3–4, the quantum-permanent paper reduces the invariant of a positive bistochastic map to a distribution \(w_\lambda\) on partitions. Proposition 2.1 gives the score coefficient \((-1)^{n-\ell(\lambda)}/\binom n{\ell(\lambda)}\) and the square-free floor \(w_{(1^n)}\ge n!/n^n\). Since all non-square-free score coefficients are at least \(-1/n\), this gives \(\Phi_n\ge (1+1/n)w_{(1^n)}-1/n\).

The finite script verifies only arithmetic from a supplied profile and supplied floor condition. The paper derives those premises using positivity and a cited square-free result.
