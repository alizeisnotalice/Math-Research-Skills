# Paulin's explicit \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb G_m\) bound

Roland Paulin, *An explicit André--Oort type result for \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb C)\times\mathbb G_m(\mathbb C)\) based on logarithmic forms*, arXiv:1403.2949v1, proves the stated bounds in Theorem 1.1 on printed PDF p. 2.  The local PDF has SHA-256 `cf6e86dea18a3dd27f568bff61756f4db20b936f36756c802fd5df4fe5b6030b` and nine physical pages.

The proof first bounds \(\varphi(N)\) through the degree of \(F(j(\tau),Y)\) and the CM class number.  It then compares a root-of-unity estimate with a Baker--Wüstholz logarithmic-form lower bound.  The reduction and inequalities are on physical pp. 7--9.  The route therefore applies only after the curve and its geometric exclusions have been established over the stated number field.

The original preprint status is retained here.  This reference supplies the explicit theorem interface and does not establish a later publication identity.
