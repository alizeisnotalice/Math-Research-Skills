# Motivic Euler-sum coaction criterion

Source reviewed: local arXiv:2309.06925v3, *On Some Unramified Families of
Motivic Euler Sums*; physical pages 2, 5--10 of the reviewed 25-page PDF.

Theorem 2.1 (p.5), attributed there to a combination of results of Glanois
and Brown, states for a weight-(k) motivic Euler sum that unramifiedness is
equivalent to (D_1=0) and (D_r\) landing in
\(\mathcal L_r\otimes\mathcal H_{k-r}\) for each odd (r<k), with the source's
coaction and quotient conventions. If every such (D_r) is zero, it is a
rational multiple of \(\zeta^{\mathfrak m}(k)\).

Theorems 1.1--1.2 (p.2) give the word families, and pp.7--10 show the
coaction-cut cancellations used in the proof. The source's explicit period
identities elsewhere can use an additional period-map identity or a stated
conjecture; those are not exported by this component.
