# Motivic Euler-sum unramifiedness

Use this skill only for motivic Euler sums, with the source's regularization
and bar/sign conventions fixed. It has two routes.

1. The source directly proves that the listed families

   \[
   \zeta^{\mathfrak m}_a(\{\bar2,3\}_\ell),\quad
   \zeta^{\mathfrak m}_a(\{\bar2,3\}_\ell,\bar2),\quad
   \zeta^{\mathfrak m}_a(\{3,\bar2\}_\ell),\quad
   \zeta^{\mathfrak m}_a(\{3,\bar2\}_\ell,3),
   \]
   and the analogous «\(\bar2,1\)» / «\(1,\bar2\)» families are unramified
   for the source's (a,\ell\geq0).
2. For a different word of weight (k), accept it only with a certified
   coaction ledger showing (D_1=0) and
   (D_r\in\mathcal L_r\otimes\mathcal H_{k-r}) for every odd (r<k),
   in the source's quotient and motivic conventions.

If every odd coaction is zero, the cited criterion further gives a rational
multiple of ζ^m(k). A numerical period identity needs a separate period-map
argument; retain any conjectural or external identity used for that step.
