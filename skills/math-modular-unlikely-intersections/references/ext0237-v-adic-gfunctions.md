# Local G-function relations for abelian-surface families

arXiv:2510.11806v2, physical pp. 1--6, Theorems 1.1--1.3, concerns a
one-parameter principally polarized abelian-surface family whose image is a
Hodge-generic curve. A distinguished \(E^2\) or \(E\times\mathrm{CM}\) point,
good reduction and the stated \(v\)-adic proximity produce bounded-degree
relations and then a height bound. The source does not provide those family or
comparison data as finite inputs.
