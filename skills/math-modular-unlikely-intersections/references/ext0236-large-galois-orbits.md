# PEL large Galois orbits under degeneration

arXiv:2306.13463v2, physical pp. 1--4, Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2,
establishes the PEL large-Galois-orbit conclusion for the stated Hodge-generic
curves with multiplicative degeneration. The conclusion must retain the
specified moduli space, endomorphism discriminant and degeneration data.
