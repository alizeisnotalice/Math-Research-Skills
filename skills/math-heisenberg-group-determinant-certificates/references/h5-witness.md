# Order-125 Heisenberg group determinant witness

## Source and exact finite statement

Horace Dong, *The integer group determinants of the Heisenberg group of order
125* (author manuscript), physical pp. 2 and 6--7, states that
M(1+x+y+y^2+xy^2)=11*5^28.

The finite replay builds the matrix f(g h^-1) itself using the paper's
coordinate multiplication rule on physical p. 3 and takes its determinant by
fraction-free integer elimination.

## Scope

This certifies only the displayed value for the displayed group-ring element.
The paper's full description of the integer group-determinant spectrum and
least-positive conclusion require its cyclotomic and 5-adic arguments; they
are outside this finite replay.
