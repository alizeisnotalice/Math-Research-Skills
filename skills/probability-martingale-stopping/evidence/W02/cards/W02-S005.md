# W02-S005 — Freedman (1975), On Tail Probabilities for Martingales

Reading evidence: all 19 PDF pages read. Annals of Probability 3(1), 100–118; DOI 10.1214/aop/1176996452.

Theorem 1.6, PDF p. 3 / journal p. 102, proof PDF p. 8 / journal p. 107: for martingale differences X_k with conditional mean zero and |X_k|≤1, define S_n=ΣX_k and V_n=ΣVar(X_k|F_{k−1}). For a,b>0,
P(exists n: S_n≥a, V_n≤b) ≤ (b/(a+b))^(a+b) exp(a) ≤ exp(−a²/(2(a+b))).

Proof chain: E[exp(λX_k)|F_{k−1}]≤exp(V_k(e^λ−1−λ)) yields a nonnegative exponential supermartingale. Stop at the first crossing, apply optional stopping, and optimize λ=log(1+a/b). The theorem controls a maximum over all n.

Assumptions: centered increments bounded absolutely by one; V is predictable conditional variance. A rescaled increment bound needs corresponding rescaling. Do not replace V by realized squared increments without a separate argument. The paper’s growth-condition extensions are distinct from this bounded-increment theorem.

Transfer gap: in adaptive sampling, establish the reveal filtration, conditional centering, increment bound, and conditional variance before applying. Unbounded rewards require a valid truncation or extension.
