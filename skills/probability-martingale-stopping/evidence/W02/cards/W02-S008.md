# W02-S008 — Fan, Grama & Liu, Hoeffding’s inequality for supermartingales

Reading evidence: all 20 PDF pages read; arXiv:1109.4359v5. SPA 122(10) (2012), 3545–3559; DOI 10.1016/j.spa.2012.06.009.

Theorem 2.1, PDF p. 4: let ξ_i be supermartingale differences with E(ξ_i|F_{i−1})≤0, ξ_i≤1, and finite conditional second moments. Set X_k=Σ_{i≤k}ξ_i and ⟨X⟩_k=Σ_{i≤k}E(ξ_i²|F_{i−1}). It bounds P(exists k≤n: X_k≥x, ⟨X⟩_k≤v²) by the exact finite-n expression H_n(x,v) in equation (10). Remark 2.1, p. 5, gives the simpler bound exp(−x²/(2(v²+x/3))).

Proof: form an exponential change-of-measure density from conditional mgfs; stop at the first simultaneous crossing of X and the conditional-second-moment budget; use the one-sided increment bound to control the tilted cumulant and optimize the exponential parameter. Main proof PDF pp. 8–15.

The increment constraint is one-sided and needs no lower bound. Under merely E(ξ_i|F_{i−1})≤0, E(ξ_i²|F_{i−1}) is not necessarily a variance. The result is discrete finite-horizon and does not itself establish a CLT.
