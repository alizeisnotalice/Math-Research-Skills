- **Proposition 1.2 (report PDF p. 3; printed p. 1):** for real-valued martingales M_n in D([0,infinity), R), finite-dimensional convergence to a continuous limit and uniform integrability of {M_n(t)} over n at each fixed t imply weak convergence in Skorokhod J1. UI is pointwise in time, not uniform over all t.
- **Proposition 2.2 (PDF p. 7; printed p. 5):** for general D-valued processes, finite-dimensional convergence to a continuous limit must be supplemented, for each L>0 and 0<epsilon<1/2, by some 0<delta<=1 for which liminf_n Gamma_Xn(L,epsilon,delta)>0. Gamma is the largest gamma such that for every stopping time T with P(T<=L)>epsilon and every 0<delta'<=delta, both conditional probabilities P(X_n(T+delta')−X_n(T)<=epsilon | T<=L) and P(X_n(T+delta')−X_n(T)>=−epsilon | T<=L) are at least gamma. It is not implied by finite-dimensional convergence.
- **Proof dependency:** Lemma 2.3 (PDF p. 8) turns stopped-increment UI for a martingale into positive Gamma. For Proposition 2.2, section 3 first obtains convergence in measure and a Dudley d_L coupling (PDF p. 10); section 4 (PDF pp. 11–14) uses a moving-average crossing time and randomized short delay to turn persistent local-supremum error into an almost one-sided post-stopping-time jump, contradicting Gamma>0. This is not a direct J1-coupling shortcut.
- **A007 limit:** This is only a conditional process-convergence template. The polling proof must separately derive the relevant process or martingale representation, finite-dimensional limit, fixed-time UI or the full stopping-time criterion, and a continuous limit. The paper proves no polling stability, renewal-age tightness, stationary-to-fluid exchange, or waiting-time law.
