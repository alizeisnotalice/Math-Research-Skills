## Snell envelope and compensator result

On the paper's usual filtered space, let \(T\in(0,\infty]\) and let the adapted càdlàg gain process satisfy \(\mathbb E\sup_{0\le t\le T}|G_t|<\infty\). Its Snell envelope \(S\) is the minimal càdlàg supermartingale dominating \(G\), with
\[
S_\sigma=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\tau\ge\sigma}\mathbb E[G_\tau\mid\mathcal F_\sigma]
\]
for stopping times in the allowed horizon. Under the paper’s convention, the Snell envelope has the unique Doob–Meyer decomposition \(S=M^*-A\), where \(M^*\in\mathcal M_{0,UI}\) (a UI martingale started at zero) and \(A\) is predictable, increasing, and of integrable variation. The authors explicitly do not impose \(A_0=0\); with \(M^*_0=0\), one has \(A_0=-S_0\). If the gain is a special semimartingale \(G=N+D\), write the predictable finite-variation part as \(D=D^+-D^-\), with mutually singular increasing parts.

Under Assumption 3.2 and the local \(H^1\) hypotheses on \(G\) (after localization), Theorem 3.3 (PDF pp. 9–12) proves the pathwise measure domination
\[
dA=\mu\,dD^-,\qquad 0\le\mu\le1\quad\text{a.s.}
\]
Thus the Snell compensator is absolutely continuous with respect to the negative-variation measure of the gain's predictable finite-variation part; it need not be a deterministic time-density.

### Proof mechanism and exact scope

After localizing into \(H^1\), approximate the predictable compensator on fine deterministic partitions. A discrete Snell-envelope comparison bounds the expected increments of \(A\) by those of \(D^-\). The paper obtains convergent averaged subsequences via Komlós' lemma when regularity is absent, then passes to the measure limit and proves the density bound. Lemmas 3.4–3.6 (PDF pp. 10–12) provide the regularity, subsequence, and finite-variation controls used in that passage. Do not replace the local \(H^1\)/integrability hypotheses with only pathwise finite variation.

## Further results read

- In the right-process Markov setting, with generator domain and integrability hypotheses stated in §3.2, Theorem 3.11 (PDF pp. 12–14) proves the Snell value function belongs to the extended generator domain; its argument represents the compensator through \(\mu Lg(X_t)^-dt\) and an additive-functional density.
- §4.1 (PDF pp. 14–15) gives the Rogers dual \(V(x)=\inf_{M\in\mathcal M^x_{0,UI}}\mathbb E_x[\sup_{0\le t\le T}(G_t-M_t)]\) and the Doob–Meyer martingale as optimizer under the paper's assumptions. The zero-start restriction is essential: without it, arbitrary constant shifts drive the infimum to \(-\infty\) (already for \(G\equiv0\)).
- §4.2 (PDF pp. 16–21), Theorems 4.3 and 4.5, proves smooth-fit regularity for specified one-dimensional regular diffusions, including killed cases, using scale/harmonic transforms, generalized Itô and local-time arguments. These are not general smooth-fit results for arbitrary Markov processes.
- Appendices on pp. 25–27 prove preliminary lemmas used in Sections 2–3; references are pp. 22–25.

The result concerns a continuous-time Snell-envelope compensator under explicit special-semimartingale, class-D and local-H¹ controls. It is not a bare optional-stopping theorem and does not remove the need to prove UI for a candidate martingale. Section coverage is in `reading_coverage.json`.
