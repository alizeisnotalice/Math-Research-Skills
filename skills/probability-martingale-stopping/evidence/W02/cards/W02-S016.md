# W02-S016 — Fan, *Exact Rates of Convergence in Some Martingale Central Limit Theorems*

## Identity and reading evidence

The local PDF is arXiv:1608.08887v9, dated 2 October 2018, 18 physical pages. I read pp. 1–18 and rendered pp. 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 15, and 16 to check the definition of the distance, theorem conditions, and stopping/truncation formulas. The exact PDF hash is in `sources.json`; review remains pending.

For martingale differences \((\xi_i,\mathcal F_i)_{i=1}^n\), put \(X_n=\sum_i\xi_i\) and \(\langle X\rangle_n=\sum_i\mathbb E[\xi_i^2|\mathcal F_{i-1}]\). The paper defines the Kolmogorov distance with an absolute value:
\[
D(X_n)=\sup_{x\in\mathbb R}|\mathbb P(X_n\le x)-\Phi(x)|.
\]

## Theorem 2.1: exact rates under conditional moments and bracket control

Assume for some \(\rho>0\), \(\epsilon\in(0,1/2]\), and every \(i\),
\[
\mathbb E(|\xi_i|^{2+\rho}|\mathcal F_{i-1})
\le \epsilon^\rho\mathbb E(\xi_i^2|\mathcal F_{i-1})\quad\text{a.s.} \tag{A1}
\]
Also assume for \(\delta\in[0,1/2]\),
\[
|\langle X\rangle_n-1|\le\delta^2\quad\text{a.s.} \tag{A2}
\]
Then
\[
D(X_n)\le
\begin{cases}
c_\rho(\epsilon^\rho+\delta),&0<\rho<1,\\
c(\epsilon|\log\epsilon|+\delta),&\rho\ge1.
\end{cases}
\]
The paper gives matching examples for the moment term. The constants depend only on the indicated moment order, not on \(n\) (PDF pp. 5–6).

**Corollary 2.1 (PDF pp. 6, 15).** If (A1) holds and \(\langle X\rangle_n\ge1\) a.s., either stopping index \(v(n)=\sup\{k:\langle X\rangle_k\le1\}\) or \(v(n)=\inf\{k:\langle X\rangle_k\ge1\}\) yields the corresponding bound for \(D(X_{v(n)})\) with the \(\delta\)-term removed: \(c_\rho\epsilon^\rho\) for \(0<\rho<1\), and \(c\epsilon|\log\epsilon|\) for \(\rho\ge1\). The two definitions are not interchangeable without retaining their respective bracket-crossing argument.

## Theorem 2.2: random bracket and large individual increments

Under (A1), for \(p\ge1\), the bound is
\[
D(X_n)\le
\begin{cases}
c_{p,\rho}\left(\epsilon^\rho+
\left(\mathbb E|\langle X\rangle_n-1|^p+
\mathbb E\max_{i\le n}|\xi_i|^{2p}\right)^{1/(2p+1)}\right),&0<\rho<1,\\
c_p\left(\epsilon|\log\epsilon|+
\left(\mathbb E|\langle X\rangle_n-1|^p+
\mathbb E\max_{i\le n}|\xi_i|^{2p}\right)^{1/(2p+1)}\right),&\rho\ge1.
\end{cases}
\]
In particular, if \(|\xi_i|\le\epsilon\) a.s., Corollary 2.2 gives
\[
D(X_n)\le c_p\left(\epsilon^{2p}+\mathbb E|\langle X\rangle_n-1|^p\right)^{1/(2p+1)}.
\]
The \(\max_i\) term is a maximum inside expectation, not a sum of moments (PDF pp. 7–8).

## Proof mechanisms and limitations

Section 3 first reduces the moment order to \(0<\rho\le1\) by conditional Hölder and proves a conditional variance bound. For Theorem 2.1 it modifies the predictable variance clock near its terminal value, then telescopes a Gaussian-smoothed test function along the martingale; Taylor remainders are controlled with conditional \((2+\rho)\)-moments and a predictable time change (PDF pp. 8–14). The stopping corollary treats the last bracket value before crossing and the first value after crossing separately; the latter overshoot is bounded using (A1) (PDF p. 15). For Theorem 2.2 the proof stops near bracket one, adds independent Rademacher increments to fill the missing bracket to exactly one, applies the fixed-bracket theorem to the augmented martingale and controls the added tail by Burkholder's inequality (PDF pp. 15–16). Appendix technical lemmas are on p. 17; references on p. 18.

This is a finite discrete-time quantitative CLT, not an optional-stopping identity or a functional CLT. A random bracket alone is not covered: keep (A1), the stated bracket error moment, and the maximum-increment term. Section coverage is in `reading_coverage.json`.
