## Polling PGF epochs and work-family boundaries

Borst (1996), *Polling Systems* (CWI Tracts 115), is available as the complete 246-page book, but only complete used units were deep-read: §1.3 physical pp.15–22, the full Property 1.4.1 discussion pp.27–28, and Chapter 3 §§3.1–3.6 pp.53–68. Its specific G09.01 interface is probabilistic event bookkeeping rather than complex boundary analysis. For the source’s stable cyclic single-server Poisson model, `F_i,G_i` are mass-one visit-start/end PGFs, while `L_i,M_i` are service-event sub-PGFs with server position and mass `λ_i/λ` on the pooled total-completion scale. Conditional type-i service-event PGFs require rescaling by `λ/λ_i` when `λ_i>0`. The pathwise count identity becomes `γ F_i+M_i=L_i+γ G_i`; for `z_i≠0`, Poisson arrivals during service give `M_i=L_i β_i(Σ_j λ_j(1−z_j))/z_i`, while at `z_i=0` retain `z_iM_i=L_iβ_i(Σ_j λ_j(1−z_j))`. Clearing denominators gives `[z_i−β_i]M_i=γβ_i(F_i−G_i)`. Same-visit iid replacement gives `G_i=F_i(...,h_i,...)`; for a fixed Q1 cycle, compose offspring PGFs recursively from n down to 1, and distinguish the zero/nonzero switch-over immigration recursions. The 1-limited discipline violates that replacement property, and the complete units deep-read here do not supply a kernel/RH solution; unreviewed book chapters and their cited methods remain outside scope.
