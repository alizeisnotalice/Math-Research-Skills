# Zeta(3), zeta(4) Hankel contradiction route

Lai, arXiv:2610.06838v2, presents this determinant route on physical pp.
3--22. Under a hypothetical integer relation \(a\zeta(3)+b\zeta(4)=c\), with
\(b\ne0\), it defines a rational Hankel determinant from the explicit rational function
in (2.2). Lemma 2.2 makes the entries rational; Proposition 3.1 supplies an infinite
nonzero subsequence; Sections 4--5 derive the denominator valuation exponent, and
Proposition 6.6 gives the analytic upper bound. Compare the two rates after verifying the stated quantitative premises.

This interface organizes a supplied determinant argument. Its analytic estimates and exponent separation remain proof obligations. Any new
use must separately supply the exact rational function, nonzero subsequence, every
prime-wise denominator bound and the matching integral/orthogonal-polynomial decay.
The source invokes standard inputs including Dirichlet's theorem, the prime number
theorem and Meixner--Pollaczek orthogonality; it does not require Fauzan's preprint as
a formal lemma after the construction is written out.
