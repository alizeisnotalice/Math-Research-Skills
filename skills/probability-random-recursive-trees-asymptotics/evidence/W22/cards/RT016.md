## Model and theorem statements

For integers `m≥2` and `t≥0`, a node stores `m−1` keys and has `m` recursive subtrees. In the main regime the root pivot ranks are `t+1,2(t+1),…, (m−1)(t+1)` in a sample of `mt+m−1` keys; the rule for small subproblems may be completed arbitrarily as specified by the authors. The subtree-size vector obeys

\[
\Pr(V_n=(n_1,\ldots,n_m))=
\frac{\prod_{r=1}^m {n_r\choose t}}{{n\choose mt+m-1}},\qquad \sum_r n_r=n-m+1,
\quad V_n/n\Rightarrow V\sim\operatorname{Dirichlet}(t+1,\ldots,t+1).
\]

`X_{n,k}` counts keys held at depth `k`; `W_n(z)=Σ_k X_{n,k}z^k` is the profile polynomial. The exact recursion (3.2) is

\[
W_n(z)\overset d= z\sum_{r=1}^m W^{(r)}_{V_{n,r}}(z)+(m-1),
\]

where the subtree profiles are independent conditional on the split sizes. The characteristic polynomial is

\[
F(\theta)=\frac{t!}{m(mt+m-1)!}\prod_{j=t}^{mt+m-2}(\theta+j),
\]

and `λ₁(z)` is the root of `F(λ)=z` with largest real part in the analytic domain specified in §3. For binary search trees `(m,t)=(2,0)`, `F(θ)=θ/2`, hence `λ₁(z)=2z`.

Theorem 1.1 (printed p. 290; physical p. 3) sets

\[
I=\{\beta>0:1<\lambda_1(\beta^2)<2\lambda_1(\beta)-1\},\quad
I'=\{\beta\lambda_1'(\beta):\beta\in I\},\quad
\beta(\alpha)\lambda_1'(\beta(\alpha))=\alpha.
\]

For the normalized depth profile, in `D(I′)` with the Skorokhod topology,

\[
\left(\frac{X_{n,\lfloor\alpha\log n\rfloor}}{\mathbb E X_{n,\lfloor\alpha\log n\rfloor}}\right)_{\alpha\in I'}
\Rightarrow (Y(\beta(\alpha)))_{\alpha\in I'}.
\]

Since the limiting process is continuous, this is equivalent to uniform convergence on compact subintervals. `I′` is a specific open interval and is strictly contained in `(α₀,α₊)`; Lemma 8.5 identifies its interval structure and the paper notes its L² restriction may be nonoptimal. Do not report the conjectured larger range as proved.

Theorem 1.2 (same pages) gives convergence `W_n/ E W_n ⇒ Y` in `H(B)`, locally uniform on a complex region `B` containing `(1/m,β(α₊))`. `Y` is analytic and satisfies

\[
Y(z)\overset d=\sum_{r=1}^m zV_r^{\lambda_1(z)-1}Y^{(r)}(z),\qquad \mathbb E Y(z)=1,
\]

where the `Y^(r)` are i.i.d. copies independent of the Dirichlet vector `V`. The theorem's uniqueness/integrability condition is local: for each real `x` in the interval there is `s(x)>1` with `E|Y(z)|^{s(x)}` finite and bounded in a neighborhood of `x`.

## Proof dependency chain

1. **Recurrence and mean:** §§2–3 (physical pp. 6–9) establish the split law, polynomial recursion and `E W_n(z)=(E(z)+o(1))n^{λ₁(z)-1}` uniformly on compact subsets of the stated analytic domain.
2. **Function-space setup:** §§4–5 (pp. 9–17) define the separable Hilbert/Bergman spaces and prove the Zolotarev `ζ_s` completeness/weak-convergence tools used later; the paper does not claim this extends to arbitrary Banach spaces.
3. **Hilbert contraction:** §6 (pp. 18–21), Theorem 6.1, uses `E Σ_r ||A_r^*||_op^s<1` for `0<s≤2` (with the centering condition for `s>1`) to obtain coefficient-law convergence; lower-order/small-subtree terms must vanish in `L^s`.
4. **Analytic contraction and gluing:** §7 (pp. 21–24), Theorem 7.1, gives local analytic convergence. Its coefficient contraction is checked locally uniformly; the limit fields are glued on overlapping neighborhoods.
5. **Apply to profile polynomials:** §9 (pp. 31–33) sets the limiting coefficients `A_r^*(z)=zV_r^{λ₁(z)-1}` and toll `b^*(z)=zΣ_r V_r^{λ₁(z)-1}−1`; Theorem 1.2 follows from the analytic contraction theorem, then add 1 to pass from the centered `X_n` formulation used in the proof to `Y`.
6. **Extract coefficients:** §10 (pp. 33–37), Cauchy integral/saddle-point mapping, derives Theorem 1.1 from analytic convergence. A uniform second-moment estimate in §8 controls the off-angle integral and imposes `λ₁(β²)<2λ₁(β)−1`; this is the reason for `I`.

Lemma 8.3 gives the uniform expected-profile saddle-point asymptotic on compact `α`-intervals in `(α₀,∞)`, with Gaussian prefactor `1/√(2π(α+β²λ₁''(β))log n)` and relative `1+O((log n)^−1/2)`. Lemma 8.4 gives a compact-uniform second moment bounded at scale `n^{max{λ₁(|z|²)−1, 2 Re λ₁(z)−2, 0}}(log n)^D`; Appendix A (pp. 40–44) supplies its detailed recurrence asymptotics. These are inputs, not optional decorations.

## Transfer boundary for A127

The theorem concerns key-depth profiles of the generalized search-tree model, including BST and m-ary search trees. Its split vector is a Dirichlet limit for this particular order-statistic construction. A CTCS Beta-splitting tree with a fixed cyclic gap pair has a different split mechanism, observable and conditioning. To reuse the contraction method, first provide a target-specific function-valued recurrence, limiting coefficient operator, a strict `L^s` contraction, centering/toll control, and a Cauchy extraction step for the target statistic. None is supplied for A127; this source is auxiliary only.
