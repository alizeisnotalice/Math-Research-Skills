## Theorem 1.5: exact input gates and output

Let \(\xi\) be an offspring variable with \(\mathbb E\xi=1\) and \(0<\sigma^2:=\operatorname{Var}\xi<\infty\). Let \(\mathcal T\) be the corresponding unconditioned GW tree and \(\mathcal T_n\) its law conditioned on total size \(n\). For a rooted-tree functional \(f\), set \(F(T)=\sum_{v\in T}f(T(v))\), and require \(\mathbb E|f(\mathcal T)|<\infty\), \(\mu=\mathbb Ef(\mathcal T)\).

All conditioned statements are along admissible sizes \(n\to\infty\) with \(\mathbb P(|\mathcal T|=n)>0\). The support can be periodic: for example, if \(\mathbb P(\xi=0)=\mathbb P(\xi=2)=1/2\), then \(S_n=\sum_{i=1}^n\xi_i\) is even and \(S_n=n-1\) is possible only for odd \(n\). Do not state these conditioned limits over sizes where the conditioning event is null.

(i) If \(\mathbb Ef(\mathcal T_n)\to0\), then
\[
\mathbb EF(\mathcal T_n)=n\mu+o(\sqrt n).
\]
(ii) If the conditional toll-square assumptions (which imply the mean condition in (i)) hold, namely
\[
\mathbb E f(\mathcal T_n)^2\to0,\qquad
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\sqrt{\mathbb E f(\mathcal T_n)^2}}{n}<\infty,
\]
then
\[
\operatorname{Var}F(\mathcal T_n)=n\gamma^2+o(n),\qquad
\frac{F(\mathcal T_n)-n\mu}{\sqrt n}\Rightarrow N(0,\gamma^2),
\]
where
\[
\gamma^2=2\mathbb E\{f(\mathcal T)[F(\mathcal T)-|\mathcal T|\mu]\}-\operatorname{Var}f(\mathcal T)-\frac{\mu^2}{\sigma^2}<\infty.
\]
By (i), the centering \(n\mu\) may be replaced by the exact conditional mean in the CLT. These hypotheses and the formula appear in Theorem 1.5, PDF pp. 4–5 (equations (1.13)–(1.18)); the equation's minus \(\mu^2/\sigma^2\) term is essential. The theorem assumes finite, nonzero offspring variance but no third/fourth-moment hypothesis.

Corollary 1.8 (PDF pp. 5–6) gives joint normal limits for finitely many fixed fringe-tree counts, with covariance specified by (1.19)–(1.20). Theorem 1.13 treats bounded local/weakly local tolls under its own conditional-mean and summability conditions; do not substitute it for Theorem 1.5 without checking locality and the printed assumptions.

## Proof chain and model representation

The tree's degree sequence is represented by i.i.d. \(\xi_i\) conditioned on \(S_n=\sum_i\xi_i=n-1\), followed by a cyclic shift (Otter/cyclic lemma), for admissible sizes. Keep two distinct limits separate: Theorem 1.3 (PDF p. 3) says a uniformly sampled fringe subtree converges annealed to the ordinary, unconditioned GW tree; §5 (PDF pp. 21–22, Lemma 5.9) says bounded root-local functionals of the conditioned tree converge to those of the size-biased Kesten tree. The latter is a root-neighborhood limit, not the uniform-fringe limit. Section 5 proves mean control. Section 6 derives exact covariance identities and variance truncation bounds; Examples 6.13–6.14 show that the hypotheses cannot be casually relaxed. For finite-support tolls, §7 reduces the additive statistic to a bounded finite-range function of the conditioned i.i.d. degree sequence. An \((m-1)\)-dependent CLT gives a joint Gaussian with \(S_n\). Equations (7.8)–(7.10), PDF p. 40, give \(\operatorname{Cov}(Y,S_n)=-\mu\) and define \(\widetilde Y_n=Y_n+(\mu/\sigma^2)(S_n-n)\); the plus sign removes the conditioning direction and leaves variance \(\beta^2-\mu^2/\sigma^2\), yielding \(\gamma^2\). Conditioning/local-limit estimates give the CLT. Section 8 proves the full theorem by truncating the toll and using variance bounds to pass to the limit; the zero-variance cases in the appendix-like end of §8 are proved separately.

## Non-transfer to A127 without a theorem

A critical Beta-splitting clade of size \(m\) splits with probabilities proportional to \(1/[i(m-i)]\); this size-dependent rule does not by itself give an i.i.d.-offspring GW tree conditioned on size. The GW cyclic degree-sequence argument and its conditional CLT cannot be imported unless an exact distributional representation is proved and all assumptions (including the offspring variance and toll decay/summability) are verified. Even such a representation would not turn the single/fringe CLTs into the fixed circular-separation two-leaf covariance limit in A127.
