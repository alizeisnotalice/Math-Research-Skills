## Theorems and inputs

Let `X_n(k)` be the number of vertices at level `k` in a random recursive tree with `n+1` vertices. Theorem 1.1 (physical p. 2) states the joint functional convergence, for all coordinates `k∈N`,

\[
\left(\frac{(k-1)!\left(X_{\lfloor n^s\rfloor}(k)-(s\log n)^k/k!\right)}{(\log n)^{k-1/2}}\right)_{k\in\mathbb N,\ s\ge0}
\Rightarrow (R_k(s))_{k\in\mathbb N,\ s\ge0}
\]

as `n→∞` in the product `J₁` topology on `D[0,∞)^N`, where `s` is the time parameter and

\[
R_k(s)=\int_{[0,s]}(s-y)^{k-1}\,dB(y).
\]

`R₁=B`; for `k≥2` these are integrated Brownian motions/Riemann–Liouville processes. The exact covariance is

\[
\mathbb E[R_k(s)R_\ell(u)]=\int_0^{s\wedge u}(s-y)^{k-1}(u-y)^{\ell-1}\,dy,
\qquad \mathbb E[R_k(1)R_\ell(1)]=\frac1{k+\ell-1}.
\]

The technical extension is Theorem 2.1 (physical p. 3). For a CMJ process generated by an ordinary random walk with i.i.d. positive nondegenerate interarrival `ξ`, assume only `μ=Eξ<∞` and `σ²=Var ξ∈(0,∞)`. If `Y_k(t)` counts generation-`k` individuals born by time `t`, then

\[
\left(\frac{(k-1)!\left(Y_k(t\,\cdot)-(t\,\cdot)^k/(k!\mu^k)\right)}{\sqrt{\sigma^2\mu^{-2k-1}t^{2k-1}}}\right)_{k\in\mathbb N}
\Rightarrow (R_k(\cdot))_{k\in\mathbb N}
\]

in the product `J₁` topology as `t→∞`. The CMJ theorem has a weaker moment input than the higher-`p` moment statement in Appendix Lemma 5.1: that lemma additionally assumes `Eξ^p<∞` for `p≥2`, and the authors explicitly warn that this cannot in general be dropped for `p>2`.

## Proof mechanism

Theorem 2.1 is proved by induction on generation. Decompose the centered generation-k count into (i) centered independent descendant shot-noise contributions, (ii) a renewal-process fluctuation term, and (iii) a deterministic renewal-function error. The first is negligible uniformly on compact time intervals by variance bounds and Borel–Cantelli; the third is negligible using the renewal-function asymptotic and Lorden-type bounds. The second inherits finite-dimensional Gaussian limits from the renewal functional CLT; covariance convergence uses the renewal-function asymptotic. Coordinate tightness is cited from earlier renewal shot-noise results rather than reproved in full. See §§2,4 (physical pp. 3–9); Appendix Lemma 5.1 and its proof are p. 9.

For the RRT, take `ξ~Exp(1)`: the CMJ genealogy stopped at the `m`th birth has the law of an RRT with `m+1` vertices. The process identity (2.1) is `X_{⌊n^s⌋}(k) \overset d=Y_k(τ_{⌊n^s⌋})`, for `s≥0`. The source's exponential birth-time process satisfies `τ_{⌊n^s⌋}/log n→s` uniformly on compact time intervals almost surely; the power is essential because `log(n^s)/log n=s`. The composition continuous-mapping theorem transfers the CMJ limit to Theorem 1.1. A linear index `ns` instead gives `log(ns)/log n→1` for each fixed `s>0`. This specific exponential embedding is not automatically available for a size-dependent split tree.

## Transfer boundary for A127

This is a one-leaf **depth-profile** result for an increasing RRT, plus a CMJ renewal-process theorem. It does not give a fixed circular-separation two-leaf law. A127 requires the exact CTCS split recursion, ordered cyclic labels, and deterministic gap conditioning; none is present here. Even a valid CMJ representation for the target would still require a separate joint/pair statistic theorem and gap-dependent covariance control. Treat as an auxiliary functional-CLT template only.
