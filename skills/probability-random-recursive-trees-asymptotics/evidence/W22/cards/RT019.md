## Model and exact hypotheses

The article treats binary search trees \(\mathcal T_n\) and random recursive trees \(\Lambda_n\), not critical Beta-splitting trees. For a tree \(T\), the additive fringe functional is \(F(T)=\sum_{v\in T} f(T(v))\), where \(T(v)\) is the fringe subtree rooted at \(v\). Theorem 1.20 (arXiv PDF physical/printed p. 10) has separate conditions and constants for the two models:

**BST case.** Assume
\[
\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}f(\mathcal T_k)}}{k^{3/2}}<\infty,\qquad
\frac{\operatorname{Var}f(\mathcal T_k)}{k}\to0,\qquad
\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{(\mathbb Ef(\mathcal T_k))^2}{k^2}<\infty.
\]
Then, as \(n\to\infty\),
\[
\frac{\mathbb EF(\mathcal T_n)}n\to \mu_F=\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{2}{(k+1)(k+2)}\mathbb Ef(\mathcal T_k),
\]
\[
\frac{\operatorname{Var}F(\mathcal T_n)}n\to \sigma_F^2=\lim_{N\to\infty}\sum_{|T|,|T'|\le N}f(T)f(T')\sigma_{T,T'}<\infty,
\quad
\frac{F(\mathcal T_n)-\mathbb EF(\mathcal T_n)}{\sqrt n}\Rightarrow N(0,\sigma_F^2).
\]
The covariance kernel \(\sigma_{T,T'}\) is defined in §1 and computed by the paper's exact joint-fringe formulas; it is model-specific.

**Random recursive tree case.** Replace \(\mathcal T_k\) by \(\Lambda_k\) in all three summability/limit assumptions. Then the mean weight is \(1/[k(k+1)]\), and the limiting variance uses the corresponding \(\widehat\sigma_{\Lambda,\Lambda'}\) kernel; the same variance limit and \(\sqrt n\)-CLT conclusions hold. Corollary 1.21 (PDF p. 10) gives a sufficient condition in each model: \(|f(T)|=O(|T|^\alpha)\) for some \(\alpha<1/2\).

The other principal results are distinct: Theorem 1.4 gives exact expected size-k fringe counts and Poisson approximation for growing \(k\); Theorem 1.5 gives a size-k count CLT for \(k=o(\sqrt n)\); Theorems 1.11/1.13–1.16 develop covariance and joint fixed-fringe normality. Each result retains its stated model, size regime and variance assumptions.

## Proof dependencies

The proofs use a BST/recursive-tree representation to express fringe indicators through cyclically indexed input data, exact one- and two-fringe probabilities to derive means/covariances, and model-specific size profiles. Section 4 constructs Stein couplings for Poisson approximation; §5 applies a dependency-graph normal bound; §§6–7 truncate to finite-shape tolls and establish the variance identities; §8 supplies applications. Theorem 1.20's summability conditions are exactly the gates that make the variance series finite and the truncation error negligible. A useful transfer requires a distributional representation preserving the model and the relevant joint fringe/toll dependence, not merely a similar-looking tree recursion.

## A127 transfer boundary

The paper's cyclic representation concerns the BST/RRT construction. It is not A127's CTCS ordered/circular leaf sequence with two leaves chosen via a uniform cyclic shift at a fixed separation. Do not reuse its CLT or Poisson approximation for A127 unless a model-preserving representation and every toll, centering, covariance and summability hypothesis are proved. A random pair/fringe profile is not a deterministic-distance pair result.
