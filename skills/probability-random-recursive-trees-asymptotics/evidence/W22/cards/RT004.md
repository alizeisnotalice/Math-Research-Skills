## Exact model and core results

The object is a uniform random leaf height in the critical Beta-splitting tree, with the same \(q(m,i)=m/[2h_{m-1}i(m-i)]\) split kernel. CTCS continuous time \(D_n\) and DTCS edge count \(L_n\) remain different variables. The paper's harmonic-descent representation turns \(D_n\) into a Markov-chain absorption time; its main sharp estimates concern a **single uniform leaf**.

**Printed-label discrepancy (PDF p. 3).** The prose immediately after (1.3) labels \(D_n\) as the DTCS hop-height and \(L_n\) as the CTCS height, but (1.4) defines \(D_n\) by absorption of the continuous-time chain and (1.5) defines \(L_n\) by absorption of the discrete-time chain. The equations and subsequent scales use \(D_n=\) CTCS continuous time-height and \(L_n=\) DTCS hop-height; this card follows that equation-consistent convention while explicitly preserving the source's literal prose swap. Do not silently interchange variables or transfer results between models.

**Meaning of the expansion symbol.** Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 1.2 use \(\sim\), with the convention in §3.1 (PDF p. 8): after ordering scales by decreasing asymptotic size, every finite truncation has an error of the order of the largest omitted scale. The formal infinite sum need not converge; for several sums, terms are interpreted after rearranging in decreasing order. These are asymptotic expansions, not equalities to exact convergent series.

* Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 4): \(\mathbb ED_n\sim(6/\pi^2)\log n+\sum_{i\ge0}c_i n^{-i}+\sum_{j,k\ge1}c_{j,k}n^{-|s_j|-k}\), where \(c_0=\zeta(3)/\zeta(2)^2+\gamma/\zeta(2)\) and \(s_j<0\) are the roots of \(\psi(s)=\psi(1)\). The printed theorem supplies the coefficients and expansion structure; the symbol has the finite-truncation meaning above, not equality to a convergent series.
* Theorem 1.2 (PDF p. 4): \(\mathbb EL_n\sim\frac{3}{\pi^2}\log^2 n+\left[\frac{\zeta(3)}{\zeta(2)^2}+\frac{\gamma}{\zeta(2)}\right]\log n+b_0+\sum_{k\ge1}a_kn^{-k}\log n+\sum_{k\ge1}b_kn^{-k}+\sum_{j,k\ge1}c_{j,k}n^{-|s_j|-k}\), with \(b_0=3\gamma^2/\pi^2+\zeta(3)\gamma/\zeta(2)^2+\zeta(3)^2/\zeta(2)^3+1/10\) (the manuscript reports approximately 0.78234); the remaining terms are given by its Mellin-root expansion and finite truncations retain the §3.1 remainder convention.
* Theorem 1.3 (PDF p. 4): \(\operatorname{Var}D_n=\frac{2\zeta(3)}{\zeta(2)^3}\log n+\frac{2\zeta(3)}{\zeta(2)^3}\gamma+\frac{5\zeta(3)^2}{\zeta(2)^4}-\frac{18}{5\pi^2}+O(\log n/n)\). This gives the explicit constant term beyond the leading CLT variance.
* Theorem 1.4 (PDF p. 5, equations (1.11)–(1.12)): for each real \(-\infty<z<1\), there is a unique real \(\rho(z)\in(-1,\infty)\) satisfying \(\psi(1+\rho(z))-\psi(1)=z\). Then
  \[
  \mathbb E e^{zD_n}=-\frac{z\,\Gamma(-\rho(z))}{\psi'(1+\rho(z))}\frac{\Gamma(n)}{\Gamma(n-\rho(z))}+O(n^{-\sigma_*}),\qquad \sigma_*=1.457\ldots,
  \]
  and equivalently the leading factor is \(-z\Gamma(-\rho(z))/\psi'(1+\rho(z))\,n^{\rho(z)}[1+O(n^{-\min(1,\sigma_*+\rho(z))})]\). Equation (1.11) is uniform for \(z<1-\delta\), any \(\delta>0\), and (1.12) uniformly on compact subsets of \((-infty,1)\). The root interval, derivative and gamma ratio are part of the statement; do not invert or drop them.
  At \(z=0\), the gamma pole is removable: source equation (12.10), PDF p. 36, gives \(-z\Gamma(-\rho(z))\to\psi'(1)\), and hence the full prefactor \(-z\Gamma(-\rho(z))/\psi'(1+\rho(z))\to1\), as required for the transform at zero.
* Remark 1.7 (PDF p. 6): this paper proves a full expansion only for the mean of \(L_n\); it does not prove a variance or CLT for \(L_n\) by its Mellin method. Such results are cited from the separate recurrence paper RT002, not attributed to this source.
* Theorem 1.5 (PDF p. 5): \((D_n-\mu\log n)/\sqrt{\log n}\Rightarrow N(0,\sigma^2)\), where \(\mu=1/\zeta(2)=6/\pi^2\) and \(\sigma^2=2\zeta(3)/\zeta(2)^3\).
* Theorem 1.6 (PDF pp. 5–6): with \(\Lambda(z)=\rho(z)\) on \(z<1\), \(+\infty\) otherwise, and its Legendre transform \(\Lambda^*\), lower and upper tails satisfy the printed sharp logarithmic asymptotics on \(0<x\le x_0\) and \(x_0\le x<x_1\), with upper bound for \(x\ge x_1\); \(x_0=1/\zeta(2)\), \(x_1=1/(\zeta(2)-1)\). This is a Gärtner–Ellis consequence of the scaled log-mgf, with a separate far-tail bound.

## Proof dependencies

The paper develops the limiting exchangeable-partition tree and an occupation measure \(\Upsilon\). Harmonic descent supplies finite-n recurrences/identities; the limiting process gives an integral representation for \(D_n\). Moments and the transform of \(\Upsilon\) are expressed by Mellin transforms; the proof differentiates/inverts the transform, controls vertical integrals, shifts contours and takes residues at digamma roots. Section 10/Appendix A provides an alternative Parseval derivation with explicit integrability justification. Higher-moment bounds feed the mgf and CLT; the large-deviation statement uses the limiting log-mgf plus a separate tail argument. The source's proof is not a pair-process argument.
