## Model, statistics and limit theorem

A simply generated rooted ordered tree has weight $\prod_{v\in T}\phi_{d^+(v)}$. When representable by a critical Galton–Watson offspring variable $X$, assume $\mathbb E X=1$ and $0<\sigma^2=\operatorname{Var}X<\infty$; $T_n$ is conditioned to have $n$ vertices, along admissible sizes for which this conditional law exists. The finite-second-moment condition is enough for the main results; an exponential moment is not required. The second-moment proof in §6 first treats span 1 and notes the routine sector modifications for span $d>1$, so periodic laws require staying on their admissible support.

For root $o$, define internal path length $P(T)=\sum_v d(o,v)$, common-ancestor sum $Q(T)=\sum_{v,w}d(v\wedge w,o)$ over ordered pairs, and Wiener index $W(T)=\tfrac12\sum_{v,w}d(v,w)$. The exact decomposition **(3.2, PDF p. 4)** is $W(T)=nP(T)-Q(T)$.

**Theorem 3.1 (PDF p. 4).** There is a positive random vector $(\xi,\eta,\zeta)$, with $\zeta=\xi-\eta$, such that
\[
\bigl(n^{-3/2}P(T_n),n^{-5/2}Q(T_n),n^{-5/2}W(T_n)\bigr)
\xrightarrow{d}(\sigma^{-1}\xi,\sigma^{-1}\eta,\sigma^{-1}\zeta).
\]
For a standard normalized Brownian excursion $e$,
\[
\xi=2\int_0^1e(t)dt,\quad
\eta=4\int_{0<s<t<1}\min_{s\le u\le t}e(u)\,dsdt,\quad
\zeta=2\int_{0<s<t<1}\bigl(e(s)+e(t)-2\min_{s\le u\le t}e(u)\bigr)\,dsdt.
\]
Thus the limiting Wiener statistic uses an unordered pair of vertices (one half of the ordered-pair sum); it is not a pair conditioned on cyclic leaf separation.

**Theorems 3.3–3.4 (PDF pp. 5–6).** Mixed moments are determined by ζ=ξ-η and
\[
\mathbb E(\xi^k\eta^l)=\frac{k!l!\sqrt\pi}{2^{(5k+7l-4)/2}\Gamma((3k+5l-1)/2)}\,\omega^*_{kl},
\]
where $\omega^*_{10}=\omega^*_{01}=1$ and recursion (3.4) is printed on p. 5. In particular, $\mathbb E\xi=\sqrt{\pi/2}$, $\mathbb E\eta=\mathbb E\zeta=\sqrt{\pi/8}$, and $\mathbb E\zeta^2=2/5$. Theorem 3.4 states convergence of all mixed moments of the scaled vector $(P,Q,W)$ under the same finite, positive variance assumptions. In particular,
\[
\mathbb EW(T_n)\sim\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{8\sigma^2}}n^{5/2},\qquad
\mathbb EW(T_n)^2\sim\frac{2}{5\sigma^2}n^5.
\]

## Proof chain

1. **Contour and common-ancestor representation (§4, PDF pp. 8–9).** If $f$ is the depth-first walk and $V(t)=\sigma n^{-1/2}f(2nt)$, Aldous's conditioned-GW contour theorem gives $V\Rightarrow2e$ in $C[0,1]$. Riemann sums give $\sigma n^{-3/2}P\Rightarrow\int_0^1 2e(t)\,dt$. For two vertices, the minimum contour height between their traversal intervals equals their last common ancestor depth up to $O(1)$; summing the resulting integral approximation gives the $\eta$ limit jointly. The identity $W=nP-Q$ then yields $\zeta$.
2. **Moment constants (§5, PDF pp. 9–14).** To evaluate Brownian-excursion functional moments, the paper uses full binary conditioned GW trees with $\sigma^2=1$ and a subcritical binary GW parameter $p=(1-\delta)/2$. The recursive decompositions (5.6)–(5.7), the differential identity for $\mathbb E_\delta(NZ)$, and transfer lemma 5.3 yield a recurrence for leading coefficients $a_{kl}$; the change $\omega^*_{kl}=2^{k+l-1}a_{kl}/(k!l!)$ gives (3.3)–(3.4). This is a calculation of the limit-vector moments, not a proof of convergence for arbitrary offspring laws.
3. **Uniform integrability under only $\mathbb E X^2<\infty$ (§6, PDF pp. 14–17).** The bivariate PGF satisfies $F(z,w)=z\psi(F(zw,w))$. Span/support estimates and a second-order expansion at $z=1$ bound derivatives; Cauchy's coefficient formula gives $\mathbb E[P(T_n)^l]=O(n^{3l/2})$. This supplies uniform integrability of scaled $P$-moments. Since $Q,W\le nP$, the same bound extends to all fixed mixed moments; together with Theorem 3.1 this proves Theorem 3.4. The span-one assumption in the displayed proof is relaxed by the stated periodic-sector modification.

## Transfer boundary for A127

The source samples the entire vertex set of a conditioned GW tree and its empirical pair-distance measure. Its CRT scaling is $n^{1/2}$ for graph distances and $n^{5/2}$ for $W$, with the offspring factor $\sigma$. A127 concerns ordered leaves in a size-dependent critical Beta tree and conditions the pair on a prescribed circular separation after a uniform shift. The Wiener-index theorem and its uniform pair average do not determine covariance at that fixed separation; a model-preserving two-leaf marked recursion and its conditional weights would still be needed. No transfer to the A127 $1-\alpha$ covariance limit follows.
