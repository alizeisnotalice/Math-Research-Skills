## RRT fringe-subtree count and exact formulas

`X_{n,k}` counts vertices in an RRT on `n` vertices whose fringe subtree has size `k`; it is not the number of vertices at depth `k`. The root split gives, for `k<n`,

\[
X_{n,k}\overset d=X_{I_n,k}+X^*_{n-I_n,k}-\mathbf1_{\{n-I_n=k\}},
\quad I_n\sim\operatorname{Unif}\{1,\ldots,n-1\},
\]

with the subtree counts independent conditional on `I_n`, and boundaries `X_{n,k}=0` for `n<k`, `X_{k,k}=1`. Theorem 1 (physical p. 3) assumes integer `k=k_n≥1`, `k=o(√n)`, and gives the optimal-order Berry–Esseen bound

\[
\sup_x\left|\Pr\!\left(\frac{X_{n,k}-\mu_{n,k}}{\sigma_{n,k}}\le x\right)-\Phi(x)\right|=O(k/\sqrt n).
\]

Theorem 2 gives, uniformly for `x=o(n^{1/6}/k^{1/3})`,

\[
\Pr(X_{n,k}=\lfloor\mu_{n,k}+x\sigma_{n,k}\rfloor)
=\frac{e^{-x^2/2}}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma_{n,k}}
\left(1+O\left((1+|x|^3)\frac{k}{\sqrt n}\right)\right).
\]

Theorem 3 assumes `k<n` and `k→∞`; then `d_TV(X_{n,k}, Po(μ_{n,k}))→0`. For `k<n`, `μ_{n,k}=n/[k(k+1)]`; for `n>2k`,

\[
\operatorname{Var}(X_{n,k})=\frac{(2k^2-1)n}{k(k+1)^2(2k+1)}.
\]

Boundary regimes differ: for `n=k`, `X=1`; for `k<n≤2k`, it is Bernoulli with success probability `n/[k(k+1)]`; for `n<k`, it vanishes. Do not apply the displayed variance formula to those boundaries.

## Proof architecture and exact limits

Section 2 (pp. 4–6) solves generic two-index moment/toll recurrences induced by the root split and derives the mean/variance formulas. Section 3 (pp. 7–13) uses refined characteristic-function recurrences and a moment-pumping induction; Proposition 2 controls derivatives of the normalized characteristic function, then small-frequency Gaussian expansion plus a global decay bound yields Berry–Esseen and local limits. The proof treats boundary split indices separately; its toll estimates are not uniform if one silently drops the `j=k` / `j=n−k` terms. Section 4 (pp. 14–17) uses a separate factorial-moment/pgf argument and Cauchy’s formula for local probabilities, then combines local limits across subranges for total variation Poisson convergence. The paper notes its resulting TV rate is poor, not an optimal quantitative rate.

Section 5 (physical pp. 17–18) states parallel results for BST fringe-subtree counts with its own split law (`I_n` uniform on `{0,…,n−1}`) and exact moments. The author explicitly omits the proof details there (“similar arguments”); do not present the RRT recurrence or proof as a fully written BST proof. Section 6 begins on p. 18 and the references continue through physical p. 19.

## Transfer boundary for A127

The object is a **vertex-indexed fringe subtree of prescribed size** in RRT/BST. It is neither a depth profile nor a pair of CTCS leaves at a fixed gap. Any transfer needs a new target-specific recurrence/point process, precise sampling measure, and factorial-moment or coupling estimates retaining leaf labels and the fixed circular gap. The source offers a possible moment-pumping/Poisson methodology only; it does not prove the A127 limit.
