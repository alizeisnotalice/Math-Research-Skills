Use this skill when a random-tree problem asks for height/profile moments, a fringe or additive statistic, pair distances/covariances, or a limit theorem. Start with the exact tree construction and statistic. Choose the proof route only after the model and sampling rule match the cited theorem.

**Input:** the tree family, its split/growth/offspring law, time or edge convention, label order, statistic/toll, sampling rule, and requested asymptotic. **Output:** the exact finite-size recurrence and boundary terms when available, a source-matched theorem with its hypotheses/constants/proof dependency, a model-specific transfer check, and any unproved interface. Keep numerical checks separate from proofs.

1. Fix the model: split/growth probabilities, rate or edge-length convention, rooted/ordered/unordered labels, toll/statistic, boundary values, and leaf/vertex sampling.
2. Write the exact finite-size recurrence, including the random child choice, hold time or edge increment, and any boundary term. Keep CTCS time-height (D_n), DTCS edge-height (L_n), maximum height and pair statistics separate.
3. Route by proof mechanism: recursive inequalities and Laplace transforms for height recurrences; harmonic descent and occupation probabilities for one-leaf chain laws; Mellin inversion for sharp one-leaf expansions; consistency/growth and exchangeable partitions for fringe structure; Markov-branching GHP limits only after verifying the weighted split-law hypothesis and leaf/vertex gate; contour and excursion methods for conditioned-GW pair-distance sums/profiles; indexed-profile covariance and width analysis for the exact RRT/BST level observable; function-valued contraction only for the source search-tree recurrence and stated parameter range; CMJ time changes for the RRT profile; fixed-fringe urn CLTs only under A1–A7 and spectral gates; split-vector toll, renewal, and pair-decomposition methods only after matching their source-specific tree construction; beta-splitting kernels only after an exact finite-n and label-order match; metric-tree limits only under the specified topology and sampling measure; finite-state conditioned-GW recursion only with coalescent spine coupling and its finite-variance hypotheses. For the exact critical beta-splitting model, Kolesnik (2025) supplies the exact uniform-leaf recurrence and Claim 3.5’s weighted recursive-comparison bound under its stated child-index law, weights, finite initial bounds, and forcing order; R12 accepts that one-lineage comparison only. The paper’s CTCS coefficient/asymptotic adaptation remains isolated and unused, and the source supplies no fixed-gap pair theorem. R12 accepts Foutel-Rodier–Schertzer's exact marked k-spine and many-to-few identities only for a type-dependent branching process satisfying the stated offspring-mark, positive-transform, factorial-moment, support, and normalizer hypotheses. The target CTCS tree has no verified representation under those hypotheses, so the fixed-gap pair transfer remains unproved. Open [the method and model-gate reference](references/method.md) for dispatch rules; check [source status](references/sources.md) for the inherited R02-accepted baseline and the restricted RT001, Kolesnik, Foutel-Rodier–Schertzer, and RT004 uses. RT001 explicitly marks the A127 correlation as Open Problem 1. L12 has also fully read HMPW (2008), a conservative fragmentation-scaling comparator with its own dislocation and sampling gates; R12 accepts its G23.07 continuum-tree scaling interface only under the source dislocation, regular-variation, integrability, and strong-sampling-consistency hypotheses; it gives no A127 pair law or UI. The marked-Gromov-weak paper DGP (2011) was full-read but rejected as lacking a direct assigned atomic method interface. Neither addition resolves A127 or certifies the whole revision.
4. Before transferring a theorem, check every hypothesis and variable match: tree family, label/order convention, time/edge metric, sampling design, admissible conditioning sizes, transform domain, moment/toll conditions, centering, variance, and asymptotic regime. Use [source cards and read coverage](references/sources.md) to locate the exact theorem, version, page, and proof chain.
5. Use local symbolic/numerical checks only to catch algebra, recurrence, or constant errors. Store the calculation and state that it is not a proof. The inherited reproducible checks are in the saved exact calculation（本机附件：`evidence/calculations/recursive_cases.py`）.

The current target A127 asks whether CTCS leaf heights of two leaves selected by a **uniform cyclic shift at specified circular separation** (r_n), with (log r_n/log n\to\alpha), have normalized covariance tending to (1-\alpha). RT001 §3.9, equation (40), physical p. 22, gives the shared-prefix Brownian/subordinator calculation as a heuristic and explicitly labels the assertion Open Problem 1. Its pp. 25–26 discussion does not prove the required dependence or maximum-height tail claim. Do not replace this with arbitrary label permutation, a pair sampled uniformly from all distinct leaves, a one-leaf CLT, or a branchpoint law for a random pair. The exact transfer gap is described in the method reference and the Beta-splitting cards.
