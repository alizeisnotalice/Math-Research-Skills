# v-adic Carlitz multiple-polylogarithm period transfer

Source: Daichi Matsuzuki, *Algebraic independence of v-adic Carlitz multiple
polylogarithms via period interpretations*, local SHA-256
`f5b00f42c5caf0b1c2c9567bd52dbaf3948e62619d86e2983825f1f8c728163e`.
The local preprint does not print the inventory's arXiv identifier.

For a degree-one finite place \(v\) and a finite prefix-closed family \(I\)
in the simultaneous convergence domains, Theorem 1.1 (physical p. 6) gives

\[
\operatorname{trdeg}_{\overline K}\overline K(\operatorname{Li}_{\tilde a,v}:\tilde a\in I)
=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\overline K}\overline K(\widetilde\pi,
\operatorname{Li}_{\tilde a,\infty}:\tilde a\in I)-1.
\]

The required bridge is not a numerical specialization: the source constructs
Anderson-motive de Rham--crystalline periods and Frobenius pre-periods (pp.
5--7, 12--13), applies Chen's v-adic ABP criterion at specialization, then
uses difference Galois comparison. Retain the degree-one place, every prefix
condition, convergence inequality, motive/deformation system and the
infinity-adic input. The Euler--Carlitz relation (p. 45) explains the `-1`.
