# Positive-characteristic period-transcendence source interfaces

This record groups three published statements in function-field transcendence theory.  It is a source map, not a construction of Drinfeld modules, t-motives, Galois groups, or algebraic-independence certificates.

## Rank-two periods and logarithms

Chang--Papanikolas, *Algebraic relations among periods and logarithms of rank 2 Drinfeld modules*, *Amer. J. Math.* 133 (2011), 359--391, DOI `10.1353/ajm.2011.0009`; local arXiv:0807.3157v1, SHA-256 `e1aff04b934f0bb95608176ec45c5d9526960caeb8125e71a832ac3c7f4d2405`, 25 pages, local pp. 1, 3--4.  For odd characteristic and a rank-two \(\mathbb F_q[t]\)-Drinfeld module without CM, the cited statement makes \(\omega_1,\omega_2,F_\tau(\omega_1),F_\tau(\omega_2)\) algebraically independent.  Under the stated logarithm and \(k\)-linear independence hypotheses, it similarly treats \(\lambda_i,F_\tau(\lambda_i)\).  The method route is Frobenius difference equations followed by a maximal motivic-Galois-dimension calculation; both are external gates.

## Third-kind periods

Chang, *On periods of the third kind for rank 2 Drinfeld module*, *Math. Z.* (2013), DOI `10.1007/s00209-012-1038-4`; local arXiv:0909.0101v1, SHA-256 `2197e4276d39bb5e0f7c10573c8f6b03468275f08670431ceb98f2c466d95482`, 19 pages, local pp. 1, 4--5.  For the displayed extension by \(\alpha\tau\), the source formula is
\[
 \lambda=-\frac{uF_\tau(\omega)-\omega F_\tau(u)}{\sqrt[q-1]{-\Delta}}+f(\theta)\widetilde\pi.
\]
The algebraic-independence conclusion requires separable CM and \(\operatorname{End}(\rho)\)-linear independence.  The period formula is a reusable symbolic target only after the t-module, branch, and all quantities are supplied.

## Prolongations and Carlitz tensor periods

Maurischat, *Prolongations of t-Motives and Algebraic Independence of Periods*, *Documenta Math.* 23 (2018), 815--838, DOI `10.25537/DM.2018V23.815-838`; local arXiv:1710.08252v2, SHA-256 `cd98a3718456281825ae4459d9dc8271e868e3e02688e205d128979deb1e03d8`, 21 pages, local pp. 1--2, 18.  If \(\det\Phi=c(t-\theta)^s\) and \(\sigma(\Psi)=\Psi\Phi\) with the stated analytic/entire trivialization, Theorem 1.1 transfers transcendence degree from \(\bar K(t)(\Psi)\) to \(\bar K(\Psi(\theta))\).  For \(n\) prime to the characteristic, the coordinates of a period-lattice generator of \(C^{\otimes n}\) are algebraically independent; the cited coordinate identity uses hyperderivatives at \(t=\theta\).  No finite matrix substitution certifies the analytic trivialization or algebraic independence.
