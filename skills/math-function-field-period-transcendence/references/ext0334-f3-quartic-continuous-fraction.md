# Quartic Laurent-series criterion over \(\mathbb F_3\)

Ayadi, Azaza and Beldi, arXiv:2010.11892v1, Theorem 1.1 on physical page 4 and
Theorem 2.3 on page 7, connect partial-quotient degree growth to irrationality measure
for a stated quartic Laurent series over \(\mathbb F_3((T^{-1}))\). The adopted use is
conditional on an all-index recurrence, valuation convention and error/degree proof for
the supplied family. It has no implication for real continued fractions. The longer E1
pattern discussion later in the paper is conjectural and excluded.
