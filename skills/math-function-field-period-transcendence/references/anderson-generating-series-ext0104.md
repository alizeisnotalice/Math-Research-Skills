# Anderson generating series from a Drinfeld module

Source. Ahmad El-Guindy and Matthew A. Papanikolas, *Identities for Anderson generating functions for Drinfeld modules*, arXiv:1302.0238v2, physical pp. 1--2 and 14. SHA-256 `770d21c142c474ca0d50ad1b95bc3877d099ebf1537bc775cd21938bcca41ad5`.

Theorem 6.13 applies to a rank-r Drinfeld module `phi_t = theta + A_1 tau + ... + A_r tau^r`, `xi = exp_phi(u)`, and the source radius condition `|xi| < R_phi`. Its shadowed-partition series `L_phi(xi;t)` converges in the Tate algebra, specializes at `t=theta` to `log_phi(xi)=u`, satisfies the displayed tau-difference relation, and equals `-(t-theta) f_phi(u;t)`. Its compatibility statement has the extra bounds `|A_i xi^(q^i)| < R_phi`.

Use only with an explicit positive-characteristic field, Drinfeld module, Frobenius convention, source radius proof and shadowed-partition coefficients. Finite coefficient calculations alone do not prove the Tate-algebra convergence, specialization or period-transcendence consequences.
