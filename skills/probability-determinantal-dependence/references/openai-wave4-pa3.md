# Wave 4 PA3: double-dimer transfer crossing interface

## The curve scaling limit of half-plane double dimers

Source SHA-256 `2e1bcd39145db94c77ef81c50c59e09edaae5859270ab0fad1767407f95deb5e`, physical pp. 5--16, Proposition 2.1 and Lemmas 2.4, 3.2--3.4.  In a corner-rooted finite Temperleyan rectangle, `N(u,v)` is the total number of alternating loop traversals between two cuts.  The source compares marked cap assignments with particle transfer sectors and obtains a Gaussian-in-`k` penalty, then recursively proves
\[
\lim_{K\to\infty}\limsup_{\delta\downarrow0}\Pr\{N(u_\delta,v_\delta)>K\}=0.
\]
For a short middle interval `I`, the two-particle insertion satisfies
\[
\|s^{-2(t_-+t_+)}T_+O_IT_-\|\le C(|S(I)|/n)^2,
\]
via a two-by-two Cauchy--Binet identity and delocalized singular modes.  Cap weights `w(A)=(2M)^{-b(A)}` make the signed marked-loop contraction nonnegative and detect incompatible short crossings.

**Scope.** Scoped reference. It requires the specified finite Temperleyan geometry, two independent uniform dimer covers, particle-sector transfer normalization, positive macroscopic propagation widths, and capped eligible arches.  It is not a generic DPP or arbitrary curve-tightness result.
