# Negative example: claims that exceed the evidence

## Invalid B051 inference

“The first coefficients match the formula and the finite truncations agree to twelve decimals, so the infinite capacity series converges.”

The calculation only evaluates finite sums defining the displayed constants. It proves neither their rigorous tail error nor any bound on later capacity coefficients. A finite-order asymptotic formula is not a convergence theorem.

## Invalid A007 inference

“A renewal process obeys a law of large numbers, so arrivals at each polling epoch are fresh and the Poisson large-switch-over limit carries over.”

The law of large numbers does not make a nonexponential renewal process memoryless at a random visit epoch. Its residual life depends on the age and observation mechanism. Boon–Boxma–Winands explicitly call the extension a conjecture; numerical agreement does not remove the missing dependence and stationary-limit arguments.

## Invalid cluster-expansion transfer

“A named polymer criterion is in the bibliography, therefore this deletion-channel entropy expansion converges.”

A convergence criterion applies to a specified model with its stated weighted activity and incompatibility conditions. The C01 original is unread in this dossier, and no verified polymer representation or uniform bound for the optimized deletion-channel information rate is available.

## Invalid transfer from an adjacent deletion result

“A run-length genie gives a (0.7918(1-d)) high-deletion upper bound, and a deletion/substitution paper gives an entropy-scale asymptotic; together they determine higher-order coefficients at (d\to0).”

The (0.7918) result is for (d\to1); the deletion/substitution result has a separate substitution probability and an explicit order of limits. Neither proves a higher-order deletion-only expansion at (d\to0). Keep each theorem’s channel, regime, and limit order attached to the claim.


## Invalid identity/transfer shortcut

“C15’s full read validates the claimed C14 citation or repairs C01.” The C14 DOI/title-author identity is unverified, and C01 still has no verified full text. A related theorem can be used only within its own model and hypotheses; it cannot validate an unresolved citation, be relabeled as a missing source, or prove a deletion-channel expansion.


Do not apply Resing's branching representation to general-renewal arrivals or to a 1-limited/Bernoulli discipline without a new proof of the corresponding assumptions. Its embedded-epoch PGF is not an arbitrary-time waiting-time law. R13's two-queue adaptive stability example is also only a bounded comparator, not the missing A007 large-setup theorem.
