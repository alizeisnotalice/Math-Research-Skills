# W16 source guide

The authoritative inventory is [`evidence/W16/sources.json`](../evidence/W16/sources.json), with 44 records: 29 complete paper reads, one acquired 324-page book with complete Chapter 2 §§2.1–2.7 read, one partial read, eleven unread candidates, and two canonical-use records with zero new reads. The strict v2 candidate has 30 distinct works: 28 sources from the installed v1 floor after excluding teaching fragments R02/R03, plus post-v1 R13 and Resing R07 reuse. R13 was separately accepted by R08 for bounded A007 use; R04 accepted the two canonical source uses in this candidate. The combined v2 scope is pending independent review. The entire Gray book is acquired, but only the explicitly listed Chapter 2 unit was fully read. Full-read coverage for each canonical source is bundled from its original W14 evidence; these are not new W16 reads.

## Full-text sources read

- Shannon (1948), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x), [combined corrected reprint](https://web.mit.edu/6.441/spring05/reading/shannon1948.pdf): W15 read all 55 physical pages of the July/October reprint. Theorem 17 (physical p. 43) uses average transmitter power `P`; Theorem 20 (pp. 46–47) uses peak allowed transmitter power `S`. Keep these constraints distinct. It supplies foundational information-theory results; it does not prove the B051 small-deletion expansion.

- Kanoria and Montanari (2013), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2013.2262020), [arXiv:1104.5546](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.5546): direct B051 second-order expansion and explicit higher-order/convergence question; full 45-page paper.
- Kalai, Mitzenmacher, and Sudan (2010), [author PDF](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/2010-Tight_Asymptotic_Bounds.pdf): small-deletion entropy-scale converse; full 5-page paper.
- Fertonani and Duman (2010), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2010.2046210), [arXiv:0810.0785](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.0785): four genie-aided upper bounds, a finite fixed-block auxiliary channel in §II (`L` input bits, `R` output bits, `D=L−R` uniformly selected deletion positions, independently across uses), and two lower bounds; full 25-page arXiv PDF, read by W15. Numerical monotonicity claims remain conjectures where the paper says so.
- Rubinstein and Con (2023), [arXiv:2305.07156](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07156), [ISIT DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISIT54713.2023.10206626): full 25-page arXiv v1 read by W15; finite auxiliary deletion DMCs and memory-efficient Blahut–Arimoto upper bounds, plus a heuristic run-law search whose selected law is inserted into a rigorous lower-bound construction. It proves no higher-order small-deletion series.
- Tegin and Duman (2026), [arXiv:2411.14771v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14771), [IEEE DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2025.3644665): full 46-page v4 read by W15; simple and Gallager insertion models have separate small-insertion expansions. This is an adjacent-model analogy only, not a deletion-channel result. The reader PDF contains an unrelated embedded sentence on p. 36, recorded in the card.
- Diggavi, Mitzenmacher, and Pfister (2007), [ISIT DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISIT.2007.4557469), [author PDF](https://www.eecs.harvard.edu/~michaelm/postscripts/isit2007b.pdf): run-length/genie upper bound and high-deletion asymptotic; full 5-page conference paper, read by W15.
- Cheraghchi (2019), [arXiv:1711.01630v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.01630), [JACM DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3281275): full 79-page paper read by W15; mean-limited channel duality and D-repeat, Poisson-repeat, and deletion-channel upper bounds. It does not prove a higher-order small-deletion series.
- Drinea and Mitzenmacher, [author-hosted Harvard TR-07-04 PDF](https://www.eecs.harvard.edu/~michaelm/postscripts/isit2004.pdf): run-structured deletion-channel lower bounds; all 7 report-PDF pages visually read by W15 because the embedded-font text layer is unusable. This report copy is not conflated with the one-page ISIT 2004 or expanded 2006 journal record.
- Kazemi and Duman (2025), [arXiv:2503.02545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02545): six-page full v3 read by W15; deletion/substitution small-error characterization, not an all-orders deletion-only result.
- Drinea and Mitzenmacher (2007), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2007.901221), [author PDF](https://www.eecs.harvard.edu/~michaelm/postscripts/toit2007a.pdf): typical-output/jigsaw achievable lower bounds; full 22-page paper, read by W15.
- Mitzenmacher (2009), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1214/08-PS141), [arXiv:0809.3746](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.3746): historical survey through 2009; full 33-page paper, read by W15.
- Khodaiemehr, Feng, and Duman (2026), [arXiv:2609.24908](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24908): run-law lower-bound constructions; full v2 PDF, 22 pages, read by W15 from D01’s shared handoff.
- Scott and Sokal (2005), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-004-2055-4), [arXiv:cond-mat/0309352](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0309352): full 97-page paper read by W15; finite repulsive-lattice-gas nonvanishing and sharp event-LLL links. The event/dependency hypotheses are model-specific.
- Bovier and Zahradník (2000), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1018631710626): full 11-page WIAS Report 536 PDF read by W15; inductive sufficient cluster criterion for abstract hard-core polymer models.
- Poghosyan and Ueltschi (2009), [Warwick WRAP article](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/2195/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3124770): full 17-page article plus repository cover read by W15. Theorem 2.1 is physical PDF p. 4 (printed article p. 3); Theorems 2.2–2.3 are physical p. 5 (printed p. 4). Preserve the complex-potential stability inequality, the separate weighted integral alternatives 2/2′, global integrability, and the route-specific rooted factors recorded in the C15 card. The separate C14 title/DOI citation candidate remains unverified; C15 does not validate or replace it.
- Ueltschi (2004), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.17323/1609-4514-2004-4-2-511-522), [arXiv:math-ph/0304003](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0304003): measure-space cluster/correlation criteria; full 11-page paper.
- Fernández and Procacci (2007), [arXiv:math-ph/0605041](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0605041): hard-core abstract-polymer criterion and tree-graph proof; full 16-page paper.
- Ruelle (1967), [IHES author PDF](https://www.ihes.fr/~ruelle/PUBLICATIONS/%5B18%5D.pdf): Gibbs pressure/entropy variational principle; full 6-page paper.
- Jansen and Kolesnikov (2022), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-022-02992-6), [arXiv:2112.13134](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.13134): correlation/activity convergence criteria; full 49-page v3 PDF, read by W15 from a local copy whose hash matches D01’s handoff.
- Tate (2013), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-013-0831-8), [arXiv:1303.6444](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.6444): virial-radius bounds from cluster-coefficient estimates; full 14-page paper, read by W15.
- Lebowitz and Penrose (1964), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1704186), [Rutgers full scan](https://cmsr.rutgers.edu/images/people/lebowitz_joel/publications-archived/jll.pub_35.pdf): complete 7-page version-of-record scan read by W15; on physical p. 3, Eq. (3.11) gives the stable-potential gas virial-radius lower bound `0.28952/((u+1)B)`, Eq. (3.13) bounds finite-volume `k|β_k(V)|`, and Eq. (3.14) bounds infinite-volume irreducible Mayer cluster integrals `k|β_k|`. This is an auxiliary classical-gas result, not a deletion-channel expansion.
- Procacci and Yuhjtman (2017), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11005-016-0918-7), [arXiv:1508.07379v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.07379): full 15-page PDF read by W15; Penrose/MST tree-graph bounds for Mayer and virial expansions of stable, tempered continuous gases, not a generic channel expansion criterion.
- Bissacot, Fernández, Procacci, and Scoppola (2011), [Cambridge DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548311000253), [arXiv:0910.1824](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.1824): full 10-page paper read by W15; Theorem 5 is an event/dependency-graph LLL criterion. The PDF title page confirms author names/order; it is not a capacity theorem.
- Boon, Boxma, and Winands (2011; arXiv version 2014), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11134-011-9247-9), [arXiv:1408.0142](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.0142): full 9-page paper, including the general-renewal conjecture.
- Sigman (2009), [Regenerative Processes](https://www.columbia.edu/~ks20/stochastic-I/stochastic-I-regen.pdf): full 10 pages; exact cycle-integrability condition recorded.
- Sigman (2018), [Introduction to Renewal Theory](https://www.columbia.edu/~ks20/stochastic-I/stochastic-I-renewal.pdf): full 15 pages; renewal-reward assumptions and residual-life formula recorded.
- van der Mei (1999), [author-hosted PDF](https://www.math.vu.nl/~mei/articles/1999/JAP.pdf), [Cambridge DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1239/jap/1032374244): full 12-page paper read by W15; Poisson arrivals plus deterministic setups and fixed gated/exhaustive service yield a uniform large-setup delay limit. The source gives a counterexample for random setups.
- Winands (2006 report; journal version 2011), [Eindhoven repository PDF](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/1931233/617175.pdf), [VU journal record](https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/branching-type-polling-systems-with-large-setups/): W15 read the full 17-page 2006 SPOR report. Deterministic setups and the separate stochastic-setup two-stage heavy-traffic limit are recorded distinctly; neither proves the general-renewal A007 conjecture.

## Separately reviewed canonical reuse

- Chernova, Foss, and Kim (2012), [arXiv:1205.6234](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.6234): W16 R13 reuses canonical W14-004 (`PR-9e6384b2ee2f`) with the same six-page PDF hash. Its bounded auxiliary use is a two-queue adaptive Poisson polling stability example involving a service-law Laplace transform. R08 accepted this use after v1, count-once for W16; it is neither a new read/global source nor a large-setup/general-renewal result.
- Resing, [*Polling Systems and Multitype Branching Processes*](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/5474/5474D.pdf): W16 R07 reuses canonical W14-020 (`PR-e193a03e210b`), the complete 18-page CWI Technical Report BS-R9128 (December 1991; exact PDF hash in `sources.json`). The related 1993 journal version is not the read artifact. R04 accepted this bounded A007 use for the v2 candidate. Property 1 and Theorems 1/3/4 apply only under the stated branching/Poisson/immigration and stationarity hypotheses; they do not prove the general-renewal large-setup limit. Neither reuse adds a new full-text read.
