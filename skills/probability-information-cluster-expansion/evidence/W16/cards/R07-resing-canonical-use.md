# W16 canonical use: Resing, “Polling Systems and Multitype Branching Processes”

- **Canonical source:** `PR-e193a03e210b`, original W14-020. The read artifact is the complete 18-page CWI Technical Report BS-R9128 (December 1991), not the related 1993 *Queueing Systems* journal PDF. Exact SHA-256 `a19bcd6990530c31d5f3d22cc9674e7b21b087dd59796d96afce35270afec16b`. Original accepted full-read card, source record, and page coverage are in [`../../canonical_reads/PR-e193a03e210b/`](../../canonical_reads/PR-e193a03e210b/); the immutable R07/R04 approval excerpts are under `../source_approvals/`. R04 accepted this W16 use as one candidate source; no new full-text read is claimed.
- **Input/output:** Section 2 Property 1 requires each customer present at a visit to be independently replaced by an identically distributed random vector of effective descendants by the next visit. Under the paper's cyclic single-server polling model with Poisson arrivals and general service times, Theorem 3 (PDF p. 9) represents queue lengths at successive visits to a fixed queue as a multitype branching process with immigration in every state when switch-over times are positive; its PGF recursion is Eq. (18), PDF pp. 9–10. Theorem 4 (PDF pp. 11–12) is a different zero-switch-over model, with immigration only at state zero. Exhaustive/gated and the stated binomial variants satisfy Property 1; 1-limited and Bernoulli service do not in general.
- **Stationarity:** Theorem 1 (PDF p. 7) also requires a primitive offspring mean matrix with spectral radius below one and an irreducible, aperiodic embedded chain; the theorem's finite log-moment condition on immigrant population is required for a stationary law. Its PGF is the product of iterated offspring PGFs. The embedded-epoch result does not give arbitrary-time queue lengths or waiting-time laws.
- **A007 boundary:** This is a Poisson-arrival branching comparator, not a general-renewal or large-switch-over theorem. A007 still needs an age/residual-life state and independent proofs of stability/tightness and the stationary-to-fluid limit exchange. Unsupported service disciplines cannot inherit Property 1.
