# Michael Mitzenmacher (2009), “A Survey of Results for Deletion Channels and Related Synchronization Channels”

- Source: *Probability Surveys* 6, 1–33 (2009), DOI [10.1214/08-PS141](https://doi.org/10.1214/08-PS141); arXiv:0809.3746. Local PDF: `evidence/fulltexts/I06.pdf` (33 pages; SHA-256 `a7149e974559fdc1515c9bc192eec9dd8dd93ddb4d86f776269da2946243ac9a`).
- Reader: W15; full PDF pp. 1–33.
- Scope: a historical survey through 2009. It is useful for tracing proof methods and older bounds; it does not establish present-day open-problem status.

## What the survey covers

Sections 2–4 discuss trace/subsequence formulations, greedy decoding for Markov inputs, and jigsaw-style lower bounds. Section 5 gives a Poisson-repeat reduction and the lower bound (C_p\ge (1-p)/9) in its stated deletion model (PDF pp. 16–17). Sections 6–7 set out an information-theoretic formulation and capacity upper-bound methods; later sections discuss trace reconstruction and synchronization variants. The survey is a map of results with source references, not a substitute for the cited primary theorem when a detailed hypothesis or current numerical comparison is needed.

## B051 boundary

It predates the small-deletion second-order theorem and the 2026 run-law learning paper. It is background for historical comparisons only; do not infer that any bound it calls best-known remains best-known now.
