# 主伪多项式的 binomial 变换条件

Delaygue--Rivoal, arXiv:2102.01534v2, physical pp. 1--6, defines a primary pseudo-polynomial by prime-modulus congruences. Its Theorem 1(i) identifies this with the all-index divisibility \(P_n\mid b_n\) of the binomial transform. Theorem 1(iii) gives a polynomiality conclusion under the stronger growth threshold \(e-1\). The broader threshold \(e\) is Conjecture 2 and remains conditional.
