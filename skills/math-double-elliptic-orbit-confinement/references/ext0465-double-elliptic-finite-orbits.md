# Finite orbits under two elliptic translations

Corvaja, Tsimerman and Zannier, *Finite Orbits in Surfaces with a Double Elliptic Fibration and Torsion Values of Sections*, local `arXiv:2302.00859v2`, physical pp. 1--8. The later published record is *Annales de l'Institut Fourier*, article `10.5802/aif.3783`.

Theorem 1.1 confines points with finite orbit under the indicated semigroup to a finite union of fibres \(F_x\). Corollary 1.2 adds the source condition that \((\lambda,\mu):X\to\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) is finite and obtains the stated finiteness conclusion for the translated orbit family.

The proof fixes a number field supporting the surface, fibrations, sections and indeterminacy points. It then invokes the paper's Betti-map and Ax--Schanuel input, a Shioda ramification condition and height estimates. These prerequisites remain caller-provided mathematical certificates.
