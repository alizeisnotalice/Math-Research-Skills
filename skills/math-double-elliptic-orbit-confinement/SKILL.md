# Double elliptic orbit confinement

Use this skill for the source model of a surface carrying two distinct non-isotrivial elliptic fibrations and their translation maps. State the number field, both fibrations and sections, the open domains of the rational maps, and the orbit convention before invoking the theorem.

The supplied theorem confines finite orbits to a finite union of fibration curves. Its stronger finiteness corollary requires the stated finiteness of the map formed by the two fibration parameters. This is a conditional geometric conclusion. It does not compute orbits, construct the exceptional curves, or replace the source's Ax--Schanuel, Shioda and height inputs.
