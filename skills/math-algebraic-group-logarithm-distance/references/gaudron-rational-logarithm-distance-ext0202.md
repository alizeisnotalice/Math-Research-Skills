# Rational-case algebraic-group logarithm distance

## Identity and version

The local 36-page file is arXiv:math/0410082v2, whose embedded text is
encoding-corrupted. Its title and author match Éric Gaudron, *Étude du cas
rationnel de la théorie des formes linéaires de logarithmes*, *Journal of
Number Theory* 127 (2007), 220--261, DOI
`10.1016/j.jnt.2007.08.001`. The author-hosted published PDF was checked for
this interface. The publication is a distinct file, so this is an identity
and statement check rather than a byte-identity assertion.

## Scoped theorem

On published p. 223, Theorem 1.1 takes a commutative algebraic group `G` of
dimension `g` over a number field `k`, a place `v0`, a nonzero logarithm
`u` of `p` in `G(k)`, and an algebraic Lie subalgebra `V=Lie(H)` of
codimension `t`. If the subgroup generated by `p` meets no proper algebraic
subgroup of `G` apart from the identity, and `log(b)` bounds the height of
`V` for `b >= e`, it asserts

\[
 \log d_{v_0}(u,V)\geq
 -c_3(\log b)^{1+(g+1)/t}\max\{1,h(p)\}^{g/t},
\]

where the source specifies the allowed dependence of `c_3`.

This is a conditional distance lower-bound interface. It does not turn a
general linear form in arbitrary complex logarithms into an automatically
certified bound. It also does not establish the nonperiodicity condition,
the required group model, an algebraic Lie-subgroup description, heights, or
the relevant local exponential/logarithm branch.

## Proof dependencies

The published abstract and pp. 220--221 identify formal-subscheme size
estimates, Raynaud's arithmetic lemma, an absolute Siegel lemma, and Roy's
ultrametric interpolation lemma as ingredients. The theorem's proof and its
source-dependent constants remain those of the published paper. A use must
preserve all local-place and height conventions.

and [publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022314X07001667).
