# Algebraic-group logarithm distance bounds

Use this skill for a nonzero logarithm of an algebraic point on a commutative
algebraic group, when the target subspace is the Lie algebra of an algebraic
subgroup. It organizes the exact inputs needed to invoke a rational-case
linear-independence measure. It does not compute the source-dependent
constant, construct an exponential map, or certify non-torsion modulo every
proper connected subgroup.

## Input gate

Record a number field `k`, a place `v0`, a commutative algebraic group `G`
over `k`, a connected algebraic subgroup `H`, the codimension `t` of
`Lie(H)`, a normalized local exponential domain, and a nonzero
`u` satisfying `exp_v0(u)=p` for `p` in `G(k)`. Supply the height of
`Lie(H)`, an admissible `b >= e` with `log(b)` bounding that height, and the
source's nonperiodicity premise for the subgroup generated by `p`.

Under these caller-provided hypotheses, the cited theorem gives a lower bound
for `log distance_v0(u, Lie(H))` of the source's stated shape. Treat its
constant as depending on `G`, `k`, `v0`, the local norm and `u`. A numerical
approximation to a logarithm or a finite torsion search does not supply the
nonperiodicity premise.

Read [the rational-case source gate](references/gaudron-rational-logarithm-distance-ext0202.md)
for the exact theorem, its publication identity, and its dependency boundary.

## 仿射代数群分支

在非交换群或矩阵表示情形，用[仿射群度量来源卡](references/huicochea-affine-group-log-distance.md)核对精确嵌入、坐标基、子空间、定量参数及共轭条件。保留该定理自己的范数与高度定义。
