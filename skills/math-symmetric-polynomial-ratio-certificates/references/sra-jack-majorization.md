# Jack and restricted Macdonald majorization contract

Source: Suvrit Sra, *On majorization inequalities for normalized Jack and Macdonald polynomials*, author preprint v1, 20 physical pages; reviewed physical pp. 1--9.  Local source identity: SHA-256 `1c1c128f84ce4c0f7f769988c74f25d8da87d486601aef7c66bec9043c4f92c6`.

The source states a normalized Jack-polynomial majorization inequality for α>0, positive variables, equal-size partitions, and the stated dominance order.  Its Jack proof passes through a positive Gelfand--Tsetlin integral and the Dixon--Anderson/Okounkov--Olshanski recursion.  The Macdonald assertions in the reviewed portion have narrower finite-​q restrictions and must be kept separate from the Jack route.

## Use boundary

This is a source contract, not an exact evaluator for arbitrary Jack or Macdonald normalizations.  Before applying it, fix the polynomial normalization, α, positive variable domain, partition sizes, and dominance direction.  A finite rational Schur calculation in the parent skill does not certify this Jack or Macdonald theorem.
