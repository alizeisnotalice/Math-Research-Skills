# Conditional specialization theorem for horizontal E-sections

Source: C. Gasbarri, *Horizontal sections of connections on curves and transcendence*, arXiv:0910.1285v1, physical pp. 3--5 and 26--28.

Theorem 1.6 (physical p. 4) is a point-value theorem, not a generic Nevanlinna-growth criterion. Let \(X\) be a smooth projective curve over \(\mathbb Q\), \(D\) an effective divisor, and \(E\) a vector bundle of rank \(m>1\) equipped with a meromorphic connection with poles in \(D\). If a horizontal analytic E-section of prescribed type \(\alpha\), based at the stated rational points and satisfying the paper's formal-geometric and analytic growth hypotheses, has Zariski-dense image, then at a permitted rational evaluation point \(q\) the theorem concludes
\[
 \operatorname{Trdeg}_{\mathbb Q}\mathbb Q(f(q))=m.
\]

Before invoking this result, provide a concrete connection, horizontal section, pole divisor, base/evaluation points, and certificates for the exact E-section (including LG-germ and growth) and Zariski-density hypotheses. The general proof in §7 relies on the paper's Arakelov/formal-geometry estimates. It supplies no automatic embedding for Euler's constant or for a merely numerically observed special value.
