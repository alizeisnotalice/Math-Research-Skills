## Conditional theorem interface

Let `f_1, ..., f_n` be nonconstant entire functions on a fixed complex affine space satisfying the
source's successive characteristic-growth separation
`T(r,f_i) = S(r,f_(i+1))`.  For a transcendental meromorphic function `F`,
Theorem 1.2 gives the lower bound n+1 for the transcendence degree of the
field generated by the `f_i` and `F(f_i)`.  Under the source's additional
finite-order and entire positive-order conditions, it gives transcendence degree 2n in the source’s one-variable case.

The notation `S(r, g)` includes the source's exceptional-set convention; it
is not a pointwise asymptotic statement and cannot be certified from finitely
many values or Taylor coefficients.

## Caller obligations

1. Specify the entire functions and the meromorphic/entire `F`.
2. Prove every characteristic/proximity inequality with the same exceptional
   sets and normalization used by the source.
3. When invoking equality, prove the finite-order and positive-order gates.

The conclusion concerns function fields over the complex numbers.  It does
not certify algebraic independence or irrationality of isolated evaluated
numbers.
