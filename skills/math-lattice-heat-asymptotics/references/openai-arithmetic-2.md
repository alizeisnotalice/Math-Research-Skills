# Primitive sextic packet: scoped cusp-tightness template

**Source.** *Equidistribution of Primitive Sextic Torus Packets*, pp. 2, 7–9,
51, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Equidistribution-of-Primitive-Sextic-Torus-Packets-October-5-2026/primitive-sextic-torus-packets.pdf).

**Input.** Totally real primitive degree-six fields with discriminant
\(D\to\infty\), their complete signed volume-weighted ideal-lattice packets,
and the source's uniform zero exclusion, residue lower bound and short-interval
Dedekind-divisor estimates.

**Procedure.** Bound a short-vector event by its count. For the sup-norm box
integrate diagonal parameters to obtain
\(V_f(p)=2^6(\log(r^6/p))^5/5!\) for \(0<p\le r^6\). Split the
\(d_K\)-sum at \(D^{1/4}\), using the source's short-interval estimate above
and an elementary six-fold divisor bound below.

**Output.** \(\mu_{K,\sigma}\{m(x)\le r\}\le Cr^c\) for \(0<r<1\),
uniformly at large \(D\), hence tightness by Mahler compactness.

**Boundary.** Tightness does not prove equidistribution; the final source
argument additionally needs its homogeneous-component, tube and root-activity
steps. This is not a template for arbitrary packets.
