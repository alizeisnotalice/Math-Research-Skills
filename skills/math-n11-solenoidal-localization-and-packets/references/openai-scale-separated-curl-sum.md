For a supplied scale-separated list of compact curl packets with the stated amplitude, displacement, collar, and support bounds, determine the finitely many packets meeting a given compact time window. Sum only those packets and apply the source's derivative bounds termwise to retain local finiteness and smoothness.

This is packet bookkeeping, not a computational-realization or Navier–Stokes claim.
