# Constructed-flow residual energy identity

Source 1. OpenAI, *A Fixed Particle Test for Computation in a Forced Viscous Flow*, physical pp.3–5 and 8–12, SHA-256 `87defbd4577b4c8ef80cd148000ffd55e7b3d2ce296d27b04efb16aae92f8c0d`.

For any supplied smooth mean-zero divergence-free periodic velocity `U`, form its exact residual force and check the mean/pressure cancellation. For another classical solution with the same data, subtract and apply the finite-time L2 difference-energy inequality with the `||grad U||_infinity` Gronwall coefficient.

This reference deliberately excludes the machine/particle-event interpretation; it is only a residual and uniqueness-energy identity.
