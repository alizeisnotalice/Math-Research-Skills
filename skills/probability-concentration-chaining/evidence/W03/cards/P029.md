## Main theorem and hypotheses

**Theorem 4 (Pickands, PDF p. 11).** Let `X(t)`, `t∈[0,p]`, be a continuous, centered, stationary Gaussian process with covariance `r(t)=E[X(t+s)X(s)]=1−|t|^α+o(|t|^α)` as `t→0`, where `0<α≤2`, and assume `r(t)<1` for every `t>0`. Then, as `u→∞`,

`P(sup_{t∈[0,p]} X(t)>u)=H_α p u^(2/α) Ψ(u)(1+o(1))`,

where `Ψ(u)=1−Φ(u)~(2π)^(-1/2)u^(-1)e^(−u²/2)` and `H_α=lim_{T→∞}H(T)/T∈(0,∞)` is defined through the local tangent field below. The source's non-degeneracy assumption is strict for all positive lags, not just locally.

## Proof chain and auxiliary results

- **Local block limits (PDF pp. 4–8).** For the two-dimensional tangent Gaussian field `χ`, `Eχ(t)=−|t₁|^α−|t₂|^α` and its increment variance is `2(|t₁−s₁|^α+|t₂−s₂|^α)`. Lemma 3 gives the local asymptotic `P(sup_{t∈u^(−2/α)T}X(t)>u)=Ψ(u)H(T)(1+o(1))` for compact `T⊂R²`, with `H(T)=E exp(sup_{t∈T}χ(t))<∞`. Lemma 4 gives its one-dimensional interval version, `P(sup_{[0,u^(−2/α)T]}X>u)=Ψ(u)H(T)(1+o(1))`, for the fractional-Brownian tangent `χ(t)=B_H(t)−|t|^α`, `H=α/2`. The source explicitly normalizes this fractional Brownian motion by `E B_H(1)^2=2` (Remark 2, physical PDF p. 7); do not silently replace it by the unit-variance-at-one convention. Corollary 2 records the subadditive upper bound `H(T)≤⌈T⌉H(1)`.
- **Comparison and separated blocks (PDF pp. 3–8).** Lemma 1 conditions a Gaussian pair by its regression decomposition. Lemma 2 proves the Bonferroni lower bound `P(∪A_i)≥ΣP(A_i)−Σ_{i<j}P(A_i∩A_j)`. Theorem 2 states Slepian comparison for separable Gaussian fields with equal pointwise means and variances and ordered covariances. Theorem 3 is Borell's upper tail: for centered a.s.-bounded Gaussian `X`, `m=E sup X`, `σ²=sup_t Var X(t)`, and `w≥m`, `P(sup X>w)≤exp(−(w−m)²/(2σ²))`. Lemma 5 bounds joint crossings of separated short intervals, with constants depending on `α,t₀,T`; retain its stated correlation bounds and small-`ε` conditions.
- **Constant lower bound (PDF p. 3).** Theorem 1 yields `H_α≥α/[2^(2+2/α)Γ(1/α)]`.
- **Theorem 4 proof (PDF pp. 12–16).** Partition `[0,p]` into blocks of length `T u^(−2/α)`. A union bound and Lemma 4 give the normalized upper limit `≤H(T)/T`. For the lower bound, Bonferroni subtracts pairwise block-crossing terms. Adjacent blocks (`A₁`) are controlled by extending to a `√T`-scale interval and applying Lemma 4; intermediate blocks (`A₂`) use Lemma 5 and a summable bound of order `T²e^(−T^α/8)`; sufficiently separated blocks (`A₃`) use Borell on `X(t)+X(s)`, whose variance is uniformly below `4` because `r(h)<1` away from zero. After first taking `u→∞` and then `T→∞`, the normalized probability converges to `H(T)/T→H_α`, with positivity and finiteness established.

## Transfer boundary

This is a one-parameter, stationary, continuous Gaussian-process extreme-value theorem. It does not by itself cover nonstationary variance profiles, multidimensional manifolds, non-Gaussian processes, or finite-threshold error bounds. Its proof uses local covariance regular variation and global strict non-perfect-correlation at every positive lag.
