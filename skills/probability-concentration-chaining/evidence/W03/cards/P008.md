## Results

For `X⊂R^n`, draw `I_1,…,I_m` as the first `m` positions of a uniform permutation of `[n]` (without replacement) and `J_1,…,J_m` independently uniformly with replacement. Put `Z=sup_{x∈X}Σ_{k≤m}x_{I_k}`, `Z′=sup_xΣ_{k≤m}x_{J_k}`, `Σ²=sup_xΣ_{k≤m}x_{I_k}²`, and `\widetildeΣ²=sup_xΣ_{k≤m}x_{J_k}²` (PDF p. 2).

Hoeffding's convex-order lemma (Lemma 2.1, p. 2) gives `Eφ(Z)≤Eφ(Z′)` for every convex increasing `φ`, and likewise controls the expected quadratic proxy. For `X⊂[-1,1]^n`, Theorem 2.2 (PDF pp. 2–4) states

`P(Z≥EZ+t)≤2 exp{−(t/C_1) log(1+t/(C_2 E\widetildeΣ²))}`, `t≥0`,

with `C_1=36`, `C_2=46`. Proposition 2.5 gives an intermediate Bernstein form `P(Z≥EZ+t)≤exp{−min(t/32,t²/(128 EΣ²))}`. The proof combines this small-deviation estimate with the large-deviation MGF bound of Lemma 2.4 (Ledoux's estimate) for sampling with replacement.

For a uniform random permutation `σ∈S_n` and a matrix `a=(a_{ij})` with `a_{ij}∈[−1,1]` and `Σ_{ij}a_{ij}=0`, define the Hoeffding statistic `f(σ)=Σ_{k=1}^n a_{k,σ(k)}`. Theorem 3.1 (PDF p. 5) states

`P(f≥t)≤2 exp{−(t/36)log(1+t/(36 EΣ²))}`, `t≥0`,

where `Σ²=Σ_k a_{k,σ(k)}²` and `EΣ²=n^(−1)Σ_{ij}a_{ij}²`. This is a one-sided upper-tail estimate for a mean-zero statistic. Remark 3.2 (p. 5) gives a centered form by double-centering `a` and using `Var(f)=(n−1)^(−1)Σ_{ij}d_{ij}²`. Theorem 3.3 (p. 6), for nonnegative `a_{ij}∈[0,1]`, gives `P(f>Ef+t)≤exp{−(t/4)log(1+t/(4Ef))}`. Section 4 gives an extension to suprema of families of Hoeffding statistics.

## Source proof discrepancy

On rendered PDF p. 4, the proof of Theorem 2.2 assumes `32t≥C_1C_2 E\widetildeΣ²`, then prints the intermediate bound `E Z′_ρ≤ρ^(−1)E\widetildeΣ²≤32tρ^(−1)/(C_1C_1)`. Algebraically the preceding assumption gives denominator `C_1C_2`; the next exponent line also uses `2α/(C_1C_2)`. This is an internal mismatch in the printed proof line. The card preserves the stated theorem and does not certify the proof chain exactly as printed; this observation alone does not refute Theorem 2.2.

The result concerns uniform permutations and bounded coordinate families; it is not a general result for dependent sampling designs.

## Transfer boundary

Distinguish the without-replacement random proxy `Σ²` from the with-replacement proxy `\widetildeΣ²` in Theorem 2.2. For Hoeffding statistics, retain both the zero-total-sum matrix condition and the random-permutation model. The theorem's printed proof has the denominator inconsistency recorded above, so seek an independently checked proof if exact derivation provenance matters.
