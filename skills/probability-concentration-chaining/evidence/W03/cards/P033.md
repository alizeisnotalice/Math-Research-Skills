## Cramér theorem and proof scope

The notes define the cumulant generating function `φ(λ)=log E e^{λξ₁}` and its Fenchel–Legendre transform `φ*(x)=sup_{λ∈R}{λx−φ(λ)}`.

**Theorem 2.1 (Cramér, physical PDF p. 9).** If `ξ₁,ξ₂,…` are iid real random variables with mean `μ∈R`, `S_n=Σ_{i=1}^n ξ_i`, and cumulant generating function `φ`, then for every `x≥μ`,

`lim_{n→∞} (1/n) log P{S_n/n ≥ x} = −φ*(x)`.

The proof’s upper bound is the Chernoff/Legendre-transform estimate established in §2.1. For the lower bound, the text first assumes `φ` is finite on all `R`. It uses differentiability/convexity to choose the exponential tilt `dP_λ/dP = exp(λξ−φ(λ))` with tilted mean `φ′(λ)=x`, then a law-of-large-numbers neighborhood under the tilted product measure and the likelihood-ratio identity to obtain the matching exponential rate. The source explicitly says the case where `φ(λ)=+∞` for some `λ` is in Kal02, p. 541; that case is cited rather than proved in these notes. No stronger proof completeness is claimed.
