## Model and assumptions

The environment `ξ_n` is iid. Given `ξ`, offspring counts are conditionally independent with generation-`n` law `p(ξ_n)`. The quenched law is `P_ξ`; all displayed results in this paper use the annealed law `P(dx,dξ)=P_ξ(dx)τ(dξ)`. With offspring mean `m_n`, let `Π_n=∏_{i=0}^{n−1}m_i`, `W_n=Z_n/Π_n`, and `X=log m_0`, `μ=EX`. The standing assumptions (PDF p. 2) are `p_0(ξ_0)=0` almost surely and `σ²=E(X−μ)²∈(0,∞)`, so `μ>0` and the process is supercritical. The paper also assumes throughout (2.1), `E[(Z_1/m_0) log^+ Z_1]<∞`; this ensures `W_n→W>0` and convergence in `L¹`. Define the standardized increment statistic

`Z_{n_0,n} = [log(Z_{n_0+n}/Z_{n_0})−nμ]/(σ√n)`.

It decomposes into an iid environmental random-walk sum and a normalized branching-martingale ratio:

`log(Z_{n_0+n}/Z_{n_0}) = Σ_{i=1}^n X_{n_0+i} + log(W_{n_0+n}/W_{n_0})` (PDF pp. 5, 9).

## Main results

- **Theorem 2.1 (PDF p. 5):** Under the standing assumptions and A1, where `E exp(λ₀X)<∞` for some `λ₀>0`, the following hold uniformly in `n₀∈N`. For `n≥2`, `0≤x<√(log n)`,

  `|log{P(Z_{n₀,n}≥x)/(1−Φ(x))}| ≤ C(1+x³)(log n)/√n`;

  for `n≥2`, `√(log n)≤x=o(√n)`,

  `|log{P(Z_{n₀,n}≥x)/(1−Φ(x))}| ≤ Cx³/√n`.

  Both inequalities also hold for the lower tail after replacing the ratio by `P(−Z_{n₀,n}≥x)/Φ(−x)`. In the moderate range `x∈[0,o(n^(1/6)))`, Corollary 2.2 gives uniform relative equivalence to the normal tail. Corollary 2.1 yields a uniform MDP over all `n₀` for any `a_n→∞` with `a_n/√n→0`, speed `a_n²` and rate `I(x)=x²/2` (PDF p. 6).

- **Theorem 2.2 (PDF p. 6):** Under A2, the exponential moment can be weakened to `E exp{λ₀ X^(4γ/(1−2γ))}<∞` for some `λ₀>0`, `γ∈(0,1/6]`. Then the same uniform normal-tail equivalence holds for `x∈[0,o(n^γ))`, for both signs. Here `X=log m_0≥0` under the standing no-zero-offspring assumption.

- **Theorems 2.3–2.4 (PDF p. 7):** Under A1, the uniform Kolmogorov distance for `Z_{n₀,n}` and `−Z_{n₀,n}` is at most `C(log n)/√n`. Under A3, `E|X|^(2+ρ)<∞` with `0<ρ<(√5−1)/2`, it is at most `C n^(−ρ/2)` for both signs. The exponent restriction is part of this paper's uniform-in-`n₀` result.

Section 3 gives confidence intervals for `μ` from `log(Z_{n₀+n}/Z_{n₀})` when `σ` is known: Proposition 3.1 uses `κ_n∈(0,1)` with `|log κ_n|=o(n^(1/3))`; Proposition 3.2 treats `κ_n→0` under `|log κ_n|=o(n)` (PDF pp. 7–9).

## Proof map and limitations

For Theorem 2.1, the upper bound splits the environmental iid sum from `(log W_{n₀,n})^+`, applies classical iid Cramér moderate deviations to the former, and Markov's inequality to the nonnegative martingale ratio for the latter. The lower bound uses a harmonic moment `E W^(−a)<∞` (Lemma 4.2) and controls `(log W_{n₀,n})^-` before applying the iid lower moderate-deviation bound. The cut at `x=√(log n)` yields the separate logarithmic factor in the smaller range. Theorem 2.2 first proves an exponential moment bound for the negative part of `log W`, then uses a Linnik-type iid estimate and the same decomposition. Theorem 2.4 combines an iid Berry–Esseen bound with uniformly bounded moments of `log W_n`; choosing `p=ρ/(1−ρ)<1+ρ` explains the restriction `ρ<(√5−1)/2` (PDF pp. 9–21).

**Printed proof notation issue:** In the proof of Theorem 2.4, equations (8.5), (8.9) and (8.13) print `δ` in the exponent `n^(−δ/2)`, although Theorem 2.4 and assumption A3 use `ρ`; the intervening derivation on p. 21 uses `ρ` and the final theorem uses `ρ`. The card records the theorem with the stated `ρ` and flags the proof's symbol mismatch instead of treating the proof display as a separate parameter.

## Transfer boundary

These are model-specific annealed results for an iid random environment and a supercritical BPRE. Keep the no-zero-offspring and `2.1` integrability hypotheses, finite positive variance, A1/A2/A3 as applicable, and uniformity over `n₀` distinct from a quenched statement. Do not remove the `log n` term in Theorem 2.3 based on the authors' conjecture, or extend Theorem 2.4 beyond its stated `ρ` range.
