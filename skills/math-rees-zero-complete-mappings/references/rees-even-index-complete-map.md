# Source note: even-index Rees construction

Source: *Complete mappings of the Brauer and partial Brauer monoids*, author manuscript, physical pp. 4--7 of 16. Local SHA-256: `35bc80dc9ec4051a1511c0ce9a0e86af7fe6efef1ae1a1b8dff586bbc0574979`.

The source defines Rees 0-matrix multiplication (p. 4) and gives an explicit even-index complete mapping over the `Z/2Z` quotient in Lemma 4.3 (pp. 6--7). The checker verifies that finite formula from its stated diagonal premise. It makes no claim about the lifting lemma, diagram monoids, or the paper's classification theorem.
