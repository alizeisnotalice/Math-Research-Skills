# Integrated matrix resolvent comparison

Source: *Sharp one-dimensional Lieb–Thirring inequalities for matrix
potentials* (OpenAI, 5 Oct 2026), SHA-256
`cca7663c4920764a4150cb69ec72cd164137f895bfdf7ca11a3a9ac3e33509a5`,
physical pp. 5–9, Theorem 3.1 and Lemma 3.2. Pinned source:
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Sharp-one-dimensional-Lieb-Thirring-inequalities-for-matrix-potentials-October-5-2026/sharp-matrix-lieb-thirring.pdf>.

For Hermitian finite matrices `B,V`, the source assumes `(sI-B)^2+V>=bI`
for every real `s`. Its integrated resolvent construction defines `N`; only
when this resulting matrix is PSD does the trace comparison apply. The
half-line boundary matrices and nonnegative correction are a matrix trace
method, not a licence to claim a Lieb–Thirring inequality without the global
symbol positivity and the required integral convergence.
