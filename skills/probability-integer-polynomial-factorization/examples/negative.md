# Negative example: an epsilon factor is not an original counterexample

A factorization may have coefficients in $[-\epsilon,1+\epsilon]$ for arbitrarily small $\epsilon>0$ and still fail the original hypothesis. In W18-X001, the quotient $B_n$ has coefficient $[z]B_n=-t_n<0$. The source proves Hare’s relaxed $\epsilon$-unfair conjecture; it does **not** give factors with nonnegative coefficients. Do not translate “negative coefficient tends to zero” into “nonnegative coefficient.”
