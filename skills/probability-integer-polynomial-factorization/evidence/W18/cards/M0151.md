# M0151 — Dvorsky, *The Unfair 0–1 Polynomial Problem and High-Degree Trinomials*

**Source/version.** Alexander Dvorsky, arXiv:2608.19173v2 (revised 2026-09-01), 53-page PDF supplied by D02. Physical p. 1 matches the title, author, and version. All physical pages 1–53 were read; hash and local path are in `sources.json`.

## Theorems and proof structure

- **Theorem 1.1 (physical p. 3).** For every integer k>=7, 0<a<1, and polynomial Q in R[x] with nonnegative coefficients, if every coefficient of (1+a x^2+x^k)Q(x) lies in {0,1}, then Q=0. The theorem combines the even-k reduction, the companion manuscript's bounded odd cases 7<=k<=339, and this paper's high-degree result; do not attribute the bounded odd cases to this paper's new proof.
- **Theorem 1.2 (physical p. 3, formal statement).** The theorem is stated for every integer k>=341 and 0<a<1. Even k are discharged directly by Lemma 1.3, which splits the even and odd coefficient subsequences; the substantive analytic proof in the body therefore assumes k is odd. After first-zero reduction, one may assume a<=1/2.
- **Recurrence and packet representation (§§2–3).** Coefficient comparison gives r_n=q_n+a q_(n-2)+q_(n-k). At the first zero N, nonnegativity forces q_N=q_(N-2)=q_(N-k)=0. The coefficients are represented by exact truncated negative-binomial packets; Theorem 3.4 proves strict log-concavity of the packet profile.
- **Analytic engine (§§4–7).** Compact-range estimates and single-translate modal estimates control the pre-zero profile. A sparse spectral bootstrap extracts a growing characteristic-root mode. In the multiple-translate regime, antiperiodic periodization produces a theta kernel with curvature/local-limit bounds; cyclic and frozen profiles are transferred back to exact anchor values. Appendices A–B record analytic error and finite endpoint calculations.
- **Terminal contradiction (§§8–10).** Theorem 8.1 bounds coefficients before the first zero. Lemma 9.1 forces an all-one continuation, and Lemmas 10.1–10.2 yield a negative terminal coefficient, contradicting nonnegativity of Q.

## Limits

The result excludes the specific trinomial factor family 1+a x^2+x^k. Odd k=7,...,339 in Theorem 1.1 depend on the stated companion manuscript. The appendix's interval/numerical endpoint certificates are author-reported and were not independently rerun in this reading pass. This does not classify arbitrary nonnegative polynomial factors or prove the unrestricted conjecture.
