## Main result and proof mechanism

- **Theorem 1.1 (p. 2).** Let `N≥2`, and let `p` be the least prime divisor of `N`. If `F∈R[X]\{0}` has nonnegative real coefficients and `Φ_N | F`, then its support diameter `D(F)=max supp(F)−min supp(F)` satisfies
  `D(F) ≥ ((p−1)/p)N`.
  Equality holds exactly for `F(X)=c X^b G_{N,p}(X)`, where `c>0`, `b∈Z_{≥0}`, and `G_{N,p}(X)=Σ_{j=0}^{p−1}X^{jN/p}`. This is about a *specified cyclotomic divisor* of a nonnegative-real polynomial; it is not a factor theorem for an arbitrary 0–1 polynomial.
- **Proof route (pp. 3–5).** Divisibility gives `F(e^{2πik/N})=0` for `1≤k<p`, since each such `k` is coprime to `N`. After removing the initial monomial and normalizing coefficients by `F(1)`, put the coefficient mass at angles `θ_a=2πa/N−πD/N`; it is a positive measure supported on an arc `[-α,α]`, `α=πD/N`, whose first `p−1` Fourier moments vanish. Lemma 2.1 (p. 3–4) tests this measure against the sine-vector polynomial `Q(z)=Σ_{j=0}^{r−2}v_jz^j`, `v_j=(−1)^j sin((j+1)π/r)`. The recurrence `v_{j−1}+v_{j+1}=−2 cos(π/r)v_j` gives `E|Q|²=S=r/2` and `E(cos θ)|Q|²=−cos(π/r)S`; since `cos θ≥cos α` on the arc, `α≥π−π/r`. Equality forces the measure onto the `r` equally spaced zeros of `(cos θ+cos(π/r))|Q|²`, and discrete Fourier inversion forces equal masses. Applying this with `r=p` proves the bound and equality case. The displayed identity `(1+2cos(t)z+z²)Q(z)=sin(t)(1+(−z)^r)` on p. 4 is visually confirmed.
- **Proposition 3.1 / Corollary 3.2 (pp. 5–6).** If `2≤r≤N` and a nonzero nonnegative-real `F` vanishes at `e^{2πik/N}` for every `1≤k<r`, then `D(F)≥N(1−1/r)`; equality is possible only when `r|N`, and then `F=cX^bΣ_{j=0}^{r−1}X^{jN/r}`. For rational-coefficient `F`, define `ρ_N(F)=min{k≥1:F(ζ_N^k)≠0}`. The paper proves `ρ_N(F)=min{d:d|N, Φ_{N/d}∤F}` and `D(F)≥N−N/ρ_N(F)`, with its equality classification. The rationality is essential for passing from one primitive root to all its conjugates: the authors give a real-coefficient counterexample on p. 5.

## Further tiling and stability tools

- **Corollary 1.2 (p. 2; proof p. 6).** For finite `A⊂Z`, `|A|≥2`, satisfying both Coven–Meyerowitz conditions (T1) and (T2), with `M=lcm(S_A)` and `p` the least prime divisor of `|A|`, `diam(A)≥((p−1)/p)M`. Equality iff `M=p^n` and `A=b+p^{n−1}{0,…,p−1}`. The paper explicitly says this does not prove the separate assertion that every finite integer tile satisfies (T2); without (T1),(T2), the asserted general diameter bound can fail (Example 4.1, p. 6).
- **Theorem 4.2 (pp. 7–8).** Under (T2), define `n_q=v_q(M)`, `E_q={e:q^e∈S_A}`, and `ℓ_q=max{h:{n_q−h+1,…,n_q}⊆E_q}`. Let `L_A=M` if every `ℓ_q=n_q`, otherwise `L_A=min_{q:ℓ_q<n_q} q^{ℓ_q}`. Then `diam(A)≥M−M/L_A`. Equality is exactly the full interval when all levels are present; otherwise `M=q^n` and `A=b+q^{n−ℓ}{0,…,q^ℓ−1}` for `1≤ℓ<n`. Proof: (T2) supplies zeros at the first `L_A−1` `M`th roots, then Proposition 3.1 gives the bound; equality forces its geometric-sum form and the divisor-set definition forces the stated prime-power cases.
- **Proposition 5.1 / Corollary 5.2 (pp. 8–9).** With `α*=π−π/r`, a probability measure whose first `r−1` Fourier moments vanish and whose support lies in `[-α,α]`, `α*≤α≤π`, obeys
  `∫d_T(θ,V_r)^2dμ ≤ π²[−cos(π/r)−cos α]/[2r(1−cos(π/r))] ≤ π(α−α*)`,
  where `V_r={−α*+2πj/r:0≤j<r} mod 2π`. For the coefficient measure of Theorem 1.1, if `D(F)=((p−1)/p)N+Δ≤N`, the real-grid second moment is at most `NΔ/4`; Markov gives mass at distance at least `h` at most `NΔ/(4h²)`. The complete constants were checked on rendered pp. 8–9.

## A175 interface and limitation

For a proposed finite probability factor `P` with nonnegative real coefficients, the theorem can bound its support span **if** one has independently proved `Φ_N|P` for a specified `N`. A175 allows arbitrary finite integer supports with gaps and real nonnegative factor coefficients; it does not provide this cyclotomic-divisibility premise. The theorem does not show either probability factor has rational/integer coefficients, nor that any factor has a cyclotomic divisor. The tiling corollaries additionally require (T1),(T2), which are not assumptions of A175.
