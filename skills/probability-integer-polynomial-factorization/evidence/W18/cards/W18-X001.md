# W18-X001 — Nguyen Xuan, Pham, and Pham Lan, *A proof of Hare’s ε unfair conjecture*

- **Read:** full 9-page arXiv v1, 2609.09215 (2026-09-06). D02 checked the first-page identity, page count, and file hash.
- **Exact distinction (physical p. 1):** Conjecture 1 is the original nonnegative-factor problem. Conjecture 3 is Hare’s relaxation: factor coefficients may lie in `[-ε,1+ε]`, so negative coefficients are allowed.
- **Theorem 4 (physical pp. 2–7):** with `m=6n+3`, the paper constructs a 0–1 polynomial `C_n` and a divisor `A_n=x³+t_n x²+t_n x+1`, where `t_n ~ 3/m²`. For the quotient `B_n=C_n/A_n`, the coefficient of x is `−t_n`. The paper proves `t_n≤α_n≤t_n(1+24/m)` for its relaxation parameter `α_n`.
- **Theorem 5 (statement p. 2; proof pp. 8–9):** for every `ε>0`, an ε-unfair factorization exists; it also proves `log(α_n)/log(deg C_n)→−2`.
- **Proof route:** choose a root `e^{iθ_*}` of a reciprocal quadratic using an intermediate-value argument for `G(θ)=2cos(mθ)+2cos(θ)+1`; divide `C_n` by the quadratic; express quotient coefficients as finite sine sums; bound these sums to control all coefficients and show `α_n→0`.
- **Critical obstruction:** the quotient’s x-coefficient is `−t_n<0`. Therefore the construction violates nonnegativity and does not refute A175’s original nonnegative-factor conjecture.

**Physical-page coverage:** p. 1 introduction, Conjectures 1–3 and original-versus-relaxed model; p. 2 Theorems 4–5 and construction setup; pp. 2–4 §2 root choice, trigonometric equation, and bounds for `t_n`; pp. 4–7 coefficient formula, quotient divisibility, and coefficient estimates proving Theorem 4; pp. 8–9 proof of Theorem 5 and the limit `log(alpha_n)/log(deg C_n) -> -2`.
