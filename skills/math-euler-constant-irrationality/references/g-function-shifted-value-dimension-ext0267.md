# Shifted G-function value-space gate

Source: Fischler--Rivoal, `arXiv:2105.07683v1`; local SHA-256
`d39e7f04271bf8c0e781ccae1d2c959f4d4c708965cf85c30233a76b449660f1`.

Theorem 1 (pp. 1--3) gives a quantitative lower dimension bound for transforms
of fixed non-polynomial \(F\), rational \(\beta\), and algebraic \(\alpha\) in
the convergence disk. Its proof builds recurrences/Pade forms and denominator
bounds before a Nesterenko-type criterion (pp. 8--13, 21--29). Use only with a
certified minimal G-operator, size data, and specified effective constants.
