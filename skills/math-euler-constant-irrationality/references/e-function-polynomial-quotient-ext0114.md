# E-function polynomial-quotient gate

Source: `arXiv:2503.20345v1`, physical pp. 2 and 6; local SHA-256
`74c98e4af3e6b7c7637aef0786d67c4516f4611ddcfed98a92ca44c63183b6df`.

Theorem 1.2 proves: if \(g^m\) is an E-function, \(L\in\mathbb Q(x)[d/dx]\),
and \(L(g)/g\) is entire, then \(L(g)/g\in\mathbb Q[x]\). Its proof uses
sectorial E-function asymptotics and an indicator/Phragmen--Lindelof step.
Keep certificates for all premises. Later common-zero/factorisation statements
are Schanuel-conditional or conjectural and are excluded.
