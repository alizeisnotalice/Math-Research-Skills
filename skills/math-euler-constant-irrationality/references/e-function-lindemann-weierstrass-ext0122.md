# E-function value-independence gate

Source: Francois Delaygue, `arXiv:2210.12046v2`, published in *J. reine
angew. Math.* 820 (2025), 75--85; local SHA-256
`1f50bbebeabf7f05d7ba4674a329e2d882af5640cc040b9da2fb0adcca41cebd`.

Theorem 1.2 (physical pp. 3--4) makes \(1,f_1(\alpha),\ldots,f_n(\alpha)\)
Q-linearly independent given E-functions, algebraic \(\alpha\), pairwise
disjoint finite singularity sets of associated G-series, and prior
transcendence of every value. The proof uses E-operators and Beukers (pp.
6--9). It is not an exceptional-value solver: the source itself assumes an
E-function oracle.
