# G-function values outside the initial disk

Source: Fischler--Rivoal, `arXiv:1811.08758v1`; local SHA-256
`b117980a881827a3d7e1abf59f5bcd9780db7a755fdea185e98a3d2eaf974967`.

Theorem 1 (pp. 1--4) gives logarithmic lower and linear upper dimension bounds
for transforms of a fixed non-polynomial G-function at a nonsingular
continuation point. The proof needs a tailored Pade problem (pp. 19--24), rank
input (pp. 32--40), and analytic/denominator estimates (pp. 41--46). Retain a
minimal operator, singularity/path data, and effective parameters; it gives no
Euler-constant representation by itself.
