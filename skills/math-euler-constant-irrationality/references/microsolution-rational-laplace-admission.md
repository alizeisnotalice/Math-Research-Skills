# Microsolutions and rational inverse Laplace transfer

This is an E/G-operator structural interface, not a direct irrationality criterion.  If a proposed construction supplies a Fuchsian G-operator with regular singularity at infinity, its finite singularity microsolutions can be assembled on a cut plane.  After Fourier--Laplace transfer, the rational inverse-Laplace map preserves algebraic coefficients and exponents in the Nilsson expansions.  This gives a route to construct bases at zero and infinity for an E-operator without silently inserting Gamma values into the rational part of the calculation.

Before applying it, provide the differential operator, proof that it is Fuchsian/G-operator, complete finite singularity list, a cut direction avoiding pairwise singularity alignments, the Fourier--Laplace convention, and a non-anti-Stokes direction for any asymptotic comparison.  Connection and Stokes constants are separate arithmetic data.  The result does not identify Euler's constant with such a constant, does not prove it is an E-value, and does not furnish integer linear forms.
