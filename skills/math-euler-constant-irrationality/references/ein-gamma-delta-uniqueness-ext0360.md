# `Ein(1)` as the unique analytic cancellation of gamma and delta

Verified source: arXiv:2510.00315v1, SHA-256 `809143ef6fea26a2c917ea6a7a5a1679f97c62737d9fa5f263f3f20e7db4116c`, 6 physical pages.

Physical p.1 records Hardy's identity

`gamma + delta/e = sum_{k>=1} (-1)^(k+1)/(k k!) = Ein(1)`,

with `delta=-e Ei(-1)`. Physical p.5 proves the source's narrow uniqueness statement: in its canonical Borel-summable divergent series `S_gamma+alpha S_delta`, isolating the factorially divergent term leaves coefficient `alpha-1/e`; the remaining rearranged double series is absolutely convergent. Thus this particular analytic cancellation occurs exactly at `alpha=1/e`.

This is a mixed-constant identity. It neither proves nor approximates an integer linear form in gamma alone. The source's arithmetic remarks rely on external work and are not adopted here.
