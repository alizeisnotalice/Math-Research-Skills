# E-算子的快速衰减周期配对

Snodgrass, arXiv:2608.06005v2, physical pp. 1--4 and Theorem 1.1, gives a rational structure for rapid-decay cohomology of a supplied smooth variety \(X\), morphism \(f:X\to\mathbb G_m\), and connection induced by an E-operator satisfying Assumption 2.12. The relevant output is a period pairing after choosing the specified basis and rational structure.

Supply the connection, E-operator, smooth variety, morphism, Assumption 2.12 verification, rapid-decay class and comparison pairing. This framework neither identifies a proposed constant with that pairing nor yields a denominator or irrationality certificate without further arithmetic data.
