# Mellin zeta-series gate

Source: Alexey Kuznetsov, `arXiv:2312.03261v2`; local SHA-256
`654e0e2bc4d574b2cc2a25742d2e6df0e48683220b80dcc76ce97367bc04e5fa`.

Theorem 1 (physical pp. 3--5) gives an absolutely and uniformly convergent
two-sided zeta expansion from an odd function with stated strip analyticity,
pole and exponential-decay hypotheses. The proof is Mellin inversion, contour
estimates and residues; pp. 6--8 give specialisations. It certifies
convergence, not a directed tail bound for an arbitrary kernel, and not a
gamma irrationality statement.
