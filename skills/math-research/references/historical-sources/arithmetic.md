## Exponents of Diophantine approximation in dimension two for a general class of numbers

- 原件 156c2fe1d5e4d16f7083b713a56cd8f893cb8f0c0331b76bf0b64ad47c0e4f65。Anthony Poëls, arXiv:2107.05618v1, 12 July 2021, 28 pages.。物理页 1, 2, 4, 25, 26。
- Parametric geometry of numbers and degree-two algebraic approximation for proper ψ-Sturmian numbers.。PDF pp. 1–4 state the class and its exponent formulae; pp. 25–26 derive ω2, ω̂_2, ω*_2 and ω̂*_2 from the Sturmian parameters.。
- 邻近材料。For the narrowly defined proper ψ-Sturmian construction, the paper computes simultaneous and degree-two algebraic approximation exponents from the determinant-growth parameter δ and slope parameter σ. It supplies no linear form in Euler's constant or an irrationality certificate for an externally supplied constant.。
- 适用范围。Applying the formulae requires the admissible integer matrix recurrence, projective convergence to (1,ξ,ξ^2), bounded contents and the stated multiplicative/determinant growth. It cannot estimate an arbitrary continued fraction or transfer to γ from numerical data.。

## Irrationality Exponent, Hausdorff Dimension and Effectivization

- 原件 a65fe8708116a939a29bf570f1846b234836070831a565f5bfbe4098a39050c4。Verónica Becher, Jan Reimann and Theodore A. Slaman, arXiv:1601.00153v2, 6 September 2017, manuscript dated 23 March 2018, 20 pages.。物理页 1, 3, 4, 5, 13, 14, 18。
- Cantor constructions prescribing irrationality exponents and Hausdorff/effective dimensions.。PDF p. 3 states Theorem 1; p. 4 states Theorem 2; pp. 5–12 define the interval constructions; pp. 13–18 prove the classical and effective-dimension assertions.。
- 邻近材料。The source constructs Cantor-like sets for a prescribed exponent a>=2 and dimension b in [0,2/a], with almost-everywhere exponent a under its uniform measure. The effective theorem additionally controls effective Hausdorff dimension.。
- 适用范围。These are existence and measure results relying on chosen fast-growing interval/prime parameters. They do not give a finite rational approximation certificate for a concrete number and do not bound the irrationality exponent of γ.。

## Modular Forms, Deformation of Punctured Spheres, and Extensions of Symmetric Tensor Representations

- 原件 2490753fc3a62bddc3ec2bb1a84984fc8bedc61368f9231ceacdeecbf253d866。arXiv:2004.04716v2, 23 Aug 2021, 15 physical pages。物理页 1, 2, 5, 9, 11。
- modular forms, Fuchsian uniformization, and Eichler-integral deformation。pp. 1–2 introduce the accessory-parameter deformation; p. 5 states Theorem 1; p. 9 supplies its Eichler-integral proof step; p. 11 states the Teichmüller variation formula.。
- 邻近材料。For an n-punctured sphere H/Γ with n≥4, the paper identifies each deformation with the first Rankin–Cohen bracket [g, h-tilde]_1, for a weight-four cusp form h and its Eichler integral. It also gives a vector-valued modular-form realization.。
- 适用范围。This requires the uniformizing Fuchsian group, a Hauptmodul, the chosen accessory-parameter family, and the stated cusp-form basis. It is unrelated to the finite tensor-Fourier positivity interface currently owned by math-f02, so no general deformation or tensor claim is admitted.。

## The Irrationality Exponents of Computable Numbers

- 原件 aad4bb9c7befbf14c9bc3a3a45840cbf9d62542e379d7fb5c2bf676d25dd858e。Verónica Becher, Yann Bugeaud and Theodore A. Slaman, arXiv:1410.1017v1, 4 October 2014, manuscript dated 21 August 2014, 12 pages.。物理页 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12。
- Computability classification of attainable irrationality exponents and Cantor constructions.。PDF p. 1 states Theorem 1; pp. 2–3 give the computability/0' characterization and Corollaries 2–3; p. 5 gives the lacunary-series truncation mechanism; pp. 8–12 construct the computable point.。
- 邻近材料。Theorem 1 characterizes exponents of computable reals as limsups of computable rational sequences. The paper gives a computability-theoretic existence construction, including a base-b lacunary mechanism for suitable prescribed exponents.。
- 适用范围。The statement depends on computable-sequence and 0' notions. It cannot turn numerical approximations to a specified constant into a computable irrationality exponent, and it gives no γ-specific arithmetic information.。

## Transcendence and measures via the refined Diophantine exponent

- 原件 2cfb651d65a9960bc0385a2658005752dd899bb4a8919b08d91c8319a18a87b2。Quang-Khai Nguyen, arXiv:2605.30606v2, dated 4 August 2026, 44 pages.。物理页 1, 4, 5, 6, 11, 19, 22。
- Refined word repetition, Subspace-Theorem transcendence criteria and algebraic approximation measures.。PDF pp. 4–6 state Theorems A and B and Corollary B.1; p. 11 proves a lacunary sequence has infinite refined exponent; pp. 19–22 display the Subspace-Theorem parameter choice and mismatch-variable reduction.。
- 构件候选。Theorems A–B provide a reusable source-gated route from an S-integer coefficient word with verified refined repetition and sublinear coefficient height to a rational-or-transcendental dichotomy, with a conditional quantitative algebraic-approximation lower bound. This differs from the existing Hankel/Mahler route because it permits controlled Hamming mismatches.。
- 适用范围。The input must certify Rdio(a)>log H(β)/log|β|_v, h(a_i)=o(i), a number-field/place/S-integer setting and the exact mismatch construction. The theorem may leave the value in K, and Theorem B excludes α in K. It does not apply to γ or prove irrationality from finite word data.。

## On spectrum of irrationality exponents of Mahler numbers

- 原件 87992e4a5f78c9c1d7b38e55477eb1d1fef3dfd2698fff9916e18395459d0c66。本机 arXiv:1806.02946v1。正式版 JTNB 31 (2019), 431–453，DOI 10.5802/jtnb.1090。物理页 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 9, 10, 16, 18。
- Laurent-series continued fractions, primitive degree gaps and exact irrationality exponents of Mahler values.。PDF pp. 1–2 state Theorem 1; p. 3 gives the two-sequence approximation estimate; pp. 8–10 define primitive gaps and prove their bounded/eventually periodic reduction; pp. 16–18 work three explicit d=3 families.。
- 构件候选。This supplies a potentially reusable exact certificate for a restricted homogeneous Mahler equation f(z)=A(z)f(z^d)/B(z): derive continued-fraction denominator degree gaps, reduce to finitely many primitive gaps, and use eventual cancellation periodicity to obtain a rational irrationality exponent. The existing Hankel skill does not implement this Laurent continued-fraction/primitive-gap branch.。
- 适用范围。The construction needs a Laurent-series continued fraction, the nonvanishing A(b^{d^m})B(b^{d^m}) condition for every m, irrationality of f(b), and proof of periodic cancellation data. Finite Hankel values or finite gap search cannot certify the theorem. It does not apply to γ.。

- Hankel determinants, Padé approximants and Mahler-type irrationality exponents.。PDF p. 2 states Theorem 1 for f(x)=A/B+C f(x^k); p. 3 gives the regular-paperfolding corollary; pp. 20–21 give the exact Padé leading-term identity and iterate the functional equation.。
- 已有技能。The paper is a predecessor-level instance of the existing Hankel–Padé route. Its nonzero adjacent Hankel subsequence with ratio tending to one yields exponent 2 for the stated functional equation, and the paperfolding application supplies μ(F(1/b))=2.。
- 适用范围。The result requires an infinite nonzero determinant subsequence, not merely a finite determinant calculation. Its particular functional equation and b>=2 condition are essential. It concerns a paperfolding value, not Euler's constant.。
