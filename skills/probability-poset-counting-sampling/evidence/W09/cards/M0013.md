## Result and locations

The search tree is built from individualized vertex sequences `ν`; the coloring `R(G,π₀,ν)` is the refinement state and `T(G,π₀,ν)` selects the next non-singleton cell. The refinement function must satisfy exactly (R1)–(R3) (PDF p. 4):

- (R1) `R(G,π,ν) ≼ π` (it refines the input coloring);
- (R2) every individualized vertex `v∈ν` is a singleton cell of `R(G,π,ν)`;
- (R3) relabeling by any `g∈S_n` commutes with refinement: `R(G^g,π^g,ν^g)=R(G,π,ν)^g`.

The target-cell selector similarly returns the empty set exactly at a discrete coloring, otherwise a non-singleton cell, and is equivariant under relabeling (T1)–(T3), PDF p. 5.

Lemma 1 (PDF p. 5) proves relabeling equivariance of the search tree. Corollary 2 gives the induced action of `Aut(G,π₀)` on its nodes. Lemma 3 (PDF p. 5) states that if `π=R(G,π₀,ν)`, then `Aut(G,π)` is exactly the pointwise stabilizer of `ν` in `Aut(G,π₀)`. For an automorphism pruning step below `ν`, the relevant group is therefore the stabilizer of the individualized sequence, not the full initial automorphism group.

A node invariant `φ` must satisfy (φ1)–(φ3) on PDF p. 6: ordered values at same-depth nodes bound all descendant-leaf values; on discrete leaves, equal values characterize equality of the labeled graph `G^π` (literal equality, not merely isomorphism); and `φ` is label-invariant. Lemma 4 builds a canonical form by maximizing `φ` over discrete leaves. Section 2.4 defines pruning operations PA, PB, and PC; Theorem 5 (PDF pp. 6–7) gives their exact validity conditions:

- (a) Any sequence of PA and PC prunings leaves at least one leaf with maximal `φ` value, preserving the canonical-form search.
- (b) Fix a leaf `ν₀`. A sequence of PB and PC prunings, where each PC step at `ν''` is allowed only if `φ(G,π₀,ν'') ≠ φ(G,π₀,[ν₀]_{|ν''|})`, leaves enough information to generate the automorphism group: `Aut(G,π₀)` is generated by the PC automorphisms used and the automorphisms taking `ν₀` to a remaining leaf.

PA/PB require distinct same-depth nodes; PC requires `g∈Aut(G,π₀)`, `ν<ν'`, and `νg=ν'` (PDF p. 6). Theorem 5's proof (p. 7) follows the lexicographically least maximum-invariant leaf for (a), and traces removed images of `ν₀` backward through the used PC generators to a remaining leaf for (b).

## Use and transfer limit

This supports symmetry-aware search and explains the stabilizer sequence behind graph-isomorphism pruning. It does not justify deleting states from an A022 counting recurrence unless the quotient retains orbit multiplicities/stabilizers and is proved count-preserving.
