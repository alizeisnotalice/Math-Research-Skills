# M0019: Poset Entropy Versus Number of Linear Extensions: The Width-2 Case

Reader: W09. Reading status: fulltext_read.

Authors: Samuel Fiorini, Selim Rexhep; 2015.

Evidence: complete 18-page article read; theorem statement and proof structure rechecked against PDF pp. 1–2 and 5–16.

## Main theorem and notation

Throughout the article, $G(P)$ is the incomparability graph of the finite poset $P$, and $H(P):=H(G(P))$ is its graph entropy under the uniform distribution on vertices. The paper uses base-2 logarithms. Theorem 3 (PDF p. 1) states: if $P$ has width 2 and $\kappa_2(P)$ is the number of two-vertex connected components of $G(P)$, then

\[
|P|H(P)\le (2-\varepsilon)\log_2 e(P)+\varepsilon\kappa_2(P),
\qquad \varepsilon=2-\frac{3\log_2 3-2}{\log_2 3}\approx0.26.
\]

Here $e(P)$ is the number of linear extensions. This is a width-2 entropy-to-count inequality; it is not a concentration theorem for linear extensions. Corollary 4 (PDF p. 2) identifies equality in the older factor-2 bound: for width-2 $P$, $|P|H(P)=2\log_2 e(P)$ iff the maximum degree of $G(P)$ is 1.

## Proof structure

The proof is by induction on $|P|$. The stable-set-polytope formulation of graph entropy makes $H(P)$ additive over disconnected incomparability graphs; a disconnected width-2 poset decomposes as an ordinal sum, and both $\log_2 e(P)$ and $\kappa_2(P)$ add. In the connected case with at least three elements, $\kappa_2(P)=0$.

Because width 2 makes the incomparability graph bipartite, the authors use the Körner–Marton characterization/algorithm to obtain the unique optimal vector and a canonical interval representation. The associated interval order $I(P)$ extends $P$ and has the same entropy, but its incomparability graph can have different connected components, so one cannot simply substitute $I(P)$ into the claimed bound. They add selected “phantom” incomparabilities to form an interval order $Q$ with connected incomparability graph, width 2, $H(Q)=H(P)$, and $e(Q)\le e(P)$ (Lemma 16, PDF pp. 9–10). The remaining induction removes a suitable incomparability edge while controlling the entropy and extension-count changes; the detailed interval/epoch cases are in §§7–9 (PDF pp. 11–16).

## Transfer limit

The inequality applies only when the input is a width-2 poset and uses the incomparability graph's graph entropy and two-vertex components. It does not directly bound the number of labelings of a square with a disjunctive exact-two-peak condition, nor establish entropy concentration or an exact sampler for that conditioned family.

## Revision response (R05)

Replaced the ambiguous complemented-graph notation by the paper's definition $H(P)=H(G(P))$ with $G(P)$ the incomparability graph. Restated the width-2 hypothesis, base-2 logarithm, exact $\varepsilon$, and the two-vertex-component correction. Expanded the proof sketch to include the canonical interval order and the phantom-edge step, including why the entropy-preserving extension cannot simply be used without correcting its component structure. Kept the conclusion separate from any peak-conditioned square count or concentration claim.
