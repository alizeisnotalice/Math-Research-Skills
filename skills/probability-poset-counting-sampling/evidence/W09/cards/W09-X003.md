# W09-X003: Counting Linear Extensions of Restricted Posets

Reader: W09. Reading status: fulltext_read.

Authors: Samuel Dittmer, Igor Pak; 2018.

Evidence: complete 34-page preprint read; the restricted-class statements and three reduction routes were checked in §§1, 6–8 (PDF pp. 1–3, 19–27).

## Complexity results

Theorem 1.1 proves that counting linear extensions is #P-complete even for height-2 posets (PDF pp. 1, 19–21). Here height 2 means no chain has three elements. Theorem 1.2 proves #P-completeness for incidence posets of input graphs, whose elements are graph vertices and edges with each edge-element below its two endpoint vertices (PDF pp. 1, 21–23). Theorem 1.3 proves #P-completeness for dimension-2 posets, equivalently point sets ordered coordinatewise in $\mathbb R^2$ (PDF pp. 2, 5–18). These are exact counting-complexity classifications.

## Distinct proof routes

For Theorem 1.1, the authors start from general #LE and build from an $n$-element poset $P$ a height-2 poset $Q_p$ for each prime $p$ in a range. Their lemmas recover $e(P)$ from a specified subcount of an auxiliary poset and show $e(Q_p)\equiv(-1)^n e(P)\pmod p$. Counts for enough primes, combined by the Chinese Remainder Theorem, recover $e(P)$; this is a modular/Turing reduction, not a parsimonious reduction (PDF §§6, pp. 19–21).

For Theorem 1.2, a separate graph construction gives an incidence poset $I_{G_p(P)}$ with $e(I_{G_p(P)})\equiv(-1)^{|P|+1}8e(P)\pmod p$. The corresponding counts over primes and CRT establish hardness (PDF §7, pp. 21–23). Theorem 1.2 also implies Theorem 1.1, but the paper presents independent proofs.

For Theorem 1.3, Lemma 3.1 identifies dimension-2 poset counts with principal-ideal sizes in the weak Bruhat order. Theorem 1.4 proves that #Bruhat is #P-complete: a parsimonious reduction first maps #3SAT to rigid-circuit counts, then a different modular construction maps those counts to #Bruhat. The latter construction uses algebraic gate parameters found by computer and checked via Macaulay2; the paper identifies this computer-assisted part explicitly. Do not describe the whole chain as parsimonious (PDF §§3–5, pp. 5–18, and §8.2, p. 26).

## Transfer limit

These results concern the exact count of all linear extensions of a poset in the specified restricted class. #P-completeness does not rule out approximation or exact sampling, and it does not classify the count of labelings satisfying an additional exact-two-peak condition. No reduction from A022 to any of these counting problems is supplied.

## Revision response (R05)

Separated the height-2, incidence-poset, and dimension-2 hardness theorems and their proof routes. Corrected the earlier conflation of parsimonious and modular reductions: Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 use prime-modulus congruences plus CRT, while the dimension-2 route goes through #Bruhat and a computer-assisted rigid-circuit construction (only the #3SAT-to-rigid-circuit step is stated parsimonious). Added the precise restricted inputs, incidence relation, and transfer limits.
