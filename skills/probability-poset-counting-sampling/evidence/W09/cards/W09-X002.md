# W09-X002: Counting Linear Extensions of Sparse Posets

Reader: W09. Reading status: fulltext_read.

Authors: Kustaa Kangas, Teemu Hankala, Teppo Niinimäki, Mikko Koivisto; 2016.

Evidence: complete 7-page paper read; the upset and variable-elimination arguments were rechecked against PDF pp. 1–4.

## Exact results

The paper gives two exact approaches (PDF pp. 1–4). The ordinary upset dynamic program uses

\[
\ell(U)=\sum_{x\in\min(U)}\ell(U\setminus\{x\})
\]

and runs in $O(|\mathcal U|w)$ time, where $\mathcal U$ is the set of upsets and $w$ is the poset width. Since $|\mathcal U|=O(n^w)$, this is polynomial for bounded width; in general its worst-case bound is $O(2^n n)$. Their sparse-poset enhancement applies the same recurrence while recursively splitting disconnected subposets; for independent components $A,B$ the combination is $\ell(A)\ell(B)\binom{|A|+|B|}{|A|}$ because their extensions can be interleaved. They compare minimal-element and maximal-element recurrences with heuristics, and also discuss static-set splitting; those heuristics are not worst-case guarantees.

Theorem 1 (PDF p. 3) states that $\ell(P)$ can be computed in $O(n^{t+4})$ time when the cover graph has treewidth $t$. The proof encodes a map $\sigma:[n]\to[n]$ by the edge-constraint product $\Phi(\sigma)=\prod_{i\prec_P j}\mathbf 1\{\sigma(i)<\sigma(j)\}$. Inclusion–exclusion removes the global bijectivity constraint, yielding a sum over maps $[n]\to[k]$ for $k=1,\ldots,n$; symmetry groups terms by $k$. Each inner sum factors over cover-relation edges and is evaluated by variable elimination on the cover graph. The width-$t$ elimination bound and summation over $k$ give the stated polynomial exponent. This is an exact counting algorithm, not an approximation scheme.

## Transfer limit

The algorithms apply to one specified poset and use its upset count, cover-graph treewidth, or recursive component structure. A peak-set condition on a square is a disjunction over labelings and is not encoded by these results without a separate reduction; the stated treewidth bound cannot be applied to the target event by inspection alone.

## Revision response (R05)

Made the two algorithms and their guarantees distinct. Added the exact upset recurrence and component interleaving factor; clarified that the minimal/maximal choice is heuristic. Corrected the treewidth proof description to the paper's inclusion–exclusion plus variable-elimination construction on the cover graph, rather than an unspecified tree-decomposition dynamic program, and retained the theorem's $O(n^{t+4})$ bound. The peak-conditioned square remains outside the stated input model absent a proved encoding.
