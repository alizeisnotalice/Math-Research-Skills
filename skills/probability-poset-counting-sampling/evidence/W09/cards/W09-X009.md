# W09-X009: Using TPA to count linear extensions

Reader: W09. Reading status: fulltext_read.

Authors: Jacqueline Banks, Scott Garrabrant, Mark L. Huber, Anne Perizzolo; 2018.

Evidence: complete 17-page preprint read; weighted-chain, bounding-chain, and TPA results checked against PDF pp. 5–12.

## Exact weighted sampler

The target count is $\#(L)$ for the set $L$ of all linear extensions of a fixed $n$-element poset. The paper embeds the count in a continuous family indexed by $\beta\in[0,n-1]$. For each extension $\sigma$, an item that moves too far right of its home position receives weight 0, one at the boundary receives the fractional factor $1+\beta-\lceil\beta\rceil$, and all closer items receive weight 1; the extension weight $w(\sigma,\beta)$ is the product of these item weights (PDF pp. 5–6). Samples at a given $\beta$ are drawn exactly from the distribution proportional to $w(\cdot,\beta)$.

The adjacent-transposition Metropolis chain has this weighted stationary law. A bounding chain and non-Markovian CFTP produce exact weighted samples; Theorem 1 proves invariance of the bounding relation under updates (PDF pp. 8–9). Theorem 2 bounds the expected cost of one sample by $4.3n^3\ln n(\lceil\log_2 n\rceil+3)$ random bits and $8.6n^3\ln n$ comparisons (PDF p. 10). Exactness here concerns samples from each specified weighted distribution; it is not an exact count.

## TPA count estimate

The Tootsie Pop Algorithm contracts a nested family of continuous weighted sets and uses exact samples at the current parameter to estimate the logarithm of a volume ratio. Theorem 3 states that for $0<\varepsilon\le1$, the two-phase estimator is within a factor $1+\varepsilon$ of $\#(L)$ with probability at least $1-\delta$. Theorem 4 gives its expected random-bit cost; asymptotically it is

\[
O\!\left(n^3(\ln n)(\ln\#(L))^2\varepsilon^{-2}\ln(1/\delta)\right)
\]

with a corresponding comparison bound (PDF pp. 10–12). The count output is a randomized approximation, not exact enumeration. The two-phase confidence/range conditions are part of the guarantee.

## Transfer limit

The exact sampler targets the weighted distribution on *all* linear extensions of a fixed poset. TPA estimates that total count through a continuous embedding. Neither theorem conditions on an exact peak set, and no rejection or conditioning cost for two peaks on a free-boundary square is proved.

## Revision response (R05)

Corrected the theorem roles: Theorem 1 is bounding-chain invariance; Theorem 2 is the exact weighted-sample cost; Theorem 3 gives the two-phase TPA approximation guarantee; Theorem 4 bounds expected random bits. Removed the unsupported claim that samples at successive $\beta$ values are coupled. Stated the weight model, exactness scope, confidence and approximation distinction, and the limitation for peak-conditioned counts.
