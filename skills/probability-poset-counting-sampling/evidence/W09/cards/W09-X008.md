# W09-X008: Markov Chains for Linear Extensions, the Two-Dimensional Case

Reader: W09. Reading status: fulltext_read.

Authors: Stefan Felsner, Lorenz Wernisch; 1997.

Evidence: complete 13-page paper read; the width-2 theorem and two-dimensional-order coupling argument were checked against PDF pp. 3–5 and 8–12.

## Markov chain and exactness

The chain is the Karzanov–Khachiyan adjacent-transposition chain on linear extensions: choose an adjacent position uniformly, and if its two elements are incomparable, swap them with probability $1/2$; otherwise hold. Its stationary distribution on the finite set of linear extensions is uniform. Coupling from the past (CFTP) returns an exact stationary sample if the shared random maps coalesce.

For a width-2 poset, a linear extension is encoded as a lattice path constrained by the comparable pairs. The pointwise path order has a minimal and maximal state, and the paper proves that the upward/downward flip random maps preserve this order. Theorem 3 (PDF p. 5) bounds the expected coupling time by $O(n^3\log n)$ steps; the authors note a matching-order lower bound $\Omega(n^3)$ (PDF p. 5). Here width is the minimum number of chains in a partition.

For a poset of dimension 2, the authors represent extensions as a principal ideal in weak Bruhat order and introduce a path order on permutation levels. They prove the relevant monotonicity of the adjacent-transposition updates, so monotone CFTP gives an exact uniform extension sampler for this broader class as well (PDF pp. 5–11). They do not prove an $O(n^3\log n)$ bound for every dimension-2 poset. Instead, combining the general Wilson–Propp coupling/mixing relation with the Karzanov–Khachiyan mixing estimate yields an expected coupling-time bound $O(n^6\log^2 n)$ (PDF p. 12); the sharper width-2 bound is separate. Their conjecture that the sharper rate holds generally is supported by experiments, not proved.

## Transfer limit

Width 2 and dimension 2 are distinct classes, and the paper's rates must keep their hypotheses. The algorithm samples an unconstrained uniform linear extension of a fixed poset. It does not sample the extensions conditioned on exactly two graph peaks on the free-boundary square, and the coupling proof gives no such conditional sampler.

## Revision response (R05)

Rechecked the primary PDF, including the abstract and the two coupling sections. Corrected the general dimension-2 result from an imprecise “polynomial mixing” description to the actual expected CFTP coupling-time bound $O(n^6\log^2 n)$ derived from the general mixing/coupling relation. Kept the $O(n^3\log n)$ expected coupling-time theorem and $\Omega(n^3)$ lower bound confined to width 2, and stated the chain's adjacent-swap rule, exactness condition, and width/dimension distinction.
