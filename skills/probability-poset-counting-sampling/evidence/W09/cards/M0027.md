## Proof chain and assumptions
Path coupling extends a one-step contraction on adjacent states to a path metric and then to total-variation mixing under irreducibility/stationarity conditions. CFTP uses shared randomness from the past and certified coalescence to obtain an exact stationary sample.

## Use and transfer limit
