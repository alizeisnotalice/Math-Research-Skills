## Direct graph-peak/counting references

- M0002, “Counting peaks on graphs”: exact recursion for prescribed graph-peak sets.

- M0004, “Twin peaks”: one-peak free-square cluster result and two-peak regular-tree results; boundary/model distinction is essential.
