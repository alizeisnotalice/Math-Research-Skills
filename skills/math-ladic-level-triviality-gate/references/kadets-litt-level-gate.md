# Kadets--Litt level gate

Borys Kadets and Daniel Litt, *Level structure, arithmetic representations, and noncommutative Siegel linearization*, arXiv:2107.02213v2, Theorem 1.1.2, states the implication used here. The local source has SHA-256 `a7efd1cdb705da3291a22281df2c4a9ef5a8738779a50e8883262e3a70313b24` and fourteen physical pages. It was published in *Journal fur die reine und angewandte Mathematik* 788 (2022), 219--238.

Physical pp. 1--2 define semisimple arithmetic through extension over a finite separable field extension and assert a constant depending only on \(X\) and \(\ell\). The source says it is in-principle explicit from the Galois action on \(H^1(X_{\bar k},\mathbb Z_\ell)\), but supplies no general finite encoding or numerical value.

Physical pp. 2--3 identify the proof dependencies: reduction to finite fields, Frobenius on a noncommutative Banach algebra, Siegel linearization, an l-adic logarithm bound, and semisimplicity. The skill therefore requires a caller-certified depth; it does not claim to compute one.
