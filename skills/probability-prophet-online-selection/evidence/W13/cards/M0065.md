# M0065 — Ekbatani, Niazadeh, Nuti & Vondrák, “Prophet Inequalities with Cancellation Costs”

**Reader:** W13. **Read status:** partial. Read arXiv v3 PDF pp. 1–5, 19–21, and 26–27; current version revised 2026-10-12; 71 PDF pages total. Review pending.

## Model and optimal characterization

Independent nonnegative random variables with known distributions arrive sequentially. At a later arrival the policy may revoke its current accepted value x and take the new value, paying cancellation cost `f x`. Its payoff is the final accepted reward minus all buyback costs; the prophet sees the full sequence and obtains its maximum. Arrival order can be arbitrary.

Main Result I (PDF p. 4) states for every `f≥0`

`α(f)=1/(2−y_f(1))`,

where `y_f` is the paper's proper solution of a nonlinear differential equation with prescribed boundary conditions (Definition 1). The result is tight: for each f a finite instance with two-point random variables witnesses the upper bound. Theorem 1 establishes existence and key properties of the proper solution (PDF p. 19); Theorem 2 constructs the online lower bound from a feasible continuous dual / quantile-acceptance construction (PDF pp. 19–21); Theorem 3 gives the matching upper bound by constructing finite scaled-Bernoulli hard instances satisfying an indifference recurrence (PDF pp. 26–29). The main result also supplies an order-agnostic polynomial-time randomized adaptive policy attaining the same ratio (Main Result II, PDF p. 5).

For `f≥1` the paper gives the closed form `α(f)=(1+f)/(1+2f)`. For `1/3≤f<1`, it derives an explicit formula from the three-variable extremal construction and proves it matches the ODE ratio. For `0<f<1/3`, it characterizes the exact ratio implicitly by the ODE, without claiming a closed form for the whole subrange.

## B052 status correction and transfer limits

The previously posed B052 range `0<f<1` is resolved by the current v3 as an exact ODE characterization, with matching algorithm and finite hard instances. Do not call it open based on the earlier v1/v2. “Exact” here means the value is uniquely specified through the proper ODE solution; it need not be an elementary closed form. Preserve the independent-known-distributions assumption, cancellation-cost convention, and offline-maximum denominator. This is a recourse model and does not transfer to irrevocable selection without a separate reduction.
