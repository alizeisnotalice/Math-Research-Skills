# Varying-prime Smith--Toda interface

Source: OpenAI, *Finite Smith--Toda Complexes at Varying Primes*, 23 September 2026, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Finite-Smith-Toda-Complexes-at-Varying-Primes-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `13dce3978089a78f874724e97692637cd2ae2c665ca3c8fb626cac64c4978b9b`.

**Scoped reference.** For each `n >= 0`, the source asserts existence at some prime `p=p(n)` of a finite `p`-local spectrum `V(n)` with
\[
 BP_*V(n)\cong BP_* /(p,v_1,\ldots,v_n)
\]
as the canonical comodule, with degree-zero generator and first powers killed. The operational sequence is to first fix `n`, construct the finite diagram and common ultrafilter-large set, lift a unit, and only then select an actual prime. It is not a statement at every prime or an instruction to interchange prime selection with the finite realization. Locus: physical pp. 1, 47, and 51--53.
