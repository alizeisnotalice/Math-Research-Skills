# Boundary-only disk-embedding obstruction

Source: OpenAI, *A boundary-only obstruction to four-dimensional disk embedding*, 24 September 2026, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/A-boundary-only-obstruction-to-four-dimensional-disk-embedding-September-24-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `cb5bb77f6a673a3d55b707a1f4e4406c6134c2c8cfc3980548c2466919d0ba6d`.

**Scoped reference.** The source's input consists of proper immersed discs and framed dual spheres in a compact oriented smooth four-manifold, satisfying
\[
 \lambda(f_i,g_j)=\delta_{ij}1,\qquad \lambda(g_i,g_j)=0,\qquad \widetilde\mu(g_i)=0.
\]
The asserted output is nevertheless that the prescribed boundary circles admit no pairwise disjoint proper locally flat embedded-disc fillings. The operation is a marked geometric construction, followed by the source's square-zero and Koszul-residue obstruction chain, not an automatic consequence of the displayed algebraic equalities. Locus: physical pp. 3, 16, 20, 40, and 47.
