# Filtered products and torus compression

Source: OpenAI, *Filtered products and boundary-preserving compression in complex cobordism*, 25 September 2026, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Filtered-products-and-boundary-preserving-compression-in-complex-cobordism-September-25-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `c47745026b3a083ca4b0af6972dc8cb230415c0061915621435300c85290daf5`.

**Scoped reference.** Given a finite pointed CW pair `G subset E`, `d >= 0`, and a based `v:E -> S^d` for which `Sigma^infty G -> Sigma^infty E -> Sigma^d MU` is null, define `U_l` inside `E^wedge l` by requiring at least `ceil(l/2)` factors in `G`. For sufficiently large `l`, the source asserts
\[
 v^{\wedge \ell}|_{U_\ell}:U_\ell\longrightarrow S^{d\ell}\quad\text{is stably nullhomotopic}.
\]
Its geometric compression interface fixes `t=2p+1`, `0<a<1/16`, a compact metrizable `Y`, `f:Y->C(t,n)`, and one bundle monomorphism `E_f -> T(f,Z_{an}(t,n)) x C^K` with `K<2pn`. It outputs, for every `U`, a `u>=U` and `G:Y^u->C(t,un)` with at least `ceil((a/8)un)` boundary slots while exactly retaining any original boundary slot. Use only after the listed CW, MU-null, compactness, and single-locus bundle hypotheses have been supplied. Locus: physical pp. 1--4.
