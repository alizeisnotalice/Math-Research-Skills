# Coset-complex stabilizer and intersection ledger

Source: *Bounded-degree coboundary expanders in every dimension*, SHA-256
`a1d158719d5e92a2f98b118d03e11cfd699acc3600108c9dcdf838a2871e4530`,
Lemma 3.1 (physical p.4).

For the directed-cycle root groups \(x_j\) and a proper edge set \(J\), the
lemma identifies \(U_J=\langle x_j:j\in J\rangle\) exactly by the allowed
matrix-entry spaces \(V_{ab}(J)\).  It gives
\(U_J\cap U_K=U_{J\cap K}\) and
\(\bigcap_{i\in A}H_i=U_{S\setminus A}\), and says reduction modulo \(t^m\)
is injective on these finite groups for \(m\ge2\).

Use this only after the source's unitriangular cycle, coefficient field,
distinguished edge, and proper-subset conditions have been supplied.  It is a
finite stabilizer/intersection input for a coset complex, not a certificate of
coboundary expansion.
