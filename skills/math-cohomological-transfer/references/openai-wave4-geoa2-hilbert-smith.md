# Hilbert--Smith p-adic exclusion interface

Source: OpenAI, *The Hilbert--Smith conjecture in every finite dimension*, 23 September 2026, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-Hilbert-Smith-conjecture-in-every-finite-dimension-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `e55fa13f40b4c35a98414519ca90d85c17bdd77c9a0b92aa6369eae8046782eb`.

**Scoped reference.** With `n>=1` and prime `p`, the source states that every jointly continuous action of `Z_p` on a connected Hausdorff second-countable topological `n`-manifold without boundary has nonzero kernel, hence factors through a finite quotient. Its finite lattice contradiction uses a cyclic partition of a lattice into `D_i=zeta^iD_0`, classes `z_i`, and the two exact relations
\[
 z_0(h)=\cdots=z_{m-1}(h),\qquad \sum_{i=0}^{m-1}z_i(a)=4u_d,
\]
which yield `z_i(a)=4u_d/m` in the rational line and conflict with a fixed integral lattice when `m>4L_d`. The operation is to establish the listed sheaf-duality, compact test, and character hypotheses before using this arithmetic contradiction. Locus: physical pp. 2 and 39--40.
