# Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss

Source: OpenAI, *Cyclic length and chromatic fixed-point loss*, 24 September 2026, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Cyclic-Length-and-Chromatic-Fixed-Point-Loss-September-24-2026/Cyclic-Length-and-Chromatic-Fixed-Point-Loss-September-24-2026.pdf), SHA-256 `1bf9f0b94473b85cecfc6746269e22509d11c918e866a31b67d3329dd3acd857`.

**Scoped reference.** For a finite `p`-group `G`, `H<=G`, and `n>=0`, let `r_n(G,H)` be the least `r` such that `K(n+r)_*(Phi^H X)=0` implies `K(n)_*(Phi^G X)=0` for every finite genuine `G`-spectrum. Let `ell(G,H)` be the shortest subnormal chain from `H` to `G` with cyclic successive quotients. The source states
\[
 r_n(G,H)=\ell(G,H).
\]
The operation is to verify the embedded subgroup and an actual subnormal cyclic-quotient chain before using the upper-bound transfer. The included exact checker only computes this combinatorial chain length for explicit finite additive abelian p-groups. It does not construct spectra, prove the lower bound, or cover nonabelian inputs. Locus: physical pp. 1, 4--5, 20, and 29--30.
