# Marked PD4 chamber obstruction

Source: OpenAI, *A PD4 group without an aspherical manifold model*, 24 September 2026, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/A-PD4-group-without-an-aspherical-manifold-model-September-24-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `ccbbf7f15686b6f7ef6e36a633ae5549ec2d9d932560ff4be2b5b9367d2414f7`.

**Scoped reference.** The source constructs a finitely presented integral PD4 group with a finite oriented aspherical Poincare complex and records the exact duality condition
\[
 H_i(G;\mathbb ZG)=0\ (i\ne4),\qquad H_4(G;\mathbb ZG)\cong\mathbb Z.
\]
The reusable interface is a *marked* chamber `(D,S,c:D->BH)`: after attaching 3-cells to a specified hyperbolic basis, `(P,S)` is a four-dimensional Poincare pair with `P ~= BH`, but no boundary-marking-preserving topological-manifold filling exists. The later stabilization retains the marking through `N # k(S^2 x S^2) -> L # k(S^2 x S^2)`, rather than asserting arbitrary stable homeomorphism. Locus: physical pp. 1, 4, and 18--20. Do not drop the marking, orientation, or specified sphere-basis data.
