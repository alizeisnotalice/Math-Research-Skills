# Chosen-inverse fracture attachment signs

**Source.** OpenAI, *The height-three chromatic overlap: an explicit filtration and its attachments*, Lemma 2.1 and Proposition 2.2, PDF pp. 7--9, with choice boundary at p. 11; SHA-256 `05577ee22db5cad1c9d62ea835144b0465170507bdd77f358ebf4904e42f973c`.

## Input contract

Work in a stable category with an exact localization `L`.  For a map `a:A -> B` whose `La` is an equivalence, explicitly choose an inverse to `La` and its inverse homotopies.  For the height-three ordered filtration, require the exact `E(2)`-local `S`-module `X`, the block maps and homotopies in Proposition 2.2(i)--(iii), including the `K(2)`, `K(1)`, and rational equivalences.

## Reuse

With `N=fib(A -> LA)`, the induced map from `cofib(a)` to `Sigma N` has boundary
`partial_a=(-Sigma i) bar(tau)`, where `i:N -> A`; retain the minus sign determined by the chosen cofiber-rotation convention.  Proposition 2.2 then makes the ordered cofiber/pullback stages and expresses their connecting maps through the height-two and positive-height fracture comparisons.

The chosen compatibility homotopies and coherent inverses are data, not dispensable bookkeeping: as stated in Remark 2.4, the source does not claim a unique or canonical attachment filtration without them.
