# Walsh decay under cotype and the approximation property

**Source.** OpenAI, *The cotype–cotype conjecture under the approximation property*, Lemma 2.1 and the setup of §1.2, PDF pp. 1, 4--7; SHA-256 `c2f55fe9562e2ee729cee24795789294679cea7160218c585b71bf15896be4b8`.

## Input contract

All spaces are real.  Given a bounded operator `T:G -> F`, define the probability-normalized Walsh transform `W_n^T` on `F_2^n`.  For the cotype transfer, keep the paper’s approximation-property hypothesis on the target space and the finite Rademacher-cotype assumptions on both the space and its dual.

## Reuse

Lemma 2.1 supplies the finite interface: `2^(-n/2)||T|| <= w_n(T) <= ||T||`, monotonicity in `n`, invariance under finite `L2` amplification, and `|w_n(A)-w_n(B)|<=||A-B||`; finite-rank `T` has a `T`-dependent `2^(-n/2)` bound.  The paper’s later mechanism combines fixed-length cotype moment lifts with AP finite-rank approximation to obtain uniform decay and then its K-convexity conclusion.

Do not promote the finite-rank decay constant to a uniform one without the cotype/AP mechanism, and do not omit the ordinary AP assumption from the source’s cotype--cotype theorem.
