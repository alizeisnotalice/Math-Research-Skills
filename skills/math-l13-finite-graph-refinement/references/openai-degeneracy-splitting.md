# Exact degeneracy splitting

Source: OpenAI, *Graph Decompositions at the Integral Expectation Threshold*, SHA-256 \`5c0b3a5fc1764ff357f2b791f559f02d6dccaad8f36726a53d106fb0b97873af\`, physical p. 4, Lemma 2.4.

For a finite \(d\)-degenerate graph and positive integer \(m\), fix a degeneracy order.  At every vertex, partition the edges to preceding neighbours into \(m\) groups of size at most \(\lceil d/m\rceil\), and assign the groups to the pieces.  Each edge is assigned once and the same order witnesses degeneracy at most \(\lceil d/m\rceil\) in each piece.

The input must include a verified degeneracy order; an arbitrary order does not suffice.  This does not supply the source's random-graph transfer or decomposition threshold.
