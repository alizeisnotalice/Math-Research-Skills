# Signed cycle intersections in a normalized drawing

Source: OpenAI, *The crossing number of complete graphs*, SHA-256 \`8b4c78b04d710b7d0e60d18b3fd9e9ea7042b5201ca6644ee72f50c5adee310d\`, physical pp. 3-5, Section 2 and Lemma 2.1.

The source uses a normalized polygonal drawing of a finite simple graph, straight near vertices and crossings and without adjacent-edge crossings.  After orienting edges, it compares signed interior crossings of cycle chains with a local form from the circular orders of spokes.  The proof separates shared tracks in vertex disks, averages left/right perturbations, and uses that total signed intersection of closed polygonal walks is zero.

Keep the orientations, normalization, and rotation system.  This is an identity in that drawing model; it does not calculate a crossing-number minimum.
