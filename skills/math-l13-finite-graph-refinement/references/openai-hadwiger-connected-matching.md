# Connected-matchings to clique-minor bookkeeping

Source: OpenAI, *A counterexample to Hadwiger's conjecture*, SHA-256 \`a97293542586d3518b9355dcea732012b09d4ce809126da73d285ba0d3906128\`, physical p. 15, Proposition 3.5.

For a nonempty finite graph \(G\) of order \(m\), the source states
\[
h(G)\le m+4cm(G)+\frac23.
\]
Consequently, if \(\alpha(G)\le2\), \(cm(G)<m/100\), and \(m\ge5\), it derives
\[
h(G)<\frac{26m}{75}+\frac23<\frac m2\le\chi(G).
\]
Use this only after fixing the finite graph, its independence bound, and the exact connected-matching convention.  It neither estimates \(cm(G)\) nor constructs a minor model.
