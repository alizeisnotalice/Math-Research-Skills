# Distance-set separator ledger

Source: *The higher-dimensional Erdős distinct-distances conjecture*, SHA-256
`37f6355365a18510e75d897dc9ab6d29305d7ff132cf148845b6f16d080ee1bf`,
Lemma 3.1 (physical p.15) and Lemma 3.12(i) (physical p.24).

For a finite \(P\subset\mathbb R^n\), put \(M=1+|\Delta(P)|\).  The source
uses the exact separators
\[
s_p(x)=\prod_{\rho\in\Delta(P)}
 \frac{\sum_j(x_j-p_j)^2-\rho^2}{-\rho^2},
\]
which satisfy \(s_p(p)=1\) and \(s_p(q)=0\) for \(q\ne p\), with degree at
most \(2(M-1)\).  Consequently a pure complex variety \(V\) of dimension
\(u\) and degree \(a\) contains at most \(C_{n,u}aM^u\) sample points.  The
same lemma gives an explicit degree-\(\le2M+1\) system whose common zero set is
exactly \(P\).

For a separate projection contract, if irreducible \(X\subset\mathbb P^n\) has
dimension \(k\), degree \(a\), and projection from \(p\) has generic degree
\(t\) and image \(Y\) of dimension \(k\), then
\(t\deg Y=a-\operatorname{mult}_pX\).  Require the named irreducibility,
dimension, and generic-degree hypotheses; neither statement certifies a
distinct-distance lower bound by itself.
