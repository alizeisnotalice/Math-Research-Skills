# Large distance-class fibre preprocessing

Source: *The weak pinned planar distance theorem*, SHA-256
`6350959db2b8310fdad1792ad04d4f8d64d6c030808cb6d00b29eb66513789f0`,
Proposition 2.2 and algebraic-coordinate reduction (physical pp.6--8).

Given the paper's supposition on a sequence maximizing its large-distance-class
functional, Proposition 2.2 replaces each finite configuration by one with real
algebraic coordinates while preserving every equality and inequality among the
finitely many squared distances.  It uses a first-order real-closed-field
transfer of the finite polynomial equalities and strict disequalities; no height
or degree bound is supplied.

The output is a directed finite graph with density \(d_n\to\theta\), fibre sizes
\(k_e\ge n^{s-o(1)}\), pointwise law comparisons in (2.2), low-in-degree control
(2.3), and the uniform truncated fibre inequality (2.4) at
\(\lambda_n=1/\log n\).  These hypotheses must remain attached when this
finite graph is passed to later variance or partition arguments.
