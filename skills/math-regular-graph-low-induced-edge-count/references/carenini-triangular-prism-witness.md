# Source note: regular-graph subset witness

Source: *A negative answer to a question of Carenini on almost independent sets in regular graphs*, author-hosted manuscript, physical p. 1 of 20.  Local SHA-256: `902d56412030f1fb983d1581e264bcb0c8ed4fe3f6fbcc7236e2a7bcb1f576c2`.

The source states a finite counterexample at `(n,d,gamma)=(6,3,1/18)`: the triangular prism has 28 subsets with at most one induced edge, whereas `K_{3,3}` has 24.  This asset independently enumerates those two supplied graphs.  It does not adopt the source's later structural or asymptotic statements.
