# Scoped reference. Positive part of a free contact line

Source: OpenAI, *Contact Fano manifolds and the LeBrun--Salamon conjecture*, pinned [PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Contact-Fano-manifolds-and-the-LeBrun-Salamon-conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf), SHA-256 `c7ef1ec44ffd8d7aa71952e4a231d60a449a5f9f2ad18fcfa897df888b829e7c`, physical pp. 8--10 and 31.

Let `f:P1->X` be a free contact line with `f^*L=O(1)` on a complex contact manifold of dimension `2n+1`.  Extract the positive direct-sum part `P_f` from the splitting of `f^*T_X`.  The source states that `P_f` is intrinsic, `f^*T_X/P_f` is trivial, `theta(P_f)=0`, and `P_f` is isotropic for the pulled-back Levi form; therefore `rank(P_f)<=n`.  In the contact-line setting it also records the splitting `f^*T_X=O(2)+O(1)^(n-1)+O^(n+1)`.

The concrete operation is to separate strictly positive from zero summands and test the asserted rank/isotropy against supplied exact bundle and Levi-pairing data.  This does not construct contact lines, prove freeness, or identify the contact Fano manifold with an adjoint variety.
