# Compatible vector-field jets and finite-presentation exit

OpenAI, *A singular normal affine surface with free tangent sheaf*, physical pp. 24--28 and 32--34, Proposition 6.2; SHA-256 `48b1a74d3131e97b2cb8ed8f5c1d29458e8f2e92b6446d5d9d0161c4ab3612db`; [PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/A-singular-normal-affine-surface-with-free-tangent-sheaf-September-23-2026/paper.pdf).

The cited passages construct compatible vector-field jet lifts and then invoke finite-presentation algebraization. The finite interface here is a list of jet coordinates, exact linear compatibility equations, truncation levels, and a proposed lift. Exact row reduction can check finite-level compatibility and expose a kernel/cokernel witness.

It cannot verify normality, convergence of a formal lift, algebraize a formal object, construct a tangent sheaf, or establish the surface conclusion. Those remain source-stated geometric premises under the OpenAI-draft default.
