# Rational filtration base change and finite Koszul factor

Source SHA-256: `eaf8e38720714e2319c52a2c62e33e11f4383614e894012624d6580c4d3af850` (43 physical pages; Wave 4 index checked). Physical p.3 states Theorem 1.2 and equation (1.3); p.25 states Lemma 5.1 and equations (5.1)--(5.2).

With `A_E=E tensor_Q A_Q`, a closed invariant support `Y`, and `F_Y A_E` defined as the image of the categorical trace, Theorem 1.2 states

`E tensor_Q (A_Q intersect F_Y A_E) -> F_Y A_E`

is an isomorphism under Assumption 1.1. The statement requires neither Hecke-finiteness nor cuspidality but does require its restricted geometric-Langlands environment.

Lemma 5.1 gives the finite support mechanism: take a finite Frobenius-stable homogeneous generator space `U` for the invariant-origin ideal, check that the transport `F_U` has no eigenvalue one, and use the finite Koszul complex `K(U)`. The trace is multiplied by the nonzero factor `det(1-F_U|U)` (equation (5.2)). In finite exact analogues, verify rational-span membership and nonzero determinant before treating scalar extension as descent.
