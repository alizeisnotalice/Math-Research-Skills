# Totally-acyclic trivial-extension transfer

Source: *A counterexample to Tachikawa's second conjecture*, SHA-256
`332c2535f60d4ad620a86b3c4d547c51510b183f881445d9a0807986b0544f67`,
Proposition 3.2 (physical pp.12--13).

For a finite-dimensional algebra \(R\), a totally acyclic complex \(P\) of
finite projective left modules, and \(Z=\operatorname{coker}(P_1\to P_0)\),
assume \(Z\) is nonprojective and
\(H^a\operatorname{Hom}_R(P,Z)=0\) for \(a>0\) and \(a\le-2\).  Then the
trivial extension \(A=R\ltimes DR\) is symmetric and
\(M=A\otimes_RZ\) is finite, nonprojective, and has
\(\operatorname{Ext}_A^a(M,M)=0\) for \(a>0\).

The negative-degree endpoint is indispensable for the injective-coresolution
calculation.  This is a transfer conditional on a supplied totally acyclic
complex and Hom-vanishing profile; it is not a search procedure for a
Tachikawa counterexample.
