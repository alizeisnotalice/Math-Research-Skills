# Sixth-power Foulkes monotonicity

Source: OpenAI, *Foulkes' conjecture for the sixth symmetric power*, SHA-256 `6439c44d77b7de4bd1d9a3425a35e2f2ef2e21036609f77652167f95c8daa94d`, p. 4 Lemma 2.2 and pp. 20--21.

For six-part partition gap vectors `c,d` satisfying `6|w(c),6|w(d)`, the source states that `Q(c)>0` implies `Q(d+c)>=Q(d)`. Thus `Q(d+6e_i)>=Q(d)`, while `F(d+6e_6)=F(d)`. Apply it only after supplying a seed highest-weight occurrence and exact gap/divisibility conventions. It is not a plethysm calculator and does not replace the finite certificate tables or prove the injection theorem.
