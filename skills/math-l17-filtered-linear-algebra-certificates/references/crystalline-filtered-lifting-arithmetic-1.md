# Crystalline filtered lifting

Source SHA-256: `640701a0ea8d8026d2f019e571e052f2d106cad3f5216cf6f7fca0d643873234` (cached OpenAI source, 36 physical pages; checked against the Wave 4 index).

Physical p.7, Lemma 2.4 states this interface. Let `R` be a complete mixed-characteristic DVR finite over `W(F)`, `X,Y/R` abelian schemes, and `f0 in Hom^0(X0,Y0)`. If the contravariant crystalline map of `f0` preserves the indicated Hodge filtrations, then `f0` uniquely lifts to a rational homomorphism `X -> Y`. A symmetric rational map to the dual gives a rational divisor class; algebraic identities among finitely many lifted maps persist.

Use it as a filtered-morphism gate: record `R`, both special fibres, the rational map, the filtration-preservation check, and every cleared denominator before claiming a lift. The source's procedure clears a common integer denominator, applies rational filtered Dieudonne full faithfulness and Serre--Tate deformation, then identifies the reduction. This is conditional geometric input, not an arbitrary cohomology-map lifting rule.
