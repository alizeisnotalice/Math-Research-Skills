# Commuting-unipotent coinvariants

Source: *Constructible tame Hecke eigensheaves in positive characteristic*, SHA
`73db2ee4a6d9f8adf7236ba7ab8cf4891a899aa74fe9a822c9d53bbf60ab1ece`,
physical p.38. Scoped reference.

For a supplied nonzero finite-dimensional representation of a finitely generated abelian group
whose generators act unipotently and commute, the augmentation ideal acts nilpotently. Hence
the coinvariant quotient cannot vanish: otherwise successive ideal powers would force the space
to be zero. A finite certificate consists of matrices, commutators, unipotence exponents and a
nonzero vector-space dimension. It does not construct monodromy or an eigensheaf.
