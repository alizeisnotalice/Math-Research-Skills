# Stable-action triangular conversion

Source: *An explicit counterexample to the Auslander--Reiten conjecture*,
SHA-256 `22f6a9d5151fca3a323b9a1b91f6eb3ccf4c84cde00e9dfdeccb760a380ed6c3`,
Proposition 3.1 (physical p.6) and the finite algebra setup (p.10).

Let \(A\) be finite-dimensional symmetric of characteristic two, let \(F\) be
a finite \(A\)-bimodule projective on both sides, and let \(v:X\to F\otimes_AX\)
be a stable map.  The source forms the triangular algebra
\(\Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\F&A\end{pmatrix}\) and the comparison maps
\[
 \delta^a:H^a\oplus H^a\to K^a,\qquad
 (g,j)\mapsto F(g)v+v[a]j,
\]
where \(H^a=\underline{\rm Hom}_A(X,X[a])\) and
\(K^a=\underline{\rm Hom}_A(X,(FX)[a])\).

The conversion only applies with the paper's required surjectivity/injectivity
profile of these maps and a genuinely projective-on-both-sides finite bimodule.
It is not a finite presentation test for Auslander--Reiten vanishing.
