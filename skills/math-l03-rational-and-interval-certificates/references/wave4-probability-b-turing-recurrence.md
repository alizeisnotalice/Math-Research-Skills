# Bounded two-increment recurrence

For a represented Borel oracle satisfying the draft's countable-fibre and Turing-subadditive hypotheses, the bounded two-increment recurrence computes rational cuts of its irrational parameter from four fixed oracle values. The construction is pointwise and depends on supplied continuity points and program constants; it neither constructs the oracle nor proves degree rigidity by itself.
