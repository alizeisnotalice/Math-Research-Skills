# Rational recovery from a real effective divisor

**Source.** *Integrable Metrics and Effectivity with Controlled Boundary*,
pp. 7–8, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Integrable-metrics-and-effectivity-with-controlled-boundary-September-26-2026/main.pdf).

**Input.** Let \(D\) be a rational divisor on a normal projective variety.
Provide a real effective representative
\(D+\sum a_j\operatorname{div}(f_j)\ge0\), retaining the finite support of
\(D\) and all displayed principal divisors.

**Procedure.** Translate nonnegativity at every support component into a finite
system of rational linear inequalities in \(a_j\). Its real feasibility makes
the rational polyhedron nonempty. Pass to the relative interior of its
smallest supporting face, solve that face's rational equations, and approximate
in its rational affine space while preserving strict inequalities.

**Output.** The recovered rational coefficients give
\(D\sim_{\mathbb Q}D'\ge0\) with \(D'\) a rational effective divisor.

**Boundary.** This only rationalizes a known real effective linear equivalence.
It does not prove real effectivity or encode the source's metric,
multiplier-ideal, or controlled-boundary hypotheses.
