# VMRT pseudoindex-five numerical splice

**Source.** *The Campana–Peternell Conjecture in Dimension Six*, pp. 18–20,
[pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-Campana-Peternell-conjecture-in-dimension-six-September-25-2026/main.pdf).

**Input.** Supply the source's pseudoindex-five VMRT ledger: a smooth
threefold \(F\), ample tangent-map polarization \(L\), a finite tangent map to
a linearly nondegenerate threefold in \(\mathbb P^5\), and the certified
relation
\[
32-20u+3u^2=0,\qquad u=\frac{c_1(T_F)L^2}{L^3}.
\]

**Procedure.** Solve the exact quadratic, giving \(u\in\{4,8/3\}\). Apply the
sectional-genus identity
\[
g(B)=1+(1-u/2)L^3
\]
and the ledger facts \(g(B)\ge0\), \(L^3\ge3\). The root \(u=4\) forces
negative genus; \(u=8/3\) forces \(L^3=3\), and the finite tangent-map degree
and image degree are both one and three respectively.

**Output.** The exact finite filter produces the degree-three VMRT branch for
the subsequent normal-scroll and recognition steps.

**Boundary.** It does not verify the intersection ledger, tangent-map
finiteness, normality, smoothness, or the external VMRT recognition theorem;
those remain separate geometric inputs.
