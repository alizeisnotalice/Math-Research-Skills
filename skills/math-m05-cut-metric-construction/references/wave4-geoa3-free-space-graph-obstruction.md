# Scoped reference. Weighted graph spaces and localized free-space linearization

**Source.** OpenAI, *Lipschitz Equivalent Separable Banach Spaces Need Not Be Linearly Isomorphic*, Lemmas 2.3--2.5 and Theorem 4.1, PDF pp. 5--7, 10--14; SHA-256 `cc670229303a1ed9044a6bb04c397dd5b09a5c4046f08aa637ec8b112d6fa879`.

## Input contract

For the graph obstruction take a weakly sequentially complete real Banach space `E`, a completely continuous bounded map `q:E -> U`, and the weighted graph space `Z_q` with its defining `c0(U)` membership condition.  For complete continuity of a Lipschitz-free linearization, take a complete separable pointed metric space `M`, a Hilbert direct sum of finite-dimensional blocks `U`, and a Lipschitz `D:M -> U` satisfying the source’s local tail-coordinate orthogonality condition (4.1) around every finite list of distinct points.

## Reuse

Theorem 4.1 yields complete continuity of `hat D:F(M)->U`; with a weakly sequentially complete domain, Lemmas 2.4--2.5 rule out the specified linear `c0` copies in the associated graph construction.  The argument uses both local orthogonality after a common tail cutoff and compact reduction, not a mere global Lipschitz bound.

The source explicitly does not turn its nonlinear identities into a Lipschitz or linear section of the quotient.  Do not reuse this entry to assert arbitrary Lipschitz-free spaces are weakly sequentially complete or that arbitrary maps into `c0`-sums are completely continuous.
