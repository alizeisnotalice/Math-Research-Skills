# Invalid transfers to reject

- **Finite $\ell_r$ to arbitrary norm.** The Latała–Strzelecka theorem has a coefficient proportional to $r$ and assumes $r<\infty$. Since the arbitrary-norm endpoint can be represented by $\ell_\infty$, this does not prove a dimension-free universal constant for C153.
- **Euclidean Paouris to arbitrary norm.** A bound for $||X||_2$ does not control every Banach norm. Whitening a general covariance also transforms the norm; it cannot be silently held fixed.
- **Unconditional to all laws.** A theorem for unconditional or independent-coordinate laws has a narrower distribution class. An auxiliary product-exponential expectation in the bound is not the same as $E||X||$.
- **KLS or Cheeger to C154.** Euclidean-ball concentration does not imply the precise all-Borel-set enlargement by $\beta Z_p(\mu)$ unless the missing body inclusion is proved with uniform constants.
- **Packing to measure expansion.** An entropy bound for a self-generated metric on $Z_p(X)$ is not an inequality for $\mu(A+\beta Z_p(\mu))$.
- **Dimension dependent to dimension free.** The general bound $\beta\le C\sqrt n$ and the moment estimate with an $n^{1/4}$ logarithmic coefficient retain explicit dimension losses.
- **Finite numerical checks to a theorem.** The exact square example in the worker notes checks moments and support values in dimension two only. It cannot establish a universal inequality.
