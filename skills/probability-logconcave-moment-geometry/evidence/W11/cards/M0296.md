## Theorems actually proved

Theorem 1.1 (PDF pp. 1–2): for isotropic log-concave $X\in\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge2$, standard Gaussian $G$, any gauge $\phi:\mathbb R^n\to[0,\infty)$, and every $q\ge1$,

$$
\|\phi(G)\|_q\le C\sqrt{\log(en)+q}\,\|\phi(X)\|_q,
\qquad
\|\phi(X)\|_q\le C(\sqrt{\log(en)}+\psi(X)\sqrt q)\,\|\phi(G)\|_q.
$$

Here $\psi(X)$ is the Poincaré constant with the paper's Section 2 normalization. Consequently the two norms are comparable in $1\le q\le c\log(en)/\psi(X)^2$. The theorem assumes isotropy and a gauge, and carries dimension and Poincaré-constant losses.

Theorem 1.3 (PDF pp. 3–4): if $X$ is isotropic log-concave and the weak linear moments of $Y$ satisfy $\|\langle t,Y\rangle\|_q\le\|\langle t,X\rangle\|_q$ for all $t\in\mathbb R^n,q\ge1$, then for any norm and $p\ge2$,

$$
(\mathbb E\|Y\|^p)^{1/p}\le C\left(n^{1/4}\sqrt{\log(en)}\,\log(e+\log(en))\,\mathbb E\|X\|+\sigma_p(Y)\right),
$$

Here $\sigma_p(Y)=\sup_{\|t\|_*\le1}\|\langle t,Y\rangle\|_p$, the weak $p$-th moment for the dual of the chosen norm. The source places the leading $C$ outside the entire parenthesis, so it multiplies both the first-moment term and $\sigma_p(Y)$. Setting $Y=X$ gives the corresponding dimension-dependent arbitrary-norm comparison. This improves the coefficient in previous general bounds but does not establish C153's absolute dimension-free $C$.

## Proof chain and restrictions

Theorem 1.1 combines first-moment gauge comparisons with Gaussian concentration and a Poincaré moment inequality. Theorem 1.3 uses dyadic chaining and a net for the dual unit ball; at each scale it takes the strongest among (i) general Latała SMP bounds, (ii) Gaussian Sudakov plus gauge comparison, and (iii) a mean-width estimate for $Z_q(X)$. The regimes use Paouris at small scales, Borell comparison in intermediate scales, and Giannopoulos–Pafis–Tziotziou mean width bounds near/above $\sqrt n\log n$. The first-moment coefficient is $n^{1/4}\sqrt{\log(en)}\log(e+\log(en))$, while the separate weak term $\sigma_p(Y)$ is also multiplied by the outside constant $C$; neither coefficient is claimed optimal. Dependency Lemma 2.1 is imported from Giannopoulos et al.; this paper does not reprove it.

Theorem 1.4's dimension-free packing result concerns self-generated metrics $d_{X,r}(s,t)=\|\langle s-t,X\rangle\|_r$ and enlargements at scale $u I_r(X)Z_r(X)^\circ$; to get $e^{Cp}$ packing it requires a $\sqrt r$ enlargement. It does not imply C154's measure expansion for arbitrary Borel $A$ by $\beta Z_p(\mu)$. Metric entropy/packing and the $Z_p$-shaped set-expansion conjecture are distinct statements.

## Relevance to C153/C154

This is a strong current partial result for the arbitrary-norm weak/strong problem, but its isotropic input and $n^{1/4}\log(en)\log(e+\log(en))$ first-moment coefficient must remain visible. It provides centroid-body entropy tools that may inform C154, but no uniform CI($\beta$) theorem. Its dated version (11 Aug 2026) also lies outside the formal problem's specified 12 Aug–12 Nov 2026 search window by one day; it is background, not in-window direct progress.
