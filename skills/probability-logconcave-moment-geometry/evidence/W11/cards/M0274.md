# M0274 — Order statistics and concentration of $\ell_r$ norms for log-concave vectors

## Exact statements

For $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$, let $X_1^*\ge\cdots\ge X_n^*$ be the decreasing rearrangement of $|X_i|$. In Section 2, the paper separately defines the **one-sided** exceedance count
$$
N_X(t)=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf 1_{\{X_i\ge t\}},\qquad t\ge0.
$$

- **Theorem 3 (PDF p. 5).** If $X$ is isotropic and log-concave in $\mathbb R^n$, then for every $1\le k\le n$,
  $$
  \mathbb P(X_k^*\ge t)\le \exp(-\sqrt{k}\,t/C),\qquad t\ge C\log(en/k),
  $$
  with a universal constant $C$.
- **Theorem 4 (PDF p. 5).** For every isotropic log-concave $X$, every $p\ge1$ and
  $$t\ge C\log\!\left(\frac{nt^2}{p^2}\right),$$
  $$
  \mathbb E\bigl(t^2N_X(t)\bigr)^p\le (Cp)^{2p}.
  $$
  This is a positive-side count; it is not stated with $|X_i|$ inside the indicators.

**Derived modulus-count corollary (not a separate theorem in this paper).** For $t>0$, put $N_{|X|}(t)=\sum_i\mathbf1_{\{|X_i|\ge t\}}$. Then $N_{|X|}(t)=N_X(t)+N_{-X}(t)$. Both $X$ and $-X$ satisfy Theorem 4, so Minkowski's inequality gives
$$
\|t^2N_{|X|}(t)\|_p\le 2(Cp)^2\le (C'p)^2,
$$
under the same threshold, after changing only the universal constant. This is the explicit two-sided passage needed when a later argument uses coordinate moduli; it must not be presented as the literal statement of Theorem 4.

## Proof chain read

Theorem 4 is proved in Section 4 (PDF pp. 10–16). Proposition 10 conditions the log-concave law on a convex event $A$ with $0<\mathbb P(A)\le e^{-1}$; the conditional law remains log-concave. Paouris' Euclidean deviation bound controls how many conditional coordinate second moments can be large. Borell-type one-dimensional log-concave tails then control each conditional coordinate exceedance. A dyadic decomposition of the coordinate variances yields the two estimates in Proposition 10: a sum bound for $\mathbb P(A\cap\{X_i\ge t\})$ and a cardinality bound for coordinates whose conditional exceedance probability is large. The proof of Theorem 4 expands $\mathbb E N_X(t)^l$ as a sum over ordered index tuples, bins tuples by the probability of their joint positive exceedance, and uses Proposition 10 together with a combinatorial counting lemma to obtain $(Cl/t)^{2l}$ for a dyadic integer $l\in[p,2p]$. Theorem 3 then follows on PDF p. 5: $X_k^*\ge t$ implies $N_X(t)\ge k/2$ or $N_{-X}(t)\ge k/2$; apply Theorem 4 and Markov, then choose the moment order proportional to $t\sqrt{k}$.

## Scope and transfer limit

The results are isotropic and coordinate-specific. The $\ell_r$-norm concentration in Section 3 has constants depending on $r$ for $r>2$; neither it nor the order-statistic bounds proves a dimension-free estimate for every norm (C153) or the all-$Z_p$, all-Borel-set expansion in C154. The pairwise decorrelation constant $c(\alpha)$ belongs to Theorem 2 of the separate source M0290 and is intentionally absent from this card.
