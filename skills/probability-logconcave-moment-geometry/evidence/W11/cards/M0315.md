## Relevant definitions and results

Property $(\tau)$ (Definition 2.1) for a probability measure $\mu$ and nonnegative cost $\varphi$ is
$$
\int e^{f\,\square\,\varphi}\,d\mu\;\int e^{-f}\,d\mu\le1,
\quad f\,\square\,\varphi(x)=\inf_y(f(x-y)+\varphi(y)),
$$
for bounded measurable $f$. The optimal cost is $\Lambda_\mu^*$, the Legendre transform of the log moment-generating function. Section 3 studies its relationship to centroid bodies.

Corollary 3.11 (printed p. 162; local PDF p. 16) states for symmetric, isotropic, $\alpha$-regular $\mu$: (i) IC($\beta$) implies CI($\le8e\alpha\beta$); (ii) CI($\beta$) plus Cheeger constant $1/\gamma$ implies IC with constant $36\max\{6e\beta,\gamma\}$. Thus conversion is not automatic without the stated assumptions and auxiliary Cheeger bound.

Proposition 3.15 (printed pp. 164–165; local PDF pp. 18–19): if $\mu$ is $\alpha$-regular and CI($\beta$), then for every norm on $\mathbb R^n$ and $p\ge2$,
$$
\left(\int|\|x\|-\operatorname{Med}_\mu\|x\||^p d\mu\right)^{1/p}
\le 2\alpha\beta\sup_{\|u\|_*\le1}\left(\int|\langle u,x\rangle|^p d\mu\right)^{1/p}.
$$
Proof: CI and $\alpha$-regularity imply exponentially small tails of the norm outside median-centered dilates of $Z_p(\mu)$. For $y\in Z_p(\mu)$, duality gives $\|y\|\le\sigma_{\|\cdot\|,\mu}(p)$, converting body expansion to a tail bound; integrate the tail and use the gamma integral to get the factor $2\alpha\beta$.

Proposition 3.18 (printed p. 166) shows that CWSM($\gamma$) plus isotropy gives $\||X|_2-\sqrt n\|_2\le\gamma$; if also $\alpha$-regular, $\|X\|_{L_p(\ell_2)}\le\sqrt n+\gamma\alpha p/2$ for $p>2$. Conjecture 3 asserts CWSM with an absolute constant for every symmetric log-concave measure; it is stated as conjecture, not proved here.

Theorem 5.27 (printed p. 185; local PDF p. 39): uniform measure on $B_p^n$ satisfies CI(C) and IC(C) for all $n$ and $1\le p<\infty$, with an absolute constant. Theorem 5.29 gives Gaussian-type Euclidean isoperimetry for the uniform measure on $B_p^n$ when $p\ge2$: if $\mu_{p,n}(A)=\Phi(x)$, then $\mu_{p,n}(A+20tB_2^n)\ge\Phi(x+t)$ for all $t>0$. These are special measures, and the latter uses a Euclidean ball; neither proves C154 for general symmetric log-concave measures.

## Proof dependency / transfer boundary

The paper's core chain is: IC $\leftrightarrow$ concentration (with constants), log-concavity and moment regularity, concentration at $Z_p$-scales, then norm tails and moment integration. C154 would supply the CI premise with $\alpha=1$ for symmetric log-concave laws, yielding the stated weak/strong moment result; the converse or an arbitrary-norm proof cannot be asserted without the actual CI hypothesis. Preserve the full $Z_p$ body. The $\ell_p$-ball theorem and Euclidean isoperimetry are not replacements for all measures/all Borel sets.
