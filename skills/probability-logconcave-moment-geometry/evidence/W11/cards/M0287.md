I read all 14 pages, including references; the theorem pages were rendered and viewed.

For $p\ge2$, the paper defines $\|t\|_{M_p(X)}=(\mathbb E|\langle t,X\rangle|^p)^{1/p}$ and $\|t\|_{Z_p(X)}=\sup_{\|s\|_{M_p(X)}\le1}|\langle t,s\rangle|$; $M_p(X),Z_p(X)$ also denote their unit balls. Problem 1 asks whether a broad centered-vector bound of order $\sqrt{(n+p)/p}$ holds; it is posed as a problem/conjecture, not proved for all vectors.

## Unconditional theorem and entropy route

**Theorem 4 (PDF pp. 5–7).** If $X$ is an unconditional log-concave $n$-dimensional random vector and $p,q\ge2$, then
$$
(\mathbb E\|X\|_{Z_p(X)}^q)^{1/q}\le C\left(\sqrt{\frac{n+p}{p}}+\sup_{t\in M_p(X)}\|\langle t,X\rangle\|_q\right)\le C\left(\sqrt{\frac{n+p}{p}}+\frac qp\right).
$$
The proof uses Lemma 5 to split the $Z_p$-norm into a sparse-coordinate part $|I|\le p$ and a Euclidean-small part, applies Hitczenko's Rademacher-sum estimate, compares the latter with Gaussian/exponential sums, and controls a net by cardinality/union bounds. **Corollary 6 (PDF pp. 7–8)** gives, for unconditional log-concave $X$ and $2\le p\le n$,
$$
C^{-1}\sqrt{n/p}\le\mathbb E\|X\|_{Z_p(X)}\le(\mathbb E\|X\|_{Z_p(X)}^{\sqrt{np}})^{1/\sqrt{np}}\le C\sqrt{n/p},
$$
with $\mathbb P(\|X\|_{Z_p(X)}\ge C^{-1}\sqrt{n/p})\ge C^{-1}$ and upper tail $\mathbb P(\|X\|_{Z_p(X)}\ge Ct\sqrt{n/p})\le e^{-t\sqrt{np}}$, $t\ge1$. The exponential-coordinate example shows the scale is sharp up to absolute constants.

**Propositions 13–14 (PDF pp. 11–13).** If a symmetric vector has density $e^h$ with $\operatorname{Hess}h\ge-\alpha I$, Proposition 13 proves an $L_2$-Sudakov minoration constant $C_X\le C\sqrt\alpha$. Given an $L_2$-Sudakov minoration constant $C_X$, Proposition 14 states for every $p\ge2$
$$
N\!\left(M_p(X),\frac{eC_X}{\sqrt p}B_2^n\right)\le e^p.
$$
The radius is $eC_Xp^{-1/2}$: the rendered theorem and first proof line on PDF p. 12 both show $p^{-1/2}$. In the proof, a hypothetical packing of more than $e^p$ points separated by this radius forces, by Sudakov minoration, $\mathbb E\sup_i\langle t_i,X\rangle>e$; the $M_p(X)$ constraints and the union/moment bound give at most $e$, a contradiction. For isotropic log-concave $X$, $2\le p\le n$, the proposition implies $(\mathbb E\|X\|_{Z_p(X)}^2)^{1/2}\le e(C_X\sqrt{n/p}+1)$. These entropy statements have premises separate from the unconditional Theorem 4.

## Scope and interface

The universal conjecture is left open in the paper. The moment-body theorem is for unconditional log-concave vectors, with $p,q\ge2$; Propositions 13–14 use symmetric semiconvex density or an assumed Sudakov constant. Neither result gives C153 for dependent arbitrary-norm moments or C154's all-Borel-set enlargement by a single multiple of $Z_p(\mu)$.
