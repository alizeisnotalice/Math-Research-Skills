## Information concentration theorem

For a random vector $X\in\mathbb R^n$ with log-concave density $f$, write $\widetilde h(X)=-\log f(X)$ and differential entropy $h(X)=-\mathbb E\log f(X)$. **Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 2)** states, for every $t>0$,
$$
\mathbb P\{|\widetilde h(X)-h(X)|\ge t\sqrt n\}\le2e^{-t/16}.
$$
**Theorem 1.2 (PDF p. 3)** sharpens the exponent in the central range:
$$
\mathbb P\left\{\frac{|\log f(X)-\mathbb E\log f(X)|}{\sqrt n}\ge t\right\}\le3e^{-t^2/16},\qquad 0\le t\le2\sqrt n.
$$
The constants are universal. The paper also gives the moment-generating bound (Theorem 7.1, PDF p. 15):
$$
\mathbb E\exp\left(\frac{\alpha}{\sqrt n}|\log f(X)-\mathbb E\log f(X)|\right)\le3e^{4\alpha^2},\qquad0\le\alpha\le\frac{\sqrt n}{4}.
$$
Corollary 1.3 extends the almost-sure normalized information convergence to log-concave processes when the entropy-rate limit exists and finite-dimensional projections have log-concave laws.

## Proof chain read

The one-dimensional starting estimate bounds the exponential moment of the centered log-density by expressing a log-concave density through its concave isoperimetric profile $I(t)=f(F^{-1}(t))$. Reverse Lyapunov inequalities for moments of positive log-concave variables then give a $p$-dependent exponential estimate for $\log\xi$ under order-$p$ log-concavity. A Lovász–Simonovits localization lemma reduces the $n$-dimensional assertion to one-dimensional measures whose density contains an affine factor to power $n-1$. The one-dimensional bound and the order-$n$ estimate combine to prove Theorem 7.1; Chernoff optimization yields Theorems 1.1 and 1.2. Borel–Cantelli gives Corollary 1.3.

## Interface and limits

This controls the information density $-\log f(X)$, an affine-invariant scalar statistic. It is not a tail bound for an arbitrary norm of $X$, does not compare all norm moments with weak moments (C153), and does not imply C154's moment-body enlargement.
