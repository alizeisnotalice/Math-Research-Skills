# M0342 — Bounds on optimal transport maps onto log-concave measures

**Source and version boundary.** Maria Colombo and Max Fathi, *Journal of Differential Equations* 271 (2021), pp. 1007–1022, DOI [10.1016/j.jde.2020.09.032](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jde.2020.09.032). The local complete 13-page PDF is arXiv:1910.09035v1 (20 October 2019), SHA-256 fbda15d6f5199feb6fb9e5ddea514f76a6fdf9306473b2922a7034608fd529f5; I read all pages, including references. The journal record and local arXiv version are distinguished; I do not claim the v1 text is identical to the 16-page journal version.

Let $T=\nabla\varphi$ be the Brenier map pushing standard Gaussian $\gamma$ onto $\mu$. **Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 2)**: if $\mu$ is centered, isotropic and log-concave on $\mathbb R^d$, then for every $x\in\mathbb R^d$,
$$
|T(x)|\le C(d+|x|^2).
$$
If instead $\mu$ is centered and satisfies Gaussian concentration with constant $\beta$ in the convention $\mu\{f\ge\mathbb Ef+r\}\le e^{-\beta r^2/2}$ for every 1-Lipschitz $f$, then
$$
|T(x)|\le12\beta^{-1/2}\sqrt{|x|^2+17d}.
$$
This second clause does not assume log-concavity; Gaussian concentration is stronger than what every log-concave measure is known here to satisfy.

**Theorem 1.2 (PDF p. 3).** Suppose $\mu=e^{-V}dx$ is centered and isotropic and, for all $x$,
$$
c_1 I_d\succeq\operatorname{Hess}V(x)\succeq\frac{c_2}{d+|x|}I_d,\qquad c_1,c_2>0.
$$
For every unit $e\in S^{d-1}$ and $p\ge0$,
$$
\left\|\frac{\partial_{ee}^2\varphi}{\sqrt{d+|x|^2}}\right\|_{L^{p+2}(\gamma)}\le\frac C{c_2}\left(1+\frac{p\sqrt{c_1}}{4\sqrt d}\right),
$$
with universal $C$. **Theorem 1.3 (PDF pp. 3–4)**, under the same Hessian bounds and $c_1\ge1$, gives
$$
\|\nabla T(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\max\!\left(\frac{C c_1^2}{c_2^2},1\right)(d+|x|^2)^2.
$$
If also $c_1\ge|\nabla V(x)|$ for every $x$, the last factor improves to $d^{4/3}+|x|^2$. These are curvature-dependent pointwise bounds, not dimension-free log-concave transport regularity.

**Proof chain.** For Theorem 1.1, the authors combine the Lee–Vempala concentration estimate for isotropic log-concave laws with a ball $B(x+4\sqrt d\,u,2\sqrt d)$ and a Lipschitz support function constructed using monotonicity of the convex Brenier map. The target measure of its image is exponentially small in $|T(x)|$, whereas Gaussian mass of the ball is bounded below by $\exp(-|x|^2-17d)$; equality of transported mass yields the quadratic growth bound. For Theorem 1.2, the Monge–Ampère equation is differenced in direction $e$; the lower Hessian bound controls the second difference, integration by parts transfers derivatives, Caffarelli contraction bounds the inverse Hessian, and $\varepsilon\to0$ yields the $L^{p+2}(\gamma)$ estimate. For Theorem 1.3, regularize the potential, maximize a weighted second-difference quotient, use concavity of $\lambda\mapsto\log\det\lambda$ and Monge–Ampère finite differences, then use the Hessian/gradient controls and choose the auxiliary exponents to get the two displayed growth rates.
