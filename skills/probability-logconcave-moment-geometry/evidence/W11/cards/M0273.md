# M0273 — Modified Paouris inequality

**Source and reading evidence.** Rafał Latała, chapter in *Geometric Aspects of Functional Analysis*, LNM 2116 (2014), pp. 293–307. Verified 12-page local PDF; read PDF pp. 1–3. Local file: evidence/fulltexts/M0273.pdf; SHA-256 8ac983f044a2ff4b9642cd4b1ef561340253d0cef851ba17540d6de7bb637ac8. Public author copy: https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/~rlatala/papers/modPaourrev0.pdf.

## Main estimates

For an isotropic log-concave $X\in\mathbb R^n$ and $p\ge1$, Theorem 1 (PDF p. 2) states
$$
\mathbb E\left(\sum_{i=1}^n X_i^2\mathbf1_{\{|X_i|\ge t\}}\right)^p
\le (C_2\sigma_X(p))^{2p},
\qquad
t\ge C_2\log\!\left(\frac n{\sigma_X(p)^2}\right),
$$
where $\sigma_X(p)=\sup_{|v|_2\le1}\|\langle v,X\rangle\|_p$ and $C_2$ is universal. The estimate controls the aggregate contribution of large coordinates and implies Paouris' Euclidean moment inequality.

Theorem 2 (p. 2) gives, for $r\ge2$, isotropic log-concave $X$, and $p\ge1$,
$$
(\mathbb E\|X\|_r^p)^{1/p}\le C_3(rn^{1/r}+\sigma_X(p)).
$$
The theorem concerns isotropic vectors and the coordinate $\ell_r$ norms; the mean term is $rn^{1/r}$, not $\mathbb E\|X\|_r$. It does not prove C153.

## Proof chain and transfer limits

The proof first establishes a truncated-square moment inequality and then uses dyadic decomposition of coordinate magnitudes for $\ell_r$ and maxima of coordinate projections. The truncation threshold depends logarithmically on $n/\sigma_X(p)^2$. The $\ell_r$ estimate is useful for isotropic coordinate geometry, but neither arbitrary norm nor arbitrary covariance is covered. No $Z_p$-body set expansion is proved here.
