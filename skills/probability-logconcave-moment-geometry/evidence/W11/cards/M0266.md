# M0266 — Isoperimetric and analytic inequalities for log-concave probability measures

## Results read

The paper studies absolutely continuous log-concave probability measures on $\mathbb R^n$, with density proportional to $e^{-V(x)}$ for convex $V$. Theorem 1.2 (printed p. 1905) gives a Cheeger-type lower bound for the isoperimetric constant in terms of the $L_2(\mu)$ size of $|x-x_0|$, where $x_0$ is the barycenter; hence every such measure has a positive Cheeger constant and a Poincaré-type inequality. Theorem 1.3 characterizes when a log-concave measure has Gaussian-type isoperimetry/log-Sobolev behavior through a finite quadratic exponential moment, and compares the optimal parameter with a corresponding Orlicz norm.

The proof uses Brunn–Minkowski/Prékopa–Leindler geometry and tail information for the Euclidean radius. The constants depend on the radial scale of the measure; this is not a dimension-free KLS result and does not provide the $Z_p(\mu)$-shaped CI expansion in C154. Euclidean isoperimetry/Poincaré bounds from this paper cannot replace an all-Borel-set enlargement by $\beta Z_p(\mu)$ without a separate body inclusion.

## Relevance and limitation

Use this as a foundation for Cheeger/Poincaré implications in the log-concave class. Keep its Euclidean metric, moment/radius parameter and isoperimetric formulation explicit. It is auxiliary to C154, not a proof of C154.
