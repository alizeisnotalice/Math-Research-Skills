## One-dimensional equivalence and constants

Let $\mu$ be a symmetric Borel probability measure on $\mathbb R$. For $h>0$ and $0\le\lambda<1$, the paper defines $M(h,\lambda)$ by
$$
\mu([x+h,\infty))\le\lambda\mu([x,\infty))\quad\text{for every }x\ge0.
$$
Define $\varphi_0(x)=x^2/2$ when $|x|\le1$ and $\varphi_0(x)=|x|-1/2$ when $|x|>1$. **Theorem 1 (PDF pp. 3–4)** says the following are equivalent:

1. $\mu\in M(h,\lambda)$ for some $h,\lambda$;
2. $\mu$ satisfies the convex Poincaré inequality $\operatorname{Var}_\mu f\le C_P\int(f')^2\,d\mu$ for every convex $f$ with bounded derivative;
3. $\mu$ satisfies the convex exponential infimum-convolution inequality
   $$
   \left(\int e^{f\square\varphi}\,d\mu\right)\left(\int e^{-f}\,d\mu\right)\le1
   $$
   for every convex $f$ bounded below, with $\varphi(x)=\varphi_0(x/C_\tau)$.

The constants in the theorem are explicit at the level stated: (1)$\Rightarrow$(3) with $C_\tau=17h/(1-\lambda)^2$; (3)$\Rightarrow$(2) with $C_P=C_\tau^2/2$; (2)$\Rightarrow$(1) with $h=\sqrt{8C_P}$ and $\lambda=1/2$. The infimum convolution is $f\square\varphi(x)=\inf_y\{f(y)+\varphi(x-y)\}$. This is a **convex-function** inequality; the paper does not assert unrestricted Maurey ICI for arbitrary $f$ from this theorem.

## Product consequences

Convex property $(\tau)$ tensorizes (Corollary 1, PDF p. 4). For independent $\mu_i\in M(h,\lambda)$ on $\mathbb R$, the product $\mu=\otimes_i\mu_i$ satisfies the convex ICI for coordinatewise convex functions, with cost $\sum_i\varphi_0(x_i/C_\tau)$. Corollary 2 (PDF p. 4) gives, for convex $A$ with $\mu(A)>0$,
$$
\mu\!\left(A+\sqrt{2t}\,C_\tau B_2^n+2tC_\tau B_1^n\right)\ge1-\frac{e^{-t}}{\mu(A)}\quad(t\ge0).
$$
Corollary 3 (PDF pp. 4, 11) gives two-sided concentration for convex $f$ with Euclidean and $\ell_1$ Lipschitz bounds $a,b$:
$$
\mu\{f>\operatorname{Med}_\mu f+C_\tau t\},\quad
\mu\{f<\operatorname{Med}_\mu f-C_\tau t\}
\le2\exp\!\left[-\tfrac18\min\{t/b,t^2/a^2\}\right].
$$

## Proof chain read

The proof begins with a shifted-tail lemma on $M_+(h,\lambda)$, which bounds an $L_2$ norm of a monotone function by its discrete difference $g(x)-g(x-h)$. A discrete-gradient estimate then bounds $\int(e^{f/2}-e^{-f/2})^2d\mu$ by the weighted squared difference of a convex $f$. A local quadratic descent estimate for $f\square\varphi$ proves tail-ratio $\Rightarrow$ convex ICI (PDF pp. 5–9). Expanding the ICI at $\varepsilon f$ to second order yields the convex Poincaré inequality; testing with $(x-u)_+$ gives the reverse tail-ratio condition with $h=\sqrt{8C_P}$ and $\lambda=1/2$ (PDF pp. 9–10). Applying convex ICI to an indicator approximation of a convex set gives enlargement; splitting the cost into quadratic and linear coordinates yields the two-level $B_2+B_1$ expansion and the function tails (PDF pp. 10–11).

## Transfer limit

The product conclusions require independent one-dimensional symmetric marginals in the geometric tail-ratio class. They are not general concentration theorems for arbitrary dependent log-concave measures, do not establish C154's single $Z_p(\mu)$ enlargement for every symmetric log-concave law, and do not establish C153.
