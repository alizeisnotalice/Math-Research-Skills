# M0341 — Volume extremals of general $L_p$-centroid bodies

**Source and read evidence.** Weidong Wang and Tian Li, *Journal of Mathematical Inequalities* 11 (2017), no. 1, pp. 193–207, DOI [10.7153/jmi-11-19](https://doi.org/10.7153/jmi-11-19). The complete 15-page local PDF evidence/fulltexts/M0341.pdf (SHA-256 1a47335a29d295c564137acee45458adcfb3c2de834bf9d01715280c3f9bfdfe) was read through the references; the publisher-hosted article verifies citation and identity.

This paper concerns star bodies and the asymmetric $L_p$-Brunn–Minkowski theory. For $\tau\in[-1,1]$, it uses $\varphi_\tau(t)=|t|+\tau t$ to define the general $L_p$-centroid body $\Gamma_p^\tau K$ through the $p$-th root of the normalized integral of $\varphi_\tau(\langle u,x\rangle)^p$ over $K$. The body is an $L_p$-Minkowski combination
$$
\Gamma_p^\tau K=f_1(\tau)\cdot\Gamma_p^+K+_p f_2(\tau)\cdot\Gamma_p^-K,\quad
f_1(\tau)=\frac{(1+\tau)^p}{(1+\tau)^p+(1-\tau)^p},\quad f_2=1-f_1.
$$
Here the endpoint bodies are the asymmetric $L_p$-centroid body of $K$ and its reflected counterpart; they are not probabilistic $Z_p(\mu)$ bodies.

**Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 4; printed p. 196).** If $K\in\mathcal S_o^n$ is a star body containing the origin, $p>1$, $\tau\in[-1,1]$, and $i=0,1,\ldots,n-1$, then
$$
W_i(\Gamma_pK)\ge W_i(\Gamma_p^\tau K)\ge W_i(\Gamma_p^\pm K).
$$
If $K$ is not origin-symmetric and $p$ is not an odd integer, equality in the left inequality occurs iff $\tau=0$, and equality in the right iff $\tau=\pm1$. $i=0$ is the volume extremal inequality.

**Theorem 1.2 (PDF p. 4).** For $K\in\mathcal S_o^n$, $p\ge1$, $\tau\in[-1,1]$, real $i\ne n$, the polar bodies' dual quermassintegrals satisfy
$$
\widetilde W_i(\Gamma_p^*K)\le\widetilde W_i(\Gamma_p^{\tau,*}K)\le\widetilde W_i(\Gamma_p^{\pm,*}K),\quad i<n\text{ or }i>n+p,
$$
and
$$
\widetilde W_i(\Gamma_p^*K)\ge\widetilde W_i(\Gamma_p^{\tau,*}K)\ge\widetilde W_i(\Gamma_p^{\pm,*}K),\quad n<i<n+p.
$$
At $i=n+p$ the quantities are equal. The same endpoint equality characterization holds when $K$ is not origin-symmetric and $p$ is not an odd integer. The $i=n$ case is excluded.

**Proof chain.** Theorem 3.1–3.4 establish affine covariance, reflection under $K\mapsto-K$, endpoint symmetry criteria and the identity $\Gamma_pK=\tfrac12\cdot\Gamma_p^\tau K+_p\tfrac12\cdot\Gamma_p^{-\tau}K$. Theorem 1.1 follows by applying the $L_p$ Brunn–Minkowski inequality for $W_i$ to the two endpoint bodies and their $\tau$-weighted combination; equality reduces to equality/dilation of the endpoints and the asymmetric-centroid characterization. For polars, (2.5) turns the $L_p$ combination into an $L_p$-harmonic radial combination. The dual quermassintegral Brunn–Minkowski inequalities give one side of Theorem 1.2. The other side differentiates $\widetilde W_i(\Gamma_p^{\tau,*}K)$ in $\tau$, expresses the derivative using $L_p$-dual mixed quermassintegrals, and uses their Minkowski inequality and equality condition. The proof handles $i<n$, $n<i<n+p$, and $i>n+p$ separately because the exponent changes sign at $n$ and $n+p$.

**Scope.** These are geometric extremal comparisons for $K$'s uniform-volume centroid bodies and their polars. No theorem here asserts an estimate for an arbitrary log-concave measure, arbitrary norm, or all-Borel-set $Z_p(\mu)$ enlargement.
