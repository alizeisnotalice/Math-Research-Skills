# M0340 — Transport proofs of weighted Poincaré inequalities for log-concave distributions

**Source and version boundary.** Dario Cordero-Erausquin and Nathael Gozlan, *Bernoulli* 23 (2017), no. 1, pp. 134–158, DOI [10.3150/15-BEJ739](https://doi.org/10.3150/15-BEJ739). Project Euclid's journal record gives this 25-page published pagination. The complete local file evidence/fulltexts/M0340.pdf is the 19-physical-page arXiv:1407.3217v1 manuscript (11 July 2014), SHA-256 3b051ea2876ddf3f32c26453d90724a9fcef3181a57dcf15ccafe8434391b17d; I read this entire local version through its references. I do not assert page-by-page equivalence to the 25-page journal version.

For $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$, let $E_{i-1}[\cdot]=\mathbb E[\cdot\mid X_1,\ldots,X_{i-1}]$ and define sequentially centered coordinates $\bar X_i=X_i-E_{i-1}[X_i]$. **Theorem 1.1 (PDF pp. 1–2)** states that a numerical $a>0$ exists such that every $n$-dimensional log-concave $X$ and every locally Lipschitz $f\in L^2(\bar X)$ obey
$$
\operatorname{Var}(f(\bar X))\le a\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb E\!\left[\mathbb E(\bar X_i^2\mid\bar X_1,\ldots,\bar X_{i-1})\,(\partial_i f(\bar X))^2\right].
$$
The proof obtains $a=(4\sqrt3+1)^2$ in its final displayed estimate. If $E_{i-1}[X_i]=0$ for all $i$, then $\bar X=X$, and the inequality applies directly to $X$. This includes log-concave martingale increments and independent centered coordinates; it does not assert the unweighted KLS Poincaré bound for general log-concave vectors.

**Transport mechanism (formula corrected against rendered PDF p. 9).** For a one-dimensional log-concave law $\gamma$, the optimal Cheeger constant in (4.1) satisfies
$$
\frac{1}{3\operatorname{Var}(X)}\le \lambda_\gamma^2\le \frac{2}{\operatorname{Var}(X)},\qquad X\sim\gamma.
$$
These are reciprocal-variance bounds. For the conditional law of $X_i$ given $X_{<i}=x_{<i}$, equation (4.3) chooses the weight
$$
\lambda_i^2(x_{<i})=\frac{1}{3\operatorname{Var}(X_i\mid X_{<i}=x_{<i})},\qquad \lambda_i(x_{<i})=\frac{1}{\sqrt{3\operatorname{Var}(X_i\mid X_{<i}=x_{<i})}}.
$$
Bobkov–Houdré's centered $N(t)=|t|-\log(1+|t|)$ inequality converts this into a conditional cost estimate. A Knothe triangular transport, inserted in the above-tangent entropy lemma, yields Theorem 4.6: $T_\mu(\bar\mu,\bar\nu)\le D(\nu\|\mu)$ for every $\nu$ with finite first moment, with
$$
c_\mu(x,y)=\frac1{16}\sum_{i=1}^n N\!\left(\lambda_i(S(x))(x_i-y_i)\right),
$$
where $R_i(x)=x_i-\mathbb E[X_i\mid X_{<i}=x_{<i}]$, $S=R^{-1}$, and bars denote laws after this triangular recentering. Linearizing the supremal convolution associated with this cost at $t\downarrow0$, perturbing the law by $(1+tg)\mu$, and optimizing the scalar parameter gives Theorem 1.1. The source also proves Theorem 1.12: for log-concave $\mu$ supported in $[-R,R]^n$, $W_2^2(\mu,\nu)\le cR^2D(\nu\|\mu)$ for all $\nu$.

**Variance consequence.** Corollary 1.6 yields $\operatorname{Var}(|X|^2)\le b\sum_i\mathbb E[X_i^4]\le Cn$ for isotropic log-concave $X=\bar X$; the final $O(n)$ follows from the one-dimensional reverse Hölder estimate. The paper also gives an orthogonal decomposition identity for $X=\bar X+X'$ and bounds relating the variances of their squared lengths. The centering condition is essential to the direct variance-conjecture consequence.

**Scope.** This is a sequentially weighted Poincaré/transport theorem for a recentered vector. It supplies neither C153 for arbitrary norms nor a $Z_p(\mu)$-shaped enlargement theorem C154.
