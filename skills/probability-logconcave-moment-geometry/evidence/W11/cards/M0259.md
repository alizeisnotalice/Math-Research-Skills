## Main theorem and assumptions

Theorem 2 (PDF p. 2) states: if a log-concave random vector $X$ takes values in a normed space $F$ that isometrically embeds in $\ell_r$ for some $1\le r<\infty$, then for every $p\ge1$,

$$
(\mathbb E\|X\|^p)^{1/p}\le C_2r\left(\mathbb E\|X\|+\sup_{\|\varphi\|_*\le1}(\mathbb E|\varphi(X)|^p)^{1/p}\right),
$$

with absolute $C_2$. The finite-dimensional formulation is Theorem 5 (p. 3), with $F=\mathbb R^n$ and the $\ell_r$ norm. The factor is linear in $r$; it is not uniform over norms.

Theorem 6 (p. 3) is a proof input: for $p\ge r$, $d_i=(\mathbb EX_i^2)^{1/2}$, $d=(\sum_i d_i^r)^{1/r}$, and $t\ge C_4r\log(d/\sigma_{r,X}(p))$,

$$
\left(\mathbb E\left[\sum_i |X_i|^r\mathbf1_{\{|X_i|\ge td_i\}}\right]^{p/r}\right)^{1/p}\le C_3r\,\sigma_{r,X}(p),
$$

where $\sigma_{r,X}(p)=\sup_{\|u\|_{r'}\le1}\|\langle u,X\rangle\|_p$. The theorem isolates large coordinates before summing them.

## Proof chain and exact limitation

The proof reduces to finite-dimensional $\ell_r$, decomposes coordinates at thresholds proportional to their marginal $L_2$ scales, and controls large-coordinate tails using log-concavity plus the weak moment. Theorem 6 feeds a dyadic level decomposition and integration to establish Theorem 5. The stated class has finite $r$; every finite-dimensional normed space can be embedded in $\ell_\infty$, but $r=\infty$ is excluded from the theorem. Therefore this result does not prove C153, which requires an absolute constant for arbitrary norms and all $p\ge1$.

## C154 relevance

This paper is a moment-comparison result, not the all-Borel-set $Z_p(\mu)$-expansion conjecture. Its coordinate truncation estimates cannot be substituted for C154 without an additional argument yielding the exact $Z_p(\mu)$ enlargement.
