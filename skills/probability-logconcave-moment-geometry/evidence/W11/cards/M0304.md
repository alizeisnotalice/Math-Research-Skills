## KLS obstruction and projection MDPs

**Theorem A (PDF p. 3)** is conditional. Let $1\le k_n\le n$, $k_n\to\infty$, and let $\xi_n$ be isotropic log-concave vectors in $\mathbb R^{k_n}$. If $X_n=\|\xi_n\|_2/\sqrt{k_n}$ satisfies an LDP with speed $s_n$ and rate $I$, then the KLS conjecture would be false if either (a) $s_n=o(\sqrt{k_n})$ and $I$ is not the singular rate $I_0$ supported at $1$, or (b) $s_n\asymp\sqrt{k_n}$ and for some absolute $t_0>1$,
$$
\inf_{t>t_0}\frac{\inf_{x\in(t,\infty)}I(x)}{t}=0.
$$
This is a conditional route to a contradiction, not a claim that KLS is currently disproved.

**Theorem B (PDF p. 4)** studies $\mathcal Z_{n,p}=n^{1/p}\|P_{E_n}X_n\|_2$, where $X_n$ is uniform on $B_p^n$, $E_n$ is an independent Haar-random $k_n$-dimensional subspace, $k_n\to\infty$, $k_n=o(n)$, and
$$
M_p(2)=\frac{p^{2/p}}{3}\frac{\Gamma(1+3/p)}{\Gamma(1+1/p)}.
$$
For $p\ge2$, $k_n^{-1/2}\mathcal Z_{n,p}$ has an MDP with speed $k_n$ and rate
$$
I(x)=\frac{x^2-M_p(2)}{2M_p(2)}-\log\frac{x}{\sqrt{M_p(2)}}\quad(x>0),
$$
and $I(x)=+\infty$ otherwise. For $1\le p<2$, the rate changes at $k_n$ of order $n^{p/2}$. In (b1), if $k_n=o(n^{p/2})$, the speed is $k_n$ and $I(x)=\frac{x^2-M_p(2)}2-\log\frac{x}{\sqrt{M_p(2)}}$ for $x>0$ (and $+\infty$ otherwise). In (b2), if $k_n=n^{p/2}$, the speed is $n^{p/2}$ and $I(x)=\inf_{y\ge m_p}\{\frac{(x/y)^2-1}{2}-\log(x/y)+\frac1p(y^2-M_p(2))^{p/2}\}$ for $x>0$. In (b3), if $k_n=\omega(n^{p/2})$, the speed is $n^{p/2}$ and $I(x)=\frac1p(x^2-M_p(2))^{p/2}$ for $x>\sqrt{m_p}$. In the other cases the rate is $+\infty$. The preprint does not define $m_p$ in its own notation, so I preserve the printed symbol and do not silently identify it with $M_p(2)$.

**Theorem C (PDF p. 4)** treats subcritical deviations for $p\ge2$. Let $k_n/n\to\lambda\in[0,1]$, $k_n\to\infty$, and $t_n\to\infty$ with $t_n=o(\sqrt{k_n})$ and either $t_n=o(\sqrt{n-k_n})$ or $n-k_n=o(\sqrt{k_n}/t_n)$. For
$$
\mathcal X_{n,p}=n^{1/p}\sqrt{\frac{\Gamma(1/p)}{p^{2/p}\Gamma(3/p)}}\|P_{E_n}X_n\|_2-\sqrt{k_n},
$$
$t_n^{-1}\mathcal X_{n,p}$ has an MDP with speed $t_n^2$ and rate $I(x)=\alpha_{p,\lambda}x^2$, where
$$
\alpha_{p,\lambda}=\frac{2\Gamma(1/p)\Gamma(3/p)^2}{(2p-\lambda(4+3p))\Gamma(1+1/p)\Gamma(3/p)^2+\lambda\Gamma(1/p)^2\Gamma(5/p)}.
$$

## Proof dependencies read

For Theorem A, the KLS conjecture would imply exponential concentration of $\|\xi_n\|_2/\sqrt{k_n}$ at scale $1/\sqrt{k_n}$; the LDP lower bound then conflicts with either stated rate-function condition. For Theorems B–C, a Schechtman–Zinn-type representation writes the projected norm using independent Gaussian and $p$-generalized Gaussian coordinates (and an auxiliary radial variable for the general $P_{W,n,p}$ class). Cramér's theorem, joint LDPs, contraction and exponential equivalence isolate the dominant terms. The $p<2$ square-moment term has speed $n^{p/2}$, creating the critical phase split. At subcritical scales, MDPs for sums of generalized Gaussian powers and Gaussian squares give a multivariate quadratic rate; contraction yields $\alpha_{p,\lambda}$, and the Taylor remainder and radial term are exponentially negligible under the stated hypotheses.

$$
I(x)=\frac{x^2-M_p(2)}2-\log\frac{x}{\sqrt{M_p(2)}}\quad(1\le p<2,\ k_n=o(n^{p/2})),
$$
with $+\infty$ outside the displayed domain.

The saved initial SymPy check (evidence/W11/notes/M0304-b1-rate-anomaly-batch4.txt), rerun and logged with the bundled project SymPy in evidence/W11/notes/M0304-b1-sympy-batch5.txt, evaluates the paper's definition at $p=1$: $M_1(2)=2$, and the printed expression at $x=1$ is $(\log2-1)/2\approx-0.1534264$. A rate function must be nonnegative, so this displayed b1 subclaim is isolated from method-level use pending an author correction/erratum or newer source. This observation does not prove the whole Theorem B false and does not alter the author-printed formula. Theorems A and C remain separately stated and scoped as above. Only page 4 of the PostPrint was checked; the complete-text reading status remains tied to the arXiv v1 PDF.

## Interface and limits

These are asymptotic results for specific Euclidean projections of $\ell_p^n$ balls, plus a conditional KLS obstruction. They do not imply a finite-dimensional arbitrary-norm moment estimate (C153) or the all-symmetric-log-concave $Z_p$ expansion in C154. Keep the LDP/MDP speed, dimension regime, isotropy and projection model attached to every use.
