# Wave 4 PA3: finite lattice-Gaussian comparison interface

## BKT universality for height and planar spin fields

Source SHA-256 `5e6a8df09e021c4a114360d4bda50622ccac64d51150045a3df299565b133152`, physical pp. 11--12, Lemma 2.1.  For a finite-dimensional lattice `L\subset E`, a symmetric precision `T` positive definite on `V=\operatorname{span}L`, and
\[
w_T(x)=\sum_{u\in L+x}e^{-(u,Tu)/2},
\]
the source uses the theta-ratio inequality
\[
w_T(0)^2w_T(x+y)w_T(x-y)\ge w_T(x)^2w_T(y)^2.
\]
It implies covariance at shift `x` dominates the centered covariance, and the centered characteristic function is strictly positive with log-Hessian at least its value at zero.  A positive-semidefinite precision increase decreases centered variances and real moment generating functions; for a centered lattice Gaussian, `\kappa(X,X,Y,Y)\ge0`.  On a free graph these claims apply only to tests annihilating released constants and to characters invariant under released translations.

**Scope.** Scoped reference. It is a finite-dimensional lattice-Gaussian statement with the stated coercivity, quotient/translation and centered-test conditions; it is not a general log-concave or interacting-spin comparison.
