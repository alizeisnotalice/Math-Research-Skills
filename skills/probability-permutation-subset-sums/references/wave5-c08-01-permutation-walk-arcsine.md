# Uniform-permutation ascent walk to an arcsine occupation law

Source: Wenpin Tang, *The occupation time of a random walk generated by the uniform random permutation -- a GPT-6 Astra proof*, local PDF SHA-256 \`f23fafe8c40fe263d3158924171a4c8e403a7cf1a8833f5bce7a225db004c78c\`, physical pp. 1-3.  Dependency check: the source gives the full reduction (0.3)-(0.7); its final named dependency is the classical Sparre--Anderson theorem.

For a uniform permutation \(\pi\) of \([2n+1]\), put \(X_k=1_{\{\pi_k<\pi_{k+1}\}}-1_{\{\pi_k>\pi_{k+1}\}}\), \(S_k=\sum_{j\le k}X_j\), and count
\[
N_{2n}=\sum_{k=1}^{2n}\mathbf1_{\{S_{k-1}\ge0,\ S_k\ge0\}}.
\]
Odd-time heights cannot vanish, so the source obtains the pathwise identity
\[
N_{2n}=2\sum_{k=1}^n\mathbf1_{\{S_{2k-1}>0\}}.
\]
Represent the uniform permutation by ranks of independent uniform variables.  Its record variables \(V_j\) yield
\[
Y_k=1-V_{2k-1}-V_{2k},\qquad T_k=\sum_{j\le k}Y_j,
\quad
N_{2n}=2\#\{k:T_k>0\}.
\]
The \(Y_k\) are iid, continuous and symmetric, so Sparre--Anderson gives the discrete arcsine law.

Check all three transformed-increment properties before using the final occupation-law theorem.
