# Finite-cell level comparison

Scoped reference. *A Modulus Proof of Cannon’s Conjecture* (OpenAI, 23 Sep
2026), SHA-256 `650697e8e95aa8cbf86c4f3898d399d75d3f1551d683f9db6e254b370aea080b`,
physical p. 14, Lemma 3.8. Given continuous `f,w`, compact `K`, interval `I`,
and a continuum `D_t` at every level with `osc(D_t) w >= b0`, the stated
cellwise oscillation sum bounds `b0 |I|`; its proof integrates a finite family
of cell-range indicators and uses Cauchy–Schwarz, without a measurable choice
of `D_t`. [Pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/A-Modulus-Proof-of-Cannons-Conjecture-September-23-2026/paper.pdf).

The level continua, closed-cell containment and uniform lower oscillation are
input hypotheses. This is not a Cannon-conjecture or modulus uniformization
procedure.
