# Coordinate-sweep finite interfaces

For the listed finite symmetric-group representation data, retain the exact premises of each component: common subprobability mass/coset cap/block-orbit conditions for trimming and Schur averaging; a direction schedule fixed before conditioning with every `d` consecutive directions a basis for matching conjugacy; and a symmetric finite kernel with a certified density moment and irreducible multiplicity for cap truncation. These are matrix/representation interfaces, not generic Markov mixing conclusions.

Sources: *From partial permutation information to Fourier bounds*, SHA-256 `88926b5e48099ed836f49f15c3ef759c14f7aabb140a14e9005ca7fe09727a8b`, physical pp. 5--9; *Random coordinate frames and partial permutation laws*, SHA-256 `0a520da2d4a92a176e8833ef81fd1d4ecf19c117954c9606577f9d819be5d394`, physical pp. 8--9; and *Routing densities and representation contraction for Thorp sweeps*, SHA-256 `4e5fbcff0dec65fd5a5d929e38c255f03e89038866b2e3cce044eee9e04cd862`, physical pp. 10 and 16--17.

Scoped reference. The conditional coordinate-sweep Schatten estimate also requires the source's fixed feasible path, sweep mixture, coordinate sizes, and potential `C(H)`; do not treat it as an arbitrary-kernel contraction.

Source: *Conditional coordinate sweeps and analytic transfer*, SHA-256 `50b927834abd484c8602aa21111ad7f2ff161035c8309c41fc39484481cc8adb`, physical pp. 3, 7--8.
