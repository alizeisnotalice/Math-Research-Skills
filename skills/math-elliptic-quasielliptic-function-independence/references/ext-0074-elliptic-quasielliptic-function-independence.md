# Function-level elliptic and quasi-elliptic independence

Source reviewed: local arXiv:2504.14041v1, *Schanuel Property for Elliptic
and Quasi-Elliptic Functions*; physical pp.3 and 17, 21--23 of the reviewed
26-page PDF.

Theorem 2.2 (p.3) is a function-level algebraic-independence result. In its
first part, it assumes (\mathbb Q\)-linear independence of the exponential
parameters and of the two lattice periods together with the (u_i). Its CM
part replaces the latter with the stated endomorphism-field conditions and
adds the (f_{u_i}(\alpha_i z)). The proof explicitly closes by Propositions
6.10, 6.12, and 6.13 (pp.17, 21--23).

This is distinct from the paper's almost-everywhere conclusions for values.
It supplies no effective decision procedure for the required linear
independence and no conclusion for an arbitrary specified special point.
