# Biased product exceptional-family cover

Source: *Talagrand’s discrete-convexity conjecture*, SHA-256
`217afc1b8177a802016efa7fe14038b86fa593cb0da2ebbd679e09806422479e`,
Lemma 2.1 and its use in Proposition 3.1 (physical pp.3--5).

Let \(H\subseteq{X\choose r}\), \(0<\rho<1\), \(1\le r\le h\), \(h\ge64\),
and \(|H|\rho^r\le2^{h+1}\).  Lemma 2.1 covers every set containing at
least \(2^{12h}\) members of \(H\) by a family \(G\subseteq2^X\) with
\(c_\rho(G)=\sum_{I\in G}\rho^{|I|}\le3\cdot2^{-h}\).  Its contract uses
large weighted-degree generators, independently sampled ordered edge-pairs,
and residual unions of \(m=2^{12h}\) distinct regular edges.

At the application level Proposition 3.1 assumes \(\mu_q(F)\ge1/2\) and
concludes that \(E_{32}(F)\) is \((q/2^{70})\)-small.  This reference is for
that finite cover/cost mechanism; it does not remove the product-measure,
threshold, or prescribed-row-count hypotheses.
