# Rational RHS perturbation to a simple polytope

Source 1. OpenAI, *Superpolynomial graph diameter of simple polytopes*, physical pp.4–6, SHA-256 `82b49afafdb3f08ff435a41b69a80884969454d02496fe5a3119b08200088548`.

For a bounded rational polyhedron with supplied strict vertex/face inequalities and a finite list of required incidences, choose a sufficiently small rational perturbation of the right-hand side. Verify that each old vertex continues with the same tight rows, all non-tight inequalities remain strict, and the designated face incidences and finite crossing obstruction persist.

This perturbation certificate does not establish the source's scheduler, probability argument, or graph-diameter bound.
