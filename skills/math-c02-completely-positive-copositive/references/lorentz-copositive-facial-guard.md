# Lorentz-cone copositive facial cases

Source: A. Nishijima and coauthors, *Facial structure of copositive and
completely positive cones over a second-order cone*, arXiv:2502.04006v2,
Lemmas 2.2--2.8, Theorems 3.1 and 3.9, and Proposition 3.10 (physical
pp. 5--16).  This is a preprint source statement.

The source treats the Lorentz cone only.  Its interior and boundary case split
uses the special representation `COP(L_n) = S_+^n + R_+ J_n` and the H(V)
witness lemmas.  Keep separately the interior, exposed-boundary, and
non-exposed-boundary outcomes; the boundary branch can include an additional
`R_+ J_n` term.

Do not promote this to a general CP/COP facial classifier or infer the special
representation from a numerical cone test.  A caller must supply the Lorentz
representation, the source hypotheses, and any needed exact witness data.
