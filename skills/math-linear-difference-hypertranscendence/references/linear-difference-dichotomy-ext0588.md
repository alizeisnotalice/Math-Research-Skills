# Linear-difference hypertranscendence dichotomy

For a solution \(f\) in the paper's designated Laurent or meromorphic field \(F_0\), Theorem 1.2 gives the dichotomy \(f\in K\) or \(f\) is hypertranscendental over \(K\). The four allowed settings are exactly the shift cases \(S_0,S_\infty\), q-difference case \(Q\), and Mahler case \(M\). The input must include the corresponding \(K,F_0,\rho,\partial\), the order and coefficients of the linear \(\rho\)-equation, and a proof that the supplied solution belongs to \(F_0\).

The local source is Adamczewski--Dreyfus--Hardouin, *Hypertranscendence and linear difference equations*, arXiv:1910.01874v2, physical pp. 3--7, SHA-256 `7bc2e72501bb5ed3055edfde8958190a0c5c7d2f8f41e4f4771399273023ef01`. Pages 3--4 define all four cases and state Theorem 1.2. Pages 5--6 describe the induction through irreducible and reducible difference-Galois groups. This source does not make a general recurrence solver a hypertranscendence oracle.
