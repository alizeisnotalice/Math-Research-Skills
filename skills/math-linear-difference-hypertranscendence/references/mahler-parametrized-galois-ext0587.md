# Mahler parametrized-difference-Galois gate

For \(p\ge2\), put \(K=\bigcup_{j\ge1}\mathbb C(z^{1/j})\) with \(\phi(f(z))=f(z^p)\). Given a nonzero Laurent-series solution of an order-\(n\) Mahler equation, the cited theorem applies when its ordinary difference-Galois group over \(K\) contains \(\mathrm{SL}_n(\mathbb C)\) and the quotient of end coefficients is a monomial. It then gives algebraic independence over \(\mathbb C(z)\) of \(f(z),f(z^p),\ldots,f(z^{p^{n-1}})\) and all their derivatives.

The local arXiv source is Dreyfus--Hardouin--Roques, *Hypertranscendence of solutions of Mahler equations*, arXiv:1507.03361v3, physical pp. 3, 17, 23--25, SHA-256 `49bdb43d42d406dab96d49d721e98777c16745336be0fb31e8364236f6a591cf`. The published identity is *J. Eur. Math. Soc.* 20 (2018), 2209--2238, DOI `10.4171/JEMS/810`. The local proof's application step imports a separate ordinary-Galois-group theorem, so an input matrix alone is insufficient.
