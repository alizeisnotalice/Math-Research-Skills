[PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/paper.pdf) · [author README](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/README.md) · [LaTeX source](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/The-logarithmic-exponent-of-finite-Fourier-transforms-November-24-2026/build/main.tex)

**Use:** Evaluate placed character-table tensors, then charge binary linear gates. For group order N and exponent e, the field must contain a primitive e-th root of unity and N must be a unit. The documented bounds concern the exact forward and normalized inverse transforms, including batching across J independent instances, with weights in that field: Ω(N√log N) and O_ε(N(log N)^{1/2+ε}); both scale by J in the admissible batch model. Source: `geometry.tex`, `quotient.tex`, `repair.tex`, `kernel.tex`. **Lean scope:** transform upper/lower and batch results; formal polynomial-multiplication upper bound also covered, but its claimed lower bound is not. Do not replace circuit operations with bit-complexity or Fourier support sizes.

**Use:** Check formal polynomial identities, allowed arithmetic gates and field (including finite fields). [PDF](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/An-exponential-bound-for-sunflowers-November-24-2026/paper.pdf) · [author README](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/An-exponential-bound-for-sunflowers-November-24-2026/README.md) · [LaTeX source](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/An-exponential-bound-for-sunflowers-November-24-2026/build/main.tex)

**Use:** Reduce a set system to transversals/words and use a coherent shared-label test. Distinguish a sunflower (all pairwise intersections coincide) from mere pairwise disjointness. Read `words.tex`, `filter.tex`, and `recurrence.tex`; any new transfer needs a genuine event-preserving transversal reduction. **Lean scope:** for each fixed r≥3, some constant C_r bounds r-sunflower-free uniform families by C_r^k, independent of ground-set size. The manuscript's quantitative log B_r dependence and nonuniform formulation are not the same Lean statement. The circuit-like gadget in the proof uses positive characteristic; the final combinatorial assertion itself does not assume a field.
