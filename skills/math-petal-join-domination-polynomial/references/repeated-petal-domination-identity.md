# Source note: repeated-petal joins

Source: *The largest domination roots of friendship graphs and of joins with repeated petals*, physical pp. 3–4 of 11.  Local SHA-256: `e0e58ebd36a299e344320071c0ae536a74ee7ca743320ecb480ba8f4fa544932`.

The source gives the domination-polynomial reduction for a clique joined to repeated copies of a fixed graph and then transforms the resulting root equation.  This asset retains the explicit finite coefficient identity only and checks it independently by exhaustive enumeration.  It excludes every root-location and limiting-radius conclusion.

Lemma 1 uses a nonempty petal and at least one copy. If the outer graph is empty, the join is the center clique. Its dominating sets are the nonempty subsets of the clique. The checker uses this separate case for zero copies and empty petals.
