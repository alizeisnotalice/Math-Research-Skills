# Rational-parameter density gate

Franc, Heisz and Nardone, arXiv:2412.02523v1, prove on physical pp. 5--8 that
for normalized rational parameters of \({}_nF_{n-1}\), eventual \(p\)-adic boundedness
is equivalent to numerator majorization in the unit class of \(p\) modulo the common
denominator \(N\). Thus the density is \(|B|/\varphi(N)\), with \(B\) obtained by
checking the finite residue orbit. The proof uses Kummer's carry identity and periodic
base-\(p\) digits of rationals.

The caller must retain the source's exclusions: every parameter is normalized in
\((0,1)\), denominator parameters avoid nonpositive integers, and the finite set of
bad primes and numerator/denominator coincidences is not part of the density statement.
Physical pp. 9--15 concern quadratic parameters; their reverse implication and exact
density invoke Conjecture 4.4 and are not admitted by this skill.
