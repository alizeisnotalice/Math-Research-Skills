# Generalized-Mahler purity filtration

The source is arXiv:2512.10661v1, 45 physical pages. Its abstract and the
Hahn-series construction on physical pp. 1--2 and 8--11 define the relevant
objects. The theorem analyzes generalized Mahler-series solutions through a
three-level arithmetic-growth filtration and establishes purity propagation in
the admitted higher layers.

This source note retains the theorem only for a caller that has already proved
the exact Hahn/Puiseux decomposition and the required arithmetic height gap.
It leaves the low-growth exceptional layers outside the route. It gives no
general algorithm for decomposing an arbitrary Mahler equation and no direct
statement about Euler's constant or E-functions.
