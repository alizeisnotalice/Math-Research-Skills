# Conditional local-parameter normalization for imaginary-quadratic modularity

**Source.** *Modularity of Elliptic Curves over Imaginary Quadratic Fields*,
pp. 1–2, 15, 28, [pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Modularity-of-elliptic-curves-over-imaginary-quadratic-fields-October-4-2026/paper.pdf).

**Input.** The specialized lifting interface requires a two-dimensional
representation at \(\ell\ne5\) with determinant \(\epsilon_\ell^{-1}\),
labelled Hodge--Tate weights \(0,1\), decomposed genericity, absolute
irreducibility over \(K(\zeta_\ell)\), an initial cuspidal realization, and
every stated local crystalline/ordinary alternative above \(\ell\).

**Procedure.** Verify those hypotheses verbatim and retain the normalization
\[
\iota\,\mathrm{WD}(H^1_{\acute et}(E_{\overline K},\mathbf Q_\ell)|_{G_{K_v}})^{F\text{-}ss}
\simeq\operatorname{rec}_{K_v}(\pi_v\otimes|\det|_v^{-1/2})
\]
for \(v\nmid\ell\). For CM curves use the cited automorphic-induction or
normalized isobaric-sum endpoint, separating potentially-good and
potentially-multiplicative reduction.

**Output.** Under these hypotheses the source gives normalized automorphic
realization and matching finite Weil--Deligne parameters.

**Boundary.** It is not a standalone modularity checker and does not establish
the geometric prime switch or its local hypotheses.
