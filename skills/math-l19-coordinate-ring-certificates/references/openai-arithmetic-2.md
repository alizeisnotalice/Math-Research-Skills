# Exact Jordan strata after determinant normalization

**Source.** *Integral Points on Character Varieties of Curves*, pp. 23–25,
[pinned PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/preprints/Integral-points-on-character-varieties-of-curves-September-25-2026/paper.pdf).

**Input.** A reduced conjugation-invariant closure \(Z\) in a framed
\(\mathrm{GL}_r\) representation variety, dense integral points, prescribed
peripheral characteristic polynomials, and a desired Jordan partition whose
column lengths are \(c_{\lambda,j}\). The source's scalar-twist invariance and
fixed extension containing the required \(r\)-th roots of integral units are
also required.

**Procedure.** Impose
\[
\operatorname{rk}(A-\lambda I)^k\le r-\sum_{j\le k}c_{\lambda,j}.
\]
Intersect with the \(\mathrm{SL}_r\) locus. The scalar-twist map is the base
change of the finite étale \(r\)-power map, preserving density. Delete the
finite union where a required positive rank is smaller. Its affine character
quotient is the exact-Jordan open locus.

**Output.** Ambient integral character points are dense in every exact
boundary-class stratum, including nonregular classes.

**Boundary.** This controls generic-fibre characters only; it neither fixes
Jordan type at finite primes nor constructs integral representatives of
semisimplifications.
