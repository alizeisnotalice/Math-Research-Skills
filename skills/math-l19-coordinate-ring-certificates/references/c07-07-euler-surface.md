# Quartic rational Diophantine quadruple source model

Andrašek--Kazalicki--Vlah, *Quartic Rational Diophantine Quadruples and the Euler Surface*, is published in *The Ramanujan Journal* 70 (2026), Article 62, DOI `10.1007/s11139-026-01439-w`.  The local arXiv:2604.19140v1 has SHA-256 `8559c461eb243d6252160bf3e54f4fd1bf7420ec790d196b8e532eb25bbefb14`, 12 pages, local pp. 1--3.  The source defines a quartic rational quadruple as pairwise distinct nonzero rationals \(a_i\) for which every \(a_ia_j+1\) is a fourth power; it states that a suitable Zariski-open subset of
\[
 X^4+Y^4=Z^4+W^4
\]
maps to such quadruples.  The reusable finite operation is only an exact certificate of a supplied quadruple and supplied fourth roots.  Producing rational Euler-surface points, excluding the exceptional set, and deriving the parametrized family require the source construction and are not inferred from six finite fourth-power checks.
