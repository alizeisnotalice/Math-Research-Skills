# W14-004 — Chernova–Foss–Kim, whole-distribution polling example

**Reading:** Full paper, PDF pp. 1–6. Local fulltext: `data/polling_distribution2012.pdf`; duplicate local copy `data/chernova_foss_kim12_distribution.pdf` is the same work. Identity checked against arXiv:1205.6234v1.

The paper gives a two-queue single-server adaptive polling example, not the three-queue model of W14-002. Queue 2 has exhaustive service; queue 1 receives an adaptive limited rule; arrivals are Poisson and travel times exponential under the paper's stated model. Theorem 1, PDF p. 4, characterizes positive recurrence of an embedded chain by \(r>\lambda_1\). The expression for \(r\) includes \(B_1^*(\lambda_2)=E[e^{-\lambda_2\sigma_1}]\), a Laplace-transform value of the service law. Therefore, in this example the stability criterion depends on more than any fixed finite set of power moments in general.

**Proof route:** compute the embedded transition probabilities using \(B_1^*(\lambda_2)\), then apply a Foster/Lyapunov drift criterion; necessity is obtained by coupling with a random walk of **nonnegative** drift, which includes the critical zero-drift boundary. Thus equality is excluded from positive recurrence as well. The PDF text extraction mangles equation (3), so this card records the theorem's structure and transform dependence but does not reproduce the garbled algebra. The result demonstrates a possible failure of first-moment fluid criteria; it does not establish the conjectured full-distribution dependence for W14-002.
