# W14-015 — Singh–Stolyar, unscaled queue differentials

**Reading:** Full paper, PDF pp. 1–9. Local fulltext: `data/singh_stolyar15_differentials.pdf`; the duplicate `stolyar_switch_differentials.pdf` has the same paper/hash. Identity checked against arXiv:1502.03793v1.

For a generalized switch with discrete time, finite service decisions/outcomes, bounded arrivals and service, and arrival densities bounded above and below on a rectangle with \(A_{\max}>S_{\max}\), the paper studies MaxWeight as the limiting arrival-rate vector approaches a maximal boundary point. Stability is interpreted as positive Harris recurrence for each prelimit Markov chain.

In the CRP case, Lemmas 9–13 establish tightness of steady-state queue deviations; Theorem 10 gives positive Harris recurrence and finite moments for each prelimit chain; Theorem 21 proves that the stationary queue-differential distributions converge weakly to the unique stationary law of an explicitly defined positive-Harris Markov chain. Its proof first shows every weak limit of the prelimit stationary laws is stationary for the limit chain (Lemma 20), then proves positive recurrence/uniqueness (Lemmas 15–19). Section 10 extends the queue-differential process to non-CRP normal cones, with an additional distance-to-relative-boundary condition (Lemma 22).

**Theorem 2:** under these hypotheses the steady-state service process is asymptotically smooth. This is a path/Markov-chain limit for queue differentials, not a full scaled queue-length joint distribution. The proof uses the projected queue dynamics and stationary tightness; no transform uniqueness claim is made.
