## Branching property and embedded process

Resing studies cyclic single-server polling systems with Poisson arrivals and general service-time distributions, observed when the server reaches a fixed queue. Property 1 in Section 2 requires each customer present at a visit to be independently and identically replaced by a random vector of effective descendants by the next visit epoch. Exhaustive and gated service, including binomial-exhaustive and binomial-gated variants, satisfy this property; 1-limited service and Bernoulli service do not in general. The branching representation therefore cannot be transferred to the 1-limited model in A006 without another argument.

Theorem 3 (physical PDF p. 9; printed §4) shows that with switch-over times the vector of queue lengths at successive visits to queue 1 is a multitype branching process with immigration in every state. Its offspring PGFs are obtained by composing the queue-specific effective-replacement PGFs $h_i$; the immigration PGF is the product of the arrival/switch-over contributions (Eq. (18), physical pp. 9–10). Theorem 4 (physical PDF pp. 11–12) treats zero switch-over times: immigration occurs only at state zero, with PGF

$$g(s)=\sum_j(\lambda_j/\lambda) f^{(j)}(s)$$

(Eq. (21)). The distinction changes the stationary PGF formula and must be retained.

## Ergodicity, stationary PGFs, and limitations

Section 3 recalls Quine's multitype-branching results. For immigration in every state, Theorem 1 (physical PDF p. 7) assumes a primitive mean offspring matrix with spectral radius below one and an irreducible, aperiodic chain; a stationary distribution exists iff the immigrant population satisfies the finite logarithmic-moment condition in the theorem. When it exists, its PGF is the infinite product over iterated offspring PGFs. Theorem 2 derives the corresponding state-zero immigration criterion by a recurrence-time argument and gives its PGF representation.

Theorem 5 (physical PDF p. 14) gives the polling stability criterion for Bernoulli-type disciplines: the Perron–Frobenius eigenvalue $\rho(M)$ of the offspring mean matrix is below one iff total load $\rho=\sum_i\lambda_i\beta_i<1$. The proof uses the offspring mean matrix and subinvariance lemmas, and treats the exhaustive special case where irreducibility may fail. Section 5 states the log-moment immigration condition and relates it to switch-over moments; Section 6 differentiates PGFs for moment formulas. The results concern joint queue lengths at the specified polling epochs, not arbitrary-time queue lengths or waiting times, and do not handle disciplines outside Property 1 by the same branching argument.
