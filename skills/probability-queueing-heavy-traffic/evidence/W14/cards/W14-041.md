## Main stability statements

The model is constrained to a convex polyhedral cone \(G\) with face-specific directions \(d_i\). Conditions 2.1 and 2.4 require a well-defined Lipschitz Skorokhod map for the constraint data. Condition 2.7 gives the coefficient and jump-growth assumptions for strong existence/Markov dynamics; the paper states them explicitly and they must be checked before applying its theorem.

For the reflected Lévy special case, Theorem 2.11 (physical PDF p. 7) uses the mean input relative to \(C=\operatorname{cone}\{-d_i\}\): if \(EX_1\in C^c\), then \(Z_t/t\to\gamma\in G\setminus\{0\}\) almost surely; if \(EX_1\in C^\circ\), the process has a compact target set with uniformly finite expected hitting times from bounded initial states.

For the state-dependent jump-diffusion, the adjusted drift is

\[
\widetilde\beta=\mathcal L\,\mathrm{id}=\beta+\int h'(\delta(\cdot,z))F(dz).
\]

Condition 2.12 requires its range to lie in \(\bigcup_{k\in\mathbb N} kC_1\) for a compact \(C_1\subset C^\circ\). Under Conditions 2.1, 2.4, 2.7 and 2.12, Theorem 2.13 (physical p. 7) gives a compact set \(A\) such that every compact set of initial states has uniformly finite expected hitting time of \(A\). Theorem 2.16 gives tight transition laws; Corollary 2.17 then gives at least one invariant measure. Positive Harris recurrence needs the additional communication Condition 2.18 and Theorem 2.19; uniqueness of the invariant probability further needs Condition 2.20, as stated in Theorem 2.21 (physical p. 8). Thus finite return, existence, positive Harris recurrence and uniqueness are separate conclusions with separate hypotheses.

## Proof route and transfer limits

Section 3 samples the process at stopping times and builds drift estimates for a Lyapunov function. Section 4 constructs a smooth, approximately homogeneous Lyapunov function from the constrained ODE, splits large and small jumps, and uses optional stopping to derive finite expected hitting times. Tightness and Feller arguments yield invariant-measure existence; communication and resolvent absolute-continuity assumptions supply the stronger recurrence/uniqueness conclusions.

Use this paper as a method source for constrained jump processes. It is not an SRBM theorem or a queueing-network stability criterion unless the queue's state process and drift/jump data are shown to satisfy its exact assumptions.
