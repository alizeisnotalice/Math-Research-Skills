# Positive example: a permitted polling branching interface

For a cyclic, single-server, infinite-buffer polling model, first verify Resing's independent-offspring Property 1 and fix the visit epoch. Under those gates, the accepted W14-020 use card（本机附件：`cards/uses/W14-020.md`） admits the multitype branching representation and distinguishes positive-switch-over immigration (every state) from the zero-switch-over state-zero mechanism. The representation alone does not establish a stationary law; the card retains the imported Quine hypotheses.

A concrete two-type PGF-composition diagnostic is in the copied R06 script（本机附件：`examples/evidence/r06_batch03_next8_local_checks.py`） and its actual output（本机附件：`examples/evidence/r06_batch03_next8_local_checks.result.json`）. SymPy differentiates one specified pair of composed PGFs and returns offspring matrix `[[3/25, 1/25], [1/10, 1/5]]`. This is an exact check of that algebraic instance only; the theorem and its hypotheses come from W14-020, and the diagnostic does not establish subcriticality, recurrence, or a general queueing result.
