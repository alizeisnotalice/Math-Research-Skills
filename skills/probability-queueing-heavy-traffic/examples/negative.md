# Negative example: invalid upgrades of queueing evidence

Avoid these moves:

- **Invalid:** fit an arbitrary analytic solution of the B054 functional equation and call it a probability transform. **Why:** holomorphic, bounded and continuous does not imply normalization, positivity, support, complete monotonicity, or a valid boundary-measure representation.
- **Invalid:** call the literal Eq. (8) analytic-class uniqueness claim proved or use scalar rescaling as a counterexample to probability-class uniqueness. **Why:** Eq. (7) is homogeneous and Eq. (8) lacks $L(0)=1$, so the literal class has the scaling obstruction; the queue-derived probability class is normalized, and its uniqueness remains a separate open interface.
- **Invalid:** cite the $2n$-exponential B054 candidate as an unconditional theorem. **Why:** Theorem 4 identifies the heavy-traffic law only under a consistent uniqueness premise and symmetric variance; the printed analytic-class condition omits normalization.
- **Invalid:** infer the A007 renewal limit from the Poisson formula and simulation tables. **Why:** the paper explicitly leaves general renewal arrivals as Conjecture 4.2; its Poisson proof uses Poisson structure.
- **Invalid:** use the two-queue Chernova–Foss–Kim example as the exact stability region for the A008 three-queue model. **Why:** the routing/adaptive mechanism differs; A008 remains open for $l_2>1$.
- **Invalid:** promote coordinatewise weak convergence of G/G/m workloads to total-variation convergence. **Why:** Foss's A011 statement is exactly the missing upgrade under stationary ergodicity.
- **Invalid:** apply Dai–Harrison Proposition 9 as $2\Gamma_{jk}=R_{jk}\gamma_j+R_{kj}\gamma_k$ for arbitrary SRBM data. **Why:** for their standard SRBM normalization, Eq. (27) is $2\Gamma_{jk}=R_{kj}+R_{jk}$ for $j\ne k$, with $\gamma=-R^{-1}\mu>0$ appearing in the exponential density $C\exp(-2\gamma\cdot z)$; the displayed criterion is not the unnormalized general-data formula.
- **Invalid:** call the B157 result undecidability for positive-service, all-infinite-buffer networks or replace its deterministic bounded-trajectory stability with positive Harris recurrence. **Why:** that extension is a conjecture in the source and the definitions differ.
