# Fixed-band Mellin--Plancherel transfer

Source: Leonard van Hemert, *November addendum to Exact Zero Detection and Compact Arithmetic Realizations for the Riemann Xi Function*, author addendum dated 24 November 2026, 3 physical pages; reviewed physical pp. 1--2.  Local source identity: SHA-256 `29f905db5fd468064d3b6d2e807ce02fdcb867c5566bb411520a6996dc91c296`.

The reusable part of Theorem 1 is a Mellin--Plancherel transfer.  Under the source's fixed \(q,H,h\) definitions, suppose the projected source has a Mellin transform \(B(z)\) that continues to the line \(\Re z=b\), represents the original source there in the distributional sense, and satisfies \(B(b+i\,\cdot)\in L^2(\mathbb R;E)\).  The source moves the Mellin contour and applies Hilbert-space Plancherel to obtain its weighted all-scale energy bound; restricting to endpoints through \(Y\) gives the stated \(O(Y^{2b})\) bound.

## Boundary

This transfer does not establish the continuation or the \(L^2\) boundary condition.  In the addendum those arise from the separate premise \(P(a)\), a one-sided interval lemma, and imported fixed-band identities.  Thus do not use this reference to infer a zero-free half-plane, a bound for an arbitrary source, or RH.
