# Smooth integers and the zeta-zero correction

Gorodetsky, *Smooth integers and de Bruijn's approximation \(\Lambda\)*, *Proc. Roy. Soc. Edinburgh A* 155 (2025), 792--820, DOI `10.1017/prm.2023.115`; local arXiv:2212.01949v3, SHA-256 `3d068572a120c81241351553c529da971e0aa0474f0a1b0d3da3ff62f5db37bf`, 22 pages, local pp. 1--3.

With \(u=\log x/\log y\), \(\Psi(x,y)\) the smooth-number count and \(\Lambda(x,y)=x\lambda_y(u)\), the source's RH-conditional correction uses \(\beta=1-\xi(u)/\log y\) and a factor \(G(\beta,y)\):
\[
 \Psi(x,y)=\Lambda(x,y)G(\beta,y)
 \left(1+O_\varepsilon\!\left(\frac{\log(u+1)}{y\log y}
 (|\psi(y)-y|+y^{1/2})\right)\right)
\]
in its stated range.  The cited conclusion also needs RH (and LI for the bias claim).  Gamma occurs in the classical lower-order de Bruijn term only; this does not give an arithmetic relation for Euler's constant.  A numerical implementation needs certified zeta-zero and prime-power corrections, which this reference does not supply.
