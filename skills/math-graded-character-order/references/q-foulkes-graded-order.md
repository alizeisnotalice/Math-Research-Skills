# Degree-law reduction

For a fixed irreducible type, Paper II defines the two degree laws from finite coefficient arrays of common total \(d_\lambda\). Its Proposition 2.2 on physical p. 4 identifies the coefficient of the divided difference by \(1-q\) with the difference of the two cumulative distribution functions. Proposition 3.3 on physical p. 5 gives the exact deficiency of ordered matching.

The script implements that finite statement. It accepts arrays supplied by the caller. It does not certify that arrays arise from the q-Foulkes modules or establish the paper's asymptotic probability bound.
