# Wave 4 PA3: canonical normalization with varying tests

## The canonical massive continuum limit of the two-dimensional O(3) model

Source SHA-256 `e75bfb353c2d3374503bbb899eb01dc21c94ed49fede6b3eb48f7ffce64527b2`, physical pp. 116--117, equations (14.22)--(14.25) and Lemma 14.7.  For a translation-invariant hierarchy with positive susceptibility `m` and positive second-moment length `\ell`, the unique positive normalization fixing both quantities is
\[
 \phi'(f)=\frac1{\ell\sqrt m}\,\phi\bigl(f(\cdot/\ell)\bigr).
\]
Indeed its relative two-point measure transforms as `\nu'=b^2\ell^2(D_{1/\ell})_*\nu`, so `m'=b^2\ell^2m` and `\ell'=\ell/\ell`; hence `\ell=\ell` and `b=(\ell\sqrt m)^{-1}` are forced.  At cutoff the normalized field is exactly
\[
 \frac1{\xi_\beta\sqrt{\chi_\beta}}\sum_x f(U_Px/\xi_\beta)\,q_x.
\]
The source then uses bounded scale multipliers, uniformly compact test supports, moment bounds, and a finite-net argument to transfer convergence to the varying dilated tests.

**Scope.** Scoped reference. Positivity and finiteness of both normalization quantities, convergence of their numerators and denominators, uniform moment/box bounds, and the stated compact-support test topology are required.  It neither constructs the O(3) hierarchy nor gives convergence for arbitrary moving tests.
