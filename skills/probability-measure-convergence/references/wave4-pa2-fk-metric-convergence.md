# Scoped FK metric and conformal convergence interfaces

For *Metric-measure limits of subcritical FK and spanning-tree planar maps* (SHA-256 `1dd659698d3a3fde21299449b3b293406538c97ccf711b334e74cb7dd138e9d0`, pp. 8--10, Lemmas 2.1--2.5), conditional iid distance-array convergence plus the covering-radius condition gives GHP convergence. The empirical step uses independent auxiliary centres and `P(d_P(mu_n,muhat_{n,m})>2r+t)<=P(R_{n,k}>r)+(1/(2t))*sqrt(k/m)`. Deterministic normalization removes only deterministic positive diameter multipliers; full support is required for measure-preserving sample completion.

For *Canonical conformal limits of subcritical FK planar maps* (SHA-256 `e7dab39caf7781761af083b5845f2ab7c6b8906eb127e098ed1b3a5a7143c320`, pp. 14--18, Lemmas 4.1, 4.6, Theorem 4.7), conditional on the local following/lifting tests, a finite cubic skeleton has a subdivided lift and face extensions to `H_n` with `sup_x rho(H_n(x),P_n(x))->0`. It does not establish local joining, conformal convergence, or an FK scaling limit.
