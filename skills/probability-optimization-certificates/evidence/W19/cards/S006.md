## Results depend on the constraint space

For finitely many decision variables and infinitely many indexed constraints, the dual depends on the specified vector space of allowed perturbations. The restricted space in the paper is `(U=span(a_1,…,a_n,b))`; the larger-space extensions are not automatic. Its Fourier–Motzkin machinery yields Theorem 3.4, `(OV=max{S,L})`, and Theorem 3.6: zero duality gap for the stated primal/dual pair iff the primal is feasible and `(S≥L)`. These formulas separate primal feasibility from the asymptotic boundary obstruction.

In §4, Theorem 4.1 constructs a dual optimizer in the restricted coefficient space for feasible bounded SILPs. Theorem 4.3 gives local exact pricing: for a perturbation `d∈U` with `b+d` feasible, there is an `ε_hat>0` such that the exact-pricing identity holds for every `0≤ε≤ε_hat`; this is not a claim for arbitrary large or infeasible perturbations. The extension in §5 is not automatic: examples show strong duality or pricing can fail in larger spaces. Theorems 5.5 and 5.7 identify conditions for extensions to `(Y_hat)`, including an FM-boundedness hypothesis for a sufficient strong-duality result. Theorem 5.12 gives two conditions DP.1/DP.2 for the pricing property; Theorem 5.15 supplies sufficient conditions for dual attainment. Example 5.16 allows pricing even without a primal optimizer, but through a limiting functional that need not be representable by a finitely additive measure/vector.

## Proof mechanism and transfer boundary

The proof extracts a convergent subsequence of finite-support price vectors along a sequence of finite projections, defines a positive linear functional by the limit on the subspace where evaluations converge, and pulls it back through the adjoint map. The rendered PDF p. 12 verifies the functional construction and Theorem 4.3 statement. In larger spaces, the resulting dual object can be singular or purely finitely additive; it must not be silently represented as an ordinary finite-support multiplier.

**Use:** state the coefficient/perturbation space before invoking a semi-infinite dual or sensitivity formula, and verify the exact feasibility, boundedness, FM-boundedness, and attainment conditions used. This source gives no automatic finite-support dual certificate and no stochastic uniformity or online policy theorem.
