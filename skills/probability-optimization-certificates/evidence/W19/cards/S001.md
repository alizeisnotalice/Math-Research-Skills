# S001 — Kager: finite-dimensional Farkas alternative

**Reader:** W13 (this W19 continuation) · **status:** complete 2-page paper read · **review:** pending

Wouter Kager, “A short simple proof of closedness of convex cones and Farkas’ lemma,” arXiv:2208.11678v2. The entire two-page local PDF was read and its identity rechecked against the primary arXiv record.

For a finite matrix (A), the finitely generated cone \(K=\{Ax:x\ge0\}\) is closed. The paper proves this by taking, for a convergent sequence of cone points, coefficient representations with minimal support; compactness after normalization yields a limit representation, while a strict reduction in support would contradict minimality. Projection onto a closed convex cone then gives the alternative: exactly one of

1. (b\in K\), or
2. there is (y) with \(A^Ty\ge0\) and \(b^Ty<0\).

The two cases are exclusive by multiplication. This is a finite-dimensional certificate with a strict separating margin. The proof depends on finite generation; it is not a license to assume arbitrary linear images or conic sums are closed. The paper explicitly illustrates failures beyond its finitely generated setting.

**Use:** validate a finite LP infeasibility certificate or a Farkas-based branch of a certificate checker. This does not establish an infinite-dimensional duality theorem or a uniform probability-model bound.
