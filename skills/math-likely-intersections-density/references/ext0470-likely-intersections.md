# Likely intersections in definable quotient spaces

Eterović and Scanlon, *Likely intersections*, local `arXiv:2211.10592v2`, physical pp. 1--3 and 12--19.

Theorem 3.4 is formulated for a definable analytic map from a complex algebraic variety to a definable complex quotient space. Under the source's Ax--Schanuel property and a well-parameterised family of special subvarieties, it gives the stated density conclusion for persistent likely intersections. Section 4 supplies specialisations to arithmetic quotients.

Supply the quotient presentation, o-minimal structure and fundamental set, the map, the source-form Ax--Schanuel witnesses, Hodge-genericity and special-family parameters. The theorem is a conditional density theorem and gives neither an effective intersection computation nor a finiteness theorem.
