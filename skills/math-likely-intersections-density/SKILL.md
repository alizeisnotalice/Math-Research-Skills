# Likely-intersection density

Use this source route only after a definable complex quotient space, its quotient presentation, a definable analytic map and a well-parameterised weakly special family have been fixed. Record the exact notion of persistent likelihood and the Ax--Schanuel witnesses required by the source.

The result gives Euclidean density of the stated likely intersections in an irreducible Hodge-generic setting. It does not establish Zilber--Pink finiteness, decide definability, or produce special subvarieties from finite data.

Read [the quotient-space conditions](references/ext0470-likely-intersections.md).
