## Model and exact claims

Let π be uniform on (S_N), and let ℓ_N be the length of its longest increasing subsequence. The limiting distribution (F_2) is the GUE Tracy–Widom law, written in the paper as
\[
F_2(t)=\exp\!\left[-\int_t^\infty (x-t)u(x)^2\,dx\right],
\qquad u''=2u^3+xu,\quad u(x)\sim-\operatorname{Ai}(x)\ (x\to\infty).
\]

- **Theorem 1.1, PDF p. 2:** ((\ell_N-2\sqrt N)/N^{1/6}\Rightarrow F_2).
- **Theorem 1.2, PDF p. 3:** every positive integer moment of the normalized variable converges. In particular, the limiting mean correction is (int t\,dF_2(t)), and (N^{-1/3}\operatorname{Var}(\ell_N)\to int t^2dF_2-(\int tdF_2)^2).

The authors also prove the analogous limits for the Poissonized longest chain in a unit-intensity planar Poisson process in a (sqrt\lambda\)-by-(sqrt\lambda) square. This is a solvable directed increasing-subsequence/Poisson-LPP benchmark, not a theorem for undirected edge FPP.

## Proof chain and dependencies

1. Poissonize the permutation count: (φ_n(\lambda)=e^{-\lambda}D_{n-1}(e^{2\sqrt\lambda\cos\theta})), a Toeplitz determinant (1.11)–(1.14), using Gessel’s identity and Robinson–Schensted/tableaux combinatorics.
2. Express the determinant through orthogonal-polynomial leading coefficients and a (2\times2) Riemann–Hilbert problem. A varying-equilibrium measure and (g)-function change regime at (γ=2\sqrt\lambda/(k+1)=1); §§5–6 construct the regular and critical local parametrices, with Painlevé II at the transition.
3. The coefficient asymptotics are summed in §7 to obtain Poissonized distribution asymptotics and upper/lower tail bounds. §8 proves the de-Poissonization inequalities; §9 transfers the distributional limit and tail integrability to the fixed-(N) model, which yields moment convergence.
4. Appendix A supplies a second proof of the Poissonized Toeplitz determinant identity and a heuristic for the (2\sqrt N) centering; the theorem proof is in §§2–9, not merely this heuristic.

## Scope and transfer limits

The constants (2) and (N^{1/6}), the (F_2) limit, and moment convergence are established for uniform permutations (and the stated Poissonized chain). No coupling or comparison here proves the same law or exponent for ordinary undirected FPP, generic directed LPP, or Eden growth. Treat as an exact-solvable reference for C094, not as a transfer theorem.
