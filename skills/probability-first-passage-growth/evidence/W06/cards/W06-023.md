## Model and hypotheses

Undirected edge FPP on (ℤ^2), with a translation-ergodic environment and a deterministic compact convex limit shape (B). The paper uses either:

- **A1:** product law with common nonnegative edge-weight distribution satisfying (E[(\min_{i=1}^4\omega_{e_i})^2]<\infty) for the four edges incident to the origin and (P(\omega_e=0)<p_c=1/2); or
- **A2:** translation ergodicity, all square-lattice symmetries, (E[\omega_e^{2+\epsilon}]<\infty) for some (ε>0), and bounded limit shape.

**A1′** adds a continuous common law; **A2′** adds unique passage times. For A2′ coalescence results additionally require the stated upward finite-energy property. Keep these packages distinct; A1 does not mean A1′.

## Main statements and quantifiers

- **Theorem 1.1 / Corollary 1.2 (local PDF pp. 5–6):** under A1 or A2, differentiability of (∂B) at (v_\theta) gives an infinite geodesic from the origin asymptotically directed in the support-line contact interval (I_\theta); an exposed differentiability point gives exact direction (θ). This is an existence statement for each fixed direction, not a simultaneous all-direction uniqueness theorem.
- **Theorem 1.6 / Corollary 1.7 (pp. 6–7):** under A1′ or A2′ plus uniformly positive curvature, almost surely every deterministic (θ) has an infinite geodesic and every infinite geodesic has a direction. The corollary replaces uniform curvature with exposed differentiability at every boundary point.
- **Theorem 1.10 (local-copy p. 8):** either A1′ alone, or A2′ together with the upward finite-energy property, is assumed. Fix a nonzero deterministic vector (v). On a probability-one event for that fixed (v) (the theorem does not assert a common event simultaneously for every uncountable choice of (v)), there is an environment-dependent sequence of parallel target lines (L_{\alpha_k}(v)), (α_k\uparrow\infty), whose geodesic graphs converge to a graph with no undirected circuits, outdegree one at every vertex, coalescence of every pair of forward paths, and finite backward clusters.
- **Theorem 1.11 (local-copy p. 9):** under the same alternative hypotheses, a fixed differentiability direction gives a family of geodesics from every lattice vertex, directed in (I_\theta), pairwise coalescing, with each vertex lying on only finitely many family members. Exposed differentiability makes their asymptotic direction exactly (θ). ## Proof dependencies

The paper replaces pointwise Busemann convergence by weak limits of joint laws of the edge environment, Busemann increments, and geodesic graphs. It averages target-boundary laws to obtain translation-invariant subsequential measures; the limiting increments are curl-free, so they are reconstructed into an additive Busemann function. Ergodic averages give a random linear slope; the shape theorem identifies it as a supporting functional at the prescribed boundary point. Geodesic-path properties survive the graph limit, and the support-line geometry confines rays to the contact sector. Coalescence is proved by contradiction: positive-probability branching produces repeated “building blocks”; upward finite-energy modifications create a high-weight edge barrier; translation invariance, mass transport, and encounter-point counting contradict a positive density of such blocks. A separate argument rules out infinite backward paths.

The exact map of §§1–7 and Appendix A is in the coverage file. The coalescence proof is long (local PDF §§6.1–6.4, pp. 28–43); it is not reduced to a one-line mass-transport claim.

## Transfer limits

All directional, ordering, and barrier arguments are planar. The theorem concerns geodesics or subsequential limiting geodesic graphs, not general-dimensional FPP, and it does not prove Busemann limits in every direction. LPP queueing results do not replace its undirected edge-FPP hypotheses.
