## Model and theorem statements

For independent geometric **vertex** weights on the first quadrant, (P(w(i,j)=k)=(1-q)q^k), (k\in\mathbb N\), (0<q<1), let (G(M,N)) be the maximum up/right path sum from ((1,1)) to ((M,N)). For (γ\ge1), **Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 5)** gives
\[
N^{-1}E G([\gamma N],N)\longrightarrow
\omega(\gamma,q)=\frac{(1+\sqrt{q\gamma})^2}{1-q}-1,
\]
and lower/upper deviations have positive rate functions at speeds (N^2) and (N), respectively. **Theorem 1.2 (p. 6)** gives
\[
\frac{G([\gamma N],N)-N\omega(\gamma,q)}{\sigma(\gamma,q)N^{1/3}}\Rightarrow F_2,
\quad
\sigma=q^{1/6}\gamma^{-1/6}(1-q)^{-1}(\sqrt\gamma+\sqrt q)^{2/3}(1+\sqrt{q\gamma})^{2/3}.
\]
The paper explicitly says it does **not** prove convergence of moments of this normalized variable (Remark 2.5, PDF p. 21).

For independent exponential(1) vertex weights, (H(M,N)) is the analogous up/right maximum. **Theorem 1.6 (PDF pp. 8–10)** gives shape ((1+\sqrt\gamma)^2), lower deviations at speed (N^2), upper deviations at speed (N), and the (F_2) limit at scale (γ^{-1/6}(1+\sqrt\gamma)^{4/3}N^{1/3}). Its endpoint perturbation version requires (a_N=O(N^{1/3})) and (d_N-(1+1/\sqrt\gamma)a_N=o(N^{1/3})); see the exact centering in (1.22), PDF p. 9. The geometric model also has a growing-Young-diagram/TASEP interpretation; this is still a directed vertex model.

## Proof chain and dependencies

Robinson–Schensted/Young-diagram combinatorics gives a discrete Coulomb-gas formula for the distribution. A large-deviation variational principle identifies the equilibrium measure and the limit shape. Orthogonal-polynomial formulas convert the finite model to a Fredholm determinant with the Meixner kernel; discrete steepest descent at a double critical point yields the Airy kernel and (F_2). The exponential model follows through the geometric-to-exponential/Laguerre limit, with the corresponding Laguerre kernel asymptotics. The (N^2) versus (N) deviation speeds arise from the Coulomb-gas energy and the separate upper-tail estimate. Do not infer moment convergence from weak convergence alone; the author expressly leaves it open here.

## Transfer limits

This is an integrable **directed vertex-LPP** result. It is a useful KPZ/F2 benchmark, not evidence that undirected edge FPP has (1/3) fluctuations or Tracy–Widom limits. It does not imply the C094 archive’s exponent-definition clauses for standard undirected FPP.
