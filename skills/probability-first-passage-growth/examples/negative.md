# Negative example: do not transfer KPZ formulas across models

**Request:** “Johansson proved \(1/3\) fluctuations in directed exponential LPP and Chatterjee proved the KPZ scaling relation, so ordinary undirected exponential FPP has fluctuation exponent \(1/3\), correct?”

**Do not answer yes.** The Johansson result concerns a solvable directed last-passage model. Chatterjee's relation assumes fluctuation and wandering exponents exist in the required strong sense; a relation does not supply either exponent value. Ordinary undirected FPP has no matching theorem in the cited sources. State the model mismatch, the conditional nature of the relation, and the precise open exponent questions.

A second unsafe shortcut is to turn “for each fixed deterministic direction, almost surely” into “almost surely for all directions.” These quantifiers permit a random dense exceptional set. The archived C097 problem asks to prove the former statement for every fixed deterministic direction individually, extending the existing deterministic full-measure set; it does not ask for the latter simultaneous event.
