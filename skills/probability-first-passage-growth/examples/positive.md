# Positive example: verify a critical-FPP transfer boundary

**Request:** “Can the known planar critical-FPP theorem establish \(E T(0,\partial B(n))\asymp\log n\) for critical Bernoulli FPP in dimension three?”

**Good response shape:** Identify the target as \(d=3\), i.i.d. Bernoulli edge weights \(0/1\), with zero probability \(p_c(3)\). Then state that the read Damron–Lam–Wang theorem is for \(d=2\), \(F(0)=p_c=1/2\), and depends on planar duality/RSW and invasion-percolation estimates. Its inverse-quantile criterion is a genuine planar result but does not prove the 3D logarithmic order. Cite the 3D question and report this as a transfer gap, not a solved claim.

If exploring, formulate the exact 3D target and compare the number of annular barriers to the cost of crossing them. Keep numerical or percolation simulations separate from a proof.
