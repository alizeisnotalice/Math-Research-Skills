# Interpolated Apéry sequences and critical L-values

For a named sequence from the source tables, retain its exact binomial interpolation, the modular parametrization and the displayed derivative identity before invoking a congruence or critical-L-value evaluation. Theorems 2.1--2.3 distinguish proven cases from source-specific modular input. In particular, an arbitrary second-order recurrence does not determine a preferred interpolation or a modular form.

The local source is R. Osburn and A. Straub, *Interpolated sequences and critical L-values of modular forms*, arXiv:1806.05207v2, physical pp. 3--11 and 15--20, SHA-256 `edf704b7b6193fb13a8b9cacf1d5fa901a4f2b38ee2ae60f9e7ed8ad339ae181`. It was published as a 2019 Springer chapter, DOI `10.1007/978-3-030-04480-0_14`. The source explicitly labels the unproved or numerical directions in its outlook.
