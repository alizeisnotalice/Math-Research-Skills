# E2-zero and Watson-inversion boundary

Source: `arXiv:2610.30025v1`; local SHA-256
`eb377f7cc6d348a79f69a03ce231c7d2c39c64b2315469042e0e908fb4cc0d90`.

Physical pp. 1--3 state the \(E_2\)-zero parametrisation, count, and cusp
displacements through Watson inversion. The arithmetic statements at a zero
invoke classical transcendence inputs; Proposition 4.1 (p. 5) also uses
Schneider-type reasoning. A call must supply a certified zero, branch/cusp
normalisation, and the inversion hypotheses. Floating-point roots do not
certify these claims.
