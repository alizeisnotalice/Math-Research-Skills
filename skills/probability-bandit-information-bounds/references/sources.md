- **W15-008** Scarlett–Bogunovic–Cevher (2017), full 20-page read; squared-exponential and Matérn minimax lower bounds for simple and cumulative regret under additive Gaussian noise, proved by localized RKHS bump alternatives and Gaussian change of measure.
