# W12-005 — Adamczak–Latała–Meller, Banach-valued Gaussian chaoses

- **Identity:** Radosław Adamczak, Rafał Latała, Rafał Meller, *Moments of Gaussian Chaoses in Banach Spaces*, arXiv:2002.01790v1 (5 February 2020), 38 PDF pages. The local title page and the official arXiv record agree on title, authors, and identifier.
- **Read:** All PDF pp. 1–38, including the decoupling setup, Theorems 2.1–2.15, the entropy argument and proofs in Sections 3–5, appendices, and references. The principal theorem is stated on p. 6; its proof is on pp. 26–28.
- **Role:** Banach-valued chaos moment/tail machinery and a useful comparison boundary for C120; it is not the desired ℓp type-2 estimate for a general tensor family.

## Theorem 2.1 and its hypotheses

Let (F) be a normed space and (A=(a_{i_1,ldots,i_d})) a finite (F)-valued array. For independent standard Gaussian families (g^1,ldots,g^d), Theorem 2.1 (PDF p. 6) bounds the (L_p(F)) norm of the decoupled tetrahedral chaos (sum_{i_1,ldots,i_d}a_{i_1,ldots,i_d}g^1_{i_1}cdots g^d_{i_d}), for every (pge1), below by

\[
C(d)^{-1}\sum_{J\subset[d]}\sum_{P\in\mathcal P(J)}p^{|P|/2}\,|||A|||_P
\]

and above by

\[
C(d)\sum_{(P,P')\in\mathcal P([d])}p^{|P|/2}\,\|A\|_{P'\mid P}.
\]

Here the partition contractions are those defined on PDF pp. 4–5; the constants depend on the chaos order (d), not on the array dimensions. The paper states that reversing the upper estimate for arbitrary Banach spaces is false in general already at order two (Conjecture 2.2 and surrounding discussion, p. 6). It proves two-sided consequences under Gaussian property α+ (PDF pp. 7–9); this property holds for type-2 spaces, and for Banach lattices it is equivalent to finite cotype. In that case the constants acquire a factor controlled by (K^{d-1}). For (L_q), Proposition 2.12 gives (K\le C\sqrt q), and Theorems 2.14–2.15 give the corresponding (L_q)-valued moment bounds.

## Proof route and scope boundary

The lower bound in Theorem 2.1 is proved by Gaussian contraction/conditioning arguments; the upper bound reduces to Gaussian-process suprema (Theorem 2.6) and the entropy estimates developed in Section 3, with the proof completed in Section 5. Theorem 2.10 then passes from homogeneous chaoses to arbitrary Banach-valued Gaussian polynomials through the expected derivatives (\mathbb E\nabla^d f(G)), and gives moment and tail bounds. The (L_q) specialization additionally uses the type-2 estimate (K\lesssim\sqrt q) and the comparison between the random contraction norms and deterministic (L_q) contractions.

The chaos order (d), moment parameter (p), and the Banach-space-valued contractions are distinct objects. These Euclidean/partition-contraction bounds do not identify a general ℓp injective norm or close the low-(p) exponent gap in C120.
