## Exact statements and proof chain

The paper uses the multivariate trace-Wishart vector
$X=(X_1,\ldots,X_d)\sim \mathrm{TW}_{p_1,\ldots,p_d}(\alpha,\Sigma)$,
with positive integer block sizes $p_i$, $p=\sum_i p_i$,
$2\alpha\in\mathbb N\cup(p-1,\infty)$, and $\Sigma$ positive semidefinite.
It is the vector of traces of the diagonal blocks of a Wishart matrix under the paper's normalization. The Laplace transform (4) shows that subvectors have the corresponding marginal trace-Wishart law.

**Conjecture 1 (physical p. 3; not proved).** For
$W\sim\mathrm{Wishart}_p(2\alpha,\Sigma/2)$ with
$2\alpha\in\mathbb N\cup(p-1,\infty)$, $\Sigma$ positive definite, diagonal blocks $W_{ii}$ of sizes $p_i$, nonnegative real exponents $\alpha_i$, and any split $d_1$, the paper conjectures
\[
\mathbb E\prod_{i=1}^d\{\operatorname{tr}(W_{ii})\}^{\alpha_i}
\ge
\mathbb E\prod_{i=1}^{d_1}\{\operatorname{tr}(W_{ii})\}^{\alpha_i}
\mathbb E\prod_{i=d_1+1}^{d}\{\operatorname{tr}(W_{ii})\}^{\alpha_i}.
\]
The integer-exponent tensor/trace reformulation printed immediately below it is likewise part of the conjecture, not a proved determinant-moment statement.

**Theorem 1 (physical p. 3).** For
$X\sim\mathrm{TW}_{p_1,\ldots,p_d}(\alpha,\Sigma)$,
$2\alpha\in\mathbb N\cup(p-1,\infty)$, positive integer $p_i$, positive-semidefinite $\Sigma$, any completely monotone functions
$\phi_i:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$, and any split $d_1\in\{1,\ldots,d-1\}$, provided the expectations exist,
\[
\mathbb E\prod_{i=1}^d\phi_i(X_i)
\ge
\mathbb E\prod_{i=1}^{d_1}\phi_i(X_i)\,
\mathbb E\prod_{i=d_1+1}^{d}\phi_i(X_i).
\]
Proof (physical p. 4): replace $\Sigma$ by its block-diagonal truncation
$\Sigma^*=\operatorname{diag}(\Sigma_{11},\Sigma_{22})$.
Fischer's determinant inequality applied to the trace-Wishart Laplace transforms yields $X\preceq_{Lt}X^*$. The multivariate Laplace-transform order theorem for products of completely monotone functions gives the inequality for $X^*$; its two groups have independent marginals and the same group marginals as $X$, yielding the statement.

**Corollary 1 (physical pp. 5–6).** Theorem 1 implies (a) negative-power trace moments with $q_i\in(-\alpha,0]$, (b)
\[
\mathbb E\prod_i e^{-X_i^{r_i}}\ge
\mathbb E\prod_{i\le d_1}e^{-X_i^{r_i}}\,
\mathbb E\prod_{i>d_1}e^{-X_i^{r_i}}
\quad (r_i\in[0,1]),
\]
and (c) a positive-semidefinite Kronecker-sum trace inequality. Part (c) uses that $t\mapsto\operatorname{tr}e^{-tA}$ is completely monotone for $A\succeq0$ (BMV/Stahl result) and then applies Theorem 1.

**Theorem 2 (physical p. 7).** For two nonnegative random pairs
$(X_1,X_2)$ and $(X_1^*,X_2^*)$ with identical one-dimensional marginals, independent $X_1^*,X_2^*$, and
$(X_1,X_2)\preceq_{Lt}(X_1^*,X_2^*)$, if $f,g$ are Bernstein functions with Lévy triplets $(a_1,0,\mu_1)$, $(a_2,0,\mu_2)$ (zero linear drift), then
\[
\mathbb E[f(X_1)g(X_2)]
\ge\mathbb E[f(X_1^*)]\,\mathbb E[g(X_2^*)].
\]
The proof first expands
$\mathbb E[(1-e^{-sX_1})(1-e^{-tX_2})]$
and uses Laplace-transform order plus equality of marginals to lower-bound it by the independent counterpart; integrate that inequality against the two Bernstein Lévy measures. Corollary 2 applies this to two trace-Wishart coordinates and $f(t)=t^{q_1},g(t)=t^{q_2}$, $q_1,q_2\in[0,1]$. The paper notes that a higher-dimensional extension of Theorem 2 is open in its 2023 text.

## Scope boundary for C205/C206

Neither Theorem 1 nor its corollaries concerns determinants of principal blocks. The paper's Conjecture 1 concerns products of **block traces** and remains explicitly a conjecture in this article. The positive completely-monotone trace-Wishart results and the two-variable Bernstein result do not imply C205 determinant moments or C206 determinant lower-orthant events. Use W12-030 only to identify a neighboring trace problem and to prevent a trace/determinant conflation.
