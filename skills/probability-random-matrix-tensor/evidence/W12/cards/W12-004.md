# W12-004 — Latała, Gaussian chaos moments and tails

- **Identity:** Rafał Latała, *Estimates of Moments and Tails of Gaussian Chaoses*, Ann. Probab. 34 (2006), 2315–2331, DOI 10.1214/009117906000000421, arXiv:math/0505313v2. Local PDF 17 pages.
- **Read:** Full PDF pp. 1–17. The main estimates and entropy setup are on pp. 1–4; the later proof, tail discussion, extensions, and references were also read.
- **Role:** Related ℓ(_2) Gaussian tensor-chaos machinery; not an ℓp type-2 theorem for arbitrary tensor families.

## Exact result

For a finite coefficient array (A=(a_{i_1,\dots,i_r})), the paper first treats the decoupled chaos
\[
S=\sum_{i_1,\dots,i_r}a_{i_1,\dots,i_r}g^{(1)}_{i_1}\cdots g^{(r)}_{i_r},
\]
where the (r) Gaussian vectors are independent. Define (\|A\|_{I_1,\ldots,I_m}) by grouping the array coordinates according to a partition of ([r]) and taking the multilinear Euclidean injective norm across those groups. Theorem 1 (PDF p. 3) gives, for moment order (q\ge2),
\[
\|S\|_q\asymp_r
\sum_{m=1}^r q^{m/2}\sum_{(I_1,\ldots,I_m)\in\mathcal S(m,r)}
\|A\|_{I_1,\ldots,I_m}.
\]
The source states two-sided estimates with constants depending only on chaos order (r). Corollary 1 gives the corresponding tail profile.

The proof program (PDF pp. 2–4) estimates entropy numbers for product Euclidean balls, then controls Gaussian-process suprema. Theorem 2 is an auxiliary random-matrix/process estimate used in this chain.

## Migration boundary

This is a scalar, decoupled-chaos moment theorem with Euclidean contractions and moment parameter (q). C120 concerns a Gaussian series in an ℓp injective-normed tensor space; neither the variable named (p) in Latała’s moment estimate nor the tensor partition norms can be replaced by C120’s geometric (p)-norm without a new reduction.
