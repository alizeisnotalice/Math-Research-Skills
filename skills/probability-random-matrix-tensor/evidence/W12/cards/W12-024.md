# W12-024 — Bekjan–Chen, convex-function martingale survey and endpoint clues

- **Identity:** Turdebek N. Bekjan and Zeqian Chen, *Noncommutative Martingale Inequalities Associated with Convex Functions*, arXiv:1509.05242v1 (2015), 17 pages.
- **Read:** Full text, all 17 PDF pages. Relevant locations: Theorem 3.3 (pp. 9–10), Theorem 5.1 (p. 15), and Remarks 6.1–6.2 (PDF p. 15).
- **Role:** Primary historical source for isolated dossier clues B171 and B172. Its 2015 “open” label is not a proof of current status.

## B171 — weak-type Orlicz split endpoint

Remark 6.1 (PDF p. 15) asks whether, for an Orlicz function Φ satisfying Δ(_2) and an (L_1)-bounded noncommutative martingale (x), there is a decomposition (x=y+z) of martingales such that for every (t>0),
\[
\Phi(\mu_t(S_c(y)))+\Phi(\mu_t(S_r(z)))
\le C t^{-1}\|\Phi(|x|)\|_1.
\]
Here μ(_t) is the generalized singular-value function and (S_c,S_r) are the column/row square functions defined earlier in the paper. The question concerns one decomposition working for all thresholds; pointwise-in-(t) decompositions would not suffice.

## B172 — upper Burkholder Φ-moment inequality across 2

Theorem 5.1 (PDF p. 15) is stated for semifinite tracial von Neumann algebras and an (L_\Phi) martingale. Its full equivalence is proved when (2<p_\Phi\le q_\Phi<\infty), and the diagonal/column/row decomposition form when (1<p_\Phi\le q_\Phi<2). Remark 6.2 (PDF p. 15) asks whether the upper (“≤”) inequality in (5.1) holds under the broader condition (1<p_\Phi\le q_\Phi<\infty), thus highlighting the cross-2 gap.

The 2015 paper cites Dirksen–Ricard for a related Φ-moment square-function upper bound, but that does not identify the exact desired Burkholder expression with both row and column square functions plus diagonal term. Check the formula and exact space before using it as a solution.
