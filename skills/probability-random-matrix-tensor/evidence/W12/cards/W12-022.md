# W12-022 — Bekjan–Chen, Φ-moment noncommutative martingales

- **Identity:** Turdebek N. Bekjan and Zeqian Chen, *Interpolation and Φ-moment inequalities of noncommutative martingales*, arXiv:1002.3670v1 (2010).
- **Read:** Full text, all 24 PDF pages. Relevant locations: Theorem 2.1 (p. 8), Theorem 5.1 (pp. 20–21), Examples 5.1–5.2, and Section 6, item (1) (p. 22).
- **Role:** Auxiliary background for B171/B172. The cross-2 Orlicz-index range is explicitly outside the theorem ranges.

## Checked claims

Theorem 2.1 (PDF p. 8) is a noncommutative Marcinkiewicz interpolation result: if a sublinear map between semifinite von Neumann algebras is simultaneously weak type ((p_0,p_0)) and ((p_1,p_1)), and the Orlicz function has (p_0<p_\Phi\le q_\Phi<p_1), then (\tau_2\Phi(|Tx|)\le C\tau_1\Phi(|x|)), with the constant depending on the endpoint exponents and Φ.

The paper’s Theorem 5.1 (PDF pp. 20–21) gives Φ-moment analogues of noncommutative Burkholder–Gundy inequalities when the Orlicz indices lie strictly below 2 or strictly above 2. Example 5.1 (PDF p. 21) uses (\Phi(t)=t^a\log(1+t^b)), (p_\Phi=a), (q_\Phi=a+b); Example 5.2 gives another cross-2 case. Section 6, item (1) (PDF p. 22) explicitly says the noncommutative Φ-moment Burkholder–Gundy inequalities for (1<p_\Phi\le2\le q_\Phi<\infty) remain open in that 2010 paper. This is the source’s historical assessment, not a claim about current status.
