## Exact item

PDF p. 2, Entry 8, Conjecture 16 considers symmetric deterministic tensors (T_1,ldots,T_n\in(\mathbb R^d)^{\otimes r}), independent standard real Gaussians (g_i), (p\ge2), and
\[
\|T\|_{\mathrm{sym}(I_p)}=\sup_{\|x\|_p\le1}|\langle T,x^{\otimes r}\rangle|.
\]
The target is
\[
\mathbb E\Big\|\sum_i g_iT_i\Big\|_{\mathrm{sym}(I_p)}
\le \widetilde O_{r,p}\!\left(d^{1/2-1/p}\Big(\sum_i\|T_i\|_{\mathrm{sym}(I_p)}^2\Big)^{1/2}\right),
\]
where the tilde hides multiplicative factors depending on fixed (r,p) and polylogarithms in (d,n).

## What the source says is known

The authors single out (r=p=2) as the symmetric-matrix Euclidean operator-norm case and cite a logarithmic matrix estimate. They state that the tensor type-2 bound from [BGJ+24] settles (p\ge2r), while the range (2\le p<2r) remains the interesting gap for this formulation. The same page explains that matrix trace-power estimates do not carry over directly to general high-order injective norms.

Do not read the matrix illustration as asserting that “spectral norm” means the diagonal symmetric ℓp norm for all (p,r). The source itself defines the latter separately. Nor does its “open” label establish a complete current-status search.
