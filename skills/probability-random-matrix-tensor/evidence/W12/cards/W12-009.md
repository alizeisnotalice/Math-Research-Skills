## Model and main theorem

Let (X=A_0+\sum_{i=1}^n g_iA_i\in M_d(\mathbb C)), where (g_i) are independent standard real Gaussians and (A_i) are deterministic. Define the free model (X_{\rm free}=A_0\otimes1+\sum_i A_i\otimes s_i), with ((s_i)) a free semicircular family. The covariance parameters (PDF pp. 8–10) are

\[
\sigma(X)^2=\|\mathbb E (X-\mathbb EX)^*(X-\mathbb EX)\|\vee\|\mathbb E (X-\mathbb EX)(X-\mathbb EX)^*\|,
\]

\[
v(X)^2=\|\operatorname{Cov}(X)\|,\qquad
\sigma_*(X)^2=\sup_{\|u\|=\|w\|=1}\mathbb E|\langle u,(X-\mathbb EX)w\rangle|^2,
\quad \widetilde v(X)^2=v(X)\sigma(X).
\]

For self-adjoint (X), Theorem 2.1 (original PDF physical p. 10) is a **one-sided spectral inclusion**. With

\[
\mathbb P\!\left\{\operatorname{sp}(X)\subseteq \operatorname{sp}(X_{\rm free})+
C\{\widetilde v(X)(\log d)^{3/4}+\sigma_*(X)t\}[-1,1]\right\}\ge 1-e^{-t^2},\quad t\ge0.
\]

The event above says every eigenvalue of (X) lies within the indicated **upper neighbourhood** of (operatorname{sp}(X_{\rm free})); it does not bound the reverse distance and must not be rewritten as a Hausdorff estimate. Corollary 2.2 (physical pp. 10–11) gives a one-sided high-probability upper bound for the spectral norm of the general complex Gaussian matrix model and the expectation bound
\[
\mathbb E\|X\|\le\|X_{\rm free}\|+C\widetilde v(X)(\log d)^{3/4}.
\]
Theorem 2.7 (physical p. 11) is a separate two-sided comparison of normalized Schatten (2p)-moments. The text on p. 11 explicitly contrasts that two-sided moment statement with the one-sided results in §2.1. In particular, do not confuse Theorem 2.1 here with W12-010, Theorem 2.2, which does state a two-sided spectral Hausdorff bound under its own assumptions. Theorem 2.10 gives strong asymptotic freeness under its separate jointly-Gaussian-entry and small-covariance assumptions. The universality result for independent bounded non-Gaussian matrix summands is separately stated as Theorem 1.4 / Theorem 2.4, not silently included in the Gaussian theorem.

## Proof mechanism and limits

The proof of Theorem 2.1 is in Section 6 (within the proof block on physical pp. 26–42). It first establishes the required moment growth by interpolating between scalar-Gaussian and Wigner-matrix coefficients; the derivative is bounded using crossing pairings, with the crossing parameter controlled by \(\sqrt{v(X)\sigma(X)}=\widetilde v(X)\). The spectral inclusion then follows from the moment estimates plus the ultracontractive bounds developed earlier; Gaussian concentration with parameter \(\sigma_*(X)\) supplies the \(t\)-term. This proof route supports the stated one-sided inclusion only. Section 8 gives an obstruction to replacing \(v(X)\) by a universally smaller parameter obeying the paper’s natural invariances.

These are operator/spectral-norm results for matrices and free operators. The spectral norm is the ℓ₂ matrix operator norm; it is not a substitute for the general-order or non-Euclidean injective norms in C120.
