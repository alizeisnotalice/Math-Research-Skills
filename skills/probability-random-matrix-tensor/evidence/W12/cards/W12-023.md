# W12-023 — Bekjan–Chen–Liu–Jiao, weak Orlicz martingale bounds

- **Identity:** Turdebek N. Bekjan, Zeqian Chen, Peide Liu, Yong Jiao, *Noncommutative Weak Orlicz Spaces and Martingale Inequalities*, arXiv:1006.0091v2 (2010).
- **Read:** Full text, all 17 PDF pages. Relevant locations: Theorem 4.1 (pp. 9–10), Theorem 5.1 (p. 12), Theorems 5.3–5.4 and Remark 5.2 (pp. 13–16).
- **Role:** Relevant weak-Orlicz background for B171; not its endpoint splitting conjecture.

## Checked result

The paper defines the weak Orlicz quasi-norm by (\|x\|_{\Phi,w}=\sup_{t>0}t\Phi(\mu_t(x))) and proves a weak-Orlicz Marcinkiewicz interpolation theorem (Theorem 4.1). For a finite von Neumann algebra with normalized normal faithful trace and its stated filtration, Theorem 5.1 (PDF p. 12) bounds the weak-Φ moment of a martingale transform with bounded symbol whenever (1<a_\Phi\le b_\Phi<\infty). Theorem 5.3 gives weak-Φ Khintchine equivalences for (1<a_\Phi\le b_\Phi<2) and (2<a_\Phi\le b_\Phi<\infty); Theorem 5.4 gives the corresponding martingale square-function decompositions/equivalences in those same two regimes.

The estimates control weak-Φ quantities under the displayed Orlicz-index restrictions; they do not supply the B171 assertion that a single decomposition (x=y+z) controls both column and row square-function singular-value tails simultaneously for every threshold (t). In particular, Remark 5.2 leaves the weak-Φ Khintchine results open when (1<a_\Phi\le2\le b_\Phi<\infty). Do not identify these weak-Orlicz bounds with the strong Φ-moment endpoint split.
