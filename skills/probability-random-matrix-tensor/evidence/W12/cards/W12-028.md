# W12-028 — Genest–Ouimet–Richards, Wishart principal-minor GPI

- **Identity:** Christian Genest, Frédéric Ouimet, Donald Richards, *On the Gaussian Product Inequality Conjecture for Disjoint Principal Minors of Wishart Random Matrices*, Electron. J. Probab. 29 (2024), Paper No. 166, DOI 10.1214/24-EJP1222, arXiv:2311.00202v3.
- **Read:** Full text, all 26 PDF pages, including Appendices A–C. Relevant locations: Conjectures 1.1 and 3.8 (pp. 5 and 10), Theorem 3.3/Corollary 3.4 (pp. 8–9), Theorem 3.12 (p. 10), and proof/finite-moment appendices (pp. 11–23).
- **Role:** Direct primary source for formal120 C205/C206.

## C205 — positive determinant moment inequality

Conjecture 1.1 (PDF p. 5): for (X\sim W_p(\alpha,\Sigma)), (\alpha>p-1), Σ positive definite, and a block partition with block sizes (p_i\in\mathbb N), Σ(p_i=p), the conjectured inequality for all ν(_i\ge0) is
\[
\mathbb E\prod_{i=1}^d\det(X_{ii})^{\nu_i}
\ge\prod_{i=1}^d\mathbb E\det(X_{ii})^{\nu_i}.
\]
Remark 1.2 says the (d=2) case was proved in Genest–Ouimet–Richards’ separate paper using a matrix-argument hypergeometric formula and a stronger quantitative result. Remark 1.3 gives finiteness by Hölder and single-minor moment formulas.

## C206 — lower-orthant event association

Conjecture 3.8 (PDF p. 10), in the setting of Theorem 3.3, asks for all (k\in\{2,\dots,d\}) and positive (t_i):
\[
\mathbb P\Big(\bigcap_{i=1}^d\{\det X_{ii}\le t_i\}\Big)
\ge
\mathbb P\Big(\bigcap_{i<k}\{\det X_{ii}\le t_i\}\Big)
\mathbb P\Big(\bigcap_{i\ge k}\{\det X_{ii}\le t_i\}\Big).
\]
This is a distributional event inequality, not an equivalent restatement of C205’s moments.

## Nearby proved results and interface

Theorem 3.3 and Corollary 3.4 (PDF pp. 8–9) prove product-type bounds for matrix-variate completely monotone functions, using a matrix-variate Laplace-transform order comparison with independent Wishart blocks. In particular, negative determinant powers satisfy bounds when (p_i−1)/2 < ν_i < α/2−(p_i−1)/2; the proof also gives an explicit upper integral bound. Theorem 3.12 (p. 10) treats matrix-variate Bernstein functions under the same Laplace-order framework. Theorem 3.14 (pp. 10–11) proves a GPI for coordinatewise monotone functions of the Wishart eigenvalue vector, whose density is MTP2. None of these statements proves C205 for positive determinant powers in general blocks or C206 for determinant-sublevel events. C205 and C206 remain distinct statements in this source; no transfer is asserted from eigenvalue MTP2 or completely-monotone Laplace inequalities to block-determinant events.
