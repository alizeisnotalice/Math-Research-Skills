# W12-029 — Genest–Ouimet–Richards, explicit two-minor moment formula

- **Identity:** Christian Genest, Frédéric Ouimet, Donald Richards, *An Explicit Wishart Moment Formula for the Product of Two Disjoint Principal Minors*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 153 (2025), 1299–1311, DOI 10.1090/proc/17077, arXiv:2409.14512v1.
- **Read:** Full text, all 14 PDF pages, including the exact formula and its proof.
- **Role:** C205 two-block base case and exact formula; not the arbitrary-block conjecture.

## Checked scope

For X ∼ Wₚ(α,Σ), α>p−1, positive-definite Σ, and two disjoint principal blocks of sizes p₁≤p₂, Theorem 3.1 (PDF pp. 5–9) gives, for νᵢ>−α/2+(pᵢ−1)/2,

E[det(X₁₁)^ν₁ det(X₂₂)^ν₂] = E[det(X₁₁)^ν₁] E[det(X₂₂)^ν₂] · ₂F₁(−ν₁,−ν₂; α/2; PPᵀ),

where A=Σ⁻¹ᐟ² and P=A₁₁⁻¹ᐟ² A₁₂ A₂₂⁻¹ᐟ². The proof transforms the Wishart density to a matrix-beta integral, applies matrix-argument ₀F₁/₁F₁/₂F₁ integral identities, then an Euler transformation. Corollary 3.4 (PDF pp. 6, 9–10) gives the sharper lower bound

₂F₁(−ν₁,−ν₂; α/2; PPᵀ) ≥ 1+|I−PPᵀ|^(α/2+ν₁+ν₂){₀F₁(α/2;ν₁ν₂PPᵀ)−1} ≥1

for ν₁,ν₂≥0. Thus it proves exactly the two-block C205 case and a quantitative strengthening, while the general joint-moment formula remains out of reach.

No implication to d≥3 follows merely by iterating the two-block result: a first grouped block changes the joint Wishart dependence relevant to the next split. The assumptions on real α, Σ, and block sizes must be retained.
