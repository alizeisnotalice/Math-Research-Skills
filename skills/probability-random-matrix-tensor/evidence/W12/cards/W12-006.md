# W12-006 — Boedihardjo, independent-entry random tensors

- **Identity:** March T. Boedihardjo, *Injective Norm of Random Tensors with Independent Entries*, arXiv:2412.21193v2 (2 January 2025), 19 PDF pages. Local title page and official arXiv metadata agree on identity.
- **Read:** All PDF pp. 1–19, including Theorem 1.1 and its proof (pp. 2–4, 12–18), the auxiliary metric/entropy lemmas, corollaries, and references.
- **Role:** A Euclidean, independent-entry injective-norm estimate; it does not establish a general correlated Gaussian-series estimate or an ℓp tensor theorem.

## Exact model and estimate

For (d,r\in\mathbb N), take independent standard real Gaussians (g_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}), deterministic coefficients (b_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}\in\mathbb R), and

\[
Z=\sum_{i_1,\ldots,i_r\in[d]}b_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}g_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}\,e_{i_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{i_r},\qquad
\|Z\|_{\rm inj}=\sup_{x_1,\ldots,x_r\in B_2^d}\langle Z,x_1\otimes\cdots\otimes x_r\rangle.
\]

Theorem 1.1 (PDF p. 2) gives

\[
\mathbb E\|Z\|_{\rm inj}\le \sqrt{2r}\sum_{k=1}^r\max_{i_{-k}\in[d]^{r-1}}\Big(\sum_{i_k=1}^d b_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}^2\Big)^{1/2}
+C r^3(\log d)^2\max_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}|b_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}|,
\]

with universal (C). Remark 1.2 on the same page gives the matching profile lower bound

\[
\big(\mathbb E\|Z\|_{\rm inj}^2\big)^{1/2}\ge\max_{k,i_{-k}}\Big(\sum_{i_k}b_{i_1,\ldots,i_r}^2\Big)^{1/2}.
\]

Theorem 1.1 also yields bounded independent centered-entry and Bernoulli versions (Corollaries 1.4–1.5, PDF pp. 3–4), with a variance-profile term and an additive (Cr^3(\log d)^2K) term. The Gaussian norm is (b_{\max})-Lipschitz in its independent standard Gaussian coordinates, giving the stated concentration about its mean (Remark 1.3, p. 3).

## Proof route and transfer boundary

The proof (PDF pp. 12–18) writes the injective norm as a Gaussian-process supremum on ((B_2^d)^r). It introduces a coefficient-weighted multilinear map τ, diagonal operators (D^{(k)}), and secondary metrics η⁽ᵏ⁾. Bounds on their Dudley entropy integrals follow from Maurey empirical covering; a Slepian–Fernique comparison with an added γ₂/Dudley error controls the process, and Gaussian concentration plus a union bound gives the coefficient-profile term. The non-Gaussian corollary uses Gaussian symmetrization, a Rosenthal-type moment bound for the coordinatewise variances, and Talagrand concentration.

The norm here is multilinear Euclidean injective norm. For (r=2) it is the matrix spectral norm; for (r>2), or for ℓp balls with (p\ne2), neither this norm nor this theorem can be substituted without a separate comparison. In particular, the theorem does not concern the diagonal symmetric ℓp norm in C120.
