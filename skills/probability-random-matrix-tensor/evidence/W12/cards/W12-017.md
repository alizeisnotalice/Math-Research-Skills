# W12-017 — Lust-Piquard–Pisier, noncommutative Khintchine/Paley

- **Identity:** Françoise Lust-Piquard and Gilles Pisier, *Non commutative Khintchine and Paley inequalities*, Arkiv för Matematik 29 (1991), DOI 10.1007/BF02384340. The local copy is the YMSC-hosted full PDF.
- **Read:** Full PDF pp. 1–20. The (C_1) Khintchine theorem/corollary and proof are on pp. 2, 8–10; later pages cover symmetric-ideal extensions, UMD multiplier results, and the factorization appendix.
- **Role:** Foundational matrix/Rademacher estimate referenced by W12-002 and Tropp; not the proof of a general tensor type estimate.

## Content used

The paper formulates Rademacher series in noncommutative (L_p)/Schatten spaces through row and column square-function norms. In the (C_1) case, Theorem II.1 and Corollary II.2 (PDF p. 9) yield the row/column decomposition form of the Khintchine estimate; the proof factors an analytic operator-valued function and controls the resulting two square functions. The paper also distinguishes the two square functions ( (\sum A_k^*A_k)^{1/2}) and ( (\sum A_kA_k^*)^{1/2}); they are not interchangeable for non-self-adjoint coefficients.

This card records the verified (C_1) section, not a verbatim restatement of all (p)-ranges of the 1991 paper. For the finite-dimensional self-adjoint Gaussian tail statement used in this skill, the directly checked formulation is Tropp’s Theorem 1.2 rather than relying on a transcription of this older PDF’s full theorem.
