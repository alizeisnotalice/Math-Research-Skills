# W12-007 — Tomioka–Suzuki, spectral norm of random tensors

- **Identity:** Ryota Tomioka and Taiji Suzuki, *Spectral Norm of Random Tensors*, arXiv:1407.1870v1 (2014), 5-page PDF. [Official arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1870); local-file SHA-256 is recorded in `sources.json`.
- **Read:** All physical PDF pp. 1–5, including the covering proof, both corollary proofs, and references. The main theorem and proof are on pp. 1–3; applications are on pp. 3–4.
- **Role:** An elementary Euclidean multilinear spectral-norm bound by nets. It is adjacent background for C120, not a Banach type-2 theorem for arbitrary correlated Gaussian tensor series.

## Model and theorem

For an order-(K) real tensor (X\in\mathbb R^{n_1\times\cdots\times n_K}), the paper uses the full multilinear Euclidean spectral norm
\[
\|X\|=\sup_{u_k\in S^{n_k-1}}|X(u_1,\ldots,u_K)|,
\qquad
X(u_1,\ldots,u_K)=\sum_{i_1,\ldots,i_K}X_{i_1\cdots i_K}\prod_{k=1}^K u_{k,i_k}.
\]
Lemma 1 (physical p. 1) assumes independent, centered entries with
\(\mathbb E e^{sX_{i_1\cdots i_K}}\le e^{\sigma^2s^2/2}\); for each fixed unit tuple it derives the two-sided scalar tail
\(\mathbb P(|X(u_1,\ldots,u_K)|\ge t)\le2e^{-t^2/(2\sigma^2)}\).
Theorem 1 (physical pp. 2–3) needs only this fixed-tuple tail assumption. With (K_0=\log(3/2)), it gives
\[
\|X\|\le\left(8\sigma^2\left[\sum_{k=1}^K n_k\log(2K/K_0)+\log(2/\delta)\right]\right)^{1/2}
\]
with probability at least (1-\delta). Thus the conclusion is for the full multilinear norm on a product of Euclidean spheres and subgaussian scalar process tails, not for general \(\ell_p\) injective norms.

## Proof and applications

Choose an \(\varepsilon\)-net in each sphere with \(\varepsilon=K_0/K\). At a maximizing tuple, expand each vector as its net point plus an error. Multilinearity bounds the sum of all error terms by \((e^{K\varepsilon}-1)\|X\|=\tfrac12\|X\|\), so \(\|X\|\le2\max_{\bar u_k\in C_k}|X(\bar u_1,\ldots,\bar u_K)|\). Each net has size at most \((2/\varepsilon)^{n_k}\); the fixed-tuple tail and a union bound yield the displayed probability bound.

Corollary 1 (physical pp. 3–4) handles a conditionally independent Gaussian-measurement construction with random subgaussian weights and the stated sample-size condition (M\ge2\log(2/\delta)). Corollary 2 (physical p. 4) handles (M) nonzero entries sampled uniformly without replacement: because no location is sampled twice, the squared fixed-tuple coefficients sum to at most one, giving the same scalar tail and hence Theorem 1.

## Limits for transfer

The proof depends on Euclidean sphere covering numbers and only yields a logarithmic factor in tensor order (K) and a dimension term proportional to \(\sum_k n_k\). It gives no estimate for the type-2 constant (C_{r,p}(d)), no general-\(p\) injective-norm bound, and no assertion for an arbitrary correlated Gaussian tensor series. In the matrix case (K=2), the norm agrees with the Euclidean operator norm; this does not identify matrix spectral norm with a general-order tensor injective norm.
