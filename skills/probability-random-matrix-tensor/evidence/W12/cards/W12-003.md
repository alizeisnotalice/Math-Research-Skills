# W12-003 — Aden-Ali, PAC-Bayesian tensor norm bound

- **Identity:** Ishaq Aden-Ali, *On the Injective Norm of Sums of Random Tensors and the Moments of Gaussian Chaoses*, arXiv:2503.10580v1, 2025. Local PDF `evidence/fulltexts/W12-003.pdf`, 12 pages.
- **Read:** Full PDF pp. 1–12. Definitions and Theorem 1.2/Corollaries 1.3–1.4 are on pp. 2–3; proof and appendix were also read.
- **Role:** Direct variance-sensitive upper bound related to C120, not a proof of its sum-of-squared-injective-norm formulation.

## Result checked

The model is (T=\sum_{k=1}^n\xi_kT_k) with independent, mean-zero, 1-subgaussian scalars and deterministic order-(r) tensors. The ℓp injective norm is the full multilinear supremum over (r) (possibly different) ℓp unit balls. Definition 1.1 (PDF p. 2) gives contraction/Frobenius variance parameters σ(_{(ℓ,p)}). Theorem 1.2 states, for (p\ge2) and β>0,
\[
\mathbb E\|T\|_{I_p}\le
\left[\left(\sum_{\ell=0}^r\beta^{1-\ell}\sigma_{(\ell,p)}^2\right)
\left(\sum_{t=1}^r d_t^{1-2/p}\right)\right]^{1/2}.
\]
Corollary 1.3 optimizes β to replace the sum by
\(\sigma_{(1,p)}^2+r\max_{2\le\ell\le r}\sigma_{(\ell,p)}^{2/\ell}\sigma_{(0,p)}^{(2\ell-2)/\ell}\).

For symmetric square tensors, Corollary 1.4 (PDF p. 3) transfers to the BGJ variance parameters and removes a log (d) from their first variance term. Its remaining mixed variance terms can still dominate. Neither the theorem nor its corollary identifies those terms with (\sum_k\|T_k\|_{\mathrm{sym}(I_p)}^2); that comparison would need a separate estimate. The paper explicitly notes that in the Euclidean matrix regime its bound may be worse than NCK when the Frobenius contribution is large.

## Proof method

The proof uses a PAC-Bayesian variational bound (Donsker–Varadhan) for a smoothed supremum, rather than the covering-number/chaining proof in W12-002. The independent subgaussian scalar assumption and the variance parameters are part of the result; do not silently replace them by arbitrary dependence or a single type-2 parameter.
