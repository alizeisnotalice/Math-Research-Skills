# Anticommuting Gaussian series: verified scope

Source: Suvrit Sra, *Beyond Small Balls: A Determinant Inequality for Functions of Matrix Gaussian
Series*, v1 author preprint (local SHA-256
`eaab9688ec2cf012277a08e90523d4e630e03007356ecbb76c6140afe7e7229f`), physical pp. 4–5,
Section 3.1.

The source gives the tensor-product anticommuting involutions and observes that their normalized
Gaussian series has absolute value `|g|/sqrt(j)` times the identity.  The helper verifies the finite
Clifford relations and a deterministic specialization of the square identity with exact integers.

The source’s averaged comparison and determinant inequality require additional Gaussian,
localization, matrix-weighted Poincare, and interval-analysis arguments.  They are outside this
finite algebraic helper.
